President Lee holds capital markets meeting at Korea Exchange… “We must create a high-level body for reform,” with a separate order to SCIA to crack down on unfair practices

At a policy meeting chaired at the Korea Exchange in Yeouido, Seoul, on the 13th, the president mentioned a high-level body to lead capital market reform.

President Lee Jae-myung chaired a capital markets policy meeting at the Korea Exchange PR hall in Yeouido, Seoul, on the 13th. There the president said, “There seem to be far too many reforms that need to be made specifically for the stock exchange and the capital markets,” and that a high-level body should be created to take charge of reform. The president went on to say that a separate order had been given to the Serious Crimes Investigation Agency (SCIA) to crack down strictly on unfair practices in the capital markets. The president cited abuse of management rights by controlling shareholders and stock price manipulation, and said there were also suspicions that share prices are being kept low to pay less inheritance tax. Saying the goal is the normalization of the asset market, the president said the government would focus on restoring market trust.

The president said the government’s will to reform the capital markets was “being put to the test once again.” At the meeting, proposals were made such as activating the Securities Market Stabilization Fund (Stabilization Fund) to reduce stock market volatility and encouraging companies to step up investor relations (IR). The Stabilization Fund is a fund set up in advance to put money into the market when share prices plunge, to cushion the shock. The name, composition and set-up date of the high-level body, and whether the Stabilization Fund will actually be activated, have not been confirmed.

Politics · Opinion Polls✓ 4 outlets · the reason for the gap between polls is unconfirmed

President Lee’s rating 39% in Gallup Korea, 44% in National Barometer Survey… polls differ; Han Dong-hoon’s return to party: 29% for, 40% against

In a Gallup Korea survey of 1,002 voters nationwide on the 10th–12th, President Lee Jae-myung’s job approval was 39% and disapproval 50%. Approval was down 2 percentage points from the previous survey. On party support, the Democratic Party of Korea stood at 39% and the People Power Party at 26%, a gap of 13 percentage points, and approval among people in their 30s was 23%. In the same survey, former leader Han Dong-hoon’s return to the People Power Party drew 29% in favor and 40% against. In the National Barometer Survey (NBS), in which four polling firms questioned 1,001 adults on the 9th–11th, approval was 44% and disapproval 47%. Approval was down 2 percentage points and disapproval up 3 percentage points. The two surveys differ in firm and method, so the figures are hard to compare directly, and it cannot be said which is right. Asked about the president stating policy direction directly on social media and YouTube, 51% said it “should be cut back” and 42% said it “should continue.”

Economy · Maps and Security✓ 2 outlets · the selection of Tmap Mobility as partner rests on one outlet’s report and is unconfirmed

Government: “Google confirmed to have met security conditions for overseas transfer of high-precision maps”… permit withdrawn if violated

On the 13th, the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport held a consultative body meeting on the overseas transfer of basic survey results, and said it had received, reviewed and confirmed a report on how Google had met the security conditions attached to the conditional permit granted in February for transferring its maps at a scale of 1:5,000 overseas. A scale of 1:5,000 is a precise map on which 1 cm equals 50 m on the ground. On the 4th, Google submitted a final compliance report covering coordinate display, image security processing, data processing and security incident response procedures. The government confirmed that coordinate removal and image security processing had been carried out, and the permit will be withdrawn if the conditions are broken.

One report said Google had selected Tmap Mobility as the partner to process the base map data needed for its domestic map service, and that personal information and movement data would be excluded. The positions of Google and Tmap Mobility on this have not been confirmed. When the transfer will actually begin and what will change for users were not confirmed in this announcement.

3-story apartment building collapses in Brooklyn, New York… at least 8 dead, 2 of them children

A 3-story apartment building collapsed in Flatbush, Brooklyn, New York, on the evening of Wednesday the 11th (local time). On the 12th, authorities said at least 8 people had died, and New York Mayor Zohran Mamdani said at a press conference that 2 of the dead were children. Injured people were also reported, and rescuers kept searching the rubble.

The death toll is based on the authorities’ announcement on the 12th and may change as the search continues. The cause of the collapse, the building’s safety inspection history before the collapse, and the final casualty count have not been confirmed.

President Lee to hold separate bilateral talks with Thai and Philippine leaders in Manila on the 16th… amid ASEAN and APEC trip
