General Secretary and State President Tô Lâm presides over ceremonial welcome for Swiss President on official visit to Vietnam

On the afternoon of Oct. 17, at the National Assembly House, the welcome ceremony was held just before the evening banquet; it is the official visit of the Swiss head of state to Hanoi.

On the afternoon of Saturday, Oct. 17, at the National Assembly House in Hanoi, General Secretary and State President Tô Lâm and his wife presided over a ceremonial welcome for the Swiss President, who is on an official visit to Vietnam. Thanh Niên, VnEconomy and Dân trí all reported on the ceremony, with a posting time of 17:15 Hanoi time. Our news-analysis system ranked the story about the welcome ceremony among the events mentioned by the most outlets in the tracking window, with five leading outlets running a full-coverage piece.

That evening, Dân trí reported that Mr. Tô Lâm hosted a banquet for the Swiss President. This report was posted later, at nearly 23:00, and so far appears in only two sources in our data, so details such as the menu, the guest list or the toast should be read from each original article rather than inferred from headlines. The newspapers have not published the full program of the working sessions that follow, so the content of the talks is not yet in the verified reports.

That the General Secretary also holds the post of State President is notable in institutional terms: the person presiding over the welcome is both head of the Party and head of state, so the ceremony for the Swiss President was placed at the highest level of Vietnam’s diplomacy. The venue is the National Assembly House, where the opening ceremony of the Second Session of the 16th National Assembly took place that morning, so the site hosted two major events on the same day.

For readers at home, the most useful information right now is what has been confirmed: the time and place of the welcome ceremony, who presided, and the evening banquet. What has not been confirmed includes the list of agreements, the specific areas of cooperation and the length of the visit. We will update when the authorities or at least two independent outlets publish the content of the talks. Until then, the visit should be understood as a state-level protocol event rather than a package of economic commitments that has already taken shape.

Second Session of the 16th National Assembly opens, Government submits 2027 growth target of 10% or more

On the morning of Oct. 17, before the opening ceremony of the Second Session of the 16th National Assembly, General Secretary and State President Tô Lâm, together with Party and State leaders and delegates, entered the Mausoleum to pay respects to President Hồ Chí Minh, per Thanh Niên and Dân trí. The speech by the Chairman of the National Assembly, published by Dân trí, stressed not using caution as a reason for delay; another report quoted him as saying the session will consider passing a large volume of laws to open the way for development in time, and eight outlets carried this. On figures, VnExpress said the Government submitted a target for economic growth in 2027 of 10% or more. This is high compared with the usual annual targets, and the report does not yet include the scenarios or conditions for achieving it, so readers cannot yet judge whether it is attainable. The same day, the Prime Minister was quoted by VnExpress as striving to complete 176,000 social housing and rental homes in 2027, a goal tied to the housing program for low-income people.

Deputy Prime Minister asks State Bank of Vietnam to cut lending rates by 1.5-2% for production and business

According to VnExpress, VnEconomy and Dân trí, Deputy Prime Minister Nguyễn Văn Thắng asked the State Bank of Vietnam to urgently implement measures to cut lending rates by 1.5 to 2% for production and business activity. The report was posted at 09:39 on Oct. 17, Hanoi time. The outlets did not say clearly which base rate the cut is measured against, which customer groups it applies to, or which commercial banks must act first.

The same day, VnExpress reported a proposal to raise the revenue threshold defining small and medium-sized enterprises to VND 400 billion. The two items fall within the same set of issues: the cost of capital and the definition of the groups eligible for support policies. If the threshold is raised, more businesses would qualify for incentives, but the proposal is only at the consultation stage and is not yet in effect. Borrowers should follow announcements from their own bank, because actual interest rates change only when the credit contract is adjusted.

Proposal to transfer investigative authority of the Supreme People’s Procuracy to the Ministry of Public Security

Dân trí and aggregator sources reported on Oct. 17 a proposal to transfer the authority of the Investigation Agency under the Supreme People’s Procuracy to the Ministry of Public Security. This is a proposal, not a decision: the document still has to go through consultation and a vote by the National Assembly. Our tracking system recorded five leading outlets running this topic together, so it is among the most mentioned items of the weekend.

A related item: the Ministry of Public Security said it will trace unsafe food back to its source and also deal with those who aid it, per three outlets. In the discussion, a National Assembly delegate was quoted by Dân trí as saying that for fraudulent electricity meters and petrol pumps, a fine alone is not enough. The three items appearing together on the opening day suggest the focus of discussion is law enforcement and handling violations, but no document has yet been passed.

Gold must be paid by bank transfer from VND 5 million, a tanker hits a naval mine in Hormuz and Ukraine calls on the EU for aid

From Oct. 16, same-day gold trades worth VND 5 million or more must be paid by bank transfer, per Dân trí and Google News Vietnam. The rule aims to leave a trail of transactions; retail sellers and small buyers should ask the shop how to pay before trading. We have not seen a report stating the penalty for violations.

Abroad, VnExpress carried Iran’s statement that a supertanker hit a naval mine and caught fire in Hormuz; this is one side’s statement, so independent confirmation is needed. Dân trí and VnExpress reported the Ukrainian President speaking about the consequences of Starlink’s speed being throttled and calling on the EU to provide nearly $79 billion and 300 Patriot missiles. WTI crude in the latest market data stood at $99.02, up 2.61%, while the S&P 500 fell 1.09% to 7,659.44.
