Prime Minister Lê Minh Hưng ends Cambodia visit: 6 cooperation documents exchanged, two countries aim for $20 billion in trade

The visit to Laos and Cambodia from Oct. 8 to 10 has ended; Tiền Phong tallies nearly 40 cooperation documents signed or exchanged over two days in both countries.

On the afternoon of Oct. 9 in Phnom Penh, Prime Minister Lê Minh Hưng held talks with Cambodian Prime Minister Hun Manet after an official welcome ceremony at the Peace Palace, then witnessed the exchange of six cooperation documents in security, drug prevention and control, trade, culture, and education and training, according to Báo Tin tức, Công Thương, VTV and Nông nghiệp và Môi trường. The two sides agreed to strive to raise two-way trade turnover to $20 billion.

On the morning of Oct. 10, the two prime ministers had breakfast and held a working session, and at midday the delegation left Techo International Airport, ending the two-day official visit of Oct. 9 and 10, according to VietnamPlus and Báo Tin tức. Mr. Hưng also met National Assembly President Khuon Sudary and Senate President Hun Sen, paid his respects to King Norodom Sihamoni and visited the Queen Mother. Earlier, in Laos, he signed 11 documents. State media have not given details of each document; the $20 billion goal is a target, not a result.

Loud blast at Riyadh airport, many airlines cancel flights as the Houthis escalate attacks on Saudi Arabia

A loud explosion sounded at King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh on Oct. 10, according to Reuters as relayed by SCMP, Cyprus Mail, Deccan Herald and ThePrint, as the Iran-backed Houthi rebels step up missile fire at the kingdom. Some outlets report several people injured; officials have not confirmed details, and Saudi Arabia’s civil aviation authority has not responded to requests for comment.

The flight-tracking site Flightradar24 shows all flights bound for Riyadh diverted or cancelled. Kuwait Airways, Etihad and Qatar Airways cancelled flights, with some cancellations running into Monday. The outlets recall that earlier in the week an attack on this airport killed four people and damaged a Saudia aircraft, just as Riyadh is about to host major energy and investment conferences. The cause of the blast has not been confirmed.

Russian diesel deal: Zelensky calls it an investment in the war, Mr. Trump says Ukraine should “get a new leader”, Democrats criticise

After the diesel deal with Russia announced on Oct. 9 (see the Oct. 10 issue), the new developments on Oct. 10 were political reactions. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky wrote on Telegram that letting Russia sell fuel is an investment in the war and linked the deal to the air strikes on Zaporizhzhia; Times of Israel and Yahoo report that those strikes killed at least 15 people. According to The Hill’s headline, Mr. Trump replied that Ukraine should “get a new leader”.

EU foreign policy representative Kaja Kallas said Europe will not ease pressure on Russia, and Democratic lawmakers in the US, including Senator Blumenthal, called for the deal to be scrapped, according to The Hill, Al Jazeera and OPB. Analysts cited by Al Jazeera say the deal does little for the global energy crisis. The volume of Russian diesel actually delivered has not been verified.

SJC gold bars rise VND 1.8 million to VND 141.8 million to 144.8 million per tael, with the buy-sell spread up to VND 5 million in some places

On the morning of Oct. 10, SJC gold bars were listed at VND 141.8 million to 144.8 million per tael (buy - sell), up VND 1.8 million in both directions from the previous day, according to Dân Việt, Công Thương, Kênh14 and Người Đưa Tin. By 1:30 p.m., the price at Bảo Tín Mạnh Hải was still around that level; the spread at this brand at times reached VND 5 million per tael.

World spot gold was about $4,184 an ounce at 4:30 a.m. on Oct. 10 Vietnam time, according to Kitco; in Friday’s session in US time, gold closed at $4,216.3 (+1.43%). One outlet reports an 8:31 opening price unchanged from the previous day, so the outlets differ depending on when they updated.

Portuguese football federation provisionally suspends Ronaldo after he leaves the national team camp

The Portuguese Football Federation (FPF) on Oct. 10 opened disciplinary proceedings and provisionally suspended Cristiano Ronaldo, 41, after he left the national team’s camp in Copenhagen ahead of the Nations League match against Denmark, according to ESPN, Irish Times, NBC News, Channels TV and Onmanorama. According to the outlets, he was unhappy with coach Jorge Jesus after staying on the bench in the match against Norway and said the coach twice went back on his word about his role.

The FPF said the suspension is a precautionary measure, not an automatic one, and the case has been given urgent priority and referred to the Disciplinary Investigation Committee. There is no final sanction, and the details of the file are kept confidential until the investigation ends. Ronaldo apologised for not greeting fans after the Norway match and for leaving his teammates, and said he will be ready to be called up after serving his punishment. ESPN notes the decision is dated Oct. 9 in the statement; the other outlets do not give this date.
