Vietnam signs free trade agreement with four EFTA countries after 14 years of negotiations

The signing took place on the morning of Oct. 18 in Hanoi, during the official visit of the Swiss President and the Liechtenstein Prime Minister.

On the morning of Sunday, Oct. 18, in Hanoi, Minister of Industry and Trade Lê Mạnh Hùng and representatives of the member states of the European Free Trade Association (EFTA) signed the Vietnam-EFTA Free Trade Agreement, abbreviated VEFTA, per Dân trí, VietnamPlus and Znews. The signing was witnessed by General Secretary and State President Tô Lâm. VnEconomy and VietnamPlus said the EFTA side included Swiss President and Economics Minister Guy Parmelin and Liechtenstein Prime Minister and Finance Minister Brigitte Haas. EFTA has four members: Switzerland, Norway, Iceland and Liechtenstein. Tuổi Trẻ and VnBusiness said the agreement closes 14 years of negotiations. The outlets cited Ministry of Industry and Trade figures showing that two-way trade before the signing held steady at nearly $2 billion a year. VnExpress and Thanh Niên said the agreement contains commitments on tariffs, technical barriers, plant and animal quarantine, customs and trade remedies, along with provisions on services, investment and intellectual property. The outlets have not published the schedule for eliminating tariffs line by line.

The signing was part of Mr. Parmelin’s official visit to Vietnam on Oct. 17 and 18, per VietnamPlus and Tuổi Trẻ. The same day, Chairman of the National Assembly Trần Thanh Mẫn received Mr. Parmelin at the National Assembly House as the two countries marked 55 years since establishing diplomatic relations. Nhân Dân, VietnamPlus and Dân trí reported that Mr. Mẫn asked Switzerland to work with the other EFTA countries to speed up ratification of the agreement so that it takes effect soon. Mr. Parmelin stressed the role of protecting intellectual property and geographical indications, expressed a wish to cooperate on clean energy, transport and vocational training, and invited Vietnamese leaders to the AI Summit in Geneva in June 2027. Also on Oct. 18, the Liechtenstein Prime Minister, who is on an official visit to Vietnam from Oct. 17 to 19, was received by General Secretary Tô Lâm and held talks with Prime Minister Lê Minh Hưng. Dân trí and VietnamPlus said Vietnam and Liechtenstein also signed the Agreement on the Avoidance of Double Taxation after the talks.

Prime Minister Lê Minh Hưng: land and real estate prices will certainly fall once the amended Land Law is passed

At the National Assembly group discussion on the afternoon of Oct. 18, Prime Minister Lê Minh Hưng said that once the National Assembly passes the Land Law (amended), the State will strongly regulate land prices and decide land prices, and he concluded that land prices and real estate prices will certainly fall, per Dân trí and Znews. Dân trí also recorded him as saying that the apartment market has gone down after the Government stepped up social housing and rental housing. Dân trí and Thanh Niên reported earlier that the draft law drops the principle of pricing land by the market; per Dân trí, land price tables and adjustment coefficients would be drawn up by provincial people’s committees and decided by provincial people’s councils. Thanh Niên, VnExpress and Tuổi Trẻ reported that the Prime Minister said the Government is determined to cut intermediate steps in the distribution of petrol and oil, electricity, medicines and medical equipment so that people do not pay high prices. The outlets did not give a specific size of decline or a timeline.

Minister of Home Affairs: provinces and cities may take the initiative in proposing to merge communes and wards that are too small or split areas that are too large

Answering delegates at the National Assembly group discussion on the afternoon of Oct. 18, Minister of Home Affairs Nguyễn Tiến Hải said provinces and cities may take the initiative in proposing to merge communes and wards that are too small or to split areas that are too large, per Tuổi Trẻ and VnExpress. VnExpress noted that proposals must go through the collective leadership of the locality and gather the opinions of residents before being submitted to the central level. Tuổi Trẻ said the Ministry of Home Affairs is studying a model of core communes and wards, with each locality choosing about 3 to 4 units, and will report to the Government and the Politburo before issuing guidance. On the morning of the same day, per Dân trí and VnExpress, Deputy Prime Minister Phạm Thị Thanh Trà said the Government cut 53% of the cost and 53% of the time of carrying out administrative procedures in one peak month. She also pointed to a mindset of avoidance and fear of responsibility among some officials, and to difficulties in running the two-tier local government system, especially at the commune level.

Economy · Ho Chi Minh CityPress ✓ 4 outlets · the city’s full-year target figure differs between outlets

Party Secretary Trần Lưu Quang: Ho Chi Minh City will not chase double-digit growth at all costs

At the National Assembly discussion group on Oct. 18, Ho Chi Minh City Party Committee Secretary Trần Lưu Quang said the city must grow sustainably, with no quick fixes, per Dân trí, VnExpress and Thanh Niên. Thanh Niên wrote that he acknowledged the risk of missing the double-digit growth target. Nhân Dân and Thanh Niên said the city’s gross regional domestic product (GRDP) grew 9.06% after nine months, so the fourth quarter needs growth of 13.23% to reach the full-year target. VnExpress noted that the city has asked for a report on growth progress every two weeks.

The same day, Dân trí, VnExpress and Nhân Dân reported that the main-span assembly of the nipa-palm-leaf-shaped pedestrian bridge over the Saigon River, weighing more than 3,400 tonnes, including a 1,400-tonne steel arch and a 2,000-tonne supporting frame, left the port on the morning of Oct. 18 and anchored overnight. The outlets said the towing convoy is expected to reach the Bạch Đằng Wharf area at about 14:00 to 15:00 on Oct. 19, and the span-closing ceremony is expected on Oct. 21.

Explosion and fire at Canadian military base, US rejects Starlink accusation, super typhoon Champi heads toward Taiwan

VnExpress, VietnamPlus, Dân trí and Tuổi Trẻ reported the explosion and fire on the evening of Oct. 17 at the Wainwright military base in Alberta, Canada. Dân trí and VietnamPlus, citing Global News, said at least 7 people died, a figure the authorities have not confirmed; Defence Minister David McGuinty confirmed there were casualties and Prime Minister Mark Carney called it a serious incident. Tuổi Trẻ said the fire was brought under control at about 23:00. Dân trí and VnExpress reported that US Secretary of State Marco Rubio rejected the Ukrainian President’s accusation that Washington deliberately limited Starlink speeds, while President Trump said he did not know who gave the order and that it was certainly not him.

VietnamPlus, Nhân Dân and VnExpress said super typhoon Champi has reached level 16, gusting above level 17, with a probability of about 10 to 20% of entering the northeastern East Sea and an 80 to 90% chance of affecting Taiwan and the Chinese coast.
