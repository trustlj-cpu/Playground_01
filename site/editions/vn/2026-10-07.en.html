Ho Chi Minh City police bust a ring that sold more than 10,000 tonnes of pork tainted with a banned substance, worth over VND 600 billion

Nine suspects have been arrested and detained; some quarantine inspectors are alleged to have taken money to issue bogus certificates, according to police information relayed by the press.

Ho Chi Minh City police announced on Oct. 5 that they had broken up a ring that used the banned substance Salbutamol to fatten pigs and then put more than 10,000 tonnes of meat on the market, worth an estimated VND 600 billion or more, according to VTV, VietnamPlus, Dân tộc và Phát triển and An ninh Thủ đô. Investigators opened criminal proceedings against nine suspects and put them in temporary detention on charges related to food safety, trading in banned goods, and giving and taking bribes.

According to police, farm owners mixed Salbutamol into feed, then stopped giving the banned substance and gave the pigs liver detox medicine or electrolytes before shipping them out, in order to get past tests. The meat was sold in Ho Chi Minh City and many southern provinces from January 2026. Some quarantine inspectors are alleged to have taken money and issued bogus certificates so that the violating meat could keep being transported. These are allegations by the investigating agency, not a court’s conclusion; police said they will continue to widen the investigation.

Foreign affairsPress ✓ 6 outlets · content from the official communiqué

General Secretary Tô Lâm holds talks with Panama’s president; the two countries exchange documents on maritime transport and fighting transnational crime

On the afternoon of Oct. 6 in Hanoi, General Secretary and President Tô Lâm held talks with the President of Panama, José Raúl Mulino, who is on an official visit to Vietnam from Oct. 5 to 8, according to VOV, VnExpress, Nhân Dân, Tiền Phong and Báo Công Thương. The two sides witnessed an exchange of documents, including an agreement on maritime transport cooperation, an accord on preventing and combating transnational crime, and memoranda of understanding on agriculture, customs, tourism, aviation and diplomacy.

According to VnExpress, President Mulino said Panama is ready to serve as a gateway connecting Vietnam with Latin America and regards Vietnam as a priority partner in the Asia-Pacific. This is an official statement; its effect will show in how the deals are carried out.

VN-Index rises for a second session to 1,759.08 points, but foreign investors net sell more than VND 2,500 billion, with HDB alone above VND 2,000 billion

The VN-Index closed Tuesday’s session on Oct. 6 at 1,759.08 points, up 5.88 points (0.34%), according to DNSE and Nhân Dân. On HOSE, 127 stocks rose and 177 fell; matched trading value was about VND 18,715 billion, much higher than in the previous session.

Foreign net selling was heavy: outlets put it at roughly VND 2,520 billion to VND 2,730 billion depending on how it is tallied, with HDB net sold by more than VND 2,037 billion, followed by VJC and PNJ. That more stocks fell than rose suggests the index gain rested on a few large stocks.

Heavy rain across a wide area of central Vietnam since Oct. 5, with warnings of flash floods and landslides in five provinces and cities

Cold air combined with the intertropical convergence zone has brought heavy rain over a wide area of the Central region since Oct. 5, according to VietnamPlus and Báo Tin tức, along with warnings of flash floods and landslides in five provinces and cities. From the night of Oct. 4 to Oct. 6, the area from Thanh Hóa to Huế has had moderate rain and heavy to very heavy rain in places, according to the meteorological agency as relayed by VTV.

Rain on steep terrain can saturate the soil and make landslides likely. This is a warning, not a damage tally; DailyDrop has not seen official damage figures for Oct. 6.

World · FinancePress ✓ 4 outlets · figures from CNBC, Yahoo Finance

S&P 500 and Nasdaq close at records, oil nearly flat with Brent at $100.58

The S&P 500 rose 0.58% to a record 7,818.93 points, the Nasdaq Composite rose 0.45% to a record 27,599.79 points and the Dow Jones rose 253.38 points (0.49%) to 51,521.28 points in Tuesday’s session on Oct. 6 US time, according to TheStreet, Yahoo Finance and CNBC. Tech stocks led as cooler oil prices let investors pay more attention to corporate earnings.

Brent crude closed at $100.58 a barrel (+0.26%) and WTI at $89.44 a barrel (+0.01%). Gold was quoted at about $4,187 an ounce, up 0.73%, according to market data. One Bloomberg report said the index pulled back from its peak on inflation worries, but the report’s timing suggests it may describe a later session; DailyDrop uses figures that match across multiple sources.
