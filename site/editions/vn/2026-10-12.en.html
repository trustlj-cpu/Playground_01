Weather · UrbanPress ✓ 3 outlets · peak tide readings not yet compiled

High tides peak on the afternoon of Oct. 11: many Ho Chi Minh City streets deeply flooded, water spills into homes, shops close

Thanh Niên reports the Saigon River’s water level passed the previous historical level; the tide peak is forecast to last into today, Oct. 12.

On the afternoon of Sunday, Oct. 11, high tides in Ho Chi Minh City reached their peak. According to Thanh Niên, Dân trí and VnExpress, many streets were deeply flooded, water spilled into homes, and many shops closed or stood empty even though it was the weekend. Thanh Niên reported at 2:39 p.m. that the water level on the Saigon River had passed the previous historical level and was still rising; the outlets have not given a final figure for the peak in the reports we cross-checked. Dân trí reported residents raising their belongings, wading to get around and sitting down to dinner in the water.

In yesterday’s issue, the outlets forecast the peak between Oct. 11 and 12, with water levels possibly exceeding alert level 3 by about 10 to 15 cm, while widespread heavy rain in the South tends to ease. High tides are a periodic rise in water that follows the cycle of the tides; when they coincide with heavy rain or releases from upstream, flooding is worse and drains more slowly. The press has not published total damage, and there are no official figures on the number of flooded households or affected streets. This morning, commuters should check conditions on each route before setting out.

Foreign affairs · National AssemblyPress ✓ 4 outlets · content from official information

National Assembly Chairman Trần Thanh Mẫn makes official visit to China: formal meeting with CPPCC Chairman Wang Huning, visit to a legislative model in Beijing

National Assembly Chairman Trần Thanh Mẫn began an official visit to China on Oct. 11, according to Thanh Niên, Dân trí and Vietnam.vn. The same day he held a formal meeting with China’s national CPPCC Chairman Wang Huning, visited a legislative model and a grassroots legislative liaison point in Beijing, then met the Vietnamese community in China.

The outlets have so far given only the itinerary and the meetings; the content of the talks and any specific agreements have not been fully published. This is foreign activity by a legislative body, so its results are usually judged by the cooperation agreements between the two bodies and the exchange of experience in lawmaking.

World · EnergyPress ✓ 7 outlets · deal details not confirmed

Mr. Trump announces Russia and Ukraine agree to an “energy ceasefire” effective immediately; Mr. Zelensky calls it “news to me”, then says he is ready to wait for details from the US

On the evening of Oct. 11 Vietnam time (around 3 p.m. GMT), US President Donald Trump announced on the social network Truth Social that Russia and Ukraine had agreed to an energy ceasefire taking effect immediately, according to Reuters, USA Today, Guardian, Thanh Niên and Dân trí. NBC News reported that Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said it was “news to me”; Ukrainska Pravda then reported that he supports an energy ceasefire and is awaiting details from the US.

Earlier in the day, Reuters said Mr. Zelensky stated that Ukraine would stop hitting Russian oil refineries if Russia stopped attacking energy infrastructure, and would hold elections if Russia accepted a 60-day ceasefire. The Financial Times said the US lobbied European capitals and Kyiv to stop hitting Russian refineries, even threatening to block weapons; Thanh Niên reported that opposition to Mr. Trump’s deal also emerged within the Republican Party. Moscow’s official reaction, what the scope of “energy” includes and the monitoring mechanism were not given in the reports we cross-checked.

World · Middle EastPress ✓ 8 outlets · casualty figures per Saudi authorities

Houthis attack Riyadh airport: 12 dead, more than 300 injured; another new attack sets a fire on Oct. 11

Saudi authorities said the attack on Riyadh airport on Oct. 10 killed 12 people and injured more than 300; the Washington Post reported 309 injured, and AP, NPR, CBS News, USA Today and the Japan Times gave similar numbers. The Iran-backed Houthi rebels in Yemen claimed responsibility and have warned passengers and airlines to avoid Saudi airports. AP reported that the country extended the closure of the airport.

On Oct. 11, the Saudi-led coalition said there was another attack on the airport that set a fire, according to Haaretz and Livemint. Reuters reported that Saudi stocks fell after the attack; US Secretary of State Marco Rubio condemned it and said the US stands with the kingdom, while NBC and NPR said President Trump is considering whether to join the fight against the Houthis. Indians were among the dead, and many Indians and Filipinos were injured. No Vietnamese outlet has reported any Vietnamese citizens affected.

Sport · Women’s footballPress ✓ 5 outlets

Vietnam’s women’s U-17s win all 3 matches, earn a place in the 2027 Asian finals; VFF awards VND 900 million

Vietnam’s women’s U-17 team beat Kyrgyzstan to complete three straight wins in qualifying and earn a place in the 2027 Asian women’s U-17 finals, according to Thanh Niên and Dân trí. The Vietnam Football Federation (VFF) awarded VND 900 million; VFF President Trần Quốc Tuấn said he is very proud.

The finals field is now complete at 12 teams, including 5 representatives from Southeast Asia, Thanh Niên said; the match schedule and live-broadcast channel have been set. The score of each match has not been compiled here.
