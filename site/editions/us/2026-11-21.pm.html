Trump Orders Federal Contractors to Check With the White House Before Answering Congressional Inquiries, Reports Say

The executive order, reported Saturday, comes as the administration expects a wave of document requests from Democrats.

President Trump signed an executive order telling federal contractors to notify the White House, or check with it, when Congress asks them for information, according to reports published Saturday, Nov. 21. An executive order is a written directive from the president to the executive branch; it is not a law passed by Congress. Federal contractors are private companies that do work for the government, from software to equipment to support services, and lawmakers often ask them for records when they examine how public money was spent. Reports describe the order as a response to the flood of requests the administration expects from Democrats, and as the latest in a series of steps aimed at blunting Democratic plans to investigate the administration.

The reports put the requirement in different words. Some say contractors must alert the White House to congressional record requests, others say they must check with the White House before responding, and one says the order seeks to limit what contractors share with Congress. Congressional oversight, the power of lawmakers to investigate how the executive branch operates, has long relied on getting documents and answers from outside companies as well as from agencies. The order's full text, any penalty for a contractor that does not comply, and how Congress will respond have not been reported.

Mexico Detains 'El Chapo Isidro,' a Cartel Boss on the FBI Ten Most Wanted List, and a Burst of Violence Follows in Sinaloa, Reports Say

Mexican forces detained Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, known as 'El Chapo Isidro,' in an operation in Sinaloa, a state on Mexico's Pacific coast, according to reports on Saturday, Nov. 21. He is on the FBI Ten Most Wanted list, the bureau's roster of its highest-priority fugitives, and is described as a top cartel boss. A cartel is an organized criminal group that traffics drugs and fights rivals for territory. One report says Mexico's elite military forces made the capture with help from the United States; that detail comes from a single report and has not been confirmed elsewhere. Reports say the detention set off a burst of violence in Sinaloa. How many people were hurt or killed, and in which places, has not been established, and an on-the-record account of the raid from a named Mexican or U.S. official has not been reported. What happens to Meza Flores next, including whether he will be prosecuted in Mexico or sent to the United States, is not yet known.

Storms Are Now Forecast for Both Coasts Before Thanksgiving, and San Francisco's Mayor Is Asking Residents Not to Travel Unless 'Absolutely Essential,' a Report Says

Forecasters expect storms on both coasts in the run-up to Thanksgiving, which falls on Thursday, Nov. 26, putting some of the year's busiest travel days at risk. One report says a major storm will bring wind and rain to the East Coast ahead of the holiday. Earlier reports said a storm was forecast for California in Thanksgiving week, along with king tides, the unusually high tides that occur a few times a year. A report on Saturday says the mayor of San Francisco is asking people in the city not to travel unless it is 'absolutely essential.'

Holiday travel was already under strain before the forecasts. A report from Friday says travelers' concerns spiked as technology glitches grew, though few were dissuaded, and another looks at what to expect from air travel after a recent Federal Aviation Administration ground stop, an order that holds flights on the ground. How much rain, wind or tide height to expect, which airports and highways will be hit hardest, and whether airlines will cancel flights have not been reported.

Zelensky Calls Putin a 'Killer' After Trump Called Him 'My Friend' at the APEC Summit, and Reports Say a Russian Missile Attack Hit Zelensky's Hometown

President Trump drew backlash this week after calling Russian President Vladimir Putin 'my friend' at the APEC summit, an annual gathering of Asia-Pacific economies. European leaders and others in foreign policy criticized the remark. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has since said that 'for us he is a killer.' Trump has defended calling Putin a friend but says he 'can't speak' to whether Putin is a friend to the United States.

Reports on Saturday say a Russian ballistic missile attack on Zelensky's hometown in central Ukraine killed two people and wounded dozens. A ballistic missile is a weapon that rises in a high arc and falls steeply on its target. Zelensky also wrote on Telegram that Russia attacked Ukrainian bridges 109 times before Ukraine's response at the Crimean Bridge, and he said the figure counts attacks, not missiles or drones. A claim in coverage says Putin steps up strikes on Ukraine after each meeting with Trump; it comes without a named source or data and is unverified. One report says Zelensky suggested a new round of peace talks could be held around the G-20 summit, with him joining Trump and Putin for trilateral talks, a claim that rests on that one report.

COP31 Climate Talks Are Deadlocked Over Fossil Fuels as Critics Say a Draft Deal Leaves Them Unmentioned

Negotiators at the COP31 climate talks are deadlocked in a fight over fossil fuels, according to a report on Saturday, Nov. 21. A report from Friday says activists and leaders criticized the talks because fossil fuels go unmentioned in the draft deal, the working text that countries are trying to agree on. COP31 is the 31st annual United Nations climate conference, where nearly every country negotiates over how to cut the emissions that warm the planet. Fossil fuels, meaning coal, oil and gas, are the main source of those emissions.

A separate report says President Trump skipped the conference while U.S. fossil-fuel interests showed up; that account comes from a single report. How the deadlock will be resolved, whether the draft will be changed to name fossil fuels, and when the talks will end have not been reported.
