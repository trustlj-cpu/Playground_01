Putin hosts Trump envoy Witkoff for Ukraine peace talks and Russia frees American Stephen Hubbard

Reuters, AP, Axios and the New York Times report the envoy visit, a proposal for Kyiv and the release of a Michigan teacher.

Russian President Vladimir Putin hosted U.S. envoy Steve Witkoff for a new round of Ukraine peace talks, and Russia freed an American citizen it had been holding, Reuters reported. The Associated Press identified the released man as Stephen Hubbard and said his release came during the visit by the Trump administration envoy. Reuters said Witkoff arrived in St. Petersburg for the meeting. Axios had reported on Tuesday evening that the White House envoy would present Putin with a peace proposal after talks with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky. The New York Times reported that President Trump said he had asked Putin to release Stephen James Hubbard, a Michigan teacher who was seized from his home in Ukraine and jailed in Russia.

Reuters separately reported that Trump has pressed Kyiv to settle, and a further Reuters dispatch said Russian strikes killed elderly Ukrainians after Trump urged Kyiv to agree to terms. Reports of Zelensky's remarks on a possible Trump-Putin-Xi meeting quoted him as saying there can be no talks on Ukraine's future without Kyiv at the table. A French-language report described Putin as emboldened by Trump's attitude toward him and dreaming of a new 'Yalta' with the United States and China. Axios said the envoy's trip followed his talks with Zelensky. The AP and Reuters accounts tied Hubbard's release to the envoy's visit. The details of the proposal Witkoff carried were not published in the accounts.

Reuters/Ipsos poll puts Trump approval at a record low of 31% as midterms near

Trump's approval fell to a record low of 31% in the latest Reuters/Ipsos poll, Reuters reported in an exclusive. Reuters said the reading came as the midterms near. The Guardian reported in its live coverage that Trump continued his midterm campaign blitz in North Carolina, where he was to stump for Michael Whatley, who faces what the paper described as an uphill battle against Democrat Roy Cooper for the Senate seat. The New York Times reported that MAGA Inc. is now flooding Facebook and Instagram with targeted ads, spending millions of dollars as it seeks to turn out Republicans at the polls. The Times described the effort as coming amid the party's midterm struggles. Reuters, the Guardian and the Times each reported their pieces on Monday night or Tuesday.

Justice Kagan says Supreme Court rulings have been 'destructive to democracy' ahead of midterms

Supreme Court Justice Elena Kagan said recent rulings of the court have been 'destructive to democracy,' the New York Times and NBC News reported. The Times said she spoke during a discussion and argued that recent decisions on voting rights, gerrymandering and related questions have intensified polarization ahead of the midterms.

NBC News reported that the liberal justice took aim at various recent rulings and accused the court's conservative majority of undermining democracy. NBC said her criticism included one decision that paved the way for new congressional maps. The Times placed her remarks Tuesday evening. Neither outlet reported a response from the court's conservative justices in the accounts.

Alabama Gov. Kay Ivey commutes death sentence of Toforest Johnson over doubts about his conviction

Alabama Gov. Kay Ivey commutes the sentence of death row prisoner Toforest Johnson, reducing it to life in prison without parole, the Guardian and NBC News reported. The Guardian said the reduction came over doubts about his conviction, and NBC reported that Ivey cited doubts about the evidence in sparing him.

Both outlets reported the decision on Tuesday. The Guardian's headline said Johnson's death penalty was reduced to life in prison without parole, a sentence that rules out release. Neither report listed an execution date for Johnson in the material summarized.

SpaceX jumps 10% premarket on earnings beat as SpaceX and Tesla boards review a merger

SpaceX shares jumped about 10% in premarket trading after the company reported surging third-quarter revenue growth and owner Elon Musk hinted at a Tesla tie-up, CNBC reported. Bloomberg likewise reported that SpaceX beat third-quarter earnings expectations and that SpaceX and Tesla discussed a potential merger.

TechCrunch reported that the SpaceX and Tesla boards formed special committees to review a merger. Musk likes the idea but said he is not on either committee, which is made up of independent directors, according to TechCrunch. None of the three outlets reported a price or exchange ratio for a deal.
