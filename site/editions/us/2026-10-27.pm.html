Israeli exit polls show no clear winner as final results are expected in the coming days, PBS NewsHour reports

PBS NewsHour headlined that exit polls indicate no clear winner in the Israeli election as Netanyahu's fate hangs in the balance, and reported that final results are expected in the coming days and may vary from the exit polls. The Financial Times and Politico used the same framing of no clear winner, and Axios headlined that the Netanyahu bloc loses but Eisenkot's is short of a clear majority.

FinancialJuice, the only outlet here to give seat counts, reported that Channel 12's poll put the Eisenkot Bloc at 52 seats and the Netanyahu Bloc at 50, with Arab parties at 18, and that Netanyahu's Likud was to win 24 of the 120 Knesset seats. FinancialJuice also reported that three Israeli exit polls see the Eisenkot Bloc leading but without a majority. A majority in the 120-seat Knesset is 61, so neither bloc reached it in Channel 12's poll as FinancialJuice reported it. No coalition has been reported. CBS News described the vote as Israel's first parliamentary election since the Oct. 7 attacks.
