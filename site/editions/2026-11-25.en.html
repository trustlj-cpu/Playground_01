Several Russian military aircraft enter air defense identification zone near Ulleungdo… Joint Chiefs: “No airspace violation”; government to summon Russian military attaché to protest

Evacuation notices went out to Ulleungdo residents on the morning of the 24th, and Russia’s Defense Ministry said it was a “planned flight” by strategic bombers

Several Russian military aircraft entered Korea’s air defense identification zone (KADIZ) in the East Sea on the morning of the 24th and came close to Ulleungdo. The Joint Chiefs of Staff said there was no violation of airspace, and the Air Force responded by sending up fighters. Unlike airspace, which is the sky above territory and territorial waters, an air defense identification zone is airspace set in advance for defense, so a foreign military aircraft entering it is subject to identification and warning measures but does not automatically amount to a violation of sovereignty. Evacuation notices went out to Ulleungdo residents and sirens sounded. There are also reports that the disaster alert text carried the phrase “presumed North Korean aircraft,” causing confusion.

The government said it will summon the Russian military attaché to lodge a protest. According to one report, the Russian ambassador said in effect that no international law governs entry into an air defense identification zone, but this was not confirmed elsewhere. Russia’s Defense Ministry said that on the 24th (local time) Tu-160 strategic bombers made a planned flight over international waters in the East Sea. It has not yet been officially confirmed whether this flight is the same one as the aircraft that came near Ulleungdo, or the exact number and type of aircraft that entered. Further explanation is also needed on what judgment led to the alert and the disaster alert text.

Swiss government keeps sanctions on Songdowon camp despite North Korea’s threat to ‘consider withdrawing Pyongyang diplomatic office’

In a statement issued on the 24th through the Korean Central News Agency, Kim Yo-jong, director of the General Affairs Department of North Korea’s Workers’ Party, objected that Switzerland had followed the EU’s Russia sanctions package wholesale and put the Songdowon International Children’s Union Camp on its sanctions list, and said North Korea would consider the withdrawal of the Swiss diplomatic office in Pyongyang. The EU listed the camp for taking part in a program that sends children from Ukraine’s occupied territories to North Korea, and Switzerland has joined the EU sanctions. The Swiss government said it will keep the sanctions despite the threat, it became known on the 25th. Switzerland opened a diplomatic office in Pyongyang at the end of last month, and the Swiss foreign minister attended the opening ceremony. Whether North Korea has actually notified a withdrawal, and what further steps Switzerland will take, have not yet been confirmed.

Nvidia opens lower the day after ‘Jensen Huang’s return’… Republican China hawks in US also criticize Trump’s involvement

On the 24th (local time), the day after Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s return, Nvidia shares opened down in the 3% range in New York trading. That gave back part of the surge of more than 30% the previous day, and some tallies put the opening drop at around 6%, so the figures differ. Nvidia’s board of directors said it voted unanimously on the 23rd to bring Huang back, that it heard views from senior US administration officials in the process, and that it judged the return posed no threat to national security. President Trump said the president had intervened directly.

Political criticism of how the return came about is growing. Some China hawks in the ruling Republican Party also objected, asking whether President Trump puts China’s interests first, and the terms “state capitalism” and “Maoism” even came up. A report citing multiple anonymous sources said Vice President Vance was also involved in the decision to bring Huang back, but there is no official confirmation. There was also a report that the US Commerce Department put Singapore chip company Megaspeed, reported to be tied to Huang’s scandal, on its sanctions list. Whether Huang will have to appear at a congressional hearing, and how export controls on China will change, have not yet been decided.

Seoul reopens bidding for Seobu Line light rail private investment project… project cost 2.3252 trillion won, converts to publicly funded project if tender fails again

Seoul City said it will again pursue the Seobu Line, a light rail line linking Saejeol Station in Eunpyeong District and Seoul National University Station in Gwanak District, as a private investment project. If the rebid to gather private operators again draws no bidder and ends in a failed tender, the project will be converted to a publicly funded project built with Seoul City’s budget, in which case it will proceed under Seoul’s Third Urban Railway Network Plan. According to one report, the rebid notice is dated the 26th, and the project cost reflecting changes has risen to 2.3252 trillion won.

The same report said the rebid came after the status of the Doosan Construction consortium, the earlier preferred bidder, was cancelled. The reason for the cancellation, the schedule for choosing a new operator and the opening date are not yet known. A private investment project is one in which the private sector puts up the money first, builds the facility and operates it for a set period to recover costs, so if no one bids, the project itself can stall.

Trade · Tariffs✓ 2 outlets · target countries and rates not disclosed

US Trade Representative likely to announce ‘overproduction tariff’ findings as early as this week… target countries and rates not yet disclosed

A report said the Office of the United States Trade Representative (USTR) will announce the results of its “overproduction tariff” investigation as early as this week. The Trade Representative’s office is the US government agency in charge of tariff policy and trade negotiations. Alongside reports that the US government has signaled this tariff, there was also an article examining whether South Korea is among the targets. One video report said the US is preparing to raise tariffs on China, the EU and others, but details were not confirmed.

The tariff rate, target countries and effective date have not yet been made public. Whether South Korea is included is also unconfirmed. Once the announcement comes, the list of covered goods and countries and the start date will be what matters. “Overproduction” is a term used for the problem of a particular country’s production and exports exceeding market demand.
