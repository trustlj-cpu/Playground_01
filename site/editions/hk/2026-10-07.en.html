Halzen wins the Nobel Prize in Physics alone, honored for the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and the discovery of high-energy cosmic neutrinos

The prize is SEK 12 million; the award ceremony takes place in Stockholm on December 10.

The Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences announced on October 6 (Tuesday) that Belgian-born American particle physicist Francis Halzen has won this year’s Nobel Prize in Physics alone. The Nobel Prize website’s official statement says he made “decisive contributions to the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and to the discovery of high-energy neutrinos from astronomical objects” (Nobel Prize website, Reuters, The Standard, Free Press Journal, Daily Maverick, China Daily Hong Kong edition). The prize is SEK 12 million, about $1.2 million, and the ceremony is set for December 10 in Stockholm (Reuters).

Neutrinos are particles with extremely small mass that almost never interact with matter. They can pass through the entire Earth, so they can only be detected by large detectors buried deep in the Antarctic ice. What the prize recognizes is a method of “observing the universe through ice.” For Hong Kong readers this is a basic-science story with no bearing on investment or policy; none of the reports we saw links this year’s laureate to a local institution, so we draw no such connection.

Finance · Hong Kong stocksPress ✓ 2 outlets · the two closing figures differ by 1 point; The Standard’s is used

Hang Seng rises 240 points on Tuesday to close at 24,280, led by tech stocks; turnover stays low

The Hang Seng Index rose by more than 300 points at one stage on October 6 (Tuesday) and closed up 240 points, or 1%, at 24,280; the Hang Seng Tech Index gained 1% to 4,223 (The Standard). Zhipu (Z.ai, 2513) rose 7.6%, Tencent 1.2% and Alibaba 2.7%. The Standard said the gains followed Wall Street’s overnight advance, but turnover was only HK$98.3 billion because mainland money did not come in; mainland markets are closed until Thursday. Trading Economics recorded the close as 24,281, a difference of 1 point, and we go with The Standard. This also squares with Monday’s close of 24,040: 24,040 plus 240 equals 24,280 (see the “Unverified” column below).

Wall Street hits fresh record highs on Tuesday: S&P 500 up 0.58% to 7,818.93, oil flat, gold up 0.73%

US stocks closed higher on October 6 (Tuesday): the S&P 500 rose 0.58% to 7,818.93. The Standard’s briefing says both the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq closed at record highs and oil prices were steady (single source). West Texas Intermediate crude futures rose 0.01% to $89.44; gold rose 0.73% to $4,187.1 (DailyDrop market data). Flat oil suggests the market has no new direction for now.

For Hong Kong stocks, a record high on Wall Street usually lifts sentiment toward Asian tech shares. At today’s (Wednesday) open, the question is whether the rally carries on, and where money flows when mainland markets reopen on Thursday. The gain in gold was more pronounced and is usually tied to safe-haven demand and the dollar, but the report does not give a reason for today, so we do not speculate.

First APEC finance ministers’ meeting in Hong Kong: the government coordinates, with the head of the Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau leading a task force

Under arrangements announced by the Central Government and the Hong Kong government last December, the 2026 Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation finance ministers’ meeting will be held in Hong Kong for the first time in October. The Ministry of Finance is the host, and the Hong Kong government handles arrangements and coordination; the head of the Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau leads a task force coordinating preparations (news.gov.hk, China Daily Hong Kong edition, The Standard). Financial Secretary Paul Chan has said the meeting is an opportunity to showcase Hong Kong’s comprehensive development and its role as a “super-connector.” The Standard’s evening briefing of October 6 also listed “Hong Kong hosts APEC finance ministers’ meeting for the first time” among the day’s news.

This year APEC is hosted by China, and the leaders’ meeting takes place in Shenzhen on November 18 and 19. The exact dates, venue and attendee list of the finance ministers’ meeting are not given in the reports we saw, so we do not state them. During the meeting, some roads may have traffic arrangements; for details, rely on announcements from the police and the Transport Department.
