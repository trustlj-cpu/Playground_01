Afridi calls supporters from across the country to Swabi Interchange on Sunday as the Centre is reported to weigh an emergency in 12 districts

New since Thursday: the march entered its sixth day heading for Charsadda, PTI named the Swabi meeting point, and The Express Tribune reported federal preparations for emergency powers.

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Chief Minister Sohail Afridi has invited people from across the country to join the PTI long march at Swabi Interchange on Sunday 11 October, Dawn reported. The march’s central demand remains the release of Imran Khan, the jailed party founder. The Express Tribune quoted him saying the party will unveil its “final plan” in Swabi, as the march entered its sixth day on Friday 9 October and headed to Charsadda, where the convoy was due to spend the night. ARY News and Minute Mirror carry the same schedule: Mardan on Saturday and Swabi on Sunday.

The Express Tribune also reported, citing sources, that the Centre had finalised preparations for an emergency in 12 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa; PTI called the proposal a violation of the 18th Amendment. No emergency or governor’s rule order had been issued by early Saturday, and DailyDrop has not seen an official confirmation of the 12-district plan.

Trump says Russia will send over 300,000 tons of diesel to the US at once, a sanctions U-turn Zelenskyy calls “a gift to Putin”

President Donald Trump announced on Truth Social on Friday 9 October that Russia would immediately supply more than 300,000 tons of diesel to the American and global market, with 500,000 tons more in November and larger volumes afterwards, ITV News, CTV News, KSAT, Kyiv Post and Avvenire reported. The U.S. Treasury authorised the import of Russian diesel through 7 April 2027. Trump framed it as lower prices for Americans before the midterm elections. The White House did not say who would pay or when the fuel would arrive.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said that easing sanctions without a lasting de-escalation deal was a sign of weakness and that Russia would “repay” the diesel with more attacks; the British government said it would keep its sanctions. For Pakistan, which buys diesel and crude at world prices, extra Russian supply could ease prices if it materialises, but nothing about the Strait of Hormuz has changed.

Navi Pillay wins the Nobel peace prize; Trump re-shares an attack on the committee

The Norwegian Nobel Committee announced in Oslo on Friday 9 October that the 2026 Nobel Peace Prize goes to Navanethem “Navi” Pillay, the South African former UN High Commissioner for Human Rights and international judge, for promoting peace and international law, ABC News and nobelprize.org reported. The committee’s chair, Jørgen Watne Frydnes, said she had worked to see that war crimes, crimes against humanity and genocide are prosecuted.

Trump re-shared on Truth Social a post from lawyer David Schoen’s account that called the choice a disgrace and accused Pillay of hostility to Israel and America, according to MeaWW and Madhyamam. Pillay joked in a video, “If you clap a little bit more, I may share this prize with Trump”, The Wrap reported. She chaired a UN inquiry that found Israel committed genocide in Gaza, a finding Israel rejects.

KSE-100 slips 352 points to 167,089 on Friday as bank shares weigh

The KSE-100 closed on Friday 9 October at 167,089.27, down 352.63 points or 0.21% from Thursday’s 167,441.91, Mettis Global reported. It traded between about 166,838 and 168,134. Fifty index stocks rose and 48 fell. HBL took 85 points off the index, UBL 75 and MCB 72. DailyDrop found no second outlet’s report on the session, so the figures are attributed to Mettis.

The week’s total: the index ended Friday about 1,000 points below the 168,155 of the previous Friday. In the interbank market the rupee closed at 277.00 to the dollar, up one paisa, according to Daily Times (APP) and Business Recorder. Markets are shut for the weekend.
