PTI convoy leaves Peshawar’s outskirts for Khairabad as the defence minister calls governor’s rule a possible last resort

New since Tuesday: the schedule has the caravans leaving the Budhni area of Peshawar at 12:30pm on Wednesday to gather at Khairabad today, where Afridi is due to announce the next step.

Afridi’s published schedule had the PTI caravans depart the Budhni area of Peshawar at about 12:30pm on Wednesday 7 October, with all convoys to gather at Khairabad, near the Punjab border, on Thursday 8 October, according to Minute Mirror and Asia News Network. The convoy had spent Tuesday night at Mattani and was due to visit Khyber before Peshawar. DailyDrop has not seen a confirmed account of the Wednesday leg from two outlets.

On the government side, Dawn reported on 7 October that Defence Minister Khawaja Asif criticised the chief minister for putting a political march ahead of the security situation in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Minute Mirror reported Asif as saying governor’s rule could be imposed if no other option remained, though the government preferred other routes. Imran Khan, the jailed founder, remains the march’s central demand.

Henri Kagan and Kenso Soai share the Nobel chemistry prize for work on why life uses one-handed molecules

The Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences announced on Wednesday 7 October that the 2026 Nobel Prize in Chemistry goes to Henri B. Kagan and Kenso Soai “for the discovery of non-linear effects and autocatalysis in asymmetric organic synthesis”, according to nobelprize.org, Khaleej Times, Prothom Alo, ThePrint and Madhyamam. They share 12 million Swedish crowns, about $1.2 million.

The work concerns chirality, the fact that many molecules exist in left- and right-handed mirror forms, and that living things use only one of the two. Kagan found a way to produce a larger excess of one form than was thought possible; Soai showed a reaction in which a product helps make more of itself. The research bears on how drugs are made, since the two mirror forms of a drug can act very differently in the body.

Word of the day
Chirality · chemistry

The property of a molecule that cannot be superimposed on its mirror image, like left and right hands. Drug makers need one form only.

Khawaja Asif sharpens his language on Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: governor’s rule “last option” and warnings of a May 9-style plot

Defence Minister Khawaja Asif has told journalists that governor’s rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa could be imposed if no other option remained, while saying the government wanted to avoid it, Minute Mirror reported. ARY News reported that on 2 October he alleged a PTI–TTP plan for “May 9-like” violence and warned the provincial government and march organisers that any such attempt would be met with force.

PTI says the march is peaceful; DailyDrop did not find a PTI response to the militant-link allegation. Dawn separately reported on 7 October that Asif faulted the chief minister for focusing on the march rather than security. None of these claims is independently verified.
