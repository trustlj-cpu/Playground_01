President Lee: “CSAT cheating reporting reward is not a maximum but at least 100 million per case”… add-on for each offender

President Lee Jae-myung said on X on the 21st that the reward is not ‘up to 100 million won’ but at least 100 million won per case of cheating. The add-on method and payment criteria have not been confirmed.

President Lee Jae-myung set the record straight on the criteria for the CSAT cheating reporting reward in a post on X on the 21st. After linking an article saying the government planned to pay up to 100 million won, President Lee wrote that the reward is not up to 100 million won but at least 100 million won per case of cheating, and that in principle it is added for each offender. The post also said that if a cheater makes a voluntary surrender while reporting other joint cheaters, the cheater gets a penalty reduction for their own cheating but the reporting reward is still paid in full. President Lee stressed that CSAT cheating is a crime as serious as corruption by public officials, an act that steals in one go the lifelong efforts of hundreds of thousands of test takers, and that it would be tracked down to the end and severely punished. How the add-on is calculated and whether there is a cap cannot be confirmed from this post alone.

The remark is an extension of the recent controversy over cheating on exams with artificial intelligence, which has become a social issue. President Lee was reported to have also mentioned exam cheating using AI at the Senior Secretaries Meeting at Cheong Wa Dae on the 20th. Reactions are split. One report said Park Yu-ha criticized the large reward as money-worship and a police-state idea. The legal basis and funding for the reward, the start date and the window where reports are received have not been confirmed.

Freight train derails at Sojeong-ri Station on Gyeongbu Line… regular trains between Cheonan and Daejeon suspended all day, Korail puts in 15 extra trains

A derailment, in which a freight train left its tracks, occurred at Sojeong-ri Station on the Gyeongbu Line in Sejong early on the 21st. The accident suspended regular train service between Cheonan and Daejeon for the whole day. The Korea Railroad Corporation (Korail) ran 15 additional trains to reduce passenger inconvenience. Four KTX trains were put on the Yongsan–Gwangju-Songjeong and Seoul–Busan routes, and the regular trains such as the ITX-Saemaeul were six from Cheonan toward Seoul and Yongsan and five in the opposite direction. Reports also continued that test takers heading for university essay and interview exams had trouble getting around. The Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport was reported to have issued a rail accident crisis alert at the ‘caution’ level. Second Vice Minister Jeong Ui-gyeong said that restoring the site would put safety first and that inconvenience to passengers would be minimized. One report said regular trains returned to normal running from the first train on the 22nd, but because it comes from a single outlet, Korail’s official notice needs to be checked. The cause of the derailment and whether anyone was injured have not been confirmed.

President Lee visits SK AI data center construction site in Ulsan: “Speed is life”… checks on Yeongnam-region mega project

President Lee Jae-myung visited the construction site of SK’s artificial intelligence data center in Ulsan on the 21st, checked progress on the Yeongnam-region mega project and promised support. It followed the president's attendance on the 12th at the groundbreaking ceremony for the Chungcheong-region mega project at the construction site of Samsung Electronics’ Onyang Campus in Asan, South Chungcheong, making it a second straight week at a large regional investment site. President Lee said that the concentration of people and resources in the Seoul area is causing all kinds of problems in South Korea, that new opportunities must be created in the provinces, and that mega projects would be a key device. President Lee also said that balanced development is South Korea’s main national task.

Remarks were reported that speed is life in global competition and, to the effect, that demand for AI data centers and memory chips will grow further. The site was reported as the first step of SK Group’s plan for AI data centers totaling 15GW nationwide. GW (gigawatt) is a unit for measuring electric power, and 1GW is roughly the output of one large nuclear power plant. The investment size, completion date and details of the 15GW plan have not been confirmed.

Seocho District expected to hit Banpo Hyundai with Gangnam area’s first reconstruction levy next month… 52 Seoul complexes in scope

Seocho District in Seoul is expected to impose a reconstruction excess-profit recovery levy on Banpo Hyundai (now Centreville Asterium) next month. The reconstruction excess-profit recovery system (abbreviated Jaechohwan) is a system under which, when the gains union members obtain from reconstruction exceed a set threshold, the state collects part of them as a levy. If it is imposed, it will be the first case in the Gangnam area, and the 52 Seoul complexes expected to be charged are on edge. An estimate was reported that the 52 complexes would be charged 110 million won each, but the basis, such as whether it is a total per complex or an amount per household, has not been confirmed.

The amounts differ from report to report. The amount Seocho District gave advance notice of to the Banpo Hyundai union was reported as 17.9 billion won in total, in the 220 million won range per union member, a big gap from 110 million won. Advance notice is a stage before objections and document review, so the final amount may differ. The actual timing and amount of the charge and the result of any objections have not been confirmed.

Kim A-lim leads solo at 18 under after round 3 of LPGA finale… one stroke clear of second, winner’s prize $4 million

On the 22nd (Korea time), Kim A-lim shot a 3-under 69 with six birdies and three bogeys in the third round of the CME Group Tour Championship, the season finale of the U.S. LPGA Tour, held at Tiburon Golf Club (par 72) in Naples, Florida. At a midway total of 18 under, 198, Kim held sole first place, one stroke ahead of Jeeno Thitikul (Thailand) and Yuri Yoshida (Japan), tied for second at 17 under, 199. Starting round 1 in a tie for the lead, Kim kept the lead in round 2 and again in round 3.

The tournament carries a total purse of $11 million, and the winner’s prize was reported as $4 million. With two players one stroke back, the standings could change depending on the final round. The final round has not yet been played, so the winner has not been decided.
