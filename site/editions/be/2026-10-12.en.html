Politics · Federal budgetPress ✓ 6 outlets · the prime minister’s statements according to RTBF and Le Soir

No deal on the budget: De Wever cancels Tuesday’s State of the Union and rejects provisional twelfths

The prime minister says the federal government still has no agreement on a new budget. MR chairman Georges-Louis Bouchez keeps saying “no” as long as VAT is on the table.

Prime Minister Bart De Wever (N-VA) confirmed on Sunday that he will not give a State of the Union on Tuesday, October 13, because “so far” no agreement on a new budget has been found in the government (RTBF, De Morgen, HLN). He says the talks continue “given the seriousness of the situation” and that he does not accept a scenario of provisional twelfths (RTBF, HLN). De Morgen writes that he also warns of an emergency budget. Vincent Van Peteghem (CD&V) calls the situation “extremely serious” (Le Soir).

According to HBVL and HLN, the talks are stuck on Bouchez’s “no”, as long as a VAT increase is in the prime minister’s table. Bouchez himself says VAT is “by no means the only problem” (RTBF). Wetstraat journalist Bart Verhulst of the VRT says the first talks between the prime minister and the parties produced nothing and that above all the distrust between the coalition partners of the Arizona coalition has to go; “there was hardly any discussion of substance”. A budget is also on the way in Wallonia: the Dolimont government presents its agreement on Monday (RTBF, La Libre). DailyDrop does not know the exact deficit or the list of measures; figures on them are not confirmed.

Public-sector strike since Sunday 10 p.m.: according to La Libre, 7 in 10 trains run on Monday

The socialist trade union ACOD has been bringing the public services to a standstill since Sunday evening at 10 p.m., until Monday evening, in protest against the federal government’s spending cuts (HLN, La Libre, VRT NWS). NMBS kicked off the strike day on Sunday at 10 p.m. Disruption is expected to public transport, refuse collection and other services (HLN).

The VRT’s unions are also on strike, so output on radio and television remains limited (VRT NWS). DailyDrop has no confirmed figures for Monday from the MIVB, De Lijn or Letec; consult the operators’ journey planners.

World · UkrainePress ✓ 6 outlets · Trump’s post on Truth Social, Zelensky’s reaction on Telegram

Trump announces an “energy ceasefire”, Kyiv waits for details and Russia hits the Kharkiv power plant

Donald Trump wrote on Truth Social on Sunday that an energy ceasefire applies “with immediate effect” in the war between Russia and Ukraine and that both parties have agreed (Reuters, BBC). Volodymyr Zelensky wrote on Telegram that Ukraine supports any real and reliable ceasefire, but is waiting for details from the United States (Reuters, The Hill). According to the BBC, Moscow has not yet reacted. Zelensky says that after the announcement Russia again hit energy installations, including the Kharkiv plant (La Libre, HLN, Kyiv Independent). Ukraine will stop its attacks on Russian oil refineries if Russia spares Ukrainian energy infrastructure, he said (Reuters).

In addition, Trump’s diesel deal with Putin is under fire: a leading Republican senator says it may be illegal (Reuters), and the Financial Times reports that lawmakers from both parties think it finances the Russian war machine. Canadian Prime Minister Carney says the deal harms the cause of peace (BNN Bloomberg). Trump also called for Zelensky’s departure, whereupon Germany backed Zelensky (Reuters). Whether Russia accepts the ceasefire is not confirmed.

World · YemenPress ✓ 6 outlets · figures according to the Saudi authorities, Houthi claims not independently confirmed

New attacks on Saudi airports after the deadly missile attack on Riyadh: Dammam hit, Saudi Arabia bombs Sanaa

The Houthis claimed several further attacks on Riyadh airport on Sunday, a day after the missile attack of Saturday, October 10 (RTBF, BBC, Bloomberg). According to the Saudi authorities, 12 people were killed and more than three hundred wounded (AP, Bloomberg); RTBF puts the toll at 13 dead in its headline. The airport remains closed (AP). According to RTBF, the scale of the attacks on Riyadh is unprecedented since the start of the war in Yemen in 2014.

On Sunday evening the airport of Dammam was also hit, HLN reports, citing the Saudi civil aviation authority. Saudi fighter jets bombed Sanaa airport in retaliation (HLN); Reuters reports an attack on Sanaa according to the TV run by the Houthis. The Saudi-led coalition says it will hit back (The Guardian), and the Houthis warn airlines and travellers to avoid Saudi airports (Reuters). Oil bosses are pulling out of an energy congress in Riyadh (Financial Times) and the Saudi stock market fell (Reuters).

Royal House · AbroadPress ✓ 4 outlets · programme as reported by the media

King Philippe and Queen Mathilde in Kazakhstan for the first Belgian state visit to Central Asia

The royal couple landed in Kazakhstan on Sunday at about 8:30 p.m. local time (5:30 p.m. Belgian time) for a three-day state visit (La Libre, Le Soir, RTL Info). According to La Libre, it is the first Belgian state visit to Central Asia. HLN writes that Philippe and Mathilde are the very first European monarchs to honour Kazakhstan with a state visit.

According to Paris Match Belgique and The Brussels Times, the visit is about economic, academic and cultural cooperation, with a meeting with President Kassym-Jomart Tokajev in Astana and a stop in Almaty. The delegation numbers about 110 people. DailyDrop has no confirmed figures on contracts or agreements to be concluded during the visit.
