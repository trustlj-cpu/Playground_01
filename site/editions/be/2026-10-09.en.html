Liège bans gatherings of more than three people, Minister Quintin points to “antifa”: 194 arrests since October 1

At the school-student protests in Wallonia, local police say 114 minors have been arrested and 43 officers injured. Mayor Demeyer issued an emergency ordinance; the minister places the responsibility on an “antifa movement”.

Interior Minister Bernard Quintin (MR) said at a press conference on Thursday, October 8, that an antifa-type movement is active in Liège and is manipulating minors, according to The Brussels Times and Brussels Signal. In his view the riots are no longer a spontaneous school protest; he also places responsibility with parents and schools. The VRT explained that day what antifa is. Critics say his statements add fuel to the fire. In the Chamber, Quintin would not say whether a bill to dissolve radical organisations could be used against antifa groups (BX1).

Mayor Willy Demeyer (PS) limited gatherings in the street to three people by emergency ordinance, from October 8 until October 16 at 11 p.m., with exceptions for families, accompanied minors and school routes. According to local police there have been 194 arrests since October 1, 114 of them minors; 43 officers were injured. Police chief Jean-Marc Demelenne says more and more rioters are adults. The movement spread to Mons, Charleroi and Brussels. DailyDrop saw no evidence for the claim that antifa is steering the protests.

Markets · BiotechPress ✓ 5 outlets · the share-price fall varies by source between 11% and 18%

argenx halts phase 3 trial in Sjögren’s: shares plunged and dragged the BEL 20 down 2.31%

The Belgian biotech company argenx said on Thursday, October 8, that it is halting its phase 3 UNITY trial of Vyvgart in Sjögren’s syndrome. An independent committee recommended this after an interim analysis, because the trial could not reach its primary goal (Morningstar, BioPharma Dive, Tikr). The safety data remained in line with the drug’s known profile. Depending on the moment and the source, the share lost 11% to 18%, in its biggest one-day fall in almost three years, according to Reuters.

The Brussels BEL 20 lost 2.31% and closed at 5,399.40 points (La Libre, DH). The results were not expected until the second half of 2027. Analysts had largely left Sjögren’s revenue out of their models. On the same day argenx reported positive phase 2 results in coeliac disease, which did not offset the fall.

Nobel Prize in Literature goes to Canadian poet and classicist Anne Carson

On Thursday, October 8, the Swedish Academy awarded the 2026 Nobel Prize in Literature to the Canadian writer Anne Carson (76), report Khaleej Times, Bluewin, The Star (Xinhua) and VRT NWS. The citation reads: for her bold and inventive oeuvre that, in playful conversation with the classical tradition, has found new forms for contemporary literature. She is the second Canadian winner in history. The prize is 12 million Swedish kronor.

Carson, born in Toronto, writes mainly in English and is known for her translations of classical Greek. Her best-known work is the verse novel Autobiography of Red (1998). According to the Academy’s permanent secretary she was surprised but very happy. The ceremony takes place on December 10.

Brent rises above $105 on fresh tensions around Hormuz; Trump says he will not attack Iran before the midterm elections

The Brent oil price rose on Thursday, October 8, by 4% to almost 6%, to above $105 a barrel, depending on the source, report Trading Economics and Political.org. Trading Economics points to more intense attacks by Tehran on tankers in the Strait of Hormuz. US WTI closed 3.60% higher at $91.50, gold 0.20% higher at $4,157.0 and the S&P 500 0.47% lower at 7,765.36 points.

Later, President Donald Trump wrote on Truth Social that the US will not attack Iran before the midterm elections of November 3, Political.org quotes. Trading Economics also notes that he stressed that the US naval blockade of Iranian ports remains fully in force. The sources contradict each other on the size of the rise; DailyDrop has not been able to reconcile them.

Economy · WorkPress · one outlet · union position according to VRT

Metal processor Team Industries of Roeselare files for bankruptcy, 135 jobs at risk

The metal-processing company Team Industries in Roeselare wants to file for bankruptcy, VRT NWS reports. Some 135 employees are in danger of losing their jobs next week. The message came at an extra works council meeting; staff were allowed to go home and are not expected back until Monday.

Management points to the geopolitical and economic situation and to a few important projects that ended or were not extended. Yves Allewaert of ABVV-Metaal says nobody had expected a bankruptcy. He points out that in a bankruptcy, unlike a closure, there is no room for social consultation, and that a bankruptcy trustee could possibly dismiss the employees next week. DailyDrop saw no second outlet and no statement yet from a bankruptcy trustee.
