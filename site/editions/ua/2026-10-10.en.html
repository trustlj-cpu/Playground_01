Russia attacks Ukraine with 136 drones, more than ninety of them jet-powered; air defense neutralizes 114

The overnight attack on Friday, October 9, hit Zaporizhzhia, the Kyiv region and Ivano-Frankivsk. The military stress a new proportion: more than two thirds of the drones were jet-powered Shaheds.

On the night of October 9 Russia launched 136 strike drones, including more than 90 jet-powered Shaheds, as well as Gerbera drones and Parodiya decoys, the Air Force reported (Ukrainska Pravda, TSN, Slovo i Dilo). Air defense neutralized 114 targets. Hits were recorded at eight locations, and debris fell at seven more. The drones flew from the directions of Oryol, Primorsko-Akhtarsk, occupied Donetsk and Crimea.

In Zaporizhzhia a man was killed in the attack, and five people, among them a child, were wounded (Fakty). In the Kyiv region the occupiers struck warehouse buildings — three people were hurt. In Ivano-Frankivsk one person was killed. We have not seen overall totals for the day; the figures may change.

Trump announces Russian diesel deliveries; Kyiv: easing sanctions will not bring peace

On October 9 Donald Trump wrote on Truth Social that Russia would immediately supply more than 300,000 tonnes of diesel fuel to the American and world markets. He gave no terms of the deal; we have not seen a published text of any agreement. Kyiv Post writes that the Ukrainian side then said that easing sanctions on Russian diesel will not bring peace, and called for an energy truce (Kyiv Post).

According to the Financial Times, relayed by Kyiv Post, Kyiv will keep striking Russian refineries, and analysts doubt that Moscow can deliver on its export promises, since refining is running at roughly 60% of capacity. Earlier Trump publicly asked Kyiv not to strike fuel targets, and Zelensky denied that any agreement on energy strikes had been reached (Newsweek, Ukrainska Pravda).

Ukraine · FrontPress ✓ 2 outlets · General Staff data

General Staff: 211 combat clashes recorded over the day, most on the Huliaipole and Pokrovsk directions

The General Staff of the Armed Forces reported 211 combat clashes on the front over the past day; most assaults fell on the Huliaipole and Pokrovsk directions (Ukrainska Pravda, Fakty). The Defense Forces, the military say, repelled more than two hundred Russian assaults. Separately, the General Staff publishes an estimate of Russia’s losses every day; these are estimates by the Ukrainian side, not independent data.

On the night of October 9 Russian drones attacked the rear, while assaults continue along the line of contact. We have no independent confirmation of the number of clashes.

Ukraine · WarPress ✓ 2 outlets · update after October 8

Kramatorsk: a day of mourning declared in the community for 33 killed in the strike on buses

After an aerial bomb struck two scheduled buses on October 8, local authorities declared October 9 a day of mourning in the Kramatorsk community (UkraNews). The number of dead remains 33 and of wounded 18, according to the State Emergency Service and Vadym Filashkin, head of the Donetsk regional military administration (Slovo i Dilo, UkraNews). Filashkin urged residents of frontline territories to evacuate to safer regions.

Earlier he halted public transport in Kramatorsk and Sloviansk; we found no information on its resumption as of Saturday morning. The investigation of the war crime continues.

Gold rises 1.43% to $4,216.3; S&P 500 +0.59%, WTI oil +0.39%

On Friday, October 9, the S&P 500 closed at 7,811.54 points (+0.59%). Gold rose 1.43% to $4,216.3 an ounce, WTI crude by 0.39% to $91.85 a barrel. No separate cause for the gold move has been established; it usually rises when investors seek protection.

Over the week WTI went from $89.43 on Monday to $91.85 on Friday. For Ukrainian drivers this means pressure on fuel prices.
