Starlink and Starshield: Musk blames Washington, the White House denies it, and Russia launched 580 drones and two missiles overnight

Zelensky says Russia took advantage of the communications disruptions for a massive attack. A military expert with the callsign “Flash” sees no link. On Thursday Musk urged Zelensky to step down.

On Wednesday, Oct. 14, Zelensky said in London that Russia was trying to exploit the problems with Starlink in Ukraine and was carrying out one of its largest attacks (NV, RBC-Ukraine, TSN, Kyiv Post). This concerns a limit on the speed at which drones that operate through the satellite system can fly. According to NV’s sources in the Defense Ministry, the limit stayed at 161 km/h that day (NV, Censor.NET). Mykhailo Fedorov, the former defense minister, reported a partial restoration of service at speeds of up to 160 km/h (NV, Ukrainska Pravda, Censor.NET). According to UNIAN, Ukraine needs a limit of up to 450 km/h, but this is a figure from the media, and our sources contain no confirmation from the authorities. Zelensky called the restriction a big mistake that would cost lives (Censor.NET, Ukrainska Pravda, RBC-Ukraine).

Who exactly set the limit is still disputed. When Fedorov asked for the restrictions on Starshield, the military version of Starlink, to be lifted, Musk replied that the final decision rests with the US president (NV, RBC-Ukraine, UNIAN, TSN, BBC). Later, according to CNBC and Quartz, he said that the US government controls the limits. Axios, citing the White House, writes that the administration has nothing to do with the restrictions and does not run SpaceX, and Ukrainian officials doubt this (NV, TSN, Censor.NET, RBC-Ukraine). On Thursday, Oct. 15, Musk urged Zelensky to leave office (AFP, Kyiv Post, Straits Times, Dawn). Zelensky had replied earlier that neither Trump, nor Musk, nor anyone else can choose Ukraine’s president (UNIAN), and that he does not intend to resign (Censor.NET, TSN, Dzerkalo Tyzhnia). According to The Record, Kyiv later reported that Starshield service had been restored. In the US Congress, a caucus of lawmakers supporting Ukraine spoke out against the restrictions on intelligence and on Starlink’s operation (NV, Censor.NET), and one senator appealed to Musk (Censor.NET).

The Russian strike was large. On the night before Thursday Russia launched two missiles and 580 drones; the Ukrainian military neutralized 536 drones (Ukrainska Pravda, NV, Kyiv Independent). Kropyvnytskyi had it hardest: for more than ten hours the city and the region were under attack, three people were killed and six wounded (Ukrainska Pravda, RBC-Ukraine, UNIAN, Kyiv Independent, Kyiv Post). In the Dnipropetrovsk region two people were killed and private houses burned (TSN, NV, Censor.NET). In Rivne part of the city and district was left without power (RBC-Ukraine, NV, Channel 24). Earlier, on Wednesday evening, a Russian strike damaged a high-rise in Odesa (Censor.NET, Ukrainska Pravda, TSN).

The parties interpret differently whether the limit and the force of the strike are connected. A source of RBC-Ukraine says that interceptors cannot keep up with jet-powered “Shaheds” because of the speed restriction. “Flash,” according to NV, believes that the massive attack with gasoline-powered “Shaheds” is not connected with the restrictions. We have seen no independent assessment. Reuters writes that Europe has no quick replacement; the EU has begun talking about the Govsatcom satellite system (TSN), and UNIAN names 2029 as the earliest date for replacing Starlink with a European alternative.

What this means. Drones and air defense now depend on communications, and even slower communications mean less time to react. Responsibility for the limit is named only in statements: Musk points to the US president, the White House to SpaceX, and there is as yet no document that would confirm it. For readers nothing changes: during alerts go to shelter at once, especially at night, because jet-powered drones reach even the west of the country.

War · Oil refiningPress ✓ 10 outlets · Zelensky’s statements; data from Reuters and the Washington Post

“We have had enough”: Zelensky announces a resumption of strikes on Russian refineries, and US intelligence, per WP, has threatened to stop sharing data

In an interview quoted by FT, Zelensky said that the ban on strikes on Russian oil refineries has been lifted and that “we have had enough” (Dzerkalo Tyzhnia, LIGA.net, UNN, Babel, TSN, RBC-Ukraine, Reuters). The day before he had said that after the “energy truce” announced by Trump, Ukraine had not hit Russian energy facilities for several days and had not even responded to strikes (NV, Censor.NET). On Wednesday Moscow accused Ukraine of striking two refineries; the Office of the President called this a fake (TSN, RBC-Ukraine, Ukrainska Pravda). On the night before Thursday drones caused a fire at the Ryazan refinery, the governor reported (Reuters, FinancialJuice). Zelensky wrote in Telegram that the Defense Forces struck an airfield in the Voronezh region and destroyed four Su-34s; a fire at the Borisoglebsk airfield is described by Censor.NET, NV, RBC-Ukraine and TSN. Other publications, citing the Navy, name six aircraft; we have seen no agreed figures.

The Washington Post writes that US intelligence warned the Ukrainian embassy that in the event of new strikes on Russian refineries the sharing of intelligence would be stopped (Ukrainska Pravda, NV, TSN, UNIAN, Censor.NET, RBC-Ukraine). According to Channel 24, Zelensky said there was a risk of losing intelligence data. There is no response from Washington to this report in our sources, and they give no sign that the transfer of data has already been halted. So this is for now a warning, not a decision. Sybiha, according to Interfax-Ukraine, said that Putin’s answer to the calls for an energy truce was missiles and drones, and Peskov, according to Kyiv Post, stated that Russia had not violated the truce because nobody had defined its terms.

Europe · SupportPress ✓ 8 outlets · statements by Prime Minister Burnham; the president’s Telegram

Burnham in London: faster air defense deliveries, the King receives Zelensky, and in Brussels the president awaits Rutte, Macron and the European Council

On Wednesday, Oct. 14, Zelensky met in London with King Charles III and Prime Minister Burnham; with the prime minister he spoke for more than an hour on Downing Street about Starlink and intelligence sharing (Ukrainska Pravda, NV, Yevropeiska Pravda, Channel 24). Burnham said that Zelensky “will not leave Britain empty-handed” and promised to speed up deliveries of British air defense systems, including lightweight multirole missiles (Censor.NET, Ukrainska Pravda, NV). British and Ukrainian outlets quote him as saying that Britain’s support will not change. Britain is also funding urgent repairs at the Chornobyl nuclear power plant, damaged by a Russian drone (three outlets in our pool). The EU, according to Kyiv Post and Yevropeiska Pravda, has paid Ukraine another roughly €2.86 billion for its most urgent needs, and the European Commission allocated more than €168 million for defense industry projects (Yevropeiska Pravda).

On Thursday, Oct. 15, Zelensky arrived in Brussels for a meeting of the European Council. According to his Telegram, the first meeting was with NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte; a conversation with the president of France is also planned for that day. Magyar, according to Ukrainska Pravda, NV and RBC-Ukraine, confirmed a first in-person meeting with Zelensky at the summit, but Sybiha said that the time and place still have to be agreed (NV, Censor.NET). Kyiv Independent wrote that Hungary and Bulgaria are again weakening the ambition of the wording on Ukraine’s accession. FT, going by its headline, writes that EU leaders plan to advise Zelensky to be more restrained in criticizing Trump; the text is behind a paywall, and we have not read it.

World · SanctionsPress ✓ 9 outlets · data from Reuters and FT; no decisions yet

Washington seeks relief for Moscow: sanctions on the “shadow fleet” and frozen assets; Europe rejects the proposal

According to Reuters, a US official said that the administration is considering easing sanctions on “shadow fleet” vessels in order to make oil shipping cheaper (Ukrainska Pravda, NV, TSN, RBC-Ukraine, Yevropeiska Pravda). FT and Reuters reported that Washington proposed that Europe unfreeze part of the frozen Russian assets in order to restart peace talks (NV, Ukrainska Pravda, RBC-Ukraine). Ukrainska Pravda, citing FT, adds that the US wants to take these funds under its own control. European officials, according to FinancialJuice citing FT, rejected the proposal. The Kremlin, according to Reuters, stated that Russia has resumed diesel exports after a deal with Trump.

Zelensky said that Trump’s new deals with Putin show him to be weak (Ukrainska Pravda, NV), and, according to Kyiv Post, called the diesel deal a victory for Moscow. Trump explained why he does not call Putin a dictator: he is, in Trump’s words, a master dealmaker and does not insult people (Censor.NET, TSN). Italian Prime Minister Meloni, according to TSN and Korrespondent.net, said that Ukraine’s capitulation would not bring peace. Axios describes Trump’s bet on his relationship with Putin ahead of the midterm elections as “Russian roulette” (NV, UNIAN); for the first time in more than 20 years the US did not invite OSCE observers to these elections (NV, RBC-Ukraine, Ukrainska Pravda). According to CNN, TSN, RBC-Ukraine and Ukrainska Pravda write, Trump is not reacting to Russian strikes on American businesses in Ukraine, including Coca-Cola and McDonald’s warehouses. Our sources name no decisions on assets or sanctions.

Power, gas and the dollar: outage schedules in force in Kyiv after the attack, Ukraine partially opens gas exports, and the dollar touched a high after hot US inflation

Outage schedules are in force in Kyiv; energy workers have stabilized the situation after the attack (NV). Ukrenergo, according to RBC-Ukraine, UNIAN and TSN, was preparing softer schedules for Thursday than the day before. Part of Rivne and the district was left without power (RBC-Ukraine, NV). RBC-Ukraine advises keeping a three-day supply of water at home. The Association of Gas-Producing Companies of Ukraine, AGKU, stated that controlled exports of gas by private producers will bring the state more foreign currency, taxes and royalty payments (TSN, UNIAN, RBC-Ukraine). Prime Minister Koretskyi, according to Censor.NET, said that the 2027 budget requires more than $52 billion in external aid; these are the words of a single outlet. Since the start of the full-scale war the EU has provided Ukraine with €228 billion in aid (Ukrainska Pravda, Yevropeiska Pravda).

In the world: after hot US inflation for September (Reuters), the dollar, per Reuters, touched an 18-month high and then fell back as Federal Reserve officials softened expectations of a rate hike; TSN and RBC-Ukraine write of a high since May 2025. Asian markets, per Reuters, slid on a sell-off of AI-related stocks. The closing figures of the US exchanges for Wednesday, Oct. 14, and numbers that two outlets would confirm are missing from our pool, so we give no figures. The IEA, per Reuters, is increasing the release of oil reserves to more than 400 million barrels, including more than 20 million barrels of diesel. Russia’s oil and gas revenues in October, per a Reuters forecast, will rise by about 10% to $12 billion year on year; this is the estimate of a single agency.
