Trump announces an “energy truce,” and the same day Russia strikes CHP-5 in Kharkiv

Kyiv says it is ready to support a halt to strikes on energy but has not yet received details from the US. Moscow is so far silent, and the strikes continue.

On Sunday, October 11, US President Donald Trump wrote on Truth Social that Russia and Ukraine had agreed to an “energy truce” taking effect immediately, and urged both sides not to violate the agreement (Reuters, Ukrainska Pravda, NV, TSN). He gave no terms, limits or duration. Zelensky told Axios that it was news to him, but that Ukraine would support the truce if Russia joined it. In Telegram the president added that Kyiv was ready not to strike Russian energy if Moscow did the same, and that he was waiting for details from the Americans. He discussed it with Mark Carney, Alexander Stubb and Mark Rutte. Trump’s statement came amid a dispute over the diesel deal: on October 9 he wrote on Truth Social that Russia would supply more than 300,000 tonnes of diesel, and Kyiv and Republicans in Congress criticized the move (Ukrainska Pravda).

The same day Russia struck CHP-5 near Kharkiv. This was stated by Zelensky, and Foreign Minister Andrii Sybiha said that Moscow had violated the truce immediately after it was announced (RBC-Ukraine, TSN, Ukrainska Pravda, Suspilne). According to Zelensky, over the week the Russians carried out more than 200 strikes on power generation and transmission and another 17 on gas and oil facilities (TSN). We found no confirmed data on casualties at the CHP plant itself. Macron said that Russia does not yet agree to a truce (TSN), and that France supports a genuine energy truce (Censor.NET). We have seen no official confirmation from the Kremlin. Reuters writes that Kyiv is waiting for details of the US proposals while Russia continues its attacks. It is unknown whether the “energy truce” covers Russian oil refineries: the US, according to the FT, separately asked Kyiv to stop striking them. A Russian lawmaker, as Kyiv Independent writes, suggested that Trump’s statement is meant to keep oil from getting more expensive. On the morning of October 11 emergency outages had already been introduced in Kyiv and the region, and for Monday Ukrenergo announced schedules for all consumers in some regions (RBC-Ukraine, TSN).

What this means in practice. Until there is a text of the agreement, no one can say which facilities it covers, who will monitor compliance and what will count as a violation. For Ukrainians the question is concrete: whether long outages can be avoided when the cold arrives. Zelensky also asked, according to ABC and Reuters, that Trump pass Putin a proposal for a 60-day truce, and promised elections on that condition. The editors believe the first 24–48 hours will show whether this is a real regime or merely a political statement: it is enough to see whether the nightly attacks on CHP plants and substations stop.

Ukraine · WarPress ✓ 4 outlets · data from the OVA and rescuers

Strike on a store near Kharkiv: three dead; in Ivano-Frankivsk region a drone killed at least two

On October 11 a Russian missile hit a grocery store in a suburb of Kharkiv: three dead and nine wounded, among them children (Ukrainska Pravda, Censor.NET, TSN). In the evening a jet-powered drone attacked Ivano-Frankivsk region. Ukrainska Pravda, RBC-Ukraine and Censor.NET report two dead, NV three; the figures are still being clarified.

In Rivne region three people were injured in an attack and houses were damaged (TSN, Ukrainska Pravda). Zelensky wrote in Telegram that search-and-rescue work in Zaporizhzhia had been completed: Russia killed 23 people there, four of them children.

World · WeaponsPress ✓ 4 outlets · the source is the FT

FT: US threatens to halt the PURL program if Kyiv does not stop striking Russian refineries

The Trump administration, according to the Financial Times, is threatening to suspend arms deliveries and intelligence for Ukraine under the PURL program, through which allies buy American weapons for Kyiv, if Ukraine does not give up strikes on Russian oil refineries (Ukrainska Pravda, Censor.NET, RBC-Ukraine; Reuters relays the FT). Earlier the US, according to the FT, urged European capitals and Kyiv to do this.

For Kyiv this is a matter of air defense and missiles: without PURL there will be fewer of them. Zelensky said in response that Ukraine would stop strikes on refineries if Russia stopped striking energy (Reuters, Ukrainska Pravda, NV). Separately, a well-known Republican senator said that Trump’s diesel deal with Moscow might be illegal (Reuters). In Congress Republicans also criticize it (Ukrainska Pravda).

Diplomacy · PoliticsPress ✓ 5 outlets · statements by the parties

Trump urges Ukraine to change its president; Zelensky promises elections under a 60-day truce

On Sunday Trump urged Ukraine to elect a new president (Bloomberg, Reuters, NV). In response Zelensky said in an ABC interview that he would hold elections if Putin agreed to a 60-day ceasefire (Reuters, Kyiv Post, RBC-Ukraine, NV). Under current law, elections are not held during martial law.

Germany backed Zelensky (Reuters). Ursula von der Leyen backed Zelensky’s call for a truce (FinancialJuice). Elon Musk, according to UNIAN and dev.ua, backed the criticism of Zelensky. We have not seen an American position on the timing of elections.
