After Zelensky’s warning, Russia hits Ukraine overnight with a massive missile and drone strike

On Tuesday evening the president said Ukraine had updated intelligence on a massive strike, and the Air Force reported that strategic bombers had taken off. Overnight into Wednesday, explosions rolled through Kyiv in waves; early reports speak of dead in the capital and in the regions.

On Tuesday, October 6, Volodymyr Zelensky said Ukraine had received updated intelligence on Russia’s preparations for a massive strike, but did not name a time (Ukrainska Pravda, Kyiv Independent). He urged people to take alerts seriously. According to the Air Force, as relayed by Kyiv Post, three Tu-160 strategic bombers took off, and cruise missiles could appear in the airspace after 3 a.m. Later, according to Kyiv Post, Kyiv shook from repeated explosions: ballistic missiles and drones hit the capital.

According to early reports by Ukrainian outlets, at least five civilians were killed and more than 20 injured; in Kyiv the bodies of two dead were found while a fire was being put out (Kyiv Post, Texty.org.ua, LB.ua). The Air Force had not published the exact number of missiles and drones at the time, and different outlets give different estimates. DailyDrop has not seen a final summary.

Bridges under fire: after Kyiv, Russia attacks a Dnipro bridge in Zaporizhzhia

On the morning of Tuesday, October 6, Russian forces attacked one of the bridges across the Dnipro in Zaporizhzhia; traffic was temporarily closed. According to Mykhailo Semikin, acting head of the regional military administration, five people were hurt, among them Red Cross workers on their way to work (TSN, Liga.net). Outlets give different names for the bridge.

This continues a campaign against river crossings. On October 1, Russian drones hit a major Kyiv bridge, the South Bridge, for the first time since the start of the full-scale war. On October 3, debris damaged the North Bridge: the roadway and the trolleybus network. Russia’s Defense Ministry confirmed it was behind this and said the bridges serve military transport (AP, Al Jazeera, Euronews). Kyiv authorities introduced new rules for crossing the bridges during alerts.

Outage schedules return to Ukraine on Tuesday from 15:00; for Wednesday they are announced for the whole day

Ukrenergo reported that on October 6, from 15:00 until the end of the day, hourly outage schedules for household consumers of up to one queue and power limits for industry would apply in some regions (Forbes.ua, Kyiv Post, kurs.com.ua). The cause is damage to energy infrastructure from shelling. No restrictions had been planned the day before.

For Wednesday, October 7, the company announced schedules from 00:00 to 23:59 for all categories of consumers — from 0.5 to 1.5 queues, and for business also power limits throughout the day (Kyiv Post, TSN). The actual time and number of queues depend on the region and may change. After the overnight strike the situation may get worse.

Official rate for Wednesday is 44.9453 hryvnias per dollar: the dollar gets cheaper for the first time in a week

The National Bank set the official rate for October 7 at 44.9453 hryvnias per dollar, 11 kopecks less than for Tuesday (45.0564), kurs.com.ua and Minfin reported. This is the first decline after several days of increases: 44.6819 for October 1, 44.8333 for October 2, 44.9857 for October 5 and 45.0564 for October 6. The NBU reference rate at 12:00 on October 7 is 44.8794, but that is a benchmark, not the official rate. The euro is 50.6394 hryvnias.

DailyDrop cannot confirm the PFTS index for October 6. The 0.25% fall in the official rate is a small move against the backdrop of a massive strike; the week will show the market’s reaction.

Wall Street higher again: S&P 500 +0.58% to 7,818.93; gold up 0.73%

On Tuesday, October 6, the S&P 500 closed at 7,818.93 points (+0.58%). WTI crude was almost unchanged at $89.44 a barrel (+0.01%), and gold added 0.73% to cost $4,187.1 an ounce. No separate cause of the moves has been established.

On Monday WTI fell 1.84%, and on Sunday OPEC+ kept its quotas for November (Nairametrics). Oil remains sensitive to news from the Persian Gulf and about the Strait of Hormuz.
