Ukraine · WarPress ✓ 5 outlets · casualty count kept changing

Russian aerial bombs hit Kharkiv: five to six dead, more than 50 wounded

A strike with guided aerial bombs on Monday, October 5, hit the Slobidskyi district. The mayor and the head of the oblast updated the figures throughout the day, so the final death toll on Tuesday morning is still being clarified.

On Monday afternoon Russia dropped guided aerial bombs on Kharkiv. According to Kyiv Post, the district took three hits: on an industrial zone and next to a residential multi-storey building. Mayor Ihor Terekhov first reported three dead and 25 wounded, a few hours later five dead and 53 wounded (UkraNews, kurs.com.ua). Kyiv Post later reported six dead and 59 wounded. Oblast Military Administration (OVA) head Oleh Syniehubov spoke of a seriously wounded 11-year-old girl, and another boy, 15, is in moderate condition.

Separately, Kyiv Independent described a “double” strike with Banderol missiles on an industrial district of the city: one person was killed, six were wounded and at least ten multi-storey buildings were damaged. The outlet sees this as proof that Russia is increasingly using fast jet-powered munitions. DailyDrop has no final figure confirmed by all sources as of Tuesday morning.

Diplomacy · PeacePress ✓ 4 outlets · Zelensky statement

US proposes a trilateral meeting with Ukraine and Russia by the end of October, Kyiv agrees at the technical level

Zelensky said on Sunday, October 4, that Washington proposes holding a meeting of representatives of Ukraine, the US and Russia by the end of the month (Kyiv Independent, Liga.net, UkraNews, Censor.NET). According to him, Kyiv supports the format at the technical level and is ready to come to the UAE or another place the Americans propose. Moscow, by his account, has not yet given final consent: the US passed it a similar proposal and is waiting for an answer. The last trilateral meeting, according to Liga.net, took place on February 26 in Geneva; since then the process has effectively stalled. A separate Ukrainian negotiating group, according to kurs.com.ua, is expected in the US on October 9 and 10; its composition and agenda have not been disclosed.

Economy · HryvniaPress ✓ 3 sources · PFTS not confirmed

NBU rate for Tuesday is UAH 45.0564 per dollar: hryvnia weakens for the third time in a row

The National Bank set the official rate for October 6 at UAH 45.0564 per dollar (kurs.com.ua, Minfin). On Monday, October 5, it was 44.9857, on Friday, October 2, 44.8333, and on Thursday, October 1, 44.6819. Since the start of the week the dollar has added about 37 kopecks, or 0.8%. The rates of banks and exchange offices shown on other sites differ from the official one.

The PFTS index has no daily public close, so we could not confirm the figure for October 5. This is not news of a failure but the normal state of this market: trading is limited, and most analysis relies on other indicators.

Ukraine · EnergyPress ✓ 3 outlets · Ukrenergo

Strikes on energy in the northeast: new outages, Ukrenergo prepares schedules for Tuesday

Ukrenergo reported that as of the morning of October 6 new outages caused by Russian attacks have been recorded in the Kharkiv, Sumy, Zaporizhzhia and Dnipropetrovsk oblasts; emergency crews are working where security allows (kurs.com.ua, Forbes.ua). The company also announced that in some oblasts, from 15:00 to the end of the day, hourly outage schedules affecting up to one queue will apply for household consumers, along with power limits for industry. The day before, Ukrenergo had not planned any restrictions.

The causes are damage to energy infrastructure and the need not to overload the equipment. Separately, emergency repairs continue in the Poltava oblast after the October 4 strike.

World · Middle EastPress ✓ 3 outlets · claims by the parties not confirmed

Yemen: Houthis say Saudi Arabia carried out 106 strikes in a day, and that they attacked Aramco

The Houthis said that over the day Saudi Arabia carried out 106 air and missile strikes on areas of Yemen they control, and that they themselves had allegedly attacked Saudi Aramco facilities in Riyadh and Khurais (Al Jazeera, Zawya/Reuters, Yeni Safak). Neither Riyadh nor Aramco has confirmed these claims; the coalition earlier called a similar claim “misleading”. The Yemeni government announced a new campaign, the “battle for liberation”, to take back territory.

For a reader in Ukraine, oil prices matter: all sides in the Persian Gulf have already cut exports to 60–80% of normal, and new strikes on Aramco would raise the risk for world prices.
