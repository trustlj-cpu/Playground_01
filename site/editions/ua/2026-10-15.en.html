Ballistic strikes hit Kyiv for the second time in a row with no warning from partners; the target was CHP-4, and the capital has power outages

According to Zelensky, overnight into Oct. 14 Russia fired 39 ballistic missiles and more than 120 drones at the energy sector of Kyiv, the Kyiv region and Kharkiv. In the morning three dead were reported; later Ukrainska Pravda wrote of five.

The ballistic strike on Kyiv came on the evening of Tuesday, Oct. 13. Zelensky said that same evening that the main target was CHP-4 (NV, RBC-Ukraine, Ukrainska Pravda). A nine-story residential building was also hit: according to Kyiv Post, at least one person was killed and 13 were injured. Censor.NET wrote that the explosions came before the alert was announced. The previous evening, Monday, Oct. 12, explosions in Kyiv had also been heard before the alert sounded. Zelensky added that partners had not warned of the launch, and that this was already the second evening in a row; a presidential adviser, according to UNIAN, confirmed that Ukraine had not received intelligence about the launch (RBC-Ukraine, UNIAN).

Overnight into Wednesday the attacks continued: the Air Force warned of ballistic missiles heading for Kyiv, the Kyiv region, Zhytomyr and Kharkiv (Censor.NET, TSN, Ukrainska Pravda). On Telegram Zelensky wrote that over the night the Russians used 39 ballistic missiles and more than 120 strike drones and loitering munitions against the energy sector and critical infrastructure; according to Channel 24, air defense shot down four ballistic targets. The president stressed that the strike was aimed precisely at energy, for which Trump had announced a ceasefire, and that intelligence, according to UNIAN and TSN, reports preparations for new launches. Separately, he said that Russia is showing how little it heeds US appeals and wants Trump to look weak (Ukrainska Pravda, TSN, Yevropeiska Pravda).

Kyiv Mayor Vitali Klitschko reported a strike on several energy facilities in the capital (Censor.NET). In the morning the city had power outages; RBC-Ukraine, Censor.NET and the English-language Ukrainska Pravda wrote of three dead and more than twenty injured. Later Ukrainska Pravda reported five dead and 36 injured in the Desnianskyi and Holosiivskyi districts. In the evening TSN reported that stabilization schedules were not in effect in Kyiv while the grid repairs continue; for Oct. 15 consumption would again be restricted in some regions, but the schedules would be softer (RBC-Ukraine, TSN). Kyiv Post, writing about strikes on power plants in Kyiv and Kharkiv, estimated that at least 600,000 people were left without power; we found this figure in only one outlet.

Moscow responded procedurally. Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov said that Trump had not specified the terms of the “energy truce,” so Russia could not have violated it (Kyiv Post, Ukrainska Pravda, RBC-Ukraine). Zelensky says that Ukraine has not struck Russian energy facilities since the truce was announced (NV); Moscow claimed new strikes on two refineries, and the president’s office called this a fake (Ukrainska Pravda, TSN, RBC-Ukraine). Zelensky discussed the strike and the lack of a warning with Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney (NV, Censor.NET).

What this means. A strike on a CHP plant as the cold sets in hits heat and electricity supply, and the lack of a warning cuts the time people have to reach shelter. Klitschko, according to RBC-Ukraine, asked the Verkhovna Rada where the air defense for the capital is and where the money allocated to protect the energy sector is. Why partners did not warn is not explained in our sources; for a possible link to intelligence, read the neighboring story on Starlink. The death toll and the reasons for the late alert are to be clarified by the city authorities and the Air Force.

War · TechnologyPress ✓ 7 outlets · Musk’s statement; WP sources

Starlink for Ukraine’s Armed Forces limited to 160 km/h, Musk points to the US; WP: Washington threatens to halt intelligence sharing

On Wednesday, Oct. 14, Zelensky called the Starlink restrictions a big mistake that will cost lives, and said that Russia exploits the disruptions to attack with hundreds of drones (UNIAN, Ukrainska Pravda, NV, TSN). This concerns a speed limit for Starshield terminals, and so primarily for drones. According to the Defense Ministry, quoted by NV, it remains at 161 km/h; Mykhailo Fedorov reported in the evening a partial restoration to 160 km/h and added that the restriction must be lifted completely (NV, Ukrainska Pravda). UNIAN writes that Kyiv is asking to raise the limit to 450 km/h. A source of RBC-Ukraine claims that because of the limit interceptors cannot catch up with jet-powered Shaheds; the expert Flash, quoted by NV, believes the mass attack by gasoline-powered Shaheds from the south has nothing to do with Starlink.

Musk replied to Fedorov that the limit for the military package is set not by SpaceX but by the US Army, and the final decision rests with Trump (NV, RBC-Ukraine, TSN, Kyiv Post). The White House, according to Axios, denies any involvement. The service is paid for by Germany until April 2027 (RBC-Ukraine, UNIAN). The EU is discussing with Kyiv the European Govsatcom system, but it will be able to replace Starlink, according to UNIAN, no earlier than 2029. Meanwhile WP reported that US intelligence warned Ukraine’s embassy in Washington that data sharing would be halted immediately if strikes on Russian refineries continue; the threat has not been carried out so far (NV, UNIAN, RBC-Ukraine, Censor.NET, Ukrainska Pravda). The co-chairs of the bipartisan Ukraine support group in the US Congress expressed concern over both Starlink and the possible halt in intelligence sharing (NV).

World · SanctionsPress ✓ 6 outlets · FT and Reuters; politicians’ statements

Zelensky “feels betrayed” by Trump’s diesel deal; US weighs easing sanctions on the “shadow fleet” and unfreezing part of Russian assets

On Wednesday, Oct. 14, Zelensky said he feels betrayed by the diesel deal with Putin announced by Trump, against the backdrop of Russian terror, and that Ukraine is losing a strong partner (Censor.NET, Ukrainska Pravda, TSN, RBC-Ukraine). Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni called the deal a very risky choice (Reuters, Ukrainska Pravda). Twelve senators from both parties wrote to Trump demanding that it be canceled (Kyiv Post, Ukrainska Pravda, Censor.NET). Kirill Dmitriev, who heads the Russian Direct Investment Fund, said that contracts with Washington for the export of Russian diesel would be signed in the coming days (Kyiv Post, FinancialJuice).

On the evening of Oct. 14 two more reports appeared. According to Reuters, Washington is considering easing sanctions on “shadow fleet” vessels to bring down record freight rates; Ukrainska Pravda relayed the same. And the Financial Times (FT) wrote that at a closed meeting in Miami, American negotiators proposed that Europeans unfreeze part of Russian assets and hand them over to a mechanism under Washington’s control in order to restart peace talks; European officials rejected this (Reuters, RBC-Ukraine, FinancialJuice). The International Energy Agency (IEA), according to Reuters, believes the diesel shortage may last into 2027, and according to FinancialJuice, that easing restrictions on Russian exports is unlikely to change the market balance substantially. How concrete these plans are, the sources do not say, and we have seen no official confirmation from Washington.

Diplomacy · EuropePress ✓ 6 outlets · government statements

Zelensky in London: Burnham promises to speed up air defense deliveries; EU pays another 2.86 billion euros, and a meeting with Magyar is expected at the Brussels summit

On Wednesday, Oct. 14, Zelensky spent more than an hour talking with British Prime Minister Andy Burnham at 10 Downing Street; they discussed Starlink and intelligence sharing with the US (NV, citing The Guardian, Ukrainska Pravda). Burnham promised to speed up deliveries of British air defense systems, including lightweight multirole missiles, and said Zelensky “will not leave empty-handed” (Censor.NET, Yevropeiska Pravda, NV, Kyiv Post). According to NV, he wants to “rebuild bridges” between Ukraine and the US while refraining from criticizing Trump. King Charles III also received Zelensky (Ukrainska Pravda, Yevropeiska Pravda).

Brussels added money: according to Kyiv Post, citing the government and the European Commission, the EU paid another 2.86 billion euros from the defense portion of the 90-billion loan to support Ukraine, while Yevropeiska Pravda wrote of 3 billion for drones. Since the start of the full-scale war the EU has provided Ukraine with 228 billion euros in aid (Ukrainska Pravda, Yevropeiska Pravda). Hungarian Prime Minister Péter Magyar confirmed that he will meet Zelensky at the EU summit in Brussels, while Sybiha says the time and place still have to be agreed (Kyiv Independent, Yevropeiska Pravda, NV). Sybiha also said Ukraine wants to resume talks with the US and is interested in restoring the trilateral format (TSN, Censor.NET). Germany, according to Ukrainska Pravda, does not expect US aid to Ukraine to stop. Slovak Prime Minister Robert Fico announced consultations in Lviv on Nov. 5 and is simultaneously preparing a session of the economic commission with Russia (Ukrainska Pravda, RBC-Ukraine).

War · RegionsPress ✓ 6 outlets · data from regional military administrations and the Air Force

Drones on Oct. 14: a Shahed strike killed six in Lutsk and three in Kropyvnytskyi; Odesa and Vinnytsia also hit

On Wednesday, Oct. 14, Russia attacked Ukraine with drones all day. According to Air Force representative Ihnat, quoted by RBC-Ukraine, there were 145 drones in the daytime alone, roughly half of them jet-powered. A jet-powered Shahed hit a wholesale warehouse in Lutsk: six people were killed, and the search and rescue operation has been completed (RBC-Ukraine, Channel 24). Vinnytsia was attacked by jet-powered drones three times during the day, and a civilian infrastructure facility was destroyed (TSN, Ukrainska Pravda). In the evening, according to Kirovohrad regional military administration head Andrii Raikovych, a massive attack began on Kropyvnytskyi and the region: the targets were logistics, transport and industrial enterprises; three people were killed and six injured (Kyiv Independent, Kyiv Post, TSN, Ukrainska Pravda).

In Odesa a drone hit a high-rise apartment building; the apartment on the 17th floor, where the destruction is greatest, was empty at the time of the strike (TSN, Ukrainska Pravda, Censor.NET). Late in the evening explosions were heard in Lutsk again (Channel 24, TSN). In Kramatorsk, according to Ukrainska Pravda, a Russian air strike left wounded and dead. The General Staff counted 222 combat clashes on the front from the start of the day until the evening; Kyiv Post writes that Russia continues to press in the Pokrovsk direction (Kyiv Post, Ukrainska Pravda). Zelensky links the scale of the attack to Starlink disruptions, while the expert Flash sees no such link regarding the attack by gasoline-powered Shaheds from the south; for more see the neighboring story.
