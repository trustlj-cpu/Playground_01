Kim Jong-un makes surprise visit to China after more than 1 year… crosses New Yalu River Bridge to Dandong, expected to return home same day

The Ministry of Unification said on the 24th that Kim Jong-un, chairman of North Korea’s State Affairs Commission, would arrive in Dandong, China, via the New Yalu River Bridge, and a ruling-camp official told Yonhap that he appears set to return home after finishing the day’s schedule.

The Ministry of Unification said on the 24th that Kim Jong-un, chairman of North Korea’s State Affairs Commission, was visiting China and would arrive in Dandong via the New Yalu River Bridge, Kyunghyang Shinmun and Dong-A Ilbo reported in breaking news. Yonhap reported that Kim’s surprise visit to China after more than 1 year had been confirmed, and Kyunghyang Shinmun said it came 1 year and 1 month after he went to Beijing last September to attend the parade marking the 80th anniversary of China’s Victory Day. Citing Japan’s Kyodo News, Yonhap reported that traffic was tightly controlled and hotels were also closed off in parts of Dandong that morning, and that a senior North Korean figure may have visited. Dong-A Ilbo reported that the New Yalu River Bridge linking Dandong and Sinuiju was formally opened on the 15th of this month, 12 years after its completion.

Yonhap reported that a ruling-camp official said Kim appears set to return home after finishing the day’s schedule in China, and Korea Economic Daily carried the same in breaking news. Kyunghyang Shinmun reported that North Korean media said on the 24th that Kim presided over and spoke at the Korean People’s Army’s 3rd Conference of Company Commanders in Pyongyang on the 22nd and 23rd, adding that Kim, the subject of death rumors, had appeared in public again. Dong-A Ilbo reported that the conference was the first all-army gathering since the 9th Congress of the Workers’ Party. Dong-A Ilbo reported speculation that the visit may be tied to the 25th, the 76th anniversary of China’s entry into the Korean War, and Korea Economic Daily ran an analysis headlined that the trip was a show of North Korea–China closeness ahead of the Shenzhen APEC. The purpose of the visit and any official Chinese announcement have not been confirmed.

Society · CybersecurityPress ✓ 4 outlets · basis for naming Lazarus and any actual breach unconfirmed

North Korean hacking group Lazarus reported preparing attacks exploiting PHP flaw… Cyfirma spots ‘Korean-speaking attacker’

Dong-A Ilbo reported that global cybersecurity firm Cyfirma (CYFIRMA) said on the 22nd (local time) that, in its collection of non-public source data, it had observed a Korean-speaking attacker conducting reconnaissance of web-accessible assets with a PHP-to-Shell vulnerability and validating publicly available exploit code on a private anonymous network (I2P) channel. Yonhap and SBS carried this under the headline ‘North Korean hacking group Lazarus appears to be preparing attacks exploiting PHP vulnerability,’ while Korea Economic Daily described it as a group ‘suspected of North Korean ties.’ Dong-A Ilbo reported that a claim had been raised that it was reconnaissance of government agencies and financial institutions around the world, including Korea, while adding that no actual breach has been confirmed. Dong-A Ilbo reported that the Korea Internet and Security Agency (KISA) issued an additional security notice in the early hours of the 24th, urging caution over a newly identified remote code execution vulnerability in PHP. According to Dong-A Ilbo, the flaw arises in handling the session, which holds login information, and in recording file-upload progress; as no official security patch is out yet, urgent interim measures are recommended. In an article on ‘the full story of the PHP security incident,’ Yonhap pointed to concerns that a server could be taken over without logging in. This is a new development following the government’s cyber crisis alert raised to ‘Caution,’ covered in the morning edition.

After Oh Se-hoon’s acquittal, ruling camp’s ‘impeach Cho Hee-de’ talk and People Power Party’s ‘stop intimidating the judiciary’ clash… President Lee’s approval rating in the 40% range

Kyunghyang Shinmun reported that after Seoul Mayor Oh Se-hoon was acquitted in the appellate trial over the ‘Myung Tae-kyun poll-payment allegations,’ Democratic Party leaders and other figures in the ruling camp all took aim at the judiciary on social media. Kyunghyang Shinmun said some likened the court to a ‘not-guilty vending machine,’ and put in its headline the opposing claims of ‘Cho Hee-de must be impeached’ and ‘low-grade pressure.’ Newsis reported that when ‘Cho Hee-de impeachment talk’ emerged in the ruling camp, the People Power Party demanded ‘an end to intimidation of the judiciary,’ while Chosun Ilbo ran side by side the ruling party’s position of ‘proceeding step by step’ and the opposition’s rebuttal of a ‘Cho Hee-de explains everything’ theory.

SBS reported poll results showing President Lee Jae-myung’s approval rating back in the 40% range for the first time in 7 weeks, and said opinion on the impeachment of Chief Justice Cho Hee-de is evenly split. No schedule for introducing or voting on an impeachment motion was confirmed in the reports.

Sports · FootballPress ✓ 4 outlets · date of the title ceremony unconfirmed

FC Seoul draw 1-1 with Gangwon to clinch K League 1 title… 7th overall, first in 10 years since 2016

Yonhap and Dong-A Ilbo reported that FC Seoul drew 1-1 with Gangwon FC in a home match in Round 33 of the Hana Bank K League 1 2026 at Seoul World Cup Stadium on the 24th, clinching the title for the first time in 10 years, since 2016. According to Dong-A Ilbo, Seoul moved to 20 wins, 7 draws and 6 losses (67 league points) and, by keeping its 16-point lead over second-placed Ulsan HD (51 league points), who drew 2-2 with Pohang Steelers at the same time, clinched the title early regardless of the results of the 5 remaining Final Round matches. Yonhap said it is the club’s 7th title overall, and Korea Economic Daily also carried the same in breaking news.

Chosun Ilbo (OSEN) reported that Gangwon’s Kang Jun-hyeok equalized 5 minutes after Moon Seon-min’s opening goal, and the match ended 1-1. Yonhap said the stadium where chants of ‘Kim Ki-dong out’ echoed early in the season was now filled with praise for ‘Ki-dong Magic.’ Dong-A Ilbo reported that Seoul’s title was not the ‘one-man show’ of any single player but a ‘harmony’ built together by many players.

Milwaukee beat Tampa Bay 3-2 in World Series Game 1… starter’s first pitch clocked at 166.6 km in first WS in 44 years

Dong-A Ilbo reported that the Milwaukee Brewers, in the Major League World Series (best-of-seven) for the first time in 44 years, beat the Tampa Bay Rays 3-2 in Game 1 in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, on the 24th (Korea time) to seize the early advantage. Dong-A Ilbo said Milwaukee, in the World Series for the first time in 44 years since 1982, when it was in the American League, and Tampa Bay, back for the first time in 6 years since 2020, are both chasing a first title in club history. Dong-A Ilbo said Milwaukee starter Jacob Misiorowski pitched 5 innings.

Chosun Ilbo said in its headline that Misiorowski threw 166.6 km from his very first pitch, and its game story covered the opening hit and escaping a bases-loaded, one-out jam in the 9th. Yonhap also reported that Milwaukee beat Tampa Bay 3-2 to take the early advantage in the World Series.
