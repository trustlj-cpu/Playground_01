Firefighter trapped while battling Bucheon paint plant fire dies in the line of duty; president visits funeral hall and posthumously confers Order of Service Merit

Kim Jin-seok, a fire lieutenant in his 40s who was trapped while fighting a factory fire in Naedong, Ojeong District, Bucheon, on the 16th, died on the 17th, and President Lee Jae-myung visited the funeral hall and posthumously conferred the Order of Service Merit.

Several outlets reported that a fire broke out around 8:38 a.m. on the 16th on the third floor of a metal-parts paint plant in Naedong, Ojeong District, Bucheon, Gyeonggi Province, and was put out after 2 hours 28 minutes. Two firefighters in their 40s who went in as the first-arriving unit were trapped in the smoke, and Newspim and News1 reported that Kim Jin-seok, a fire lieutenant taken to hospital in cardiac arrest, died around 12:55 a.m. on the 17th. The other man in his 40s who was trapped with him was seriously hurt and is being treated. The number of injured differed from one report to the next. YTN and Newsis said 8 people were hurt, including 6 firefighters, and later reports tallied it as 7 injured besides the one killed: 5 firefighters and 2 people connected to the plant. From around 9:30 a.m. on the 17th, 23 officials from the police, fire authorities, the National Forensic Service and the Korea Electrical Safety Corporation carried out a joint inspection. Segye Ilbo and Financial News reported that the inspection team believes the fire started in the third-floor thinner washing workshop and that dozens of cans containing thinner were found at the scene.

On the 17th, President Lee Jae-myung visited the funeral hall set up at Soonchunhyang University Bucheon Hospital, offered incense and posthumously conferred the Order of Service Merit on the deceased, Newspim and Segye Ilbo reported. In the guest book he wrote: “In the safe everyday life you protected, the people will remember your sacrifice forever.” Oh My News and Kukmin Ilbo, citing a Cheong Wa Dae briefing, reported that the president fell silent for a while when he was told that the deceased's daughter is seven years old. According to Newspim, the government will support a one-rank special promotion from fire sergeant to fire lieutenant and burial at the National Cemetery, and the president instructed Interior and Safety Minister Yoon Ho-jung to support the treatment and recovery of the injured firefighters. The funeral will be held as a Gyeonggi provincial government funeral, and the farewell ceremony will take place at Bucheon Indoor Gymnasium on the morning of the 19th. Police formed a dedicated 33-member investigation team headed by the chief of the wide-area crime investigation unit. Insight reported that about 100 20-liter containers of Class 2 petroleum products (about 2 tons) were at the scene but there was no record of a hazardous-materials handling permit and no sprinklers, and Yonhap reported that the site was found to have no explosion-proof equipment.

Gyeonggi Governor Choo Mi-ae apologizes at fallen firefighter's funeral hall four days after ‘fire service fiscal straw’ remark

Gyeonggi Governor Choo Mi-ae visited the funeral hall of Kim Jin-seok, the fire lieutenant who died in the Bucheon fire, on the 17th and, after paying her respects, told reporters, “Since you want an apology, I apologize with all my heart” and “I had no intention whatsoever of belittling firefighters,” Financial News and Kyunghyang Shinmun reported. It is her first official apology, four days after she said at the National Assembly Public Administration and Security Committee's parliamentary audit of Gyeonggi Province on the 13th that fire service funding is “like the biggest, most troublesome fiscal straw.” MBC reported that Choo said the expression came as she was explaining the burden of personnel costs, and that she acknowledges “a slip of expression that came while I was talking about a structural fiscal problem.” Weekly Kyunghyang reported that the fire headquarters of the Korean Government Employees' Union issued a statement on the 14th demanding an apology, and MBC reported that the firefighters' union protested that the remark was “insulting.” Gyeonggi Province explained that the point was to stress that although firefighters were switched to national civil servants, local governments bear most of the funding and the share of national treasury support is shrinking. According to YTN, retired firefighters and union officials at the funeral hall blocked Choo's way, shouting “Apologize” and “Resign.” People Power Party chief spokesperson Park Sung-hoon called her explanation right after the apology “long-winded self-justification,” and Democratic Party lawmaker Jung Sung-ho said, in effect, that a sincere apology should come before any explanation, Kyunghyang Shinmun and Herald Business reported.

Economy · Stock marketPress ✓ 6 outlets · forced-liquidation tally cites Korea Financial Investment Association data

KOSPI rebounds 2.05% on the 16th after 7 straight losing sessions; forced liquidation of borrowed-money stock bets totals 298.1 billion won in ten days

Korea Economic Daily, Seoul Economic TV and others reported that KOSPI fell for 7 straight trading days from the 6th through the 15th, sliding to 5554.22 on the 15th, and that circuit breakers were triggered that day in both the KOSPI market and the Kosdaq market. On the 16th it closed at 5668.05, up 113.83 points (2.05%), rising for the first time in 8 trading days. The Kosdaq ended at 794.78, up 33.96 points (4.46%), and a buy-side sidecar was triggered. According to eToday and Seoul Economic TV, foreigners were net buyers of 343.0 billion won in the KOSPI market, ending a net-selling streak that had run for 8 straight trading days, and Samsung Electronics closed up 2.62% at 215,500 won and SK hynix up 0.79% at 1,272,000 won. During the session KOSPI rose as high as 5809.94 before giving back much of the gain.

According to the Korea Financial Investment Association, actual forced liquidations relative to unpaid brokerage balances from the 6th to the 15th totaled 298.1 billion won, with 89.3 billion won on the 14th, when the index plunged, and 80.5 billion won on the 15th, Ajunews and Herald Business reported. Forced liquidation is when a brokerage forcibly sells the shares of an investor who bought on a margin loan and cannot make up a collateral shortfall. Seoul Economic TV reported that over the same period individual investors bought domestic and foreign bonds and, in the US market, bought large amounts of 3x leveraged semiconductor ETFs.

Trump: “Hyundai to invest 748.6 billion won in West Point, Georgia”… the Hyundai Transys expansion Gov. Kemp announced on the 8th

eToday and Seoul Economic TV reported that U.S. President Donald Trump, at a midterm-election rally in Dalton, Georgia, on the 16th (local time), listed overseas companies' investments in the U.S. and said, in effect, “Hyundai just announced that it is investing $560 million in the expansion of its West Point, Georgia plant.” In won that comes to 748.6 billion won, and some outlets converted it to 749.0 billion or 750.0 billion won. Segye Ilbo, Sisa Journal and others reported that the investment is a plan of Hyundai Transys, a parts affiliate of Hyundai Motor Group. At the same rally Trump also mentioned that Belgian drugmaker UCB would invest $2 billion and Italian tire maker Pirelli $1.2 billion in Georgia.

Media reported that the plan had been unveiled in a press release by Georgia Governor Brian Kemp on the 8th, before Trump's remark. Kemp said it is “part of an ongoing effort to support global automotive customers operating in North America” and that the company plans to create 320 new jobs. Money Today and Munhwa Ilbo reported that the funds will go to modernizing the existing Plants 1 and 2 at West Point and building a new Plant 3, to expand production capacity for electrified powertrains for hybrid vehicles. Atlanta K News put the site's current headcount at about 1,250.

KIA beat LG 5-1 in Semi-Playoff Game 1… Kim Do-yeong 2 homers and 4 RBIs, Naile 7 innings and 1 run

MK Sports, Jaekyung Ilbo, Sidae Ilbo and others reported that KIA beat LG 5-1 in Game 1 of the 2026 KBO Semi-Playoff, a best-of-five series, at Gwangju-Kia Champions Field on the 17th. Kim Do-yeong went 2 for 3 with 2 home runs and 4 RBIs. His go-ahead solo homer in the 4th made it 2-1, and with two out and a runner on first in the 8th he hit a two-run homer after a full count to make it 5-1. Starter James Naile threw 92 pitches over 7 innings, allowing 4 hits and 1 run with 6 strikeouts, to earn the win, and Seong Yeong-tak, Kwak Do-gyu and Jeon Sang-hyeon followed him. LG's starter was Anders Tolhurst. Newspim reported that only the suspended-game rule applies in the postseason when it rains.

Cheonji Ilbo and Seopeul reported that in the top of the 3rd, with the score 1-1, the KIA battery read LG's bunt play and caught the runner in a rundown, and that a double play came in the top of the 6th. KIA manager Lee Bum-ho said their response to LG's tactics was the key. 21st Century Issue reported that teams that won Game 1 of the Semi-Playoff went on to the Playoff 12/17 (70.6%) of the time. Game 2 will be held at the same park at 2 p.m. on the 18th.
