Firefighter trapped while battling Bucheon paint plant fire dies in the line of duty; president visits memorial altar, confers Green Stripes Order of Service Merit posthumously

Fire Lieutenant Kim Jin-seok, in his 40s, who was trapped while fighting a factory fire in Naedong, Ojeong District, Bucheon, on the 16th, died on the 17th, and President Lee Jae-myung visited the memorial altar and posthumously conferred the Green Stripes Order of Service Merit.

A fire broke out around 8:38 a.m. on the 16th on the third floor of a metal-parts paint plant in Naedong, Ojeong District, Bucheon, Gyeonggi, and was put out after 2 hours 28 minutes, several outlets reported. Two firefighters in their 40s who went in as the first-arriving unit were trapped in the smoke, and Fire Lieutenant Kim Jin-seok, who was taken to hospital in cardiac arrest, died around 12:55 a.m. on the 17th, Newspim and News1 reported. The other firefighter in his 40s who was trapped with him was seriously injured and is being treated. The number of injured differed from report to report. YTN and Newsis said 8 people were hurt, including 6 firefighters, and later reports settled on 7 injured besides the one killed in the line of duty: 5 firefighters and 2 plant officials. From around 9:30 a.m. on the 17th, 23 officials from the police, the fire service, the National Forensic Service and the Korea Electrical Safety Corporation carried out a joint inspection. Segye Ilbo and Financial News reported that investigators regard the point of ignition as a third-floor thinner washing workshop, and that dozens of cans containing thinner were found at the scene.

On the 17th, President Lee Jae-myung visited the memorial altar set up at Soonchunhyang University Bucheon Hospital, offered incense and posthumously conferred the Green Stripes Order of Service Merit on the deceased, Newspim and Segye Ilbo reported. In the guestbook he wrote, “In the safe daily life you protected, the people will remember your sacrifice forever.” OhmyNews and Kukmin Ilbo, citing a Blue House briefing, reported that the president was left speechless for a while when told that the man’s daughter is seven years old. According to Newspim, the government will support a one-rank special promotion from Fire Sergeant to Fire Lieutenant and burial at the National Cemetery, and the president instructed Interior and Safety Minister Yun Ho-jung to support the treatment and recovery of the injured firefighters. The funeral will be held as a Gyeonggi provincial funeral, and the memorial service will take place at Bucheon Indoor Gymnasium on the morning of the 19th. Police have formed a 33-member dedicated investigation team headed by the chief of the Metropolitan Crime Investigation Unit. Insight reported that there were 100-odd 20-liter containers of Class 2 petroleum products (about 2 tons) at the scene, yet there was no record of a hazardous-materials handling permit and no sprinklers, and Yonhap reported that it had learned there was no explosion-proof equipment.

Gyeonggi Governor Choo Mi-ae apologizes at the altar of the firefighter killed in the fire, four days after her ‘fiscal straw’ remark on fire services

Gyeonggi Governor Choo Mi-ae visited the memorial altar of Fire Lieutenant Kim Jin-seok, who died in the Bucheon fire, on the 17th and, after paying her respects, told reporters, “Since you are asking for an apology, I apologize with all my heart” and “I had no intention at all of disparaging firefighters,” Financial News and Kyunghyang Shinmun reported. It is her first public apology, four days after she said at the National Assembly Public Administration and Security Committee’s parliamentary audit of Gyeonggi Province on the 13th that the fire budget is “like the biggest headache, a fiscal straw.” MBC reported that Choo said the phrase came out while she was explaining the personnel cost burden, and that she acknowledged “a slip of expression that came out while I was talking about a structural fiscal problem.” Weekly Kyunghyang reported that the fire service headquarters of the Korean Government Employees’ Union issued a statement on the 14th demanding an apology, and MBC reported that the fire union protested that it was “insulting.” Gyeonggi Province explained that the point was that although firefighters have been converted to the national civil service, local governments still bear most of the cost and the share of national treasury support is shrinking. According to YTN, retired firefighters and union officials blocked Choo at the altar and shouted, “Apologize” and “Resign.” People Power Party chief spokesperson Park Seong-hoon called her explanation right after the apology “long-winded self-justification,” and Democratic Party lawmaker Jung Sung-ho said, in effect, that a sincere apology should come before any explanation, Kyunghyang Shinmun and Herald Business reported.

KOSPI rebounds 2.05% on the 16th after seven straight sessions of losses; forced liquidation of debt-funded bets reaches 298.1 billion won in ten days

The KOSPI fell for seven straight sessions from the 6th to the 15th, sinking to 5554.22 on the 15th, and circuit breakers were triggered that day on both the KOSPI market and the Kosdaq market, Korea Economic Daily, Seoul Economic TV and others reported. On the 16th it closed at 5668.05, up 113.83 points (2.05%), rising for the first time in 8 sessions. The Kosdaq ended at 794.78, up 33.96 points (4.46%), and a buy-side sidecar was triggered. According to E-Today and Seoul Economic TV, foreigners net bought 343.0 billion won of shares on the KOSPI market, ending eight straight sessions of net selling, and Samsung Electronics closed up 2.62% at 215,500 won and SK Hynix up 0.79% at 1,272,000 won. Intraday the KOSPI rose as high as 5809.94 before giving back much of the gain.

According to the Korea Financial Investment Association, actual forced liquidations against unsettled brokerage receivables from the 6th to the 15th totaled 298.1 billion won, including 89.3 billion won on the 14th, when the index plunged, and 80.5 billion won on the 15th, Ajunews and Herald Business reported. Forced liquidation is when a brokerage forcibly sells the shares of an investor who bought on margin loans and cannot make up a shortfall in collateral. Seoul Economic TV reported that over the same period individual investors bought domestic and foreign bonds, and in the U.S. market bought heavily into triple-leveraged semiconductor ETFs.

Trump: “Hyundai to invest 748.6 billion won in West Point, Georgia”… Hyundai Transys expansion that Gov. Kemp unveiled on the 8th

At a midterm-election rally in Dalton, Georgia, on the 16th local time, U.S. President Donald Trump, listing foreign companies’ investments in the United States, said in effect, “Hyundai just announced it is investing $560 million to expand its West Point, Georgia, plant,” E-Today and Seoul Economic TV reported. In won that comes to 748.6 billion won, and some outlets converted it to 749.0 billion won or 750.0 billion won. Segye Ilbo, Sisa Journal and others reported that the investment is a plan by Hyundai Transys, a parts affiliate of Hyundai Motor Group. At the same rally Trump also mentioned that Belgian drugmaker UCB would invest $2 billion and Italian tire maker Pirelli $1.2 billion in Georgia.

Outlets said the plan had been made public by Georgia Governor Brian Kemp in a press release on the 8th, ahead of Trump’s remarks. Kemp said it is “part of ongoing efforts to support our global automotive customers operating in North America” and that 320 new jobs would be created. Money Today and Munhwa Ilbo reported that the funds will go to modernizing the existing Plants 1 and 2 at West Point and building a new Plant 3, and to expanding production capacity for electrified powertrains for hybrid vehicles. Atlanta K News put current employment at the site at about 1,250.

KIA beat LG 5-1 in Game 1 of the Semi-Playoff… Kim Do-yeong 2 homers, 4 RBI; Naile 7 innings, 1 run

KIA beat LG 5-1 in Game 1 of the 2026 KBO Semi-Playoff, a best-of-five series (first to three wins), on the 17th at Gwangju-Kia Champions Field, MK Sports, Jaekyung Ilbo, Sidae Ilbo and others reported. Kim Do-yeong went 2 for 3 with 2 home runs and 4 RBI. He made it 2-1 with a go-ahead solo shot in the 4th and, with two outs and a runner on first in the 8th, hit a two-run homer after a full count to stretch the lead to 5-1. Starter James Naile threw 92 pitches over 7 innings, allowing 4 hits and 1 run with 6 strikeouts, to earn the win, and Seong Yeong-tak, Kwak Do-gyu and Jeon Sang-hyeon followed. LG’s starter was Anders Tolhurst. Newspim reported that in the postseason only the suspended-game rule applies in case of rain.

In the top of the 3rd with the score 1-1, KIA’s battery read LG’s bunt play and caught the runner in a rundown, and a double play came in the top of the 6th, Cheonji Ilbo and Seoplus reported. KIA manager Lee Bum-ho said the response to LG’s tactics was decisive. 21st Century Issue reported that teams that won Game 1 of the Semi-Playoff went on to the Playoffs at a rate of 12/17 (70.6%). Game 2 is at 2 p.m. on the 18th at the same venue.
