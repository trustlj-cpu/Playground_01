According to the U.S. Side, Leaders of the U.S., China and Russia to Hold Their First Summit on the 19th; Nuclear Arms Reduction and Iran Talks on the Agenda, Ukraine Not Covered; China and Russia Unconfirmed

The White House said a three-way summit of President Trump, President Xi Jinping and President Putin will be held on the 19th. It would be the first time the three leaders have met together, and the U.S. side has put the reduction of nuclear weapons on the agenda. China and Russia have not yet confirmed the date or the agenda.

On the 14th, the U.S. government said that a summit of three leaders, President Trump, China’s President Xi Jinping and Russia’s President Putin, will be held on the 19th of this month. It would be the first time the three leaders have met together. This is the U.S. side’s announcement, and China and Russia have not yet confirmed it. The venue is reported to be Shenzhen in southern China. A senior U.S. administration official indicated that the agenda centers on nuclear arms reduction and talks over Iran, and that Ukraine is not to be taken up. The spokesperson said the summit would advance U.S. interests. Nuclear arms reduction means talks aimed at cutting the number of nuclear weapons each country holds.

As for a meeting of the three, it was reported in October that President Putin proposed it in a U.S.-Russia phone call, and President Trump spoke favorably of it in front of reporters. With this announcement, the outline of the date and agenda has been shown for the first time. Still, China and Russia have not confirmed the date and agenda. How the two countries view it, and whether the venue has been formally decided, cannot be confirmed at this point. What will actually be decided at the summit will have to wait until the 19th.

Britain, France, Germany, Italy, Poland and the EU Expected to Join Next Round of Ukraine Peace Talks; European Leaders Agree

On the 13th, at an informal summit of EU leaders held in the Irish capital, Dublin, European leaders agreed that representatives of five countries, Britain, France, Germany, Italy and Poland, and of the EU would join the next U.S.-mediated round of peace talks between Russia and Ukraine. EU President Costa said Europe must raise a clear and united voice. The talks so far have been carried out among three countries, including the United States, with Europe not at the table. An informal summit means a meeting at which the leaders of EU member states gather to coordinate views without making formal decisions. The date of the next talks has not yet been given. How the United States, the mediator, and Russia, a party to the war, view Europe taking part in this lineup has also not been confirmed. In shaping the terms of peace, matters that bear directly on European security, such as security guarantees after a ceasefire and the handling of borders, are the focus. Security guarantees are an arrangement setting out which countries will pledge to defend, and how, so that there is no new attack after a ceasefire. Whether the representatives of the five countries and the EU actually enter the talks will be decided by the announcement of a date and the responses of the countries concerned.

U.S. Temporarily Halts Departures Nationwide; Reports Conflict on 4 to 5 Hours Until Lifted; Transportation Secretary Reportedly Blames Overloaded Communications Equipment

The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) halted aircraft departures at all airports across the United States at around 4 p.m. Eastern time on the 13th, citing a communications failure in air traffic control. A nationwide departure halt had not been ordered since January 2023. The halt was lifted on the 14th, Japan time, but the time it took to lift is conflicting across reports, at 4 to 5 hours. According to reports, Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy explained that the cause was that the switches and routers that relay communications were overloaded and stopped, and that communications and radar were not completely cut off. A router is a device that sorts data and sends it toward its destination. Air traffic control is the work of directing, from the ground, the order of takeoffs and landings and altitudes so that aircraft can fly safely.

Even after the halt was lifted, cancellations and delays continued in various places. The exact numbers of cancellations and delays have not been confirmed. Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin said there is so far no evidence of a cyberattack or the like. How far the equipment overload explains the path to the nationwide departure halt will have to await further explanation from U.S. authorities. How far operations return to normal after the lifting will be clear from today’s flight information.

Up to 3.37 Million Records at Keisei Electric Railway, Up to Over 220,000 People at Keihan Holdings Possibly Leaked; Unauthorized Access to Reservation System and LINE Contractor

On the 14th, Keisei Electric Railway announced that the seat reservation system for the “Skyliner” limited express linking Narita Airport and central Tokyo had suffered unauthorized access, and that information on up to 3.37 million records, including users’ names, phone numbers and email addresses, may have been obtained by a third party. Unauthorized access means an act in which a person without authority gets inside a system by using someone else’s ID or a weakness in the system. The 3.37 million is an upper limit, and the number actually taken has not been confirmed.

Keihan Holdings also announced on the same 14th that a company entrusted with managing its official LINE account had suffered unauthorized access, and that information on up to over 220,000 people may have leaked. What may have leaked is user IDs, and some also include names. In both cases the cause and the extent of the unauthorized access are still under investigation. Because the leak came from a party to which the handling of personal information had been entrusted, caution is needed against nuisance messages that pose as notices reaching users.

Tamahome Asks Non-Life Insurers to Let It Close Its Insurance Agency Business; Company-Wide Internal Probe of All Stores Under Way, External Investigation Under Consideration

On the 14th, the housing builder Tamahome disclosed that, over the problem in which suspicions have emerged that it had made fraudulent claims for fire insurance payouts, it has told the non-life insurance companies of its policy to close its insurance agency business. It is also considering an external investigation, in addition to the internal probe it is carrying out at all of its stores. As of the 13th, it had been reported that two major non-life insurers had decided to stop new sales through Tamahome, and that one more was heading the same way.

This time, it was Tamahome, which had been entrusted with the sales, that signaled its own intention to leave the agency business. The suspicion of fraudulent claims has not yet been established, and the extent of the suspicion has not been made clear. Whether the non-life insurers accepted the request, how fire insurance contracts already concluded will be handled, and when an external investigation will begin have not been confirmed.
