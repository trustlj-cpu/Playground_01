Japanese and Chinese Experts Hold Direct Dialogue in Shanghai, First Since the Taiwan Contingency Remarks, Japanese Side to Run Its Own Opinion Poll as Early as January

At a dialogue of Japanese and Chinese experts held in Shanghai by the private organization Genron NPO, some saw the absence of dialogue between the two countries as a “dangerous situation.” The Chinese side did not respond to a joint opinion poll, and the Japanese side plans to conduct one on its own as early as January

Japanese and Chinese experts held a face-to-face “strategic dialogue” in Shanghai on the 28th. It is the first time experts from the two countries have met for dialogue since Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi gave Diet answers on a Taiwan contingency in November 2025. The host was Genron NPO, a Japanese private organization, and at the venue some saw the current state in which dialogue between governments has broken off as a “dangerous situation.” Genron NPO has long handled private dialogue between Japan and China, and in November 2025 it had announced the postponement of the Tokyo–Beijing Forum, which it had held every year. This dialogue is a move, one year after that postponement, to keep open the window of dialogue that remains among private citizens. At the venue, the significance of continuing dialogue was reaffirmed.

As another development, the joint Japan-China opinion poll will be conducted by the Japanese side alone as early as January. The poll has been conducted jointly by Genron NPO of Japan and an organization on the Chinese side, and in 2025 the release of results remained stopped from the planned date of November 4. The Chinese side has not responded to the poll this time. The venue for dialogue has reopened, but a gap remains in conducting the poll jointly. When and with what items the Japanese side will conduct the poll, and how the Chinese side will react to the handling of the results, are not yet known.

Gaza Board of Peace Senior Representative in Tokyo: “Now Is the Time to Disarm Hamas,” Stresses It as Top Priority for Recovery and Reconstruction

Mladenov, Senior Representative of the Board of Peace, the interim governing body for the Gaza Strip in Palestine, attended a symposium held in Tokyo. Mladenov stressed the view that the disarmament of the Islamic organization Hamas is the top-priority issue in advancing Gaza's recovery and reconstruction, and appealed that disarmament should proceed “now.” The Board of Peace is a framework led by US President Trump, and the visiting official is reported to have said humanitarian challenges ahead of Gaza's winter are heavy. Disarmament has become the biggest point of contention over Gaza's ceasefire and reconstruction. In August the board stated the view that Israeli forces' withdrawal from Gaza would come only after Hamas's disarmament is complete, and Hamas, which argues that withdrawal must come first, pushed back. Hamas has not agreed to disarm and is said to still hold about half of Gaza. What Mladenov asked of Japan and how the Japanese government will respond are not yet clear.

Two Unidentified Bodies Found in Mountains of Miyagi Prefecture, Kanagawa Police Arrest Man on Suspicion of Kidnapping Missing Female High School Student and Are Confirming Identities

Two bodies whose identities are unknown were found in the mountains of Shichikashuku Town, Miyagi Prefecture. Kanagawa Prefectural Police are working to confirm the identities, saying one of them may be a female high school student who had gone missing in Yokohama. The prefectural police arrested a man from Ogawara Town, Miyagi Prefecture, on suspicion of kidnapping the female student. The discovery of the bodies was reported on the 27th, and the arrest of the man was reported one after another through the 28th.

The man and the female student are reported to have possibly met through social media. Neighbors near the man's home said the man was “quiet and serious-looking,” and shock has spread. At the stage of reports, the identities of the two bodies, how and when they died, and whether the man admits the allegation have not been made clear. How far the prefectural police announcement shows identities and circumstances is the next point to confirm.

German Bundestag Reported to Pass 2027 Budget Bill, Defense-Related Budget Including Special Fund Tops ¥24 Trillion, Largest Since End of Cold War

Germany's Bundestag was reported to have passed the draft budget for fiscal 2027. Of it, the defense-related budget, said to be to respond to the threat from Russia, comes to more than ¥24 trillion in Japanese yen when combined with the military's special fund, and is said to be the largest since the end of the East-West Cold War.

Only one outlet is reporting this at present, and the vote date, vote count and breakdown of the defense-related budget cannot be confirmed. The special fund is money set aside separately from the regular budget to equip the military, and its size shows the scale of what Germany is directing to defense.

Russian Upper House Approves 2027 Budget, Defense Spending 35% of Expenditure, Largest Since Invasion of Ukraine

On the 27th, the upper house of the Russian parliament approved the draft budget for next year, 2027, with defense spending the largest since the invasion of Ukraine began; its share of total expenditure reaches 35%. In other words, a little over a third of the money the state spends goes to the military.

Economic experts critical of the Putin government point to the shape of this budget as “at an impasse.” The total amount of the budget, and how spending in other fields such as education and health care changed, are not reported in detail at the stage of reports.
