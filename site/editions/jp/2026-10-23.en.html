Serious Flaw in PHP: Prime Minister Urges “Immediate Action” on X, Tokyo Police Hold Emergency Meeting With 59 Critical Infrastructure Operators

After a fixed version of PHP, widely used on websites around the world, was released, an unusual appeal from the prime minister was followed by a string of temporary shutdowns of bank, railway and municipal sites.

A serious vulnerability has been found in the PHP programming language, and its developers released a fixed version on the night of the 21st, Japan time. According to Asahi Shimbun, Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi, in a post that night on her own X account titled “An Urgent Request,” urged people to check whether the websites they publish use PHP and to apply the patch (the fix program) at once. She said that where that is difficult, temporarily taking the site down is also a way to reduce risk. ITmedia reported that it is unusual for a prime minister to call directly on social media for technical action, and that some engineers on the front line praised it for getting a sense of crisis across to management.

According to NHK, through the 22nd a string of companies and government bodies temporarily suspended publication of their websites, with the effects spreading to bank account-opening services, railway company sites and more. According to Mainichi Shimbun and Jiji Press, the Metropolitan Police Department on the 22nd gathered security officials from 59 critical infrastructure operators in Tokyo for an emergency meeting and asked them to use the fixed version of PHP and, where they cannot update, to take their sites down. Jiji Press reported a total of 81 participants. The meeting was held under the framework of the department’s Cyber Terrorism Countermeasures Council, and participants were also urged to encrypt data, disconnect backups, and coordinate with the police toward active cyber defense. According to Mainichi Shimbun, the number of suspicious accesses recognized this year has exceeded 13,000, a record high.

Rengo to Seek Wage Hikes of “Around 6%, at Least 5%” in 2027 Spring Wage Talks, With a First Medium-Term Target

According to NHK and Asahi Shimbun, on the 22nd Rengo (the Japanese Trade Union Confederation) presented a draft policy for its 2027 spring wage talks demand: at least 5% as the basic line, including scheduled raises, with 6% as the center. According to Mainichi Shimbun, within that, the base pay increase is at least 3%, centered on 4%. The Japan Times reported that it will urge small and midsize unions with fewer than 300 members to demand at least 6%. Rengo will also set its first medium-term target of raising wages by about 15% by 2030. According to The Japan Times, the average wage increase in the 2026 spring talks was 5.01%, below the 5.25% of 2025. The policy is to be formally decided at the central committee in December, and negotiations typically reach their peak in March.

Akita’s Koyoshi River Gets Prefecture’s First Special Flood Warning; Drainage Work the Morning After, One Man Dead

According to Mainichi Shimbun and NHK, overtopping was confirmed on the night of the 21st on the Koyoshi River, which flows through Yurihonjo, Akita Prefecture, and the Akita River and National Highway Office and the Akita Local Meteorological Observatory issued a Level 5 special flood warning. It was the first such issuance in the prefecture. The city issued an emergency safety measures alert. According to Jiji Press, the Japan Meteorological Agency switched it to a flood advisory before dawn on the 22nd. According to NHK, on the morning of the 22nd a man in his 80s was found in cardiopulmonary arrest on a riverbank near a tributary, and his death was later confirmed. Police are looking into whether it is linked to the heavy rain. Asahi Shimbun reported that at the site the morning after, drainage work on flooded farmland is under way, and residents who went through the flood two years ago are saying, “I’m worried about the next rain.”

Giants Pick 14-Year-Old Junior High Third-Year in Developmental Round 5, Youngest Draft Pick Ever

According to NHK, at the professional baseball draft on the 22nd, the Giants selected catcher Rentaro Seki (14), a third-year student at Mukaigaoka Junior High School in Kawasaki, as their fifth-round developmental pick. According to Nippon Professional Baseball (NPB), he is the youngest player ever picked in the draft. According to Asahi Shimbun, the club explained, “We rate his potential highly.” Seki is a catcher who captained the U-15 Japan national team, and a club official indicated the plan to develop him while he attends high school. He says he wants to decide his future after consulting his family. At this draft, a total of 51 developmental players were picked across the 12 teams.

Capcom’s “CAPCOM ID” Leak Hits 11.77 Million Records; Comic CMOA Also Up to 4.2 Million

According to ITmedia and Yahoo! News, Capcom announced on the 22nd that its member service “CAPCOM ID” was hit by unauthorized access and that 11,774,247 email addresses and other data had leaked worldwide. Account creation dates and times and last login times were also included, and for 43 records dates of birth and gender also leaked. Unauthorized changes to login passwords were confirmed in 196 cases. Credit card information is not included. The company says it found traces on the 19th and blocked the access, and reported to the police and the Personal Information Protection Commission.

According to ITmedia, NTT Solmare, which runs the e-book store “Comic CMOA,” announced on the 21st that the leak could reach up to about 4.2 million records. Most were email addresses and dates of birth in system log files; registration data in the member database came to 319 records. Identification and blocking of the intrusion route is complete, and preparations are under way to resume the service, which has been suspended since the 15th.
