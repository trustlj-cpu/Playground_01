Nvidia Advanced Chips Reported Found at Shanghai Facility, Suspected Leak to China of Items Under Embargo

Reports came one after another from the 27th to the 28th that high-performance semiconductors the United States bars from supply to China are installed at data centers of two companies in Shanghai tied to Alice. The number of units and how they were obtained have not been made clear

Reports came one after another from the 27th to the 28th that advanced semiconductors made by Nvidia had been found at a facility in Shanghai, China. What was reported is that high-performance semiconductors of the Blackwell generation are installed at data centers of two companies tied to Alice. Chips of this kind are banned from supply to China under U.S. export controls. It is said to be the first time that an item subject to the embargo has been reported confirmed at an installation site inside China. The equipment is said to be a large unit about 2 meters tall and weighing about 1.5 tons, and some reports say the two companies are firms involved in business in the Yangtze River Delta. If banned items were in fact brought in, it could amount to smuggling that slipped past the controls. However, the number of units, how they were obtained and the identity of the two companies are not settled at the stage of the reports.

At Nvidia, Huang announced a resignation on November 12 and returned as CEO 11 days later, on the 23rd. The reports came just after that return. In the United States, lawmakers are moving to call for an investigation of Nvidia and questions for Huang, and Huang is reported likely to appear at a U.S. Senate hearing. The date of the hearing and what explanations will come out in Congress are not yet known. How Nvidia and the U.S. government respond to these reports will be the next focus.

OpenAI Countersues Apple Over Device Development, Claiming Apple "Pressured Parts Suppliers" and "Stole Trade Secrets"

OpenAI of the United States has filed a countersuit against Apple in the lawsuit that Apple brought over the development of OpenAI's consumer device. OpenAI claims that it was Apple that stole trade secrets. It also alleged that Apple pressured parts suppliers and tried to make them avoid OpenAI's device. OpenAI plans to put its own device on sale, and if that is realized it would compete with Apple, which has the iPhone. The lawsuit casts a shadow over that plan. Whether the countersuit's claims are accepted awaits the court's decision. How Apple will respond to the countersuit has not yet been reported.

Voting Under Way in Taiwan's Unified Local Elections, Fate of Shuangbei in Focus, Seen as Prelude to 2028 Presidential Election

Voting is being held on the 28th in Taiwan's unified local elections. Seen as a prelude to the presidential election of 2028, the overall picture is expected to become clear on the night of the 28th. One focus is reported to be the fate of Shuangbei, Taipei City and New Taipei City combined. Whether the Kuomintang, the largest opposition party, which calls for improved relations with China, keeps its momentum, or the Democratic Progressive Party, the ruling party of the Lai Ching-te administration, claws back ground greatly changes the meaning of the result.

The result is drawing attention for its bearing on how the Lai Ching-te administration runs things from here and on China's Taiwan policy. There were also people returning to their hometowns to vote. As the count proceeds into the night, the win or loss in Shuangbei and the gap in each party's vote totals will be the points to watch. The vote-count results are not yet out.

U.S. Military Attacks Cargo Ship Trying to Break Naval Blockade of Iran, Ship Reported Disabled

It was reported from the 27th to the 28th that the U.S. military attacked a cargo ship that left Iran and tried to break through the "naval blockade." The attack was carried out by U.S. Central Command, and the ship is said to have been disabled. The ship is reported to be the "Pacific Archer," and a U.S. military aircraft is said to have fired a precision-guided munition into its stern. It is also reported that this is not the first time the U.S. military has neutralized a ship trying to break the blockade.

The U.S. stopped the ship by force, citing a violation of the blockade it has imposed on Iran. The Iranian side is reported to have signaled that it would stand up to the blockade. The ship's owner, its cargo, damage to the crew and the exact time of the attack were not clear as of the reports. How the U.S. government explains the attack and how the Iranian side moves will be the next focus.
