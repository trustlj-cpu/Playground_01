Quad Leaders Meet for the First Time in Two Years; Joint Document Reportedly Touches on Concern Over Economic Coercion

The four countries of Japan, the United States, Australia and India held a leaders' meeting for the first time in two years, and the joint document is reported to have touched on concern over economic coercion, with China in mind.

U.S. President Trump arrived in Manila, the capital of the Philippines, on the night of the 17th, Japan time, and began a series of diplomatic engagements in Asia. Next on the schedule is a leaders' meeting of the Quad, a framework of four countries: Japan, the United States, Australia and India. Prime Minister Takaichi was also to attend, with a plan to express “serious concern” with China in mind. The Quad leaders' meeting is being held for the first time in two years. After the meeting, the joint document was reported to have touched on concern over economic coercion, but the full text of the document has not yet been confirmed.

Economic coercion means using economic means, such as restrictions on trade or investment, as political pressure on another country. While the joint document was reported to have expressed concern on this point, it has not been confirmed whether the document named a specific country or in what words each leader spoke. Trump has only just arrived in Manila, and details of the counterparts and agenda for the rest of the Asian diplomacy are also not yet known. As for why two years passed between meetings, no specific explanation has come to light.

Two Bears in a Residential Area of Maebashi; One Man Injured, One Cub Captured With Tranquilizer Gun

On the 17th, two bears, apparently a parent and cub, were sighted one after another in a residential area of Maebashi, Gunma Prefecture, and a man living nearby was attacked and injured on the head and elsewhere. One of them, a cub, fled along a river in the city and was put to sleep with a tranquilizer gun and captured at around 2 p.m. A tranquilizer gun is a tool that puts an animal to sleep and immobilizes it with a dart loaded with a drug. The other bear's whereabouts are unknown, and no word of its capture has come. The extent of the man's injuries and how the bears came into the residential area have also not been explained in detail.

Former Pro Baseball Pitcher Yutaka Enatsu Dies at 78; Known for “Enatsu's 21 Pitches”

Yutaka Enatsu, who pitched with distinction for the Hanshin and Hiroshima teams of professional baseball, among others, has died, as of the 17th. Enatsu was 78. Called the “legendary left-hander,” Enatsu has been remembered for pitching in a single game that came to be talked about as “Enatsu's 21 Pitches.” Baseball fans have voiced their regret at the news of the death of the left-hander who left a name in the baseball world.

Enatsu is described as a pitcher who filled the role of the “closer,” the pitcher who comes in late in a game to protect a win, at a time when the role had not yet taken hold. However, the detailed figures for career statistics and awards have not been confirmed in this coverage. Neither the cause of death nor the funeral schedule has been reported.

Japan National Soccer Team Loses 2-1 to Paraguay in Moriyasu's Last Match as Manager

The Japan national soccer team lost 1-2 to Paraguay in a warm-up match played in Singapore, failing to mark the last match under manager Hajime Moriyasu with a win. Shio Fukuda scored a first national-team goal, but it did not carry the match. A warm-up match is a friendly game arranged not as an official tournament fixture but for a team's preparation and testing.

The match is Moriyasu's final game as national team manager. No announcement about the successor setup or the upcoming schedule has been confirmed in this coverage.

Tokyo Stocks on the 17th: AI and Semiconductor Shares Sold; Nikkei Average Reportedly Down 801 Yen

On the Tokyo stock market on the 17th, sell orders spread, centered on shares related to AI and semiconductors, and share prices fell. The Nikkei Stock Average is reported to have closed at 58,860.43 yen, down 801.59 yen from the previous day. Because this figure could be confirmed in only one report, it should not be treated as final and needs to be checked once the exchange publishes its figures.

Another report says the Nikkei average has fallen about 20% from its high and is nearing the edge of a bear market. A bear market is a term for a phase in which share prices keep falling sharply from a recent high, and a 20% decline is taken as one yardstick. The cooling of the AI boom was reported as a background factor, but the specific cause remains the reports' view and is not a confirmed fact.
