Incumbents Win as Mayor of Kumamoto, Governor of Ehime and Mayor of Shinjuku Ward; Newcomer Nakano Elected Matsuyama Mayor for the First Time

In races for local heads held on the 15th, incumbents won one after another. Kumamoto's mayor won a 4th term and Ehime's governor a 5th, while a newcomer was elected Matsuyama's mayor for the first time.

In the local executive elections held on the 15th, incumbents won one after another. In the Kumamoto mayoral race, Kazufumi Onishi won a 4th term, taking a contest among five candidates, incumbent and newcomers. In the Ehime governor's race, Tokihiro Nakamura, an incumbent with no party affiliation, was decided the winner of a 5th term, coming through a field of three, incumbent and newcomers. In Tokyo's Shinjuku Ward mayoral race too, incumbent Kenichi Yoshizumi beat three newcomers and was decided the winner of a 4th term. A person serving as head four, five terms in a row is described as serving multiple terms.

In Ehime's Matsuyama mayoral race, meanwhile, newcomer Taisei Nakano secured a first victory. The three who ran were all newcomers, and no incumbent's name was on the ballot. In the Shinjuku Ward race, a candidate with the endorsement of the Liberal Democratic Party and Komeito reportedly waged an organization-based campaign. In the Amagasaki mayoral race in Hyogo, incumbent Makoto Matsumoto was reported to have won re-election, beating newcomer Katsuya Fukunaga. Confirmed vote counts and turnout cannot be verified until the election commissions' announcements are complete.

Iran's Revolutionary Guard Says It Attacked U.S. Base in Bahrain; Four Countries Including Saudi Arabia Condemn It

Iran's Revolutionary Guard announced on the 15th that it had attacked a U.S. military base in Bahrain, an island country in the Middle East. The Guard claims it was retaliation for U.S. “hostile acts.” Bahraini authorities issued a threat alert, the first since September. Four countries, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Jordan and Egypt, issued a statement condemning the attack through Bahrain's state news agency. The attack reportedly involved unmanned aircraft, but the means and the scale of damage have not been confirmed. The U.S. and Iran remain at odds over the Strait of Hormuz, and the situation is at a stalemate. Earlier, U.S. President Trump indicated in a social media post that no agreement with Iran is needed, and reportedly also wrote that the stage of aiming for a deal had “long since passed.” Vice President Vance was also reportedly instructed to be taken off the Iran negotiations and put in charge of domestic affairs. The attack was reportedly the second in three days, but this cannot be confirmed elsewhere. The U.S. reaction and whether there were casualties have not been confirmed.

ASEAN Summit Opens in Manila; Debate on Myanmar's Full Return Reportedly Put Off

The summit of ASEAN (the Association of Southeast Asian Nations) began on the 15th in Manila, in the chair country, the Philippines. The focus was the dispute between China and the Philippines and others over territorial claims in the South China Sea, and the situation in Myanmar. On Myanmar, member countries were divided, and discussion toward a full return was put off, it was reported based on interviews with people involved. The measure barring the military government's leader from leader-level meetings has reportedly lasted five years, and the meeting was also reported to have reaffirmed its stance of continuing dialogue with Myanmar and its policy of advancing the peace plan.

Work by ASEAN and China on a code of conduct for the South China Sea again reportedly failed to be wrapped up. A code of conduct is a document that sets rules of behavior that the parties concerned follow in order to avoid clashes, and negotiations have gone on for a long time. Following Timor-Leste's accession, a rice ear was reportedly added to ASEAN's flag, and Timor-Leste is also reported to serve as chair in 2029. The content of the summit's outcome document has not yet been confirmed.

Samurai Japan Wins Third Straight Asia Pro Baseball Championship; Rui Munakata Named Most Valuable Player

The final of the Asia Pro Baseball Championship was held on the 15th, and Samurai Japan beat South Korea to win a third straight title. The team reportedly won the tournament without a loss. Restoration of Shuri Castle's Main Hall Reportedly Complete; Completion Ceremony on the 22nd, Interior Scheduled to Open From the 23rd

At Shuri Castle in Naha, Okinawa, restoration work on the main hall has reportedly been completed, and the interior has become a space of deep vermilion. The main hall was the building at the center of the Ryukyu Kingdom's politics and ceremony; it burned in a fire in October 2019, and rebuilding has been under way. Traditional techniques such as lacquering and coloring are reportedly used in the restoration.

In June, the government had announced that it would hold the restoration's completion ceremony on the 22nd at Shuri Castle Park. The general opening of the main hall's interior is scheduled to begin on the 23rd (Monday, a holiday). How the ceremony goes and the details of how the opening is run will need to be checked from here on.
