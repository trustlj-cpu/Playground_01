Nasdaq Composite Reported to Fall More Than 5% on the 20th, Losing About $4 Trillion in Market Capitalization This Week; Drop Widens Further From the Morning Edition's “More Than 3%”

From the drop during trading, the decline on the 20th is reported to have widened further. No person or institution explaining the cause of the decline has been identified.

On the New York stock market on the 20th, the Nasdaq Composite, which has a high share of tech stocks, is reported to have fallen more than 5%. It is a further widening from the “more than 3%” drop during trading that the morning edition reported. The 30-stock Dow Jones Industrial Average is also reported to have fallen sharply. The Nasdaq Composite includes many volatile companies in AI, semiconductors and the like, so it tends to move more than the market as a whole. Someone holding 10,000 yen would have seen 500 yen or more vanish in a single day. U.S. media report that the Nasdaq's market capitalization this week has fallen by a combined $4 trillion or so. Market capitalization is a company's price: the share price multiplied by the number of shares outstanding. A simple calculation at 1 dollar = 158 yen puts the figure at about 630 trillion yen.

On the Tokyo market on the 20th, the Nikkei average closed at 56,436.65 yen, down 5.06% from the previous day. In the United States on the 20th, the S&P 500's closing price was 6,910.39, down 3.7% from the day before. No person or institution explaining the cause of the decline by name has been identified. Japan's markets are closed on Saturday the 21st and on the 23rd for Labor Thanksgiving Day, and will next open on the 24th (Tue.). In the United States trading resumes on the 23rd (Mon.), and the focus is whether its price moves will affect Tokyo trading on the 24th.

LDP Secretary-General Suzuki Reported to Say Education Minister Seki's Fate Will Be Decided With Top Priority on Passing the Consumption Tax Cut Bill and the Lower House Seat-Reduction Bill

Liberal Democratic Party Secretary-General Suzuki is reported to have indicated on the 21st that the fate of Minister of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology Seki will be decided with top priority on passing a bill related to a consumption tax cut on food and a bill to reduce the number of seats in the House of Representatives. The remark came as the opposition considers submitting a censure motion against Minister Seki. Suzuki is reported to have said that “an extremely sensitive judgment is required” and also to have voiced an intention to remove factors that would hinder passage of the bills. A censure motion is a resolution by which the Diet holds a minister to account and signals its will to demand resignation. Over a political fundraising party, calls for Minister Seki to resign are reported to be coming one after another from the opposition. The minister is reported to have explained that the party was held in compliance with the law and to have said that how to proceed from here will be considered. As for the seat-reduction bill, the ruling parties are aiming to pass it through the House of Representatives within the month, while the opposition is reported to have voiced concern, saying, for example, that minority views are not reflected. Whether the minister will resign or stay on, when a censure motion will be submitted and the vote schedule for the bills have not been confirmed.

514 Households, 948 People Isolated in Kitashiobara, Fukushima; National Route 459 Reported Closed for About 18 Km by Fallen Trees; Yamagata Shinkansen Suspended All Day Between Yamagata and Shinjo

According to Fukushima Prefecture, National Route 459 in Kitashiobara Village has been closed by fallen trees, and 514 households with 948 people are reported to be isolated. On National Route 459, wet snow since the 20th has brought down trees one after another, and the road is said to be closed for roughly 18 km between Inawashiro Town and Kitashiobara Village. Wet snow holds a lot of water and is heavy, so tree branches and trunks easily break or fall when it piles on them.

The snow is also affecting the railways. The Yamagata Shinkansen is reported to have called off all service on the 21st between Yamagata and Shinjo because of heavy snow. How food and fuel will be delivered to the isolated households, when the national road will be restored and when the Yamagata Shinkansen will resume service have not been confirmed.

Mercari Reported to Say a Contractor of Its AI Tool Provider Was Attacked and Customer and Partner Information Entered by Employees May Have Leaked

Mercari, the major flea-market app company, is reported to have disclosed that personal information of customers and business partners entered by employees may have leaked after unauthorized access to a contractor of the company that provides an AI tool used in its work. Unauthorized access is the act of a person without authority getting into a system. A distinctive point is that the target was not Mercari's own system but a contractor of the AI tool provider.

A “list-based attack” was also reported to have been mentioned. A list-based attack is a technique in which ID and password combinations leaked from another service are entered one after another on other sites to try to log in. The number of people who may have been affected, the types of information and whether there were cases of misuse have not been confirmed. The report comes from 1 outlet only.

Tokyo Electric Power Reported to Have Proposed to Negotiating Partner That the State Keep a De Facto Veto Over Key Decisions Even After a Capital Alliance

Tokyo Electric Power Company Holdings, which is considering a capital alliance with another company as part of rebuilding its management, is reported to have proposed to its negotiating partner that the state keep a de facto veto, even after the alliance, over decisions on matters important to management such as the stable supply of electricity. A capital alliance is a tie between companies on the financial side, such as one company holding shares in another. A veto is the right to stop a policy that is about to be decided.

A power outage has a large impact on daily life and industry, so who holds the judgment on supply carries weight as a condition of the alliance. However, the name of the negotiating partner, the size of the stake and the timing of the alliance have not been confirmed. The report comes from 1 outlet only.
