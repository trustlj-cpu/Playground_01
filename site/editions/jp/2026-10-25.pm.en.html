Prosecutors Indict U.S. Serviceman on Robbery-Murder Charge in Killing of Woman at Naha Hotel; Said to Have Returned to Base, Changed Clothes and Gone Out After the Crime

In the case in which a woman’s body was found at a hotel in Naha, the District Prosecutors Office on the 25th indicted a U.S. serviceman on a robbery-murder charge.

Jiji Press reported in a news flash on the 25th that, in the case in which a woman’s body was found at a hotel in Naha, the District Prosecutors Office had indicted a U.S. serviceman on a robbery-murder charge. Mainichi Shimbun reported that the indictment was over suspicion of killing a woman in Naha and taking her backpack. Kyodo News also reported that a U.S. serviceman had been indicted on a robbery-murder charge. Asahi Shimbun reported that the indicted serviceman is believed to have returned to base after the incident, changed clothes and gone out.

In a separate article, Asahi Shimbun reported that a shopkeeper in Koza voiced anger over the serviceman captured in security camera footage, saying, “Nothing’s changed.” Ryukyu Shimpo had reported that about 19 hours after the incident came to light, the prefectural police arrested the serviceman based on his image caught by security cameras. The paper also reported that the serviceman belongs to Futenma Air Station and denied the allegation when arrested. Whether he admits or denies the charge after the indictment, and the trial schedule, have not been confirmed in what has been reported.

Typhoon No. 30: Kumamoto City Issues Evacuation for the Elderly and Others Citywide; Expected to Turn Into Extratropical Cyclone on the 26th, Heavy Rain Warnings for Eastern and Western Japan

NHK reported on the 25th that Kumamoto City issued, at 3 p.m., information on evacuation for the elderly and others covering the entire city, 315,456 households and 740,822 people, and opened evacuation centers at 20 locations in the city. Jiji Press reported that Typhoon No. 30 is expected to turn into an extratropical cyclone west of Kagoshima on the 26th, and that vigilance against heavy rain is needed in eastern and western Japan. Kyoto Shimbun reported that warning-level heavy rain is possible in the Kinki region from the 25th and that there appear to be two peaks of strong rain. NHK says that, because warm, moist air from the typhoon is flowing into a weather front stalled near Honshu, heavy rain is possible over wide areas of eastern and western Japan.

Masatora Kawano Wins Marathon Race Walk in World-Record 2 Hours 55 Minutes 14 Seconds in His First Attempt, at the All-Japan Race Walking Meet in Takahata, Yamagata

NHK reported on the 25th that a meet for the new event “marathon race walk,” contested over 42.195 kilometers, the same distance as the marathon, was held in Takahata, Yamagata Prefecture, and that Masatora Kawano won, setting a world record of 2 hours 55 minutes 14 seconds as a preliminary time. TBS NEWS DIG reported that Kawano set the mark in his first attempt, and that at the same meet Hayato Katsuki cleared the selection standard and was provisionally selected for the World Championships in Athletics team.

Kyodo News reported that Kawano, of Asahi Kasei, set a world record in the marathon race walk at the All-Japan Race Walking Takahata meet, and Niigata Nippo also reported the same in a news flash. The 2 hours 55 minutes 14 seconds is a preliminary figure, and no announcement on official recognition of the record has been confirmed in what has been reported.

LDP Eyes Passage This Session of Bill to Cut 31 Proportional Seats in Lower House; Opposition Shows Understanding and Dissent on NHK’s Sunday Discussion

NHK reported on the 25th that, on the seat reduction in the House of Representatives, Diet affairs committee chairs of ruling and opposition parties holding seats in the House appeared on the Sunday Discussion program. The Liberal Democratic Party (LDP) indicated that if agreement can be reached on a plan that includes cutting 31 seats in proportional representation, it would submit a bill to the current Diet session and aim for passage. Asahi Shimbun also reported that LDP Diet affairs chief Murai showed eagerness for passage in the current session.

According to NHK, some in the opposition voiced understanding of the LDP plan, while dissent was also voiced. NHK had reported on the 24th that leader Tamaki of the Democratic Party for the People said of the new plan, “One basis for the cuts has been shown.” On the 23rd, NHK reported that the LDP had presented the 31-seat cut plan, that there was dissent among the opposition, and that the parties would hold further talks next week.

Going to Sky Wins Kikuka Sho; Favorite Robchen Finishes 14th, Missing Out on Ninth Triple Crown in History

NHK reported on the 25th that in the Kikuka Sho, an autumn Classic race in horse racing, 3rd favorite Going to Sky, ridden by jockey Yutaka Take, won. No. 1 favorite Robchen finished 14th, and the Triple Crown of the Classic races was not achieved. Mainichi Shimbun reported that it fell short of becoming the ninth Triple Crown winner in history.

Nikkan Sports reported that Going to Sky came from behind to win and that Take took an unprecedented 6th Kikuka Sho win. Yomiuri Shimbun reported that Bastelle was second and Danon History third. The reason Robchen lost, finishing 14th, has not been confirmed in what has been reported.
