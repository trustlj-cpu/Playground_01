Bank of Japan Judges for First Time That 2% Price Target Has Been “Reached”; Policy Rate Held at Around 1.25%

It is the first such judgment since the target was introduced in 2013. Annual ETF sales will be increased to 860 billion yen.

At its monetary policy meeting on the 30th, the Bank of Japan held the policy rate at around 1.25%. The policy rate is a benchmark for the interest rate at which banks and others lend money to one another over the short term, and it also feeds through to variable mortgage rates and deposit rates. In the Outlook Report released at the same time, the bank expressed the view that “underlying inflation,” which excludes temporary factors, has reached the 2% target. It is the first time it has judged the target reached since setting the 2% price target in 2013. The 2% level is meant as a guide for a state in which prices keep rising gently by about 2% a year. Underlying inflation is an approach that tries to measure the firm growth in prices by stripping out temporary movements in the weather and exchange rates. The Outlook Report is the document in which the Bank of Japan compiles its outlook for the economy and prices once every three months. The bank also decided to increase its annual sales of the exchange-traded funds (ETFs) it holds to 860 billion yen. ETFs are investment trusts that can be bought and sold on the market like stocks, and the bank had bought them with the aim of supporting the economy.

In the vote, one Policy Board member proposed an additional rate hike to 1.50% and dissented. Keeping the policy unchanged was decided by a majority. On the Tokyo stock market, the Nikkei average closed at 63,290.11 yen, down 2.1% from the previous day. The previous day’s close was 64,659.47 yen. With the bank having declared that 2% has been reached, attention shifts to when the next rate hike will be judged. Whether an additional rate hike will be made at the December meeting has not been indicated. Reaching the price target and prices staying stable at around 2% are separate matters, and the bank will make its judgment while watching future wages and prices.

LDP, Ishin and Democratic Party for the People Formally Agree to Cut 31 Lower House Proportional Seats; Democratic Reform Group Calls It “Heavy-Handed”

On the 29th, the secretaries-general of the LDP, Nippon Ishin no Kai and the Democratic Party for the People formally agreed to cut the number of proportional representation seats in the House of Representatives by 31. The agreement document states that the three parties will jointly submit a bill in the current Diet session and aim for its passage. The ruling parties had initially argued for a cut of 45 proportional seats, but the LDP presented a compromise plan of 31, which Ishin and the Democratic Party for the People accepted. At the same time, the allocation of proportional representation seats will be changed to the “modified Sainte-Laguë method,” which makes it easier for small and medium-sized parties to win seats. The Sainte-Laguë method is a calculation that allocates seats by dividing vote totals by the odd numbers 1, 3, 5 and so on; the modified version makes the first divisor smaller so that seats are more likely to go to small parties as well. On the 30th, Ogawa, leader of the Democratic Reform Group, criticized the agreement to cut only proportional representation as heavy-handed and indicated an intention to work with the opposition parties that oppose it. According to a calculation, applying it to the results of the February House of Representatives election would raise the LDP’s seat share from 68% to 72%. Opposition parties are calling for priority to be given to discussion of the consumption tax, and resistance is expected in deliberations on the bill. When the bill will be submitted and whether it will pass in the current session have not yet been decided.

Customer Data on Up to 27.26 Million Records Feared Leaked From Muji’s Official App; Users Who Closed Accounts Also Affected

Ryohin Keikaku announced on the 30th that unauthorized access to the “MUJI app” may have caused customer information on up to about 27.26 million records to leak outside the company. Users who have already closed their accounts are also included. The items at risk of leaking are three: email address, date of birth and gender. The company explains that credit card information, names, addresses and phone numbers are not included. After detecting the unauthorized access, the company blocked access to the affected part and made a fix. It says no leak to the dark web or elsewhere, and no damage from misuse, has been confirmed.

The company will contact affected users in turn. It says “spoofed emails” using this information may arrive, and is urging people not to open URLs in emails or enter personal information. Spoofed emails are a ruse that disguises itself as official communication to lure people to fake sites. How the intruder got in and when the unauthorized access occurred have not been disclosed; 27.26 million is a “maximum” figure, and the actual number and scope are under investigation. This month, leaks from email delivery companies and others have followed one after another.

Female Student Stabbed With Kitchen Knife by Classmate at Tokyo Metropolitan Roppongi High School; Arrested in the Act on Suspicion of Attempted Murder

At about 1 p.m. on the 30th, at Tokyo Metropolitan Roppongi High School in Minato Ward, Tokyo, a female student was stabbed in the back with a kitchen knife by another female student. The Metropolitan Police Department made an arrest in the act of the student who did the stabbing, on suspicion of attempted murder. An arrest in the act is a procedure for seizing a person during or immediately after a crime without a warrant. The stabbed student is said to be conscious. On the extent of the injuries, reports are divided between those saying serious injury and those saying not life-threatening, and it is not clear. There is also information that the incident occurred near the school library.

The Metropolitan Police Department is investigating the relationship between the student who did the stabbing and the student who was stabbed, and how the blade was brought in. It has been reported that a school cultural festival was scheduled for the same day. The motive for the stabbing has not yet been made clear. The students’ names and ages had not been released as of the evening of the 30th. The responses of the school and the Tokyo Metropolitan Board of Education, and the effect on other students, also await future explanations.

Doshisha Board Chairman Holds First Press Conference Since Henoko Accident, Apologizes: “Could Not Protect Life”

Eiji Hatta, chairman of the board of the Doshisha school corporation, held a press conference on the 30th, the first since the boat capsizing off Henoko, Okinawa Prefecture, and apologized: “I offer my sincere apologies.” Regarding the accident in which a female student at Doshisha International High School died, the chairman said that the school corporation takes seriously its responsibility for having failed to protect the student’s life. The chairman also apologized that the press conference came about seven and a half months after the accident, saying the delay in explanation had caused anxiety and distrust.

Doshisha explained the state of its efforts to prevent recurrence and also disclosed that it has set up a third-party committee to consider educational neutrality. Of the subsidies private schools receive from the national government (private school subsidies), the disbursement for Doshisha has been put on hold, and a final decision will come in January 2027. The chairman said support will continue while listening to the wishes of the bereaved families and the injured students.
