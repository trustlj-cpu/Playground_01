GMO Attacker’s Site Taken Down at the Domain Level; Company Offers 150 Million Yen Reward for Information

The attacker-side site that had been publishing data said to be user information went down on the 28th. The Metropolitan Police Department is investigating on suspicion of violating the Unauthorized Computer Access Law, among other offenses.

On the 28th, GMO Internet Group announced that it had confirmed the suspension of the domain of the attacker-side site on which personal information believed to relate to users had been posted in connection with unauthorized access to the company’s services. The domain is the name part of a site, equivalent to an address on the internet. It was stopped as a result of working with the businesses concerned. On the 27th, the day before, the attacker side had published data said to be user information online, together with a statement demanding money. TBS NEWS DIG reports that the suspension appears to be an action by a U.S. provider. GMO’s president had directly warned that a criminal complaint would be filed unless the data was deleted by the deadline. A criminal complaint is a procedure in which a victim reports a crime to an investigative authority and seeks punishment. The company says that if a similar site is newly opened, it will move immediately, as soon as it confirms it, toward a takedown, that is, contacting the operator to have the site removed.

GMO announced a reward of 150 million yen for information leading to the identification of the culprit and set up a dedicated contact point. The Japan Times puts the reward at up to 1 million dollars, and a site to collect information was also opened. The Metropolitan Police Department began an investigation on suspicion of violating the Unauthorized Computer Access Law, among other offenses. It is a law that prohibits breaking into a computer, for example by using someone else’s ID or password without permission. ITmedia NEWS points out that AI-powered cyberattacks have begun in earnest. The line it takes is that the use of AI in defense and government neutralization of attack bases, that is, making equipment used in attacks stop functioning, are called for. The damage reaches the trading front as well. According to Tominaga of Central Tanshi FX, individual investors’ FX (foreign exchange margin trading) volume has not dropped much, though some are closing accounts. The identification of the attackers and the scope of the leak have not been confirmed.

LDP, Ishin and Democratic Party for the People Back 31-Seat Lower House Cut; Three Parties to Discuss Drafting a Bill

Talks between the ruling and opposition parties on reducing the number of Lower House seats were held on the 28th, and the Japan Innovation Party (Ishin) and the Democratic Party for the People expressed their intention to back moving ahead with consideration of a plan presented by LDP working-level officials. The number of Lower House seats is the total number of members of the House of Representatives, and it is fixed by law. The plan would cut 31 seats in proportional representation. Proportional representation is an election method that allocates seats according to the votes each party wins. The LDP called on the three parties to draft a bill together. With Ishin accepting the 31-seat cut and the Democratic Party for the People also in favor, it has become possible to pass the bill in the current Diet session, Asahi Shimbun reports. Jiji Press flashed that the Democratic Party for the People had decided to back it and that Ishin had decided to accept it. The size of the cut differs from the Democratic Party for the People’s position. It came around nonetheless because a review of the electoral system, which it had also been seeking, was added. If Ishin agrees, a bill related to the seat reduction could pass in the current Diet session, Yomiuri Shimbun reported. Behind the voices for cutting lawmakers lies distrust of politics. Asahi Shimbun, in a separate article, introduced the view of an expert who says people are saying “They don’t do their jobs, so cut them.” The timing of the bill’s submission has not been confirmed.

FRB Holds Policy Rate at 3.75-4.00%, First Hold in Two Meetings; Two Dissent, Arguing for a Hike

The U.S. Federal Reserve Board (FRB) decided at its Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) meeting on the 28th to hold the policy rate at 3.75-4.00%. The FOMC is the FRB meeting that decides the policy rate in the United States. The policy rate is the short-term interest rate a central bank sets to manage the economy and prices. It was the first hold in two meetings; the previous meeting had brought a rate hike. A rate hike is a central bank raising its policy rate to hold down rising prices. This time the decision appears to be a judgment to gauge the effect on inflation (NHK). Inflation is a state in which prices keep rising and the value of money falls. The decision was not unanimous. Two members dissented from the hold and argued for consecutive rate hikes.

Attention also fell on the communication after the meeting. According to Bloomberg TV, Chair Warsh said the FOMC schedule would change from autumn next year. On another program, economist Sahm said the FRB’s communication had become hard to understand. On the same network, economist Gopinath expressed the view that the FRB could forgo a rate hike. The direction of the next meeting has not been confirmed.

JAL and JTA: Signs That Reservation Data of About 92,000 People Was Viewed After Unauthorized Access at Reservation System Provider

Japan Airlines (JAL) and Japan Transocean Air (JTA) announced on the 28th that the provider of the system that manages reservations, check-in and other procedures had suffered unauthorized access, and that there were signs that the reservation information of about 92,000 users had been viewed from outside. Passengers’ names and reservation-related information may have leaked. For about 2,000 of them, telephone numbers and other data are also included. Passport numbers were included in some reservations. The case is at the stage of signs of viewing; whether the data was actually taken outside has not been shown so far. The 92,000 people are on the scale of the population of a large city.

The entity that suffered the unauthorized access was the contractor Amadeus, and the reservation information of JAL and JTA was affected. According to Aviation Wire, the JAL and JTA announcements say no credit card numbers or passwords had leaked. The scope of the information for which signs of viewing were confirmed, and the timing of the unauthorized access, have not been confirmed.

Trains Collide on Spanish High-Speed Railway; Death Toll Rises to 68

On the 28th in central Spain, high-speed trains collided, and 68 people had died as of early on the 29th. It happened in Toledo province, on a line connecting Madrid with a region in the south. A following high-speed train reportedly rammed the rear of the train ahead, which was stopped, and two cars derailed. The authorities are investigating the details. The death toll stands at 68, and at least 179 people are reported injured. With the following train entering where the train ahead was stopped, it became a major accident involving the derailment of two cars.

The death toll rose as reports piled up. Jiji Press and Mainichi Shimbun reported 30, TV Asahi reported at least 50 dead and at least 179 injured, and NHK later put it at 68. A spokesperson for the local government told Spain’s public broadcaster TVE that the death toll will “almost certainly rise.” There had also been a derailment in Spain in January. The cause of the accident is under investigation.
