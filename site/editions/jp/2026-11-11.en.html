SoftBank Group Posts Final Loss of 768 Billion Yen for April–September, Hit by Changes to US Data Center Plan

The company says the loss stems mainly from changes to a data center plan it is pursuing in the United States. At the earnings briefing, CFO Yoshimitsu Goto rejected the “default” claim in Musk's X post as “entirely without basis.”

SoftBank Group announced its interim results for April to September 2026 on the 10th. Its final profit and loss was a deficit of 768 billion yen. Interim results are the results covering the first six months of a fiscal year. April through September spans 183 days, so spread evenly over the days, the loss works out to about 4.2 billion yen per day. The company says the main factor was a loss booked in connection with changes to a data center plan it is pursuing in the United States. A data center is a dedicated facility that houses large numbers of computers used for AI processing.

At the earnings briefing on the 10th, CFO Yoshimitsu Goto denied the claim of a “default” that SpaceX CEO Elon Musk posted on X, calling it “entirely without basis.” A default means being unable to repay borrowed money as promised. The wording of Musk's post could not be confirmed. Goto also called the possibility that the situation surrounding the US midterm elections could lead to delays in the business “one of the scenarios.” How the plan was changed, what the loss consists of and how quarterly profit and loss moved have not yet been made clear.

Japan Chamber of Commerce and Industry Discloses Information on Up to About 980,000 Takers of Bookkeeping and Other Exams May Have Leaked

The Japan Chamber of Commerce and Industry announced on the 10th that there had been unauthorized access to a contractor's server and that personal information on people who took bookkeeping and other exams over the internet may have leaked, for up to roughly 980,000 people. It covers people who took exams in April 2022 or later, and names, pass/fail results, scores and the like are said to possibly be included. Unauthorized access means an unauthorized third party getting into a system without permission. Notifying 980,000 people at 10,000 a day would take 98 days. For now this is a stage of “may have leaked”; whether the information actually went outside, and whether any case of misuse exists, have not been confirmed. The time of the unauthorized access, the route of the intrusion, the range of exams covered and how takers will be contacted also cannot be confirmed from this announcement. Bookkeeping is the skill of recording a company's money coming in and going out in account books. This month, possible leaks of personal information have also been announced at Sagawa Express, Nitori and Sony Life, and this time information tied to exam results is affected.

US October Consumer Prices Up 0.4% From Previous Month; FRB Governor Waller Declares Support for December Rate Hike

US consumer prices for October rose 0.4% from the previous month. Consumer prices are an indicator of how the prices of goods and services households buy are moving, and the basic figure for measuring the speed of inflation (the rise in prices). The increase was reported to have exceeded market expectations. The US Bureau of Labor Statistics had said it would not publish the rate of increase compared with the same month a year earlier for October, but no explanation of the reason could be confirmed. If a 0.4% rise continued for a year, prices would go up by about 5%.

Governor Waller of the US Federal Reserve Board (FRB) said on the 10th that support would be given to a rate hike of at least 0.25 percentage point at the December meeting. Waller also said a 0.5 percentage point hike would be supported if inflation keeps accelerating. A rate hike is a means of raising the policy rate to make borrowing harder and hold down rising prices. The remarks came before the price statistics were released. But the policy rate is decided at meetings, not by Waller alone, and whether there will actually be a hike in December, and by how much, has not been decided.

Yusaku Maezawa's Fake Ad Lawsuit: Tokyo District Court Dismisses Claim, Does Not Find Meta Responsible

Entrepreneur Yusaku Maezawa sued the US IT giant Meta for damages and other relief, claiming that fake ads using Maezawa's name and image without permission were placed on social media and infringed portrait rights. On the 10th, the Tokyo District Court dismissed the claim. Portrait rights are the right not to have one's face or appearance carelessly photographed or used. The court found that the ads were posted not by Meta itself but that the company merely provided a place for posting, and judged that it could not be said to have left the fake ads unattended.

After the ruling, Maezawa commented on X, “I will consider an appeal,” and also posted a comment asking, “So does that mean leaving it unchecked is fine?” An appeal is a procedure in which the side dissatisfied with a ruling asks a higher court to review the decision. Because the claim was dismissed, no ruling ordering Meta to pay damages has been issued. The detailed reasoning of the ruling, the amount claimed and the effect on similar lawsuits have not been confirmed this time.

Death of 3-Year-Old in Iwaki, Fukushima: Mother Had Consulted Police About Partner's Violence Around Summer

In the case in which a 3-year-old boy in Iwaki, Fukushima Prefecture, was assaulted by his mother's partner and later died, it has been learned from interviews with the police that around the summer of this year the mother consulted police, saying that her son and she herself were being subjected to violence by the partner. It is also reported that information about the violence of the person who later became a suspect had reached each of the police, the administration and the community.

How the police responded to the consultation, and how information from the administration and the community was handled, have not been confirmed this time. There are also reports that the charge will be switched to bodily injury resulting in death and the case referred to prosecutors, but a police announcement has not been confirmed. Bodily injury resulting in death is a crime charged when a person is injured and, as a result, dies.
