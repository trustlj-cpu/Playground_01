Nasdaq Briefly Falls Over 3%, Anthropic Shares Reported Down 14% in U.S. Stock Market on the 20th

Selling is reported to have spread across a broad range of stocks, semiconductors included. All are intraday values; the closing price for the 20th and the cause of the fall have not been confirmed.

On the New York stock market on the 20th, the Nasdaq Composite, which has a high share of tech stocks, briefly fell more than 900 points from the previous day, and the decline briefly exceeded 3%, it is reported. Selling spread across a broad range of stocks, starting with semiconductors. Shares of the U.S. AI developer Anthropic, listed on the Nasdaq on the 19th, are reported to have fallen as much as 14% from the previous day on the 20th. On the first day, the 19th, they are reported to have ended 17% above the offer price, which means, by the arithmetic, that they gave back nearly all of the first day's gain and fell to near the offer price. The Nasdaq is also reported to have lost its gains since the start of the year, but all of these are intraday values, and the closing price for the 20th has not been confirmed.

On the Tokyo market on the 20th, the Nikkei average ended down 5.06% from the previous day at 56,436.65 yen. Asian stock markets are reported to be heading for their worst weekly fall since 2020. As for the cause of the fall, no explanation that says whose assessment it is has been found. The 21st is a Saturday, when Japanese markets are closed, and the 23rd is also a market holiday for Labor Thanksgiving Day, so Tokyo's next session will be on the 24th (Tue). In the meantime, the closing prices of the U.S. market for the 20th will be settled, and whether those values affect Japanese trading at the start of the week is the focus.

Record 93% Say They Do Not Think Japan-China Relations Are Good, Cabinet Office Opinion Poll Finds

In an opinion poll on diplomacy conducted by the Cabinet Office, 7% of respondents said they think relations between Japan and China are good, and 93% said they do not think so. It is the highest share for “do not think they are good” since the survey began under the current method. The 93% is reported to combine “bad” and “somewhat bad.” For relations between Japan and Russia too, the result is reported to be the worst level on record for current relations, but the share has not been confirmed. An opinion poll can be compared accurately with the past only once the survey period and the number of respondents are known. For this poll, the survey period and number of respondents are not shown, as far as could be confirmed.

Government Reportedly Agrees to Triple, From the Previous Level, the Stockpiling Capacity of Domestic Crude Oil Tanks Lent to Saudi Arabia

The government is reported to have agreed to triple, from the previous level, the stockpiling capacity, the amount of crude oil that Saudi Arabia can keep in tanks in Japan that are lent to it. The agreement comes as securing crude oil remains a challenge given the situation in the Middle East. It is said to let Japan buy the oil first in an emergency.

Under this arrangement, an oil-producing country places its own crude oil in tanks in Japan, uses it as export inventory in normal times, and lets Japan buy first when supply is at risk of being disrupted. However, the amount of oil that will actually go into the tanks, when the agreement will start operating and who will bear the cost have not been confirmed. The reporting is limited to 1 outlet.

Komeito Demands Amendments Including Explicit “Transitional Relief Measures” in Consumption Tax Cut Bill; Regional Public Hearing Hears Concern About Price Rises

Over the related bill on a consumption tax cut, Komeito on the 20th demanded amendments to the bill, including a requirement that “transitional relief measures” be written in. Transitional relief measures are steps that soften the sudden burden or price changes when a tax rate changes. The same day, a regional public hearing on the consumption tax cut was held, and concern that “prices will rise by more than the tax cut” is reported to have been voiced.

A public hearing is an occasion at which a Diet committee hears opinions from experts and residents before deliberating a bill. The worry about whether the tax cut will be reflected in prices has been raised from the ground level. How far Komeito's demand for amendments will be accepted, what the amendments would contain if made, and the vote schedule for the bill have not been confirmed.

Fukuoka Prefectural Assembly Member Resigns From Article 100 Committee Probing Money Allegations, Reported to Be Former Speaker's Ex-Secretary

A prefectural assembly member who had been a secretary to former Speaker Kurauchi resigned on the 20th from the Article 100 committee of the Fukuoka Prefectural Assembly that is investigating the allegations over the giving and receiving of money involving the former speaker. In resigning, the member is reported to have said that the situation in which the investigation has stalled is “not what was intended.”

An Article 100 committee is an investigative body of an assembly that, under the Local Autonomy Act, can summon witnesses to testify and demand the submission of documents, and perjury is punishable. The specific content of the allegations, the former speaker's side's explanation, the next schedule of the committee and whether a successor to the resigning member will be chosen have not been confirmed.
