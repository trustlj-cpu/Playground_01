GMO’s Kumagai Apologizes at First Press Conference, Admits Failure to Act on April 2024 Internal Warning; Pay Cut of Up to 30% for Four Executives

The press conference previewed in the morning edition was held on the afternoon of the 2nd. The president admitted, “There was a security problem.”

GMO Internet Group Chairman and President Masatoshi Kumagai held a press conference in Tokyo on the afternoon of the 2nd and apologized over the unauthorized access to “Onamae.com Desktop Cloud,” a VPS (virtual private server) for automated FX trading. “I sincerely apologize,” Kumagai said, admitting that there had been a security problem. It is the first time the company’s top executive has explained the matter in public since the attack came to light.

At the conference Kumagai explained that points for improving security had been raised from within the group in April 2024, but that they were not put to use in operating the service. That means the company knew of a vulnerability, a weakness in a system, yet could not prevent the attack. The company announced that pay for four executives, including Kumagai, will be cut by up to 30% for three months. Kumagai also said that consideration was lacking in Kumagai’s own social media posts. Who carried out the attack and the full picture of compensation for users have not been confirmed. The leak of 45,030 people’s data reported in the morning edition was a separate case, which occurred in a different service.

Personal Data on About 100,000 People Leaked at Suntory Holdings; Unauthorized Access Exposes Information on About 90,000 Employees and Job Applicants

Suntory Holdings announced on the 2nd that a Microsoft cloud service it uses for business had suffered unauthorized access, and that personal information on roughly 100,000 people had leaked. The company says it detected unauthorized access by a third party on Oct. 28. What leaked is information on about 90,000 employees of group companies and others, in addition to the names of about 7,200 people who applied for 2026 new-graduate hiring and the email addresses and other details of about 7,300 people registered for a company experience program. Names, email addresses and phone numbers are included, but payment information such as credit card numbers is not, the company says. At present the company says it has confirmed no case of the leaked information being misused. A cloud service is a system in which data is kept in a provider’s facilities rather than the company’s own equipment, and if the ID and password are broken, everything stored there can be reached at once. A detailed explanation of the intrusion route and of how far the information was taken outside has not been given.

First Hearing in Firebomb Attack on LDP Headquarters; Defendant Does Not State a Plea, Defense Seeks Dismissal of the Indictment and Contests Everything

The first hearing was held on the 2nd at the Tokyo District Court for the defendant charged with offenses including attempted murder, accused of throwing a firebomb at a riot police officer in front of Liberal Democratic Party headquarters in Nagatacho, Tokyo, the year before last, and of ramming a car into a fence in front of the Prime Minister’s Official Residence. The defendant did not state a plea on the charges and tore up a paper with the Hinomaru flag drawn on it in the courtroom.

The defense said it will contest the charges in full and sought dismissal of the indictment. Dismissal of the indictment is a ruling that ends a trial without examining the merits, on the ground that the prosecution’s indictment itself has a procedural problem. A plea means the answer on whether the charged facts are admitted or denied. The case shocked society because the center of the state was targeted in succession, a party headquarters and the Prime Minister’s Official Residence. The trial will rule on whether the procedure is proper as well as on the facts.

Death of One Japanese Confirmed in Spain Train Crash; Efforts Continue to Identify Another Japanese National

In the rear-end collision of high-speed trains in central Spain on Oct. 28, local authorities announced on the 1st that the death of one Japanese person had been confirmed. A high-speed train struck another train from behind, and more than 60 people have died.

Three of the dead are still unidentified. Spanish authorities are also working to identify the other Japanese national. The cause of the accident has not been determined.

Posting Applications Accepted; Teruaki Sato, Kaima Taira and Hiromi Ito to Negotiate With MLB Clubs

Major League Baseball accepted posting-system applications for three players in Japanese pro baseball, Hanshin’s Teruaki Sato, Seibu’s pitcher Kaima Taira and Nippon-Ham’s pitcher Hiromi Ito, and notified all 30 clubs. Negotiations open from the 2nd, local time, and MLB clubs can talk contract terms with the three. As reported in the morning edition, Nippon-Ham had consented to Ito’s move on the 1st.

Posting is a system in which, after a Japanese club approves a player’s move, the player can negotiate with MLB clubs. There is a deadline for negotiations, and some conclude early. Which club each of the three will sign with, and for how much, has not yet been made clear.
