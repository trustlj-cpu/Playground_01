Gold Bullion of About 20 Kilograms and About 25 Million Yen in Cash Stolen From Home in Nishinomiya, Hyogo; Police Investigate as Theft

Unknown persons are reported to have broken into the home in the early hours of the 21st. The culprit and the method of entry have not been confirmed.

At a home in Nishinomiya, Hyogo Prefecture, about 20 kilograms of gold bullion, about 25 million yen in cash and other items have been confirmed stolen, and police are investigating the case as theft. The victim is a person involved in running a company, and unknown persons are reported to have broken into the home in the early hours of the 21st. The bullion is also reported to be worth 400 million yen at current gold prices, but this figure rests on a single outlet's report and has not been confirmed. The resident's title is split between “business owner” and “company officer” depending on the report, and neither the company name nor the resident's name has been confirmed. As the “and other items” wording suggests, things besides cash and bullion are said to have been stolen as well, but what they are has not been made clear.

Theft refers to the crime of taking another person's property against that person's will. In this case, the information that forms the basis of an investigation has not been confirmed: where the intruder got in, around what time the items were taken, whether anyone was home, and whether security cameras kept any footage. Some reports give the weight of the bullion as “about 20 pieces,” and because the expression differs between kilograms and a count, “20 kilograms” by weight is used here. From here, the next focus is whether police announce the route of entry and the full list of items stolen, and whether information about a suspect emerges.

Mao Shimada Wins Grand Prix Series Round 5 in Finland, Two Wins From Two Starts Secure a Place in the Grand Prix Final

At Round 5 of the figure skating Grand Prix series, the Finland event, the women's singles free skating was held on the 21st, and 18-year-old Mao Shimada won. Shimada had also led after the short program in the first half, and carried that lead through to the title. In a senior debut season, this made two wins from two starts after the Canada event, and a place in the Grand Prix Final, contested by the top six in the series, is secured. Winning twice in a row in a Grand Prix debut season is reported to be the first since Rika Kihira. It is also reported that Japanese women have won five events in a row from the start of this season's Grand Prix series, but this figure rests on a single outlet's report. The dates and venue of the Final, and the next event Shimada will enter, have not been confirmed.

COP31 Gets Session Extension as Split Over Transition Away From Fossil Fuels Keeps Talks Going

COP31 (the 31st Conference of the Parties to the UN Framework Convention on Climate Change), the United Nations conference on climate action, failed to reach agreement on the 20th, its scheduled final day, and talks are continuing under a session extension. Countries are reported to be divided over matters including the absence of any mention of a transition away from fossil fuels in the draft outcome document, and negotiations involving nearly 200 countries are reported to have reached an impasse.

A transition away from fossil fuels means reducing the use of oil, coal and natural gas. COP decisions are in principle made by unanimity, and if even one country strongly objects, a document is hard to agree on. How long the session will be extended, and whether fossil fuel wording will go into the outcome document, have not been confirmed.

Denmark to Expel Russian Defense Attaché, Alleges Involvement in Plot to Assassinate Rapper; Russia Denies

Denmark's foreign minister is reported to have announced on the 20th the expulsion of the Russian defense attaché in the capital, Copenhagen. The reason is that the Danish side suspects Moscow was behind a plot to kill a rapper of Ukrainian origin before a performance in Denmark, it is reported. The plot is said to have been foiled before it was carried out. Meanwhile, the Russian embassy is reported to have denied the claim.

A defense attaché is a military officer serving as a diplomat, attached to an embassy and responsible for gathering and relaying information on the military affairs of the host country. The substance of the evidence the Danish side has presented, the people involved in the plot and whether Russia will take countermeasures have not been confirmed.

Coalition Backing Ukraine Opens First Large-Scale Military Exercise to the Press, Reportedly Confirming Coordination Among National Forces

The first large-scale military exercise by the coalition of the willing supporting Ukraine was opened to the press on the 20th, it is reported. Forces from the participating countries confirmed their coordination, assuming operations on the ground toward security guarantees for Ukraine. The coalition of the willing refers to a framework in which countries that agree come together and act jointly, rather than an alliance based on a treaty.

Security guarantees means a mechanism to protect Ukraine so that Russia does not attack again after fighting stops. Where the exercise was held, the number of participating countries and the scale of forces, and when and under what conditions troops would actually be sent to Ukraine, have not been confirmed.
