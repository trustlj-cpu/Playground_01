July 30 Currency Intervention Totals 7.9756 Trillion Yen, Largest Ever Yen-Buying Intervention in a Single Day

According to intervention figures released by the Ministry of Finance on the 9th, the yen-buying funds spent on July 30 came to 7.9756 trillion yen, the largest amount ever for a single day.

Of the currency interventions the government and the Bank of Japan carried out intermittently from July to August, the funds spent on July 30 came to 7.9756 trillion yen, the Ministry of Finance announced. As a yen-buying intervention, in which dollars are sold and yen bought, it is the largest ever for a single day. An amount of about 8 trillion yen is unprecedented for one day of intervention. Currency intervention is when a government buys and sells a currency in the market to curb sharp swings in its value, and a yen-buying intervention props up the yen and works to push its value higher.

The interventions were carried out intermittently from July to August, and July 30 was one of those days. This announcement made the amounts clear day by day. The total amount of the interventions from July to August, the full breakdown for each day and over how many days the interventions ran could not be confirmed this time. No explanation has been given by the government or the Bank of Japan on whether the interventions will continue.

Nvidia Board Opens Independent Investigation Into CEO Huang; CEO Admits Extramarital Affair and Apologizes in Message to Employees

Chipmaker Nvidia announced that it will begin an independent investigation led by its board of directors into matters concerning CEO Jensen Huang. In a message addressed to employees, CEO Huang admitted to an extramarital relationship and apologized. An independent investigation is a mechanism for examining the facts from a position separate from management, so as to avoid influence from the party being examined. Republican members of the U.S. Congress and others are demanding an explanation over the relationship between CEO Huang and a person said to be connected to a Chinese intelligence agency. Neither the company nor the parties involved have offered evidence on whether that person cooperated with an intelligence agency, and this cannot be confirmed. No leak of confidential information to outside parties has been confirmed either. The scope and duration of the investigation and when the results will be made public have not been disclosed.

Trump Eager to Meet Chinese and Russian Leaders at APEC in China; Denuclearization and Iran Among Topics

U.S. President Trump indicated that he intends to attend the leaders’ meeting of Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC), to be held in China in mid-November, and meet the leaders of China and Russia. The meeting is scheduled for November 18 to 19, and Trump named denuclearization and Iran, among other things, as topics for the talks. Trump said he expects to meet President Xi Jinping of China and President Putin of Russia. Denuclearization refers to efforts to eliminate nuclear weapons or to keep countries from having them.

However, it has not been confirmed whether the meetings will actually take place, or their schedule and format. It has also not been shown which country or region the denuclearization refers to.

Nikkei Average Closes at 58,775.50 Yen, Rebounding 2.33% After Friday’s 5.96% Drop

On the Tokyo stock market on the 9th, the Nikkei Stock Average ended trading at 58,775.50 yen, up 2.33% from the previous day. The close on the 6th, the previous Friday, was 57,434.77 yen, after a 5.96% fall in a single day. The gain on the 9th was 1,340.73 yen, recovering part of Friday’s decline. The closing price means the last price set in a day’s trading.

During trading, from the market open it started 525 yen higher, and at one point it was up by more than 2,000 yen. The close was below the day’s high. No explanation from the parties concerned for the rise has been confirmed.

Finale of TV Drama “VIVANT” Draws 21.8% Viewership Rating; Film Opens Dec. 4

The finale of the TV drama “VIVANT” drew a viewership rating of 21.8%. It was also announced that the film version will open on December 4. The gap from the drama’s finale to the film’s release is short, less than a month. The film’s cast and the details of its story could not be confirmed this time. The survey area and the survey company for the viewership rating also could not be confirmed.
