New York Stocks Fall: Dow Down $739, Nasdaq Drops 2.5%; Trump Says Return of Former CEO Huang Is “Perfectly Fine”

New York stocks fell sharply on the 16th. The selling is reported to reflect concern over turmoil in Nvidia's management, and President Trump showed no objection to a return of former CEO Huang.

U.S. stocks fell sharply on the 16th. The Dow Jones Industrial Average of 30 stocks lost $739, and the Nasdaq Composite, which has a high share of tech stocks, dropped 2.5%, or about 620 points. Selling spread through semiconductor and big tech stocks, and the backdrop is reported to be uncertainty over the outlook for AI development and concern over turmoil in Nvidia's management. At the company, founder Jensen Huang has resigned as chief executive officer (CEO), and the search for a successor continues. The drop came ahead of an earnings announcement scheduled for soon.

U.S. President Trump said on the 16th that a return of Huang as CEO is “perfectly fine,” indicating no objection. Elon Musk has also joined a petition calling for the return, and it is reported to have passed 160,000 signatures, but the company is reported to have rejected the move. The information that Treasury Secretary Bessent said the successor should be brought in from outside the company rests on a single report. Whether Huang is actually exploring a return has not been confirmed in explanations from Huang or the company.

U.S. Commerce Department Issues New Rule to Block Advanced-Chip Circumvention Aimed at China; Use of Overseas Computing Resources to Be Limited Too

The U.S. Department of Commerce has put forward a new rule to prevent a similar situation, amid reports of suspicion that Nvidia's advanced semiconductors had been passing through third countries to China, which is subject to export controls. In this kind of export circumvention, products that cannot be sold directly to a restricted country are delivered through a company in another country. Another report says the rule would also limit countries in conflict with the U.S., such as China, from using computing resources such as overseas data centers. Computing resources means the computing power used to develop AI.

When the rule will apply, which countries or companies will be covered, and whether there is a mechanism for trading with permission have not been confirmed. As for the suspicion itself, whether circumvention actually took place has not been confirmed either. Nvidia's share price has been moving sharply because of its management turmoil, and how the rule will affect its results cannot be known at this point.

Former Jungle Pocket Member Shinji Saito Given Four-Year Unsuspended Prison Sentence; Tokyo District Court, Defense Appeals the Same Day

On the 16th, the Tokyo District Court sentenced Defendant Shinji Saito, a former member of the comedy trio “Jungle Pocket,” to four years' imprisonment with labor, an unsuspended prison term, for sexual assault on a fellow performer. The defense had pleaded not guilty, but the ruling did not accept this. An unsuspended prison term is a sentence whose execution is not suspended, so the person goes to prison.

The defense filed an appeal within the day, and the ruling is not final. An appeal is a procedure for asking a higher court to review a judgment when a party is dissatisfied with it. One report says the ruling touched on “a difference in position,” but this rests on a single report, and the detailed reasoning of the ruling has not been confirmed. The schedule of the appeal trial has not been reported either.
