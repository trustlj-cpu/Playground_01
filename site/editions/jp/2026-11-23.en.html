COP31 Host Turkey Gives Up on Adopting an Outcome Document, Unable to Agree on Fossil Fuels

In talks on the 22nd, with the session extended, the divide over moving away from fossil fuels remained to the end.

At COP31, the UN climate change conference being held in Turkey, host country Turkey revealed on the 22nd, as talks continued under an extended session, that it was giving up on adopting an outcome document summing up the conference as a whole. A COP is a Conference of the Parties, a meeting of the countries that have joined the Framework Convention on Climate Change to decide how to proceed on countermeasures, and this one is the 31st. An outcome document is an agreement text that gathers the conclusions of the negotiations during the session into one, and it cannot be adopted unless the participating countries all consent. The text proposed in the final stage made no mention of moving away from fossil fuels, and voices of opposition were raised against it. Some reports also tell of the negotiations breaking down, and the divide over how to treat fossil fuels was never closed.

The negotiations have been run by host Turkey and co-host Australia. On the afternoon of the 22nd, a new text put forward by the two countries was reported; it kept the goal of advancing electrification worldwide, but the mention of fossil fuels was still missing. Some reports say the first draft of the 16th had two mentions of fossil fuels, but that they were gone from a revised version around the 19th. Last year's COP30, too, ended without writing a phase-out of fossil fuels into its decision document. Whether to put a path for reducing fossil fuels into the document has been the biggest point of contention at the conferences of recent years, with oil-producing countries set against countries that want moving away from them stated outright. After the giving up on adoption, whether the conference will formally close or countries will leave an agreement in some other form has not been confirmed.

Yokozuna Onosato Wins Kyushu Grand Sumo Tournament Playoff for 7th Title, Reports Say

The final day of the Kyushu Grand Sumo Tournament was the 22nd, and yokozuna Onosato is reported to have won the championship playoff against maegashira Wakamotoharu, with whom the yokozuna had tied at 13 wins and 2 losses, for a 7th title. It is the first back-to-back title since being promoted to yokozuna, and it follows the cup from the September Autumn Tournament. A championship playoff is a system in which wrestlers who have tied in record after their final-day bouts face each other again on the spot to decide the title. Maegashira are the wrestlers of the top makuuchi division other than yokozuna, ozeki, sekiwake and komusubi. Also, in juryo, the division just below makuuchi, Wakatakakage, returning for the first time in three tournaments after emergency surgery on the left thigh, won the title at 14 wins and 1 loss. Juryo wrestler Enho posted a career-best 11 wins and is reported to be aiming at a return to makuuchi. Among the three special prizes, Tamawashi received the Fighting Spirit Prize at 42 years 0 months, reportedly the oldest recipient of the three special prizes. The next tournament is the New Year Tournament in January next year, where Onosato will be going for a third straight title.

Education Minister Seki Reveals Ministry Staff Attended 3 More Parties; Tamaki Says Censure Motion Is Warranted

Minister of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology Seki, on the matter of ministry staff attending a political fundraising party the minister had held, has revealed, in the form of correcting Diet answers and other statements, that staff attended 3 more parties. Tamaki, leader of the Democratic Party for the People, said in the Diet on the 22nd that giving an explanation at odds with the facts is serious, that Seki deserves a censure motion, and called for a decision on whether to stay in office. The secretary-general of the Constitutional Democratic Party is reported to have pointed to Prime Minister Takaichi's responsibility for the appointment.

Earlier reports say that at a gathering in June 2024 an official of the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare attended and explained the clinical trial system, and that at another gathering in March 2024 a section chief of the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry was invited as a speaker. Seki has indicated the view that it is not in itself legally problematic for ministry staff to give administrative explanations at gatherings attended by related companies. On the 21st, talk of Seki resigning was reported to be spreading within the ruling parties. Whether a censure motion will be submitted, and whether Seki will stay in the post, have not been confirmed.

Japan's Probe Mio Reportedly Enters Orbit Around Mercury Together With European Probe

Japan's probe Mio, which has been flying since 2018 toward Mercury, the planet closest to the Sun, has been placed together with the European probe it is joined to in orbit around Mercury, the European Space Agency is reported to have announced. Mio is part of BepiColombo, a joint Japan-Europe Mercury exploration program, in which the Japanese probe studies Mercury's magnetic field and surrounding particles and the European probe studies its surface and interior.

Because Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun, a probe comes under the influence of the Sun's strong heat and gravity, making it a hard planet to enter orbit around. In the plan set in advance, insertion into orbit began on the 21st and proceeds through 16 thruster firings continuing until March 2027. The two probes are to be separated from December 9 to 10, and full-scale scientific observation is expected to begin around April 2027. However, this is the advance plan, and whether the actual schedule shown in this announcement, or the timing of the separation, has changed has not been confirmed.

Tower Records' Online Store Hit by Unauthorized Access, About 11 Million Records of Personal Information Reportedly Leaked

Tower Records, which sells music software and other goods, is reported to have announced that its online store suffered unauthorized access and that about 11 million records of personal information, including customers' email addresses and names, leaked. The announcement was reported on the 22nd.

Unauthorized access is the act of using another person's ID, password or the like without permission to get into a system one has no authority to enter. In the case of an online store, the important points are when the intrusion took place, the full set of items that leaked, and whether passwords or credit card information are included. None of these is given in this report, and it has also not been confirmed whether about 11 million is a number of people or a total count of records. A company hit by unauthorized access generally confirms the leaked items and the people affected, then proceeds to notify them and make a public announcement.
