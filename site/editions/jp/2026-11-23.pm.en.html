Restoration of Shuri Castle's Main Hall Complete, Open to the Public at Noon on the 23rd, Seven Years After the Fire

A completion ceremony was held under a blue sky, and the vermilion main hall has appeared for the first time in seven years.

The main hall of Shuri Castle in Naha, Okinawa Prefecture, has been restored about seven years after it was destroyed in the fire of October 2019, and a completion ceremony hosted by the national government was held on the 22nd. A ribbon-cutting took place at the ceremony, and a celebration ceremony hosted by Okinawa Prefecture was also held on the morning of the 23rd. The main hall opens to the public at noon on the 23rd. The main hall was the center of politics and ritual of the Ryukyu Kingdom, and nine buildings, including the main hall, were lost in the fire. The restoration is reported to reflect study of old documents and Meiji-era photographs, and the carving behind the kohai (the part under the roof projecting from the entrance of the building) at the front has been changed from the previous “single peony and arabesque” to a design combining three peonies and arabesque with a pair of lions.

Over the restoration, a debate that also involves views of history has continued over how to set the orientation of the great dragon pillars standing in front of the main hall. The great dragon pillars are stone pillars carved with dragons in front of the main hall, and researchers are divided over which way they should face. The opening of the main hall marks a milestone in the recovery of Shuri Castle, but buildings such as the one equivalent to the residence where the Ryukyu Kingdom's heir lived are to be restored in the future, so the work continues. The number of visitors on opening day, the method of admission and the crowding situation have not been confirmed.

U.S. Treasury Secretary Says Return of Nvidia's Former CEO Requires “Determining Who Is Responsible,” Differing From Trump

U.S. Treasury Secretary Bessent said on the 22nd that responsibility needs to be determined before Jensen Huang, the former CEO of chip giant Nvidia, returns. This differs from the position of President Trump, who holds that Huang's return may be allowed. Bessent also said it is necessary to confirm which tier of management knew about the company's chip sales to China. Huang stepped down as CEO last week, and chief financial officer (CFO) Colette Kress is now serving as acting CEO. Bessent has previously asked the company to provide an explanation within 10 days, and the deadline is said to be the 25th. The issues are how advanced semiconductors subject to export controls flowed to China and who oversaw compliance (adherence to laws and regulations) inside the company. Whether the board of directors will choose a successor from inside or outside the company has not been confirmed.

Trump Reported to Consider Renewed Attack on Iran in the New Year if No Deal Is Reached

U.S. President Trump is reported to have indicated that if no agreement is reached with Iran, a renewed attack on Iran would be considered after the start of the new year. The United States is said to be continuing a maritime blockade and economic pressure, and whether to resume a large-scale military operation jointly with Israel is reported to be among the options. Candidate targets are said to include nuclear-related facilities, bridges and power plants. While Israeli Prime Minister Netanyahu is calling for a resumption of attacks, close aides in the U.S. administration and leaders of Gulf countries are said to be advising that time be given until the economic pressure takes effect.

Iran is said to have put forward a new proposal on the nuclear issue and peace through Qatari mediation, and the United States is reported to be examining its content. Trump is said to have stated that no decision will be rushed. The timing and scale of a renewed attack and the terms of a deal have not been confirmed.

U.S. Democrats Projected to Reach 258 Seats in the Lower House (Count Ongoing), Reported as Biggest Win Since 1992

In the U.S. midterm elections held in early November, the Democrats are reported to be projected to reach 258 seats in the lower house (count ongoing). Midterm elections are congressional elections held partway through a president's term, and all 435 lower house seats are up. The majority mark is 218 seats, so the Democrats are on course to exceed it by a wide margin. There are reports of the biggest win since 1992, and the total is projected to be the largest since the Congress of 1991 to 1993.

Reports give figures in the high 250s, and the final seat count will change depending on the results in districts where the outcome has not been decided. The new Congress is to convene on January 3 next year, and the Democrats are projected to hold a majority in the lower house. Under the Trump administration, the governing party is projected to no longer fully control both chambers of Congress. The final seat count has not been confirmed.

Russian Ship Explodes at Port in Eastern Libya, Drone Attack Suspected

An explosion occurred on the 22nd on a Russian ship moored at the port of Benghazi in eastern Libya, and it caught fire. The government in eastern Libya issued a statement saying that, according to a preliminary investigation, a drone attack is suspected as the cause of the explosion aboard the ship. A drone is an aircraft that flies with no one aboard, by remote control or automatically.

Some reports suggest an attack by a Ukrainian naval drone. However, the party that carried out the attack has not been identified, and the Russian side's explanation, the scale of the damage and whether there were casualties have not been confirmed in what has been reported.
