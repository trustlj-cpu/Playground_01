Unauthorized Access at NTT Data Contractor; Data on 2,678 Kintetsu GHD Corporate Bond Buyers May Have Leaked

Names and purchase amounts may have been exposed. Whether addresses or account information are included has not been confirmed.

NTT Data announced on the 31st that a contractor running a service that grants perks to people who buy corporate bonds had suffered unauthorized access. Kintetsu Group Holdings, which uses the service, disclosed that the names and purchase amounts of people who bought its corporate bonds may have leaked. The data covers 2,678 records, information on investors who bought 1 million yen or more of its Series 135 unsecured corporate bonds. A leak has not been confirmed; it is still only at the stage of a “possibility.”

A contractor is an outside company to which a business entrusts part of its work. Even if its own systems are safe, information can get out if the company it entrusted is attacked, and this case fits that pattern. What has been reported is names and purchase amounts, and whether addresses, account numbers or card information are included has not been confirmed. When the unauthorized access began, and whether users of other companies that use the same contractor are affected, are also unknown. The announcement of unauthorized access involving personal information follows those by GMO and Club Tourism.

FBI Says North Korea Was Involved in Binance Attack; Impact About 1 Trillion Yen by the Company’s Estimate

The U.S. Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) announced on the 31st (local time) that North Korea was involved in the cyberattack on Binance, a major crypto asset exchange. The FBI says North Korea stole more than $6 billion in crypto assets around Oct. 29. Binance estimates the affected assets at about $6.3 billion. That comes to about 1 trillion yen, but it is Binance’s own estimate, not a confirmed loss. Binance announced that it covered about 95% of it with its own funds and resumed withdrawals in stages from the 1st. It had earlier said 90%, and raised the share. The FBI named the North Korean state-backed group known as “TraderTraitor.” The FBI says it also confirmed signs of money laundering, such as the stolen assets being swapped into other currencies. Money laundering means making the origin of funds obtained through crime hard to trace, by repeating transactions or swapping into other currencies. The specific method, where the stolen funds ultimately went, and when withdrawals will fully return to normal have not been confirmed. The cost of the coverage is said to be borne by the company itself, but whether users can withdraw everything immediately is also unknown.

Waseda Wins First Title in 16 Years, Sixth Overall, With Meet Record; Two Titles in University Ekiden Along With Izumo, Three Section Records

The 58th All-Japan University Ekiden, run over eight sections along the Ise route from in front of Atsuta Shrine’s west gate with 27 teams, was held on the 1st, and Waseda University won by greatly breaking the meet record. It was their sixth title and first in 16 years; following October’s Izumo All-Japan University Selected Ekiden, it gave them two titles this season among the three major university ekidens.

In the race, Aoyama Gakuin took the lead in section 1 and Waseda entered section 2 in second. In section 2, first-year Yota Masuko took the lead with a section record, Ryoki Niitsuma in section 3 held the lead, and Ruin Suzuki in section 5 also ran away alone with a section record. Waseda set three section records. Aoyama Gakuin, which had finished 11th at Izumo, lost by a wide margin. During the race, several schools vanished from the live-tracking screen showing runners’ positions because of a GPS device malfunction, and the meet’s official X account apologized. What remains is the Hakone Ekiden at New Year, where Waseda is one win away from a triple crown.

Opting Out of AI Training to Be Specified in One Step via robots.txt; OpenAI, Google and Microsoft Back Common Mechanism

OpenAI, Google and Microsoft have said they will support a common mechanism that lets website operators specify in one step whether their content may be used to train AI. An Anthropic representative also said it would be implemented in a personal capacity. Operators need only write a common token in robots.txt to manage each company’s crawlers in one place. The intended use is to decline AI training only, while still allowing crawling for search. The common specification is said to be introduced in 2027.

robots.txt is a small document placed at a site’s entrance that tells visiting robots “you may enter here” and “do not enter here.” Until now, operators had to list crawler names company by company, a heavy burden for those who wanted to decline AI training but still appear in search. However, whether to honor the designation is left to each company, and there is no enforcement power to stop operators that do not. The specific token name and the handling of data already collected have not been confirmed.

13 Candidates File for Fukuoka Mayoral Election, a Postwar Record; First Without an Incumbent in 40 Years; Kumamoto Mayoral Race Also Opens

Official notice for the Fukuoka mayoral election was issued on the 1st, and 13 newcomers filed their candidacies. It is a postwar record, and also the most ever for a mayoral election in a designated city. It is the first election without an incumbent running in 40 years. Because of the large number of candidates, poster display boards were added.

With so many filings, it is possible that no one reaches the statutory vote threshold needed to win, forcing a new election. For mayoral elections the statutory vote threshold is one quarter of the total valid votes. Official notice was also issued for the Kumamoto mayoral election the same day, where an incumbent seeking a fourth term faces four newcomers. Voting is on the 15th. In Tokyo, official notice will be issued on the 8th for executive elections in Shinjuku and Shinagawa wards and Koganei city.
