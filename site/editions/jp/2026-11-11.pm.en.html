Outage at NTT Docomo Since Shortly After 10:30 a.m. on the 11th; No Unauthorized Access Confirmed

Voice calls and data communications became hard to connect, and the company is investigating the cause. There was also a failure in its 5G services on the 5th of this month.

A network outage began at NTT Docomo shortly after 10:30 a.m. on the 11th, leaving voice calls and data communications on mobile phones hard to connect. As of shortly before 3 p.m. the outage was still continuing, and the company is investigating the cause. So far it has not been confirmed to be the result of unauthorized access. Accounts of the extent of the impact differ: some say voice calls and data communications are hard to connect nationwide, some describe a failure of 5G services, and some say it is limited to some users. Docomo also caused a communications outage in its 5G services on the 5th of this month.

The failure on the 5th began at around 11:18 a.m. and was fully restored at around 1:20 a.m. the next day, the 6th. Docomo explained the cause as “congestion” at switching equipment housing 5G SA subscribers. Congestion means that requests for communication concentrate on particular equipment, like a traffic jam on a road, and cannot all be processed. Whether the cause this time is the same as on the 5th is not yet known. The number of users affected and the outlook for restoration have not been confirmed either.

Bill to Cut Consumption Tax on Food Enters Deliberation in Upper House; Prime Minister Takaichi Says Considering Support Payments for 30% or More of Workers

The bill on cutting the consumption tax on food, said to be the biggest focus of the current Diet session, entered deliberation in the House of Councillors on the 11th. To enter deliberation means that questioning on a bill begins in earnest. On the 10th, the ruling and opposition parties had formally agreed at the Steering Committee to begin deliberation on the 11th. Deliberation, which had been only scheduled until the morning of that day, has now actually begun. In questioning, Prime Minister Takaichi said that, regarding the support payments tied to income, the government is considering covering 30% or more of workers so that more people can receive them. The amount of the payments, the cutoff for who is covered, when the tax cut would start and the schedule for a vote have not been made clear. Whether the bill will pass has not been decided either.

Next Spring’s Unified Local Elections Set for Voting on April 11 and 25; Special Law Fixing the Schedule Enacted

A special law fixing the schedule for next spring’s unified local elections was passed and enacted at the House of Councillors plenary session on the 11th. Voting for the first half, covering governor elections and others, will take place on April 11 next year, and voting for the second half, covering elections for heads of cities, wards, towns and villages and others, on April 25. The unified local elections are a system that aligns the timing of elections in local governments across the country.

Because the first and second halves are two weeks apart, voting day differs by area. For example, an area with a governor election votes on the 11th, and an area with only a municipal head election votes on the 25th. The official campaign start dates for elections in each area, and moves by people declaring their candidacy, will become clear from now on.

Nakano Broadway Luxury Watch Theft: Metropolitan Police Department Arrests Suspect in 30s, Learned Through Interviews With Investigators

In a case last month in which a man ran off with a luxury watch worth about 46 million yen while trying it on at a watch shop in the Nakano Broadway commercial complex in Nakano Ward, Tokyo, the Metropolitan Police Department on the 11th arrested a suspect in their 30s on suspicion of theft. This was learned through interviews with investigators. The suspect is reported to have been taken into custody in Nagoya. Theft is the crime of taking another person’s property away without permission. The 46 million yen is an amount that would take 38 years to save even by putting aside 100,000 yen every month.

The suspect’s name, whether the suspect admits the allegation, and the motive have not been confirmed. No official explanation from the Metropolitan Police Department on where the stolen watch is has been confirmed either. One outlet has a report that the watch was returned by courier, but it has not been confirmed elsewhere.

JR Freight Employee Arrested on Suspicion of Bribery, Allegedly Took Several Million Yen in Cash; Second Arrest After September

Fukuoka Prefectural Police on the 11th arrested an employee in their 30s at JR Freight’s Kyushu branch on suspicion of violating the JR Companies Act, which bans the giving and receiving of bribes (bribery). The employee is suspected of receiving a combined several million yen in cash in return for arranging favors so that contractors for electrical work could get in as subcontractors. Bribery means receiving money or goods in return for something connected with one’s work.

This is the second JR Freight employee to be arrested, following one in September. Whether the arrested employee admits the allegation, what the money received was used for, and any link to the September case have not been confirmed. How the company explains this arrest has not been confirmed either.
