GMO Breach: Group Company Flagged Vulnerability Two Years Ago; President and Others to Hold Press Conference on the 2nd

A security company within the group had pointed out a flaw in the system. GMO will hold its first press conference since the problem came to light.

In the unauthorized access to GMO Internet Group’s “Onamae.com Desktop Cloud,” a service for automated FX trading, it has emerged that a security company within the group pointed out a vulnerability in the system in April 2024. The company had called for a fundamental review. Measures were taken at the group’s direction at the time, but they proved insufficient. A GMO executive says, “We should have temporarily suspended the service and taken more thorough action.”

GMO will hold a press conference on the afternoon of Monday, November 2, attended by Chairman and President Masatoshi Kumagai and others. It is the first since the problem came to light, and it is expected to explain the course of events, how users are being handled and future policy. According to a security firm’s investigation, a suspicious site calling on victims to share information has been made public, and a link to the hackers has been pointed out. Anxiety has spread among FX users, and some say they lost about 4.3 million yen overnight. The route of intrusion and the full picture of the damage cannot be confirmed at this point.

Club Tourism Member Data on 4.32 Million Records May Have Leaked; Website Temporarily Suspended

Travel company Club Tourism announced on the 31st that its servers had been hit by unauthorized access and that about 4.32 million records of personal information on internet members may have leaked. It detected the unauthorized access at 5:45 a.m. on the 30th, isolated the servers concerned and cut off communications. The website’s functions have been suspended to prevent the damage from spreading and to investigate, and the company says it will resume them as soon as safety is confirmed. The items that may have leaked are listed as name, address, telephone number, email address, member ID and date of birth. Some information also says nationality and emergency contacts are included, and the full list of items cannot be confirmed. The company explains that login passwords and credit card information are not included. On the 30th, Muji and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, among others, also announced possible information leaks, and announcements of damage from unauthorized access continue.

Tax-Free Shopping Switches to a “Refund Method” From the 1st: Consumption Tax Refunded on Departure, as a Measure Against Illegal Resale

The tax-free system used by foreign travelers and others changes to a “refund method” for purchases made from November 1. Until now, goods could be bought at a price with consumption tax deducted at the store. From now on, travelers first pay a price that includes consumption tax, and the amount equivalent to the consumption tax is refunded after customs at an airport or port confirms on departure that the goods are being taken out. The review responds to illegal resale, in which goods bought tax-free are diverted and sold within Japan. There is no overlap period for the old and new methods; the new method applies to all sales from November onward. Travelers must leave Japan within 90 days of the purchase date, and no refund is made after the deadline. Food, drink, cosmetics and the like that are consumed in Japan are also not eligible for a refund. The method and timing of refunds differ from store to store.

Iran’s Supreme Leader Orders Military to Respond “Without Geographic Limits” if U.S. Resumes Attacks

Iran’s Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei has instructed senior military officers that if the United States carries out a major attack again, they “must take an immediate, powerful response not bound by geographic limits.” The Supreme Leader’s office made this known on the 31st. Khamenei is said to have met with commanders of the military and the Revolutionary Guard. The date and place of the instruction have not been disclosed, and no video or voice recording has appeared. The previous supreme leader was killed in the attack by the United States and Israel on February 28. The remarks come amid speculation that military operations may resume after the November 3 U.S. midterm elections, and they served as a warning to the U.S. side.

Chief Cabinet Secretary Kihara Cautious on Declaring Deflation Over, Even After the BOJ’s “2% Reached” Judgment

Chief Cabinet Secretary Minoru Kihara, asked at a press conference on the 30th whether to issue a declaration of an end to deflation, restated the government’s long-standing policy that the economy must be in a state where it will not return to deflation, and maintained a stance of judging cautiously. The question came after the Bank of Japan judged that day that the underlying inflation rate had reached its 2% target.

A declaration of an end to deflation signals that the government has confirmed that prices are no longer persistently falling and are not expected to return to that state. The judgment looks at several indicators, including consumer prices, supply and demand, and wage trends. Opposition parties are calling for anti-inflation measures to curb high prices to take priority over a declaration. No timing for a declaration has been given.
