Nikkei Closes Down 5.06% at 56,436.65 Yen After Falling More Than 2,800 Yen at One Point; Selling Reported to Spread Through AI and Semiconductor Shares

On the Tokyo stock market, selling led from soon after trading opened, and the day ended sharply lower. Explanations of the background vary from report to report and have not been confirmed.

On the Tokyo stock market on the 20th, the Nikkei average came under selling from soon after trading opened and at one point fell more than 2,800 yen, dropping below 57,000 yen. The decline widened from more than 1,300 yen in the morning to more than 1,700 yen and then more than 2,000 yen, and by around 10 a.m. it is reported to have passed 2,800 yen. The selling is said to have centered on shares related to AI and semiconductors. The Nikkei average is an index calculated from the share prices of the 225 main stocks on the Tokyo Stock Exchange. It closed at 56,436.65 yen, down 5.06% from the previous day.

As for the background, some reports cite concern about the outlook for the AI and semiconductor industries, but whose assessment this is has not been shown and it has not been confirmed. On the U.S. market on the 19th, the day before, the U.S. AI company Anthropic listed. A view that the listing did not ease anxiety over the AI market is also reported. The Tokyo market on the 19th extended its gains in the morning session, and expectations for the listing were said to have been a support. Whether this decline will continue has not been confirmed at this point.

Anthropic's Market Capitalization Reported at 282 Trillion Yen, Surpassing Meta; Report Says 470 Billion Yen Raised in Japan

Anthropic, the U.S. AI company that listed on the Nasdaq on the 19th, is reported to have reached a market capitalization of about 282 trillion yen, surpassing Meta. Market capitalization is the share price multiplied by the number of shares outstanding, and it shows how the market has priced the size of a company. The figure differs from the valuation of about 239 trillion yen, based on the offer price, that the morning edition reported, but which point's share price was used for the calculation has not been shown and it has not been confirmed. In Japan, the amount raised for the company's shares is reported to have reached 470 billion yen, with 4 times the investor demand. Another report says orders of more than 1.5 trillion yen came in, more than 3 times the amount offered for Japan, so the multiple differs from report to report. There is also a report that about 5,000 employees became millionaires, that is, people holding assets of $1 million or more. All of these are single reports, and the breakdown of the funds raised and the mechanism of sales in Japan have not been confirmed.

First Hearing in Gifu Ground Self-Defense Force Shooting Range Case: Defendant Reported to Admit Charges, While Defense Contests Responsibility and Argues Not Guilty

The first hearing in the case in which 3 personnel were shot with rifles at a Ground Self-Defense Force shooting range in Gifu City 3 years ago, leaving 2 dead and 1 seriously injured, was held on the 20th at the Gifu District Court. The defendant, a former Self-Defense Official candidate who is charged with robbery-murder and other crimes, is reported to have admitted the charges as indicted. The defense, however, contested criminal responsibility and argued that the defendant is not guilty. The defendant is said to have been 18 at the time of the incident.

Criminal responsibility is the capacity to judge whether one's own act is right or wrong and to act accordingly; if it is found to have been markedly lacking, the person is not punished or the sentence is reduced. In this trial, how to evaluate the defendant's mental state at the time of the incident is said to be the point at issue. The timing of the verdict and the schedule of the next and later hearings have not been confirmed at this point.

Minister in Charge Katayama Calls Rakuten Securities Information Leak “Extremely Regrettable” and Reportedly Urges Customer Protection

Over the leak of Rakuten Securities customer information, Minister in Charge Katayama is reported to have said on the 20th that it is “extremely regrettable” and to have called for customers to be protected. On the 19th, the Financial Services Agency issued the company an order to submit a report, making it report on the course of the leak through unauthorized access and other matters.

An order to submit a report is a procedure in which the authorities ask a business operator to report on its operations and the course of events, and it is used first to establish the facts. As for the scale of the leak, only 1 outlet has reported that the company announced that IDs and other data for up to about 11.25 million accounts may have leaked, and the method of the unauthorized access has not been confirmed. The specific measures the minister called for have not been shown either.
