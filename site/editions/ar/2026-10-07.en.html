Argentina rout Benin 3-0 in Lionel Messi’s farewell; he scored a penalty and made two assists before a packed Monumental

The captain retired from the national team on a night of tributes at the stadium in Núñez, three days after the 7-0 win over Burkina Faso. La Nación and other outlets describe it as an unforgettable night.

Argentina’s national team beat Benin 3-0 on Tuesday, October 6, at the Monumental, in Lionel Messi’s farewell match, according to Infobae, La Nación, Ámbito and Perfil. The first half ended without goals, with three chances for Messi, a free kick that hit the post and a save by the opposing goalkeeper. In the second half Messi converted a penalty in the 26th minute, his 126th goal in the national shirt, and made two assists, according to Infobae. The stadium was full, with some 85,000 people according to Infobae.

At the end of the match there were hugs with teammates and opponents, tears and a lap of honor, Perfil reported. Messi had announced his retirement from the national team on August 31 on Instagram. Three days earlier, on Saturday the 3rd, Argentina had thrashed Burkina Faso 7-0 at the same stadium with an alternate team; their opponent arrived with a mostly under-23 squad and landed about five hours before the match, according to La Nación. The next window for friendlies will be between November 9 and 17. Lionel Scaloni’s contract expires at the end of the year, and an unofficial report indicates he is likely to renew, which is not confirmed.

Politics · EducationPress ✓ 4 outlets · ruling according to Infobae, Perfil, El Cronista and Ámbito

A judge declared unconstitutional the decree with which Milei halted the University Funding Law and ordered it applied; the Government said it will appeal

Federal judge Martín Cormick ruled on Tuesday, October 6, that Decree 759/2025 is unconstitutional in the part that made the application of the University Funding Law conditional, and ordered the Executive Branch to comply with it immediately and issue the necessary rules, according to Ámbito and Infobae. In the judge’s view, the decree was an “extra-constitutional mechanism,” a kind of disguised veto that the Constitution does not provide for, after Congress insisted with two thirds and the law was promulgated. He cited a calculation by the Congressional Budget Office, which put the cost of the law at around 0.23% of projected GDP.

An earlier injunction had already become final: the Supreme Court rejected the Government’s extraordinary appeal on June 25, according to Ámbito. El Cronista and Perfil reported that the Casa Rosada said it will appeal and closed the door to any negotiation; Infobae spoke of a Government that seeks “not to comply” with the law. The rectors met at the Cabildo to demand compliance, according to Ámbito. DailyDrop has not yet seen the appeal filed, nor a detailed official position from the Ministry of Human Capital.

Markets · ArgentinaPress ✓ 3 outlets · figures according to Infobae and Ámbito

Country risk fell 26 points to 573, more than 80 less than on Friday, with Wall Street’s record and bonds rising 1%

The country risk fell 26 units, to 573 basis points, on Tuesday, October 6, according to Infobae. It has accumulated a drop of more than 80 points in two days from the ten-month high on Friday the 2nd (655). Dollar bonds rose 1% on average, after a rebound of more than 2% on Monday. Among the ADRs, Satellogic gained 5.3% and IRSA 2.5%. Infobae attributed the improvement to another positive session on Wall Street, with gains of 0.5% to 0.6%.

DailyDrop could not confirm the S&P Merval’s Tuesday close in two sources, so it does not publish it. The wholesale rate closed at $1,520, unchanged, according to Ámbito, and the volatility of global bonds remains a warning sign, according to Infobae. If the country risk stays below 600, the Treasury would have an issuance window to refinance debt, although the Government did not announce a placement.

Politics · CongressPress ✓ 3 outlets · votes according to Infobae and Perfil

The opposition pushes for a special session on October 15 to repeal DNU 70/23; the Government believes it will not reach a quorum

Eight opposition blocs are asking for a special session in the Chamber of Deputies for Thursday, October 15, at 2 p.m., with 32 signatures, to take up the rejection of DNU 70/23, household indebtedness and the disability emergency, according to Perfil. Their numbers are around 122 deputies, seven fewer than the 129 that the quorum requires, Perfil reported. Infobae reported on October 2 that there is optimism in the Government that the opposition will not manage to repeal the decree and that “14 key days” are beginning for negotiating with allies.

La Nación noted on Monday that the opposition is still far from the number. The Supreme Court called in two substitute judges over a tie on the article that repealed the Rural Land Law, a step that Infobae links to the possibility of the Government avoiding the session. Everything depends on whether the allied governors do or do not provide a quorum. DailyDrop has seen no definitive vote count.

Markets · DollarPress ✓ 2 outlets · quotes according to Ámbito and El Cronista

The blue dollar fell to one-month lows and the gap with the official rate hit its lowest level since mid-year

The wholesale dollar closed on Tuesday, October 6, unchanged at $1,520, according to Ámbito. The blue closed at $1,520 to buy and $1,540 to sell, a one-month low, and the gap with the official rate fell to around 1.3%, its lowest level since mid-year, according to Ámbito. The MEP closed at $1,538.14 and the CCL at $1,603.65. Ámbito recorded other figures in different stories during the day, so the closing figures are used.

El Cronista published different values, with a wholesale rate of $1,536.90 and a blue of $1,550, which do not match Ámbito’s and are left out. DailyDrop could not confirm the figure with a third source. With the wholesale rate far from the ceiling of the band, the Central Bank does not need to intervene.
