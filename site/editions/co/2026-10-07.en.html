Economy · Public financesPress ✓ 4 outlets · no amounts or conditions confirmed

De la Espriella Government asks the IMF for an assessment, and a Finance Ministry delegation will travel to Washington this week

Minister Miguel Gómez says the State spends about 40 trillion pesos a month and takes in some 29 trillion. No amounts or rates of any possible loan are known.

President Abelardo de la Espriella announced on Sunday, September 27, in a national address, that he had instructed the Finance Ministry to open talks with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) to seek a negotiated way out of the fiscal crisis, according to Vanguardia, Forbes and El País. The Government formally asked the IMF to begin the Article IV Consultation, an independent assessment of the economy and of the Executive’s plans. The Fund confirmed that it received the request, and Vanguardia reported on October 1 that a Finance Ministry delegation would travel to Washington the following week.

Finance Minister Miguel Gómez maintains that the State spends about 40 trillion pesos a month and receives some 29 trillion. The plan includes the 2027 budget and an Economic Rescue Law. Forbes recalls that Gómez had earlier ruled out an agreement with the Fund, and the Pacto Histórico questioned the approach (Asuntos Legales). DailyDrop found no amounts, rates or conditions of a possible loan, so talk of a “rescue” is premature.

Sport · National teamPress ✓ 5 outlets · goals by Velásquez and Arcila

Colombia beat Peru 2-0 in Miami and closed out the FIFA window with its first win of the tour

The Colombian national team beat Peru 2-0 on Tuesday, October 6, at Nu Stadium in Miami, with goals from Samuel Velásquez, shortly before the hour mark, and Daniel Arcila, in the 90+2, according to El País, El Universal, Noticias RCN, Radio Nacional and Andina. Peru finished with ten men after the sending-off of Miguel Araujo, the UNI agency reported, a single source for that detail.

Earlier, Néstor Lorenzo’s side had drawn 1-1 with Mexico and lost 1-0 to Paraguay. Radio Nacional presents it as the first win since the 2026 World Cup. DailyDrop found no post-match remarks from Lorenzo to quote.

Politics · ClimatePress ✓ 3 outlets · directive of Tuesday the 6th, according to Cambio

President demands an emergency plan for El Niño from five ministries, and the Government denounces the previous administration’s lack of preparation

The Casa de Nariño sent an urgent directive to five ministries to present, together with the UNGRD, an emergency plan for the El Niño phenomenon before the next cabinet meeting, according to Cambio. Portafolio reported that the De la Espriella Government criticized the Petro administration for its lack of preparation, and that the Minister of Mines and Energy, María Nohemí Arboleda, spoke of a firm energy deficit of 8.6%. Minuto60 reported a meeting of the president with Caribbean governors on the subject.

Firm energy is the capacity of plants to generate in dry seasons. The warning comes while the Government is also managing the reconstruction after the August earthquake. DailyDrop found no figures for additional resources for El Niño and no text of the directive, and the exact dates of each meeting are not clear in the sources.

Justice · Securityone outlet, attributed to El Universal

Government prepares an Anti-Terrorism Statute to present on Thursday to the Criminal Policy Council

El Universal reported on October 6 that the De la Espriella Government is preparing a draft Anti-Terrorism Statute, which will be presented this Thursday, October 8, to the Criminal Policy Council. Because it would be a statutory law, it would need an absolute majority in Congress and prior review by the Constitutional Court, according to the same outlet. The article details the proposed changes; DailyDrop could not verify them with a second source.

The announcement comes after the attack on the Aguachica police station and the arrests in the Catatumbo. Vanguardia reported on October 8 that a Submission Law (Ley de Sometimiento) would be filed the following week, but that happens after this edition closes. The text and the position of the parties in Congress remain to be seen.

Markets · ColombiaPress ✓ 4 outlets · Rankia, Rio Times, Noticias RCN and Portafolio

COLCAP closed Tuesday at 2,589.04 points (+0.25%) and the dollar at 3,215.92 pesos; the S&P 500 touched 7,818.93

The MSCI COLCAP closed on Tuesday, October 6, at 2,589.04 points, a rise of 0.25% after Monday’s 2.69% jump, according to Rankia and Rio Times.
