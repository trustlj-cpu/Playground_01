Economy · PricesPress ✓ 5 outlets · figures from Dane

Annual inflation rose to 6.29% in September, above the Banco de la República’s tolerance range

Dane reported a monthly change of 0.37%. Restaurants and hotels, health and education were the divisions with the most annual pressure.

The National Administrative Department of Statistics (Dane) reported that the Consumer Price Index rose 6.29% over the twelve months to September 2026, up from 6.24% in August, according to reports from Noticias RCN, Blu Radio, Bloomberg Línea, Cambio and Valora Analitik. In the month, prices rose 0.37%, and the year-to-date total is 5.74%. The divisions with the biggest annual change were restaurants and hotels (9.44%), health (7.79%), education (7.49%), furniture and household items (6.75%) and food (6.74%).

Valora Analitik notes that the figure was in line with expectations: the median of the Banco de la República’s survey was 6.33%. Another outlet says the monthly change came in below the 0.41% that analysts expected. Bloomberg Línea headlined that inflation “finds no ceiling.” The Bank’s target is 3%, with a tolerance range of 2% to 4%, which Colombia has gone more than five years without meeting, according to Ecosistemas Startup. DailyDrop did not see an official reaction from the Bank’s board.

Markets · ColombiaPress ✓ 3 outlets · Valora Analitik, Rio Times and Noticias RCN

The MSCI COLCAP fell 2.09% on Wednesday, to 2,534.92 points, and the dollar rose above the TRM, to about COP 3,240

The MSCI COLCAP closed on Wednesday, Oct. 7 at 2,534.92 points, down 2.09%, according to Valora Analitik and Rio Times. Grupo Cibest’s preferred share was the most active stock and fell 3.09%. Volume was COP 126,277 million, 11.90% less than the previous session. The year-to-date figure is 22.58%.

The dollar averaged COP 3,239.09 against a TRM of COP 3,216.01 (Rio Times), and Noticias RCN put its close near COP 3,241.58, amid tension in the Middle East, Brent above US$102 and anticipation of the Federal Reserve minutes. DailyDrop found no official explanation for the stock market’s decline.

Politics · Justiceone outlet, attributed to El Universal

De la Espriella vowed to identify everyone responsible for the assassination of Miguel Uribe and announced that his father will be ambassador to the United Kingdom

At the launch event for the Valiente Leadership Center, President Abelardo de la Espriella vowed a “hunt” for those responsible for the murder of Senator Miguel Uribe Turbay and said he will identify all of the perpetrators, according to El Universal. Miguel Uribe Londoño, the senator’s father, will be appointed Colombia’s ambassador to the United Kingdom, the same outlet reported.

DailyDrop found only that source, and the story is dated Oct. 8 although it refers to Wednesday, so the exact date of the event should be verified. It saw no reaction from the family or from the Fiscalía, which is in charge of the case. Possible criticisms are that a president should not anticipate the results of a judicial investigation.

Economy · Government Planone outlet, attributed to El Universal

The government launched the regional meetings for the 2026-2030 National Development Plan from Pereira, with a portfolio of 334 projects worth COP 535.1 trillion

President De la Espriella launched, on Wednesday, Oct. 7, from Pereira, the regional meetings to build the 2026-2030 National Development Plan, according to El Universal. The National Planning Department (DNP) put together a portfolio of 334 major projects, with an estimated value of COP 535.1 trillion. Those projects must go through study, prioritization, structuring and a viability assessment before entering the plan.

DailyDrop found no second source or schedule for the meetings. Nor did it see how the COP 535.1 trillion would be financed amid the fiscal crisis and the approach to the IMF, a contrast the government itself will have to explain.

Politics · Anti-corruptionPress ✓ 2 outlets · Vanguardia and El Universal, announcements of Oct. 4 and 5

De la Espriella announced a Search Bloc against corruption and undercover agents to investigate officials

On Sunday, Oct. 4, President Abelardo de la Espriella announced a Search Bloc against corruption with the phrase “the party is over for them,” according to Vanguardia and El Universal. On Monday, Oct. 5, Vanguardia reported that the government anticipated sanctions and the addition of undercover agents to investigate cases of state corruption.

There is no decree or official summary with a budget, members or deadlines. DailyDrop also did not see the position of the Procuraduría or the Fiscalía on the use of undercover agents within the administration, a figure with legal limits. Opposition criticism was not available in the sources.
