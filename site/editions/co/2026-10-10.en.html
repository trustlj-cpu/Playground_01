Justice · UNGRD casePress ✓ 5 outlets · Supreme Court decision of October 9

Supreme Court frees former congressmen Iván Name and Andrés Calle after the legal time limit expired in the UNGRD case

The ruling does not acquit them or close the case. They must post bail of ten monthly minimum wages or present a surety policy, and sign an undertaking.

The Supreme Court’s Special Chamber of First Instance ordered release papers issued for Iván Name and Andrés Calle, who were held in La Picota prison in Bogotá, because the legal time limit had expired, according to Noticias RCN, Vanguardia, Cambio, Blu Radio and Asuntos Legales. Their freedom still depends on conditions: each must post bail of ten monthly minimum wages or present a surety policy, and sign an undertaking. Both are defendants in the scandal over diverted contracts at the National Unit for Disaster Risk Management (UNGRD).

The Court applied a twelve-month limit, not six, because evidence abroad had been ordered in the case: an expanded testimony from Carlos Ramón González, who has asylum in Nicaragua. The indictment became final on October 7, 2025, and the limit ran out on October 7, 2026. The defense teams had failed before: in July the Chamber denied the same request. The ruling is not an acquittal, and the trial continues.

Security · CaucaPress ✓ 3 outlets · Armed Forces tally

Army sergeant killed in ambush on the Popayán–Timbío road; four suspected dissidents captured

On the night of Friday, October 9, armed men attacked a Sijín patrol and Gaula soldiers with explosives and rifles on the Pan-American Highway at Mi Bohío, between Popayán and Timbío. Sergeant First Class Ángel David Ordóñez Ortiz was killed and two police officers were wounded, according to El País and Minuto60. The Army captured four people who, by its account, belong to the Carlos Patiño Front of Iván Mordisco’s dissidents, and seized two rifles and four handguns.

Early reports, such as Publimetro’s, spoke of one police officer dead and seven wounded and of the road being closed; the Armed Forces tally confirmed that the dead man was the Army noncommissioned officer. The detainees were placed at the disposal of the Prosecutor’s Office. DailyDrop saw no official statement from the Government and no confirmed reopening of the Pan-American Highway.

World · PrizesPress ✓ 4 outlets · Nobel Committee announcement of October 9

South African jurist Navi Pillay wins the 2026 Nobel Peace Prize; Trump posts criticism of the committee on Truth Social

The Norwegian Nobel Committee announced in Oslo on Friday, October 9, that the 2026 Nobel Peace Prize goes to South African jurist Navanethem “Navi” Pillay, according to Publimetro México, Diario del Sur, Conclusión and Ara. The committee noted that she has paved the way toward a more complete global legal order and that her work was key to having war crimes, crimes against humanity and genocide prosecuted. Pillay was a judge of the Rwanda Tribunal and of the International Criminal Court between 2003 and 2008.

The prize carries 12 million Swedish kronor, about $1.2 million, and the ceremony will be on December 10 in Oslo. The committee received 287 nominations, 208 individuals and 79 organizations. Last year’s winner was Venezuela’s María Corina Machado. President Donald Trump posted a message on Truth Social criticizing the committee and the laureate. The sources disagree on whether Pillay led the UN commission on Gaza, a point DailyDrop could not verify.

Markets · ColombiaPress ✓ 3 outlets · Noticias RCN, Minuto60 and Rio Times

Dollar closes Friday at 3,194.88 pesos, below 3,200 for the first time since September 22; COLCAP close not confirmed

The dollar closed on Friday, October 9, at 3,194.88 pesos, with a low of 3,184.50 and a high of 3,220, according to Noticias RCN. The TRM was 3,218.75. Minuto60 also put it below 3,200, and Rio Times describes it as the peso’s strongest level since September 22, with lower US bond yields and a weaker dollar worldwide.

The MSCI COLCAP was trading at 2,531.15 points (+0.21%) at 3:05 p.m., according to Rio Times, which notes the figure is provisional. The official close had not been published, and on Thursday it had closed at 2,525.90 (−0.36%). With markets closed over the weekend, the next reference is Monday. In the US, the S&P 500 closed at 7,811.54 (+0.59%).
