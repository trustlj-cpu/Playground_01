Opposition's march on the Election Commission stalls at the barricades. Rahul Gandhi climbs one, and the Supreme Court says it cannot act on a news report

The INDIA bloc wants CEC Gyanesh Kumar to go. Police shut Parliament Street and detained leaders; the BJP calls the charges lies; a Kerala corruption probe from 2006 has been reopened.

Opposition parties of the INDIA bloc marched from Parliament towards the Election Commission on Tuesday, demanding the removal of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar. Police shut Parliament Street for much of the day and held several leaders. Rahul Gandhi climbed a police barrier and waved the tricolour; later, Congress shared a video of him entering the Akashwani Bhawan complex on the way to the Commission's office. The Biju Janata Dal, which has kept its distance from the bloc, joined the march, which The Indian Express called a sign that the gloves were off against the BJP. Mr Gandhi said the CEC's abuse of position was proven. The BJP called his remarks "full of lies". Mr Kumar postponed a two-day visit to Chhattisgarh.

In court, the hearing of the plea asking whether the CEC can act alone produced two lines worth keeping. According to Hindustan Times, the Supreme Court refused to restrain Mr Kumar, saying it could not pass such an order on the basis of a news report. According to The Indian Express, it issued notice to the Election Commission, said it did not clear a change to Form 6 and that it can annul unilateral decisions; Chief Justice Surya Kant added that no modified Form 6 had the court's approval. Separately, The Indian Express and Mint report that a 2006 bribery probe in Kerala involving Mr Kumar is being reopened. The Special Intensive Revision of electoral rolls remains at the centre of the row. Meanwhile, a Delhi ACP accused by a woman journalist of harassment at Jantar Mantar has had his promotion put on hold.

SportsPress ✓ 6 outlets

Harmanpreet Kaur quits as India's women's captain in all formats. Smriti Mandhana takes over

Harmanpreet Kaur stepped down as captain of the Indian women's team in all formats on Tuesday, after a meeting with the BCCI. "I have done my duty," the World Cup-winning captain said in a statement that The Indian Express published in full. Hours later the BCCI named Smriti Mandhana as the new captain. NDTV Sports reported that Harmanpreet had been offered the captaincy in one format and refused; the board has not confirmed that, so we treat it as one outlet's report. Her coach said she would now be able to bat with freedom, and former captain Mithali Raj said India needed a fresh mindset.

A separate NDTV Sports report spoke of a rift between the two players. Neither has commented, and no second outlet carried it.

At least 12 Indian sailors hurt in attacks on ships in the Black Sea and Hormuz. MEA says targeting of commercial shipping must end

India condemned the latest attacks on commercial vessels after Indian crew were hurt in two theatres on Tuesday. The Hindu reports that 11 Indian crew members were injured when a ship was hit by a projectile in the Strait of Hormuz; The Indian Express counts at least 12 injured across attacks in the Black Sea and Hormuz. One person was killed in a strike on a Turkish vessel that had an Indian crew. The Ministry of External Affairs said the targeting of commercial shipping must end. It did not name who fired.

The incidents add to a run that began last week, when five Indians were rescued from a tanker in Hormuz and an Indian-run pharma plant in Ukraine was destroyed. Officials say over 4,500 Indian seafarers work on ships in the region. Families should contact the recruiting agency and the Directorate General of Shipping helpline.

World · IndiaPress ✓ 6 outlets · motive rests on investigators' reports

Indian captain Smit Machchhar underwent surgery after a flydubai cockpit attack. His co-pilot told investigators it was 'revenge for Gaza'

Captain Smit Machchhar, an Indian pilot, is recovering after an Omani co-pilot attacked him in the cockpit of a flydubai flight, using the crash axe, according to Mint and NDTV. The captain's skull was fractured and he underwent emergency surgery in Saudi Arabia, where the aircraft turned after the attack. NDTV reports the co-pilot told investigators it was "in revenge for Gaza"; Hindustan Times and NDTV report that Israel was ready to shoot down the plane before it turned toward Saudi Arabia. The Indian embassy said the captain was making steady progress, and the US House honoured his courage.

The airline has not publicly explained why the flight had two extra pilots on board, a question The Indian Express raised. The co-pilot was grounded in Oman and later moved to Morocco, but is not cleared to fly. Investigators have not published their findings.

Shreyas Iyer hits a maiden T20I century as India beat West Indies by eight wickets

Shreyas Iyer scored his first T20 international hundred as India won the opening match against West Indies by eight wickets. Reports in The Indian Express, The Hindu, Sportstar and Hindustan Times called it a cakewalk once Iyer settled. The innings lifted him sharply in the ICC T20I rankings, NDTV Sports reported. A Punjab Kings teammate said Iyer had changed his hitting technique, and the Indian Express wrote that he "found the perfect timing".

Elsewhere in cricket, India named Bhuvneshwar Kumar in the T20I squad for the New Zealand series after four years, with an eye on the 2027 World Cup, and Jurel will lead India A.
