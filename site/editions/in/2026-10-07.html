RBI raises the repo rate to 5.50% and turns to 'calibrated tightening'. The rupee still slides to 96.78 and the Sensex sheds 429 points

It is the first hike since February 2023. The Governor said the next move is a hike or a pause, not a cut. The RBI now sees inflation at 5.2 per cent this year and growth at 7.1 per cent.

The Monetary Policy Committee raised the repo rate by 25 basis points to 5.50 per cent on Wednesday and changed its stance from neutral to calibrated tightening. It is the first increase since February 2023, ending a pause at 5.25 per cent that had lasted through four reviews. Governor Sanjay Malhotra told reporters the central bank would be either raising or pausing from here, and ruled out a cut in the near term. The RBI raised its forecast for consumer price (CPI) inflation in 2026-27 to 5.2 per cent from 5.0, with the third quarter expected to peak at 6 per cent, citing a deficient monsoon, El Niño, and costly energy and commodities. It raised its growth forecast to 7.1 per cent from 6.7, after the economy grew 7.8 per cent in April–June. One outlet describes the vote as unanimous; we have not seen it confirmed elsewhere.

Markets took it badly. The Sensex fell 429.11 points, or 0.59 per cent, to 72,638.70 and the Nifty lost 0.76 per cent to 22,603.05, after a two-day rise. The rupee settled at 96.78 per dollar by Business Standard's count (PTI had 96.75), its weakest close since 20 May and within about 0.2 per cent of the record low of 96.97. Bloomberg reports the RBI's reserves fell $51 billion in the four weeks to 2 October as it defended the currency. The Indian Express says home-loan EMIs will rise; The Hindu argues that the heavy lifting on inflation now falls to the government.

Day two of the INDIA bloc's protest: Rahul and Priyanka Gandhi lie on the road and are hauled away, while allies voice unease

The opposition's campaign against CEC Gyanesh Kumar went into a second day. Rahul Gandhi and Priyanka Gandhi were forcibly removed and detained after a lie-in on a Delhi road, Mint, The Indian Express and The Hindu reported. Mr Gandhi said protests would continue until the Chief Election Commissioner resigns. A lunch hosted by Congress president Mallikarjun Kharge was followed by an INDIA bloc MPs' bus trip towards Jantar Mantar that brought Janpath to a halt, Hindustan Times wrote.

Not everyone was in step. The Indian Express reported that allies were unhappy with the Gandhis' impromptu protest; the Samajwadi Party skipped the lunch, which an official blamed on delayed communication (Hindustan Times); NDTV reported a DMK U-turn on the march. Citizens' groups have planned a separate protest at Jantar Mantar for 9 October.

Narendra Modi completes 25 years in public office and says a 'we will do it' attitude will deliver development goals

Prime Minister Narendra Modi marked 25 years in elected executive office on Wednesday, counting from his first oath as Gujarat Chief Minister in 2001. Speaking at a Viksit Bharat Sankalp Sabha in Delhi, he said a "we will do it" attitude would achieve development goals, and The Indian Express read his line "I won't stop" as a message to the opposition. The Union Cabinet congratulated him on 25 years of continuous leadership. World leaders sent greetings, Hindustan Times reported, and the BJP called it a "golden phase". The Prime Minister met veteran leader L.K. Advani, NDTV reported.

Newspapers used the day to take stock. The Hindu ran both a celebratory piece and a question, "In 25 years, has Modi made lasting changes?"; the Indian Express carried a personal memoir. Such pieces are opinion, and readers should weigh them that way. Bike rallies were held in Varanasi to mark the anniversary, according to The Economic Times.

Tanker with 23 Indian crew catches fire after a Ukrainian drone attack off Russia's Black Sea coast. All are rescued

An oil tanker carrying 23 Indian crew members caught fire after a Ukrainian drone attack off Russia's Black Sea coast, Hindustan Times and The Indian Express reported. All the Indians were rescued. The incident comes a day after the Ministry of External Affairs condemned attacks on commercial shipping that left at least 12 Indian sailors injured in the Black Sea and the Strait of Hormuz.

The reports did not name the vessel or give details of the rescue or of any injuries. India has said more than 4,500 Indian seafarers are serving on ships in the region. The MEA has not yet commented on this latest case.

Messi plays his last match for Argentina: 'I don't want to leave. Not being with Argentina hurts'

Lionel Messi ended his international career on Tuesday night as Argentina beat Benin in a farewell friendly, The Hindu reported. In his farewell speech, reported by The Indian Express, he said he did not want to leave and that not being with Argentina hurts. The Indian Express also records that he finishes second on the all-time list of international goals. Cristiano Ronaldo, who has his own dispute with the Portugal coach, hailed Messi with "Leo, all my respect for you". NDTV Sports reported that Benin is full of pride at its part in the occasion.

Ronaldo, meanwhile, has deferred his own retirement from the Portugal team and wants an apology and "punishment" after the walkout, The Indian Express reported.
