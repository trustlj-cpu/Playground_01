Rubio and Jaishankar hold talks in Mumbai as US and India aim to finalise trade deal

The US Secretary of State landed in Mumbai on Tuesday; energy, technology, security and critical minerals were on the agenda.

US Secretary of State Marco Rubio landed in Mumbai on Tuesday, 20 October, for talks with External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar, The Hindu reported. After arriving, Rubio and his wife visited the Gateway of India, accompanied by the Maharashtra Chief Minister, the newspaper said, and the two ministers were due to meet later the same day. Hindustan Times, in a preview published earlier on Tuesday, said trade, security and technology were on the agenda, and that discussions were expected to cover energy, technology, security and critical minerals. A roundup of the day's top news in Indian media listed the Jaishankar–Rubio talks on a trade deal as its first item, and other outlets described the meeting as an effort by the two sides to finalise a trade agreement.

Reports on the Mumbai meeting said the United States and India aim to finalise the trade deal. No tariff figures, product lists or signing date had been announced in those reports. In Mumbai, Jaishankar also held talks with the UAE's Foreign Minister and described the situation in West Asia as a matter of 'deep concern', news reports said; he also backed the safe flow of trade, according to the same accounts. The Wall Street Journal reported that US stocks made a U-turn on Tuesday on hopes of US-Iran diplomacy. In Delhi, Hindustan Times reported a three-layer security cover and traffic diversions around the Dussehra celebrations at which Prime Minister Narendra Modi was expected, while Modi, Home Minister Amit Shah and other leaders posted Vijayadashami wishes. Indian equity markets were shut on Tuesday for Dussehra, AajTak reported, so the Nifty 50 has not traded since its 19 October close of 22,888.95, down 1.11 per cent. USD/INR closed at 94.77, flat on the day.

CJP seeks urgent night hearing in Delhi High Court against judicial custody of Saurav Das and Ashutosh Ranka

The CJP moved the Delhi High Court on Tuesday, 20 October, challenging the judicial custody of Saurav Das and Ashutosh Ranka, The Hindu reported. 'We have moved the Delhi High Court and requested an urgent night hearing,' Ratna Singh, the CJP's legal head, said, according to the newspaper. The report did not say whether the court had listed the plea or fixed a time. ABP News reported that Mamata Banerjee reacted angrily after the custody of Ashutosh and Saurav was extended, and that the CJP responded to her remarks on social media. Separately, The Hindu reported that the CJP's Abhijeet Dipke thanked the organisers of a Delhi Ramlila in Pitampura for 'free publicity' after a cockroach effigy featured at the annual event; the Shree Keshav Ram Leela Committee, which organises it, has said the effigy was part of the programme. The charges behind the custody orders were not detailed in the reports. A daily news roundup also listed the CJP's plea among Tuesday's top stories.

Mumbai Police team leaves Kota with three alleged assailants in Tiger Shroff firing case

A Mumbai Police team left Kota on Tuesday with three alleged assailants in the firing on actor Tiger Shroff, The Hindu reported. The police have registered a case under the provisions of attempt to murder and the Arms Act, the newspaper said. News reports named the three primary suspects as Rehan alias Sukka, Hritik Kurmi alias Roshan and a minor.

The Times of India reported that Salman Khan made his first public appearance since the gunfire on Shroff, and was spotted at an airport. The reports did not say what motive the police attribute to the attack, and no court appearance by the suspects had been reported when the team left Kota.

Gujarat government 'gifts' part of disputed Vadodara plot to Yusuf Pathan

The Gujarat government has proposed to give part of a disputed Vadodara plot to Yusuf Pathan, The Hindu reported on Tuesday. The proposal comes nearly four months after Pathan, a Lok Sabha MP, broke away from the TMC as part of a rebel group, the newspaper said.

BBC Hindi reported that the cricketer-turned-politician joined the NCPI party after leaving the TMC, in order to link up with the NDA, and explained the background of the plot case. Neither report said the allotment had been finalised, and the size of the plot was not given in the excerpts reported.

India–New Zealand FTA comes into force with new visa categories and zero-duty access for exports

The India–New Zealand FTA came into force on Tuesday, The Hindu reported. The agreement includes two new Temporary Employment Entry visa categories, called TEE visas, for Indian nationals, the newspaper said. It also provides zero-duty access for Indian exports to New Zealand, according to the same report.

The Hindu's account did not list every product covered by the zero-duty provision or the number of visas to be issued in each TEE category. Indian equity markets were closed on Tuesday for Dussehra, AajTak reported, and the rupee ended the day at 94.77 per US dollar.
