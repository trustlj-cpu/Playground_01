Nineteen die and 25 are hurt in a stampede at north Kolkata's Chorbagan Durga Puja pandal, and seven accused are in police custody

The crush happened on Saturday evening, October 17; the state announced ₹25 lakh and a government job for each victim's family, and a judge-led SIT will investigate.

At least 19 people died and 25 were injured in a stampede at the Chorbagan Sarbojanin Durga Puja pandal in north Kolkata on Saturday evening, October 17, during Maha Saptami, according to The Hans India, Asianet News and OpIndia. West Bengal Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari said 17 of the 19 died on the spot and that 24 of the injured were out of danger while one remained critical, the same outlets reported. The Week reported that a native of Bihar was among the dead. Earlier reports on Saturday night had put the toll at 17. According to The Hans India and OpIndia, a large crowd was waiting in the narrow Radha Madhab Saha Lane to enter the pandal when heavy rain began and the crowd surged forward, and Adhikari's preliminary account pointed to an announcement by a private security agency and the blowing of whistles. India TV, Business Standard and The Daily Pioneer reported that Kolkata Police pointed to sudden rain, mismanagement, a misleading announcement, narrow gates and a narrow approach road, and to too few police and private guards for the size of the crowd.

A senior police officer said the force had gathered enough evidence of gross mismanagement by the puja organisers, India TV, Business Standard and The Daily Pioneer reported, and investigators are trying to identify who made an announcement urging people to leave because the structure was said to be about to collapse. Police said more arrests will follow. The Bankshal Court on Sunday remanded seven accused to police custody until October 28, according to ANI, Outlook India and Asianet News; police had sought custody until November 1. Outlook India and Asianet News reported that the accused include the puja committee's secretary and vice-president and people linked to pandal construction, crowd management and private security, and that police registered a case of culpable homicide not amounting to murder. A defence lawyer told the court that his clients had no role in organising the event and helped with the rescue, The Daily Jagran reported. Adhikari announced ₹25 lakh (2.5 million rupees) and a Group D government job for the family of each person who died, and ₹5 lakh for each injured person with treatment costs met by the state, according to Business Today and Outlook India. Outlook India reported that he said the state would ask the Chief Justice of the Calcutta High Court to nominate a sitting judge to head a Special Investigation Team (SIT) with officials from the Special Task Force (STF) and the Criminal Investigation Department (CID). Prime Minister Narendra Modi announced ex gratia for the victims' families, according to reports on Saturday night.

A truck kills 14 or 15 devotees outside Gwalior's Sheetla Mata temple, and a senior police officer is removed and eight personnel are suspended

A truck ran over devotees outside the Sheetla Mata temple in Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh, early on Sunday, October 18, during Navratri. The Quint, The Hans India and The Week reported 15 dead, while the Free Press Journal, Northeast News and Northlines reported 14 dead and 28 injured. The Hans India reported that the truck ran over several devotees and then rammed into the temple's entrance gate, and that seven of the injured were in critical condition. The Week reported that preliminary findings point to a mechanical failure, possibly brake failure, and that the driver of the overloaded paddy truck may have used Google Maps to take a shorter route; both angles are under investigation. Chief Minister Mohan Yadav removed the city superintendent of police and suspended eight personnel, the Free Press Journal and The Hans India reported; The Hans India listed three station house officers, a traffic inspector, a head constable and three constables. The Week reported that the driver, Mohit, and the owner, Dilbag Singh, were arrested, while The Hans India gave the two names in the opposite roles. The state announced ₹4 lakh for the family of each person killed, free treatment for the seriously injured and ₹50,000 each for minor injuries, and the Prime Minister's Office announced ex gratia relief, the reports said. Relatives asked how a heavy vehicle could enter the crowded temple area, one report said.

Actor Tiger Shroff escapes unhurt as gunmen fire at his car in Mumbai's Juhu, and a post in the name of Lawrence Bishnoi's gang claims the attack

Gunmen fired at actor Tiger Shroff's car near Pushpa Park in Mumbai's Juhu on Sunday evening, October 18, and Shroff was unhurt, according to ThePrint, The Hindu, Hindustan Times and Business Standard. Hindustan Times reported that three shots were fired and that his driver was among two people injured; ThePrint reported that his driver and an autorickshaw driver were hurt, while an early Business Standard report said no injuries had been reported. A post on social media in the name of the gang of jailed gangster Lawrence Bishnoi claimed responsibility, Telangana Today and Hindustan Times reported. The post was attributed to Anil Pandit and said that anyone who works with Salman Khan will be next. Mumbai Police had not confirmed the gang's involvement.

Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis asked Police Commissioner Deven Bharti for details and for a quick arrest of the assailants, and spoke to Shroff by phone, ThePrint reported. The Hindu and ThePrint reported that police launched a manhunt and are checking CCTV footage. Maharashtra Congress president Harshwardhan Sapkal called the situation 'jungle raj' and demanded Fadnavis's resignation on moral grounds, and NCP (SP) MLA Rohit Pawar noted that the firing came days after the killing of Shiv Sena (UBT) leader Pradeep Purnekar in Thane.

Accidents · Punjab✓ 6 outlets · the driver's arrest not confirmed

A speeding car kills eight MGNREGA workers and injures 12 on a Punjab road in Sangrur, and the driver flees

A speeding car ran over MGNREGA workers on the Lehragaga-Jakhal road in Punjab's Sangrur district on Sunday, October 18, killing eight and injuring 12, according to India TV, The Federal and Oneindia. Sangrur Deputy Commissioner Poonamdeep Kaur confirmed the figures, Hindustan Times reported. The workers were near Alisher village, and police said the car came from the direction of Jakhal and the driver abandoned it and fled. India TV reported that the car was a Chevrolet Magnum and named the driver as Pargat Singh, alias Kala. Reports said all eight dead belonged to Alisher village and that three to four of the injured were critical and were moved to Sangrur Civil Hospital.

Chief Minister Bhagwant Mann announced ₹2 lakh for the family of each person killed and free treatment for the injured, and said on X that the absconding driver would be arrested soon, according to Asianet News and The News Mill. Defence Minister Rajnath Singh posted condolences on X, calling the deaths heart-wrenching. A local resident alleged that emergency services reached the spot about an hour after the accident, one report said. The driver had not been arrested at the time of the reports.

Politics · Delhi✓ 6 outlets · evidence for the alleged plot not reported

Union Minister Kiren Rijiju says some politicians and 'anti-India gangs' planned Bangladesh- and France-style violence over the SIR protests

Union Minister Kiren Rijiju said on Sunday, October 18, that some politicians and 'anti-India gangs' had planned to stir up violence in India during protests against the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls, comparing the plan to the unrest in Bangladesh and the riots in France, according to Outlook India, ANI and Asianet News. In a post on X, he said India is neither Bangladesh nor France and that regime change is possible only through elections, not through George Soros, Outlook India reported. ANI quoted him as saying that some politicians colluded to incite youth and spread violence on the streets. OrissaPOST described the remarks as an allegation of a plot to dislodge the BJP-led government.

Prashant Bhushan defended the call for a change of government, saying the protesters aim to protect electoral democracy, Outlook India reported. Business Today and Gulf News reported that the protests demand the removal of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar over alleged irregularities in the voter rolls linked to the SIR.
