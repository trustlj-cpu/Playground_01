Rubio and Jaishankar hold trade-deal talks in Mumbai before the US secretary visits Reliance's Jamnagar refinery

US Secretary of State Marco Rubio met External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar as both sides aim to finalise a trade deal.

Top-news roundups on October 21 reported that US Secretary of State Marco Rubio and External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar held talks in Mumbai as the two countries aim to finalise a trade deal. The Hindu reported that Rubio then arrived in Jamnagar, Gujarat, to visit the Reliance refinery, and said the visit assumes significance in the context of his remarks on India's oil refining capabilities and his call on the subject. BBC Hindi, in a separate report, recalled earlier visits by members of the Trump family to Jamnagar, including one by Donald Trump Jr. last November, and asked whether Rubio's trip says something about India's importance to Washington.

The Hindu also reported that US President Donald Trump, speaking to reporters in the Oval Office on Tuesday (October 20, 2026), said the India–US trade deal would be 'much different'. Bloomberg reported that Washington wants India's Reliance to refine more Venezuelan oil. BBC Hindi reported that, during his India visit, Rubio said Pakistan is a country with which the United States must maintain a relationship. None of the reports seen said a trade agreement had been signed, and the final terms of the deal were not published.

Delhi High Court agrees to urgent hearing on pleas by CJP's Saurav Das and Ashutosh Ranka

The Hindu reported that the Delhi High Court agreed to urgently hear pleas by Saurav Das and Ashutosh Ranka of the CJP, along with a plea by Neha of the AISA. The paper said Das and Ranka have challenged the SEM's October 19 order remanding them to two days' judicial custody. BBC Hindi reported that CJP leaders Abhijit Dipke, Ashutosh Ranka and Saurav Das were arrested in connection with separate protest cases, and carried an explainer on the legal manoeuvring behind the extension of their custody. Headlines in top-news roundups said Delhi Police sought a three-day custody extension for Das and Ranka, and that a Delhi court ordered their release after the ACP accepted sureties. The reports seen did not give a single confirmed timeline for these steps.

Environment · Delhi-NCR✓ 1 outlet · single named source for the order; full list of curbs not verified

GRAP-1 curbs imposed in Delhi-NCR as air quality deteriorates to 'poor'

Livemint reported that the Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM) has invoked Stage-I of the Graded Response Action Plan, or GRAP, after Delhi's Air Quality Index (AQI) deteriorated to the 'poor' category. The curbs apply across Delhi-NCR.

A separate roundup headline on the measures said they include no tandoor at eateries and a ban on open burning of waste. Another summary described the package as the list of curbs under Grap-1. Livemint's report did not, in the portion seen, give the exact AQI reading that triggered the order.

SBI probes 3,897 customer records on the dark web as eight eMudhra web security keys leak

The Hindu reported that State Bank of India (SBI) is probing the dark web exposure of nearly 3,900 customer records. A preliminary analysis of around 3.75 lakh records (375,000) available on the dark web identified 3,897 SBI customer records, the paper said.

In a separate report, The Hindu said eight web security keys of eMudhra, a firm whose certificates are used by the government and PSUs, were leaked. Many government websites, including those of the Aadhaar issuer UIDAI and the income tax filing portal, use eMudhra certificates, the paper reported. How the data and keys were obtained, and whether any customer suffered losses, was not stated in the reports seen.
