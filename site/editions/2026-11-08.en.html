Indiscriminate shooting in Kraków market square… 6 dead, 15 injured; interior minister cites signs suspect was “drawn to far-right ideology”

A man in military clothing drove into the square and opened fire at about 3 p.m. on the 7th, killing 6 and injuring 15.

A shooting took place at about 3 p.m. on the 7th (local time) in the central market square of Kraków, Poland’s second-largest city. A man who drove into the square, wearing military clothing, fired indiscriminately, and the toll so far is 6 dead, 4 women and 2 men, and 15 injured. The first bulletin reported 5 dead and 12 injured, but the count rose afterward. Together the dead and injured come to 21. The market square is a leading tourist attraction in the middle of the Old Town, and the fact that it happened in a public place at the heart of the city added to the shock. Prime Minister Donald Tusk headed to Kraków after hearing the news.

On the motive, Poland’s interior minister said there were signs the suspect was ‘drawn to’ far-right ideology. This is, however, an account from the early stage of the investigation. The suspect’s identity, the specific motive and the final custody status have not yet been confirmed, and it has not been disclosed how many of the 15 injured are seriously hurt. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky condemned the shooting and conveyed condolences to the victims’ families, and Tom Rose, the US ambassador to Poland, wrote on X that it was a horrific mass shooting. Further changes in the investigation and in the counts of dead and injured are expected to follow as Polish authorities announce them.

People Power Party calls for President Lee’s impeachment at street rally in central Seoul… Democratic Party hits back: “shielding political prosecutors”

The People Power Party held a street rally in central Seoul on the 7th and denounced the ‘fabricated-prosecution special counsel act’ that passed the National Assembly the day before. The bill passed the plenary session on the 6th with 167 in favor and 1 against, and the People Power Party did not take part in the vote. At the rally, claims were made that President Lee Jae-myung should be impeached, and Rep. Han Dong-hoon called the law the ‘Lee Jae-myung privilege act.’ A street rally is a political rally held on the streets outside the National Assembly, a way for an opposition party that could not block a bill by a vote inside the Assembly to appeal to public opinion. The Democratic Party asked the same day what on earth the People Power Party was taking to the streets for, and criticized the rally as an attempt to shield the political prosecutors of the Yoon Suk Yeol government. President Lee said on the 7th that praise and criticism of politicians are necessary but must not lead to religious adulation or worship. Attendance at the rally has not been confirmed, and nothing has been decided about how long the People Power Party will continue its street campaign.

Houthis claim “27 killed in Saudi airstrike near Sanaa, Yemen”… Saudi Arabia issues no statement

Yemen’s Houthi rebels claimed on the 7th that the village of al-Shariya near the capital Sanaa was hit by a Saudi Arabian airstrike and that 27 people died. Houthi health ministry spokesperson Anis al-Asbahi first said 22 had died, and the count later rose to 27. The Saudi-led coalition did not comment on the claim. The death toll and who carried out the airstrike have not been independently confirmed.

The Houthis and Saudi Arabia are trading accusations over responsibility for an attack on a market in southern Yemen the day before. The Houthis claimed to have struck a Saudi international airport and Aramco oil storage tanks, and the Saudi-led coalition said it intercepted one drone near Mecca, the Islamic holy city. Saudi civil defense issued danger warnings for the southern areas of Jazan and Najran, then announced that the danger had passed.

Samsung beats kt 10-5 in Korean Series Game 4… three straight wins after a loss, one win from first title in 12 years

The Samsung Lions beat the kt wiz 10-5 in Game 4 of the Korean Series (best of seven, first to four wins) at Daegu Samsung Lions Park on the 7th. After losing Game 1 7-8, they won Games 2 through 4 to lead the series 3 wins to 1. They are one win from their ninth title overall, and the first in 12 years since the combined title of 2014. Game 5 is played at the same venue on the 8th. Samsung sends its homegrown ace Won Tae-in, and kt sends foreign pitcher Daniel Davis after four days of rest. If Samsung wins, the title is clinched.

World Food Price Index falls 1.2% to 133.9 in October… first decline in 4 months, sugar up 6.6%

On the 7th the Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs said the October World Food Price Index of the United Nations Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) was 133.9 points, down 1.2% from the previous month (135.6 points). The rise that had continued since June was broken after 4 months. Cereal prices fell as wheat and corn prices dropped, and meat and dairy fell too. By contrast, prices of vegetable oils and sugar rose, and sugar was up 6.6%.

The FAO surveys international prices of 24 items and publishes the index every month by commodity group (cereals, meat, dairy, vegetable oils and sugar), calculating it with the 2014–2016 average price set at 100. A figure of 133.9 means international food prices are nearly 34% higher than in that period. The 1.2% fall is not large, and the direction differs from group to group.
