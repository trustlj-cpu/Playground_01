Markets · BondsPress ✓ 3 outlets · levels according to CNBC, Handelsblatt and Bloomberg

Long-term bond yields surge in the US and UK, and European stocks fall by more than 1%

The yield on ten-year US Treasury bonds reached 5.35%, the highest since 2002, and that on 30-year British bonds 6.036%, the highest since 1998. The Fed minutes show that most participants expect one more rate rise this year.

The yield on ten-year US Treasury bonds rose about eight basis points to near 5.35% on Wednesday, the highest level since 2002, according to CNBC. The $39 billion auction of ten-year bonds closed at a yield of 5.300%, the highest for this maturity since November 2000, but with solid demand. In London, the 30-year yield rose as high as 6.036%, the highest since January 1998, and the FTSE 100 closed 0.79% lower, at 10,458.50 points (Bloomberg). In Frankfurt, the Dax lost 1.35%, to 25,104.36 points, and the Euro Stoxx 50 fell 1.47%, to 6,180.29 (Handelsblatt).

The minutes of the Fed’s September meeting show that the quarter-point rise, to 3.75%–4.00%, was unanimous and that most participants foresee one more rise before the end of the year; futures put the probability of another rise at the October 28 meeting at about 20%. Pressure also comes from oil, back near $100 a barrel. On Wall Street, the S&P 500 slipped 0.22% to 7,801.77 points. DailyDrop did not see the PSI close or Portuguese bond yields.

Sport · National teamPress ✓ 4 outlets · Jesus’s reaction according to the press

Jesus read Ronaldo’s statement but refuses to respond; tension with the captain continues

Jorge Jesus, the national team coach, confirmed on Wednesday that he had read Cristiano Ronaldo’s long text, but avoided responding to the accusations. He said only that, as every day, he was going to the federation to work and to think about the team and the national side, and that he had nothing more to say (RTP, Portal Tela, Gazeta Esportiva). The international press speaks of lingering tension, despite the player’s apology (La Tribuna).

The Portuguese Football Federation has so far announced no sanction. The disciplinary regulations provide for between one and six months’ suspension for anyone who abandons the national team. A Portuguese television station is said to have stated that Ronaldo will not return while Jesus is the coach, according to dgabc; DailyDrop has not seen confirmation of this version, and the player himself wrote that he is available after serving the punishment.

Nobel in Chemistry goes to Henri Kagan and Kenso Soai for nonlinear effects and autocatalysis in asymmetric synthesis

The Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences on Wednesday, October 7, awarded the 2026 Nobel Prize in Chemistry in equal shares to Henri B. Kagan, of Paris-Sud University in France, and Kenso Soai, of Tokyo University of Science in Japan, for the discovery of nonlinear effects and of autocatalysis in asymmetric organic synthesis (Nobel Committee, Khaleej Times, Ynet, UNB).

In simple terms, the work shows how a small initial imbalance between two mirror-image forms of a molecule, known as chirality, can be amplified by a chemical reaction. This matters because the two versions can behave very differently in the body, for example in medicines. Each prize is worth 12 million Swedish kronor, about $1.2 million.

World · FrancePress ✓ 3 outlets · figures from the interior minister

France suspends stun grenades at high-school protests after a teenager loses a hand

French Interior Minister Laurent Nuñez announced on Wednesday, on RTL, that he is suspending the use of stun grenades at high-school protests “until it is clarified” what happened in Lens on Monday, where a 15-year-old lost his left hand (Le Monde, Les Échos, Le Figaro). The General Inspectorate of the National Police is investigating 18 cases of injuries during the movement.

According to the minister, there have been 6,547 arrests since the protests began, 488 on Tuesday alone, and more than 700 police officers and gendarmes injured. The exact circumstances of the explosion have yet to be established. Organizations of high-school and university students are asking to be received by Prime Minister Sébastien Lecornu, who will present the first decisions “at the end of the month.”

World · IranPress ✓ 2 outlets · statements by the US vice president

Vance says Iran must reduce uranium enrichment “significantly,” but not to zero

US Vice President J. D. Vance told Reuters that Iran must “significantly” reduce its capacity for uranium enrichment for Washington to agree to end the war that began on February 28, and asked why a country that does not want the bomb would need fuel enriched to 60% (CBS News, The Independent). Before the war, Trump demanded “zero enrichment,” so several outlets read the remark as a retreat.

A senior Iranian official said the ideas do not match Tehran’s demands, but admitted discussing enrichment levels if the US lifts the blockade of Iranian ports. Secretary of State Marco Rubio said Tehran “lost complete control” of the Strait of Hormuz; Vance added that Iranian forces can still attack ships, but not stop energy exports. Brent was trading near $100.
