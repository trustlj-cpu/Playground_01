World · International justicePress ✓ 5 outlets · sanctions announced by Washington

Nobel Peace Prize for Navi Pillay, former ICC judge; hours later, the US sanctions the court itself

The 85-year-old South African jurist was honored on Friday by the Nobel committee. Secretary of State Marco Rubio announced sanctions on the International Criminal Court, and eight US-allied countries signed a statement of support for the court.

The Norwegian Nobel Committee awarded the 2026 Nobel Peace Prize to Navi Pillay, a South African, former judge of the International Criminal Court and former UN High Commissioner for Human Rights, who led the Rwanda genocide tribunal and a UN commission that accused Israel of genocide in Gaza (Diário de Pernambuco, Folha de Pernambuco, Reuters). Hours later, Marco Rubio announced sanctions on the ICC, which he called a “dishonest court.” The measures go further than the earlier ones against more than a dozen judges and may penalize companies that provide services to the court; the reports link them to the arrest warrants against Benjamin Netanyahu and Yoav Gallant.

The ICC called the sanctions an attack on the rule of law, and the prosecutor said the work continues. The foreign ministers of the United Kingdom, Canada, Denmark, France, Germany, Italy, Japan and the Netherlands issued a statement of support; Portugal, a party to the Rome Statute, is not on the list, and DailyDrop saw no Portuguese reaction. Netanyahu accused the Nobel committee of bias and called Pillay “sectarian” (Folha de Pernambuco).

Energy · RussiaPress ✓ 5 outlets · volumes according to Trump and Moscow

Trump announces Russian diesel for the world market after talking to Putin; Zelensky calls it an “investment in the war”

The US president said on Friday, October 9, that he had spoken with Vladimir Putin and that Russia will release 300,000 tonnes of diesel immediately to the US and world markets, another 500,000 in November, 1 million after that and 3 million “soon,” depending on Russian refineries (Reuters, AP, BBC, CBS, Washington Post). Russian Deputy Prime Minister Alexander Novak said the restrictions on diesel exports were lifted immediately and placed the 1-million-tonne tranche in December.

The US Treasury issued a temporary general license for diesel of Russian origin valid until April 7, 2027. The Kremlin said the 90-minute call centered on Ukraine and touched on Iran, and that Putin will consider when to resume peace talks. Zelensky called the announcement an “investment in the war,” and Axios wrote that Kyiv was caught by surprise. Brent closed at $104.72 (+0.42%) and WTI at $91.85 (+0.39%).

Politics · BudgetPress · one outlet (RTP)

Government praises “solid accounts” in Budget; Chega defers its vote and the PS doubts two conditions

After the Government delivered the proposal for the 2027 State Budget, the Executive praised the “solid accounts” and the opposition criticized it (RTP). Chega leader André Ventura said the Government had presented a “bad budget,” that the party is not the Government’s “crutch,” and that how it will vote will be decided “in due course.” Chega will table “many amendment proposals,” notably on tolls, VAT on fuel and the VAT exemption on essential foods.

The Socialist Party (PS) expressed doubts over whether the Government met two of the conditions it set for allowing the Budget to pass, but did not call into question its abstention in the vote in principle, scheduled for October 28. The final overall vote is on November 24. DailyDrop saw this coverage in only one outlet and could not confirm which two conditions are in doubt.

World · Middle EastPress ✓ 5 outlets · deaths according to Saudi authorities

Houthi attacks on Riyadh airport leave three dead; European aviation safety agency widens its warning

Saudi authorities said three people died in attacks claimed by the Houthis on Riyadh airport (Reuters, AP). The European Union Aviation Safety Agency widened its warning on Saudi airspace after Lufthansa and Indian airlines suspended flights to Riyadh. At the UN Security Council, the United Kingdom condemned the Houthis’ escalation “unequivocally.”

FinancialJuice wrote that a Saudi-led coalition said it is conducting a large-scale operation against Houthi forces in Yemen. Separately, Reuters and the Guardian reported that the Emirates’ prosecutor’s office says a flydubai co-pilot planned a suicide attack on a flight to Tel Aviv. Casualty tolls may change.

Environment · USPress ✓ 4 outlets · damage yet to be assessed

Hurricane Isaias makes landfall on Florida’s coast and about three quarters of Gulf oil production is halted

Hurricane Isaias hit the coast near Destin, Florida, at about 8:30 p.m. local time on Friday, as a Category 2 hurricane with winds of 170 km/h; earlier it had reached Category 3, with winds of up to 190 km/h (NBC, PBS, CBS). NPR called it the first hurricane of the Atlantic season. The National Hurricane Center warned of life-threatening storm surge, extreme winds and possible tornadoes. Florida and Alabama declared a state of emergency and several counties ordered evacuations.

Bloomberg reported that almost three quarters of oil production in the Gulf of Mexico was halted, and CBS said Chevron and Shell were evacuating platforms. No confirmed count of damage, injuries or deaths was found. For Portugal, the immediate effect is the oil price, which was already above $100.
