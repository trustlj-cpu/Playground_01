Korea Investment Savings Bank hack leaks personal data of 86,433 customers… largest in the savings bank sector

The company announced that abnormal external access to its mobile website began on the night of the 22nd, and the number of savings banks with confirmed hack damage since September has risen to seven.

A hacking incident at Korea Investment Savings Bank, a subsidiary of Korea Investment Holdings, has leaked the personal data of 86,433 customers. On the 25th the company announced that there were two rounds of abnormal external access to its mobile website, from 10:42 p.m. on the 22nd to 10:02 a.m. on the 25th, and that customer information was taken in the process. A savings bank is a second-tier financial company with a lower threshold than a commercial bank, and many customers with middle or low credit ratings use it. This is the largest confirmed leak of personal data in the savings bank sector. From the night of the 22nd, when access first began, to the morning of the 25th, when the last access ended, is a little over 59 hours. Yegaram Savings Bank, which had suffered the most before, said the number affected was estimated at about 40,000, so this scale is roughly double that. The chain of hacks across the financial sector surfaced at the end of September when a hacker’s access to Yegaram Savings Bank’s server was confirmed, and damage reports then spread to commercial banks, capital companies and Saemaul Geumgo. With this case, seven savings banks have had a security breach confirmed since September.

In the same period OK Savings Bank confirmed that the information of 518 customers had gone out, and Hana Savings Bank 552. The number of victims at Korea Investment Savings Bank is more than 150 times that of these two. Among commercial banks, Shinhan Bank has confirmed a leak of about 25,000, and KB Kookmin Bank and Hana Bank 119 and 89 respectively. The mobile website is not an app but a site reached through a phone’s web browser, and the company identified it as the point where the external access took place. Even basic information such as names and contact details can be used in calls and texts impersonating a financial institution, so which items went out is the key question, but the range of leaked items has not been confirmed. The hacker’s identity, the intrusion route and whether financial authorities have begun an inspection are also not yet known.

Treasury bond yields fall across the board a day before rate meeting, 3-year at 3.583%… Bank of Korea to decide on base rate hike on the 26th

The Monetary Policy Board of the Bank of Korea decides on the 26th whether to raise the base rate. On the 25th, a day before, Treasury bond yields fell across the board, and the 3-year, the benchmark bond, fell to 3.583%. A fall in bond yields means bond prices rose. This is the last Monetary Policy Board meeting of the year, and securities firms have forecast the base rate rising to 3.25%. SK Securities expected one more rise, to 3.50% in February next year. On the same day the KOSPI closed down 6.16% at 4,697.51, but neither the Bank of Korea nor the government has said what caused bond yields to fall. When the rate goes up, interest on floating-rate loans increases and there is room for deposit rates to rise. One survey of 23 economists was reported to show 21 expecting a hike, but the same figure was not confirmed elsewhere. The Bank of Korea’s explanation of whether it hiked, by how much, how the board members voted and the path of rates from here will be released on the 26th.

Director Roh So-young files written statement refusing Chairman Chey Tae-won’s request for a settlement recommendation… “If sincere, withdraw the final appeal at once”

On the 25th the side of Roh So-young, director of Art Center Nabi, submitted to the court a written statement saying in effect that it does not agree to the request for a settlement recommendation from the side of Chey Tae-won, chairman of SK Group. In a statement, Roh’s lawyer said that if Chairman Chey is sincere about an early resolution, a withdrawal of the final appeal at once is what is hoped for. Chairman Chey had filed a written statement on the 23rd with Division 2 of the Supreme Court (presiding Justice Kim Seong-su), which is handling the second appeal hearing in the property division case, and this refusal is the reply. On the same day Chairman Chey reacted by saying in effect that the sincerity was in doubt, and said the intent had been belittled and left a heavy heart.

A settlement recommendation is a procedure in which the court, rather than ending the case with a judgment, puts forward a proposal or urges the two sides to reach an agreement. If one side does not agree, it is hard for it to take hold. If the final appeal is withdrawn, the Supreme Court makes no further ruling and the appellate judgment is finalized as it stands. The specific settlement terms and amount have not been confirmed, and the court has not said whether it will make a settlement recommendation or when it will deliver a ruling.

Public Prosecution Service seeks arrest warrant for Pastor Jeon Kwang-hoon… on suspicion of donating 10.2 billion won of church money to the Liberty Unification Party

On the 25th the Seoul Northern District Public Prosecution Service sought an arrest warrant for Pastor Jeon Kwang-hoon of Sarang Jeil Church. Pastor Jeon is suspected of donating about 10.2 billion won of church funds to the Liberty Unification Party. Earlier, the anti-corruption unit of the Seoul Metropolitan Police Agency’s wide-area investigation division applied for the warrant on the 23rd, and the Public Prosecution Service said it would hold an interview with the suspect from 10 a.m. on the 25th, before seeking the arrest warrant, and decide whether to seek it after reviewing the interview result and the police investigation record. Pastor Jeon appeared at the office at about 9:43 a.m. that day, and charges including violation of the Political Funds Act were applied.

A warrant being sought does not mean detention has been decided. The court holds a warrant review hearing and decides whether to issue it by weighing how well the suspicion is substantiated and the risk of flight or destruction of evidence. The Political Funds Act in principle bars corporations and organizations from donating political funds, so the issue is whether giving church funds to a political party falls under this. There was a report that Pastor Jeon said in the interview that being a retired pastor, there was no awareness of it, but this was not confirmed elsewhere. The court’s review schedule is not known.

Security · National Assembly✓ 3 outlets · plenary vote schedule not confirmed

Cheonghae Unit deployment extension motion clears National Assembly Defense Committee subcommittee… President Lee: “We will widen the mission but keep to the principle of non-involvement in war”

The revised motion to extend the Cheonghae Unit’s dispatch to Somali waters and the open sea south of Oman cleared the National Assembly Defense Committee’s bill subcommittee on the 25th. The core of it is widening the area of operations from the Gulf of Aden off Somalia to the sea south of Oman, and the motion spells out non-involvement in war, top priority on the safety of service members and compliance with the principle of self-defense. On the 25th President Lee Jae-myung said: “We will widen the Cheonghae Unit’s mission to secure the safety of our ships and sea lanes, but we will clearly keep to the principle of non-involvement in war.”

By the president’s explanation, the Strait of Hormuz and its vicinity are excluded from the Cheonghae Unit’s area of activity. President Lee said there must not be the slightest lapse in protecting the lives and safety of the people, and that securing the sea lanes that are the economy’s lifeline and the safety of ships is also an important duty of the state. A deployment extension motion normally has to pass a full standing committee meeting and a plenary vote, so clearing the subcommittee is the first gate. The schedules for the Defense Committee’s full meeting and the plenary session are not known.
