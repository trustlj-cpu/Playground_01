Houthi missile strike on Riyadh airport kills at least 12 and wounds more than 300, Saudi authorities say

The attack on Saturday closed the airport and set off flight cancellations across the region. Donald Trump condemned it and said he is weighing whether to join Saudi strikes on the Houthis.

At least 12 people were killed and more than 300 wounded when a missile hit Riyadh's airport on Saturday 10 October, Saudi authorities said, according to CBS News, NPR and ABC News. The Houthis of Yemen claimed the attack. Al Jazeera and the New York Times described it as the second attack on King Khalid International Airport in a week; DW and the Financial Times reported that Saudi Arabia suspended operations at the airport, and early reports from several outlets had put the injured at "dozens", before the toll rose.

NPR reported that Trump is weighing joining the fight against the Houthis, and El Universal that he called the attack "unacceptable". Airlines cancelled flights to Riyadh, the Economic Times reported that Air India and Air India Express extended cancellations, and Spiegel reported that the German government advised against flights over Riyadh. This is a separate and larger attack than the one on 7 to 9 October in which Saudi authorities counted three dead. For Kenya, the effects are on air travel through Gulf hubs and on oil, with the Energy and Petroleum Regulatory Authority's next price review due on Wednesday 14 October.

Russian glide bombs kill at least 15 in Zaporizhzhia hours after Trump's diesel deal with Putin

A Russian glide-bomb attack on Zaporizhzhia killed at least 15 people on Saturday, according to the BBC, with CBS News, Al Jazeera and ANSA also reporting deaths, after Trump announced a deal for Russia to supply diesel. The toll climbed through the day: early reports spoke of 5 to 7 dead, then 11, 12, 15 and finally 17 in Italian reports. Ukrainian president Volodymyr Zelenskyy wrote on Telegram that Russia "thanked" the US for easing diesel sanctions with the bombing, and Ukraine struck a Russian fuel site hours after the deal, Al Jazeera reported.

The Mirror and CBS also reported that Trump signed the deal after Zelenskyy ignored his requests to stop attacks on refineries. For Kenya the link is diesel: if the deal eases world supply, it could lower costs; if it is judged to breach sanctions, it could be reversed. DailyDrop has not seen any independent estimate of how much diesel would reach East Africa.

Mazingira Day: Kenyans plant trees on a public holiday, with national celebrations at Endebess

Kenya marked Mazingira Day on Saturday 10 October, a public holiday, with national celebrations at Endebess in Trans Nzoia County, according to Citizen Digital, Capital FM, the Kenya News Agency and People Daily. Before the day, Citizen Digital reported that the government wanted to mobilise 200,000 Kenyans to plant six million trees in one hour, and that more than 17 million trees were targeted for Mazingira Week. The Kenya News Agency said Trans Nzoia alone aimed for 85,000.

The targets differ between reports, so DailyDrop does not give a single number. It has not seen a final tally of trees planted. The Nairobi Securities Exchange was closed for the holiday and for the weekend, so NASI and the shilling last traded on Friday 9 October.
