South African jurist Navi Pillay wins the 2026 Nobel Peace Prize

The Norwegian Nobel Committee honoured her for promoting peace and international law. Donald Trump posted about the committee on Truth Social the same day.

The 2026 Nobel Peace Prize went to Navanethem "Navi" Pillay, a South African human rights lawyer and former judge of the International Criminal Court, on Friday 9 October, according to CBS News, ABC News, NPR and DW. The Norwegian Nobel Committee cited her efforts to promote peace and international law. Pillay dedicated the prize to survivors of international crimes, El Economista reported, and El Universal reported that she criticised attacks on the court.

Trump reacted the same day: he posted about the committee on Truth Social, and El Universal reported that he said he deserved the prize. For Kenya and the continent, the prize puts an African jurist and the International Criminal Court, which has dealt with Kenyan cases in the past, in the global spotlight. DailyDrop has seen reaction reports only as headlines and summaries, not the committee's full citation.

Three Saudis killed, a pilot among them, in Houthi attacks on Riyadh airport; airlines suspend flights

Saudi authorities said three Saudi nationals, including a pilot, were killed in Houthi attacks on Riyadh airport, according to AP, the BBC, The New York Times, CNBC and The Guardian. A Saudia aircraft was reported hit, and CNBC said the Saudi-led coalition vowed a "firm" response. The Guardian reported that airlines suspended flights to the capital; Indian carriers IndiGo, Air India Express and Akasa cancelled Riyadh flights, per Hindustan Times.

This is the earlier attack of 7 to 9 October. DailyDrop will report any later strike separately. Kenyans travelling through Saudi airports should check with their airline and with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and oil traders are watching for further escalation.

Trump says Russia will supply 300,000 tons of diesel; Brent eases but pump prices have not yet followed

President Trump posted on Truth Social on Friday 9 October that Russia will immediately supply over 300,000 tons of diesel to the American and global market. Reuters reported that Trump's moves to boost diesel supplies have not yet lowered prices, The Guardian's business live blog that oil fell as Trump paused Iran attacks and China restarted fuel exports, and ANSA that Brent slipped below US$103 a barrel, down 1.2%.

WTI nevertheless closed Friday 0.39% higher at US$91.85, the S&P 500 rose 0.59% to 7,811.54 and gold rose 1.43% to US$4,216.3. For Kenya, diesel drives transport and food costs, and the monthly Energy and Petroleum Regulatory Authority review is on the 14th. DailyDrop has not seen how much Russian diesel could reach East Africa.
