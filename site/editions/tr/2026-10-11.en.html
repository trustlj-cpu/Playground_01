At least 12 killed in Russian strike on Zaporizhzhia; Zelensky ties attack to the day after Trump eased diesel sanctions

Three children are among the dead. The Kremlin said Putin relayed to Trump Iran’s proposal to end the conflict; the proposal was not disclosed.

At least 12 people were killed and 14 wounded in a Russian attack on the Ukrainian industrial city of Zaporizhzhia on Saturday; Reuters and AP reported that three children were among the dead (The Globe and Mail, Cyprus Mail, The Star, TRT World). One report, from JPost, gave a death toll of 17; this figure does not match other agency reports, and DailyDrop uses 12. The attack came a day after Trump announced a diesel deal with Putin and a temporary US suspension of sanctions on Russian diesel.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky wrote on Telegram that Russia had marked the easing of sanctions with a “thank you” of five glide bombs dropped on residential buildings; he said the deal, made while peace talks continue, had surprised Kyiv. Zelensky’s words are one side’s comment; a causal link between the attack and the deal has not been proven. Trump had justified the deal as a way to lower high fuel prices.

On Saturday the Kremlin said that in his phone call with Trump, Putin had relayed Iran’s views on ending the war; Peskov said Putin was acting in agreement with President Pezeshkian. Putin aide Ushakov said that the offer to take Iran’s highly enriched uranium to Russia was on the table. The details of Iran’s proposal were not disclosed; Washington’s response is also unknown (CNBC, WION).

World · GulfPress ✓ 5 outlets · limited Saudi confirmation

New attack reported at Riyadh’s King Khalid Airport, flights halted; Houthis claim responsibility

The Saudi Civil Aviation Authority said on Saturday that operations at King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh had been halted after an attack that caused injuries; all flights to Riyadh were diverted or cancelled (JPost, Deccan Herald, Euronews). Euronews noted that Saudi officials had not confirmed a new attack or an evacuation; in single-source reports, casualties are not certain.

In Thursday’s Houthi attack, three people died and 36 were injured, according to The National; the affected services include flights to Istanbul. Air India, flydubai, the Lufthansa Group and PIA had suspended flights to Saudi airports; the US, Canadian, German and Spanish embassies urged their citizens to stay away from the airport. The Houthis declared Saudi airspace a military operations zone after an offensive launched on Monday by the Saudi-backed Yemeni government.

Economy · Fund investigationPress ✓ 6 outlets · report contents not disclosed

SPK audit report on Fatma Betül Sayan Kaya and her husband İlyas Kaya sent to prosecutors

According to reports, the Capital Markets Board (SPK) completed its audit report on former AK Party Deputy Chair Fatma Betül Sayan Kaya and her husband İlyas Kaya and sent it to the Istanbul Chief Public Prosecutor’s Office (Karadeniz Gazete, Line Haber, Karar, Memurlar.Net, Yatırımx, Elips Haber). The report has not been shared with the public. According to the statements relayed, İlyas Kaya bought about 150,000 lots and Fatma Betül Sayan Kaya about 250,000 lots of Özata Denizcilik shares in April 2026 and sold them in August-September; the couple’s gain was relayed as about 2 billion lira.

The couple argued that nobody had directed their share purchases and that no inside information was given. The application of the couple, whose accounts were frozen, under the SPK’s voluntary payment method was examined, and the Chief Public Prosecutor’s Office allowed a payment to be sent to the voluntary refund account of the Savings Deposit Insurance Fund (TMSF). AK Party Secretary General Eyyüp Kadir İnan had announced that Kaya’s party membership had ended. MHP leader Bahçeli’s criticism of accusations made before the investigation is concluded appeared in an agenda summary; DailyDrop did not see this in a second source.

Politics · Local governmentPress ✓ 6 outlets · claims attributed to Gökçek

People’s Alliance candidate Serdar Orhan wins Ataşehir acting mayor election with 18 votes; scuffle breaks out in session the CHP and Yeni Parti groups did not attend

The Ataşehir Municipality council in Istanbul held an acting mayor election on Saturday; Serdar Orhan, the joint candidate of the AK Party and MHP, received 18 votes as the sole candidate, and two ballots were blank (Cumhuriyet, Habertürk, TRT Haber, Sondakika, Vatan, BirGün). The CHP and Yeni Parti groups did not attend the voting, which lasted four rounds; in the fourth round a scuffle broke out in front of the District Election Board. The sources differ on the details of the first rounds: Cumhuriyet and Sondakika write that no absolute majority was reached in the first three rounds, while Habertürk writes 18 votes in the first round.

The election was held after the arrest of the previous acting mayor, Murat Güneş. Mayor Onursal Adıgüzel had been arrested on April 22, and Güneş had been elected on April 30 as the CHP candidate with 22 votes. Former Ankara Metropolitan Mayor Melih Gökçek claimed that Orhan was elected through threats and inducements; this claim is Gökçek’s own and is not verified. Vatan wrote that the municipality had passed to the AK Party.

Agenda · Official GazettePress ✓ 5 outlets · text of the regulation not seen

Official Gazette carries age-verification regulation for children on social media, a rise in İller Bankası capital to 250 billion lira and railway passenger rights

The Official Gazette dated October 10 published a regulation governing services for children and age verification by social network providers; according to one report, services cannot be provided to children who have not turned 15, but the sources give the detail differently (Gazete Merhaba, Yeni Şafak, Gündem Sivas, Kayseri Anadolu Haber). DailyDrop did not see the text of the regulation.

Presidential Decision 11856 raised İller Bankası’s capital to 250 billion lira. The regulation on railway passenger rights was amended; it was also decided that TCDD Taşımacılık will carry humanitarian aid to Afghanistan by rail. The Gazette also contains urgent expropriation decisions for energy projects, an amendment to the school food communiqué and the cancellation of the operating licence of Parakolay Elektronik Para A.Ş.
