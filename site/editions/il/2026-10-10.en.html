UAE: flydubai co-pilot planned a suicide attack at the terminal at Ben Gurion Airport, after two reconnaissance flights

The UAE public prosecution also said the suspect met the company’s security requirements and, by his own admission, acted alone. The investigation into possible accomplices continues.

On October 9, the Attorney General of the United Arab Emirates, Hamad Saif Al Shamsi, said that the 29-year-old co-pilot from Oman on Flight FZ1073 planned to seize the plane and attack it in a suicide attack at the passenger terminal at Ben Gurion Airport. Xinhua, ynet, Haaretz, Anadolu, TBS and Gulf News reported this. According to the reports, he used two earlier flights to Tel Aviv to study the route and the airport, and struck the captain with an emergency axe in the cockpit.

According to Xinhua, the suspect met all professional and security requirements, including a valid pilot’s license and background checks, with no signs of extremist views or plans. He worked for several companies, among them Oman Air and Royal Air Maroc, before joining flydubai in January 2026. ynet reported that he confessed to acting alone, and Gulf News wrote that the signs of extremism appeared in 2017 while he was studying in Australia.

The UAE prosecution is the only source for the content of the confession, and DailyDrop has not seen the material itself. The finding partly contradicts earlier reports that he had been flagged in Oman as dangerous, and the gap between the accounts has not yet been explained. flydubai has suspended its flights to Tel Aviv until further notice, according to its CEO.

Nobel Peace Prize to Navi Pillay; hours later the US imposed sanctions on the International Criminal Court

On October 9, the Norwegian Nobel Committee awarded the Nobel Peace Prize to South African judge Navi Pillay, who chaired the UN commission that determined that Israel committed genocide in Gaza, for her efforts to promote peace and international law. CNN and Al Jazeera reported this. Israel sharply criticized the decision, the Times of Israel reported.

Hours later, US Secretary of State Marco Rubio announced sanctions on the International Criminal Court as an institution, saying: “Either the court stops the threats, or we will stop it.” NBC News and Al Jazeera reported; the court rejected the sanctions and said its work would continue. Trump posted criticism of the Nobel Committee on Truth Social.

TA-35 falls 0.69% to a one-month low, led by banks, financials and insurance

The TA-35 index closed on Friday down 0.69% to a one-month low, led by declines in bank, financial and insurance stocks, Investing.com reported. The index level was not published in the sources found, and calculated from the October 8 close (4,089.94) it stands at about 4,062 points. Breadth was positive: 281 stocks rose against 174 that fell, and Mega Or was the best on the index, up about 2.96%.

The representative rate published by the Bank of Israel for October 9 is 3.0550 shekels to the dollar, and the market rate on Investing.com closed at 3.0611. The Bank of Israel’s next decision on the interest rate is expected on October 21, and the rate stands at 3.25%.

Houthi attack on Riyadh airport kills three; airlines cancel flights, and agencies warn against flying over Saudi Arabia

The Houthis attacked Riyadh airport on October 8, and according to Saudi Arabia three people were killed, among them a pilot, Al Jazeera, CNN and UPI reported. The airport reopened on October 9. The European aviation safety agency EASA and the advisory body OPS Group warned airlines against using Saudi airspace, and Lufthansa suspended flights to Riyadh until October 16.

The governments of Britain, France and Germany advised avoiding non-essential travel to Riyadh. DailyDrop has not seen a travel warning from Israel’s Foreign Ministry on the matter, and the sources found contain no confirmation of a direct link to Israel.
