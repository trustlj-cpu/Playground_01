Three years since October 7: memorial ceremonies at more than 400 sites, and the dispute over responsibility enters the final weeks of the campaign

The siren sounded at 6:29, and the main civil ceremony was held in Tel Aviv. The debate over reports that Netanyahu was warned in advance continues; he denies it.

On Wednesday, October 7, Israel marked the third anniversary of the massacre. According to CNN, ceremonies, memorial events and protests were held at more than 400 sites, and the day opened with a national moment of silence at the hour the attack began. Haaretz and Israel Hayom reported that thousands gathered in Yarkon Park in Tel Aviv for a civil ceremony, and that CNN mentioned tens of thousands of participants. President Isaac Herzog spoke at the site of the Nova festival.

Prime Minister Netanyahu lit a memorial candle in his Jerusalem office and, according to Haaretz’s live blog, vowed to settle accounts with all the murderers. At the same time, Israelis were debating, NPR wrote, reports that Netanyahu had been warned before the massacre. He denied them and called them fake news. The link between the debate and the October 27 election is conspicuous, because the opposition has pledged to set up a state commission of inquiry.

An analysis piece in Haaretz said that three years after the massacre Israel has yet to begin confronting its failure; this is the writer’s position, not a factual finding. The election will be held in 19 days.

Channel 13 poll: Netanyahu bloc 55 seats to the Eisenkot bloc’s 50, and neither side reaches 61

A Channel 13 poll published on the evening of October 7 gave the Netanyahu bloc 55 of 120 seats, the Eisenkot bloc 50 and the Arab parties 15, Haaretz and the Times of Israel reported. The poll surveyed 1,013 respondents, and the margin of error is 2.5%. A Zman Yisrael poll showed the opposite picture, 55 for the opposition and 51 for the Netanyahu bloc.

The difference between the polls is a reminder that they are not a forecast. Haaretz attributed the opposition’s deficit, among other things, to its refusal to cooperate with the Arab parties. According to the report on the poll, when asked who is better suited to be prime minister, Eisenkot got 45% and Netanyahu 36%.

TA-35 plunges 2.44% to 4,096.45 points amid Fidelity’s sale of Nextvision shares

The TA-35 index closed on Wednesday at 4,096.45 points, down 2.44%, a one-month low. It was the sharpest drop in recent days, and it stemmed mainly from Nextvision, a maker of stabilization systems for aircraft, Investing.com, ad-hoc-news and Globes reported. The stock plunged 11.4% on Wednesday after a 12.7% fall the day before, according to CTech.

The Fidelity fund sold about 5.7 million shares, 65% of its holding, for 1.3 billion shekels. A market source described the sale as technical, stemming from the fund’s policy, and the company said there is no change in its business. The dollar traded at the close around 3.069 shekels. An official representative rate for October 7 was not verified.

Wall Street dips, oil down 1.30% and gold down 1.11%

The S&P 500 closed on Wednesday at 7,801.77 points, down 0.22%, after a record high on Tuesday. WTI oil futures fell 1.30% to $88.28 a barrel, and gold fell 1.11% to $4,140.7 an ounce, after a 0.73% rise the day before.

DailyDrop found no verified explanation for the moves. When the prices of gold and oil fall together, investors sometimes react to expectations about the interest rate or to diplomatic signals, but this is a general assessment, not a finding. Those tracking the data will focus on the employment figures and on statements about the contacts with Iran.

Tanker attack off Qatar: UKMTO reports a hit by multiple projectiles, and Iran declares the Strait of Hormuz closed

A tanker was hit on Wednesday by multiple projectiles about 94 km north of the Qatari coast, within Qatar’s exclusive economic zone, and casualties were reported, the British maritime agency UKMTO said, as published in Arab News, the Tribune and Yahoo. The number of casualties was not given. Two reports identified the vessel as the tanker Akers; UKMTO itself did not name it.

According to UKMTO, nine tanker attacks were reported in the Strait of Hormuz in October, and an adviser to the commander of the Revolutionary Guards said, according to the Fars news agency, that the strait is closed. No one claimed responsibility for the attack, and DailyDrop has not attributed it to any party.

Unverified — what we could not confirm

“Netanyahu was warned before the massacre.” A Haaretz report from late September; Netanyahu denied it and called it fake news, and NPR noted that the public debate continues. There is no independent confirmation.
“Police opened a criminal investigation against senior Transport Ministry officials.” This is a preliminary examination. No one is being questioned under caution, and in one case only disciplinary proceedings may be possible.
“Iran attacked the tanker off Qatar.” UKMTO did not attribute the attack. The Guards adviser’s statement that the strait is closed is not an admission of the attack.
