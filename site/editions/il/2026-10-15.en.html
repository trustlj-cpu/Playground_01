12 days to the election: in Channel 13 News poll the Netanyahu bloc falls to 51 seats, and in Likud they are already talking about a rotation with Eisenkot

Eisenkot’s Yashar! remains the largest party in the poll, with 20 seats, and Likud falls to 18. But neither bloc comes close to 61, and on the right and in the center there is already talk of a joint government.

The Channel 13 News poll published on the evening of October 14 gives the Netanyahu bloc 51 seats. Maariv writes that the bloc lost four seats since the previous poll; Haaretz notes that Likud alone fell by two seats compared with last week and stands at 18. Gadi Eisenkot’s Yashar!, led by the former chief of staff, holds at 20 seats and remains the largest party in the poll. The Eisenkot bloc stands at 50, as in the previous poll, after some of the bloc’s parties declared a boycott of the Arab parties, and it is the boycott that keeps it at that number (Haaretz). A simple calculation shows that neither bloc comes close to 61, a Knesset majority, and that 19 seats remain outside both. From 51, Netanyahu is ten seats short, and Eisenkot, without the Arab parties, eleven.

The poll puts two small lists into the Knesset. Hendel, chair of the Reservists list, passes the electoral threshold, about 3.25% of the votes. The second list appears in two versions: Haaretz notes Winter in its headline, and Maariv and Reshet 13 Zelicha, and the publications available to us do not explain the difference.

At the same time, voices not heard in Likud before were heard there. Minister Miki Zohar said on the Knesset Channel that if Eisenkot leads in seats and in bloc size, he will propose a rotation to him, with Netanyahu serving first because of his role in the regional arena; Maariv writes that this is the first time a senior Likud figure has spoken this way. Israel Hayom adds that in the party they still doubt the idea of a broad government. Srugim attributes to Deri the sentence “I am in favor of a broad government with Eisenkot,” and Eisenkot said, according to the Davar website, that if the change bloc does not reach 60 Knesset members, he will swear in a minority government.

Likud also appealed to the chair of the Central Elections Committee and to the head of the Shin Bet, asking them to examine contacts that foreign diplomatic representatives held with Haredi figures, to find out whether there is a chance of Haredi support for a government headed by Eisenkot (Israel Hayom, Maariv). Ynet wrote that European embassies in Israel are monitoring the Haredi sector and working to bring Haredi parties into such a coalition, to enable a stable government without reliance on Arab parties. The contacts were reported; no finding was published defining them as interference in the election.

There is movement inside the camps too, but in reports by one outlet at a time. Israel Hayom reports that Hamilov, a former deputy mayor of Ofakim who was wounded on October 7 while trying to rescue his daughter, is leaving Likud and joining Liberman. Ynet reports that in the Gur Hasidic dynasty, which holds the senior slot in Agudat Yisrael, they have not yet begun calling on their people to vote, and its mouthpiece makes no mention of the election, against the background of an internal rift after MK Porush and the candidate Eliakim Starck left the party.

Background: on October 27 the 120 members of the 26th Knesset will be elected, and according to Ynet more than 7 million eligible voters will be able to vote. Forming a government requires 61, and the president assigns the task to whoever seems to him to have the best chance, not necessarily the head of the largest list. An analysis in Israel Hayom said that Netanyahu has begun a round of interviews on Channel 14, Army Radio and Kan 11 after the latest polls.

DailyDrop notes: one poll is a snapshot, not a forecast. The sample size and margin of error did not appear in the publications available to us, and another poll, by Channel 14, is given in the headline of Kikar HaShabbat the wording “Bennett jumps by 4 seats,” with no detail we can check. The talk of a rotation, too, is statements by politicians in campaign days, not a party commitment.

Politics · SecurityPress ✓ 5 outlets · the claims are a plaintiff’s, not findings

Ronen Bar sues Netanyahu for defamation and reveals warnings before October 7; Netanyahu: “did not want to cooperate”

On October 14 Ronen Bar, the former head of the Shin Bet, filed a defamation suit against the Prime Minister, after Netanyahu accused him of “crimes” (Ynet). In the statement of claim he describes dozens of warnings, discussions and documents that, he says, were passed to Netanyahu from November 2022 until a few days before the massacre, including the information that reached his military secretary on the morning of October 7. Maariv brings the central claim: that Netanyahu forbade him to meet with the defense minister and deliberately blocked a preemptive attack, and that he reveals notes that, he says, Netanyahu wrote on a Shin Bet document. In the warnings themselves, according to Maariv, an unusual message from the enemy, a recommendation for a preemptive strike and preparations to assassinate senior figures. Ynet also published a full timeline of Bar’s version, from November 2022 until 6:15 a.m. on October 7, 2023.

Netanyahu replied that evening in an interview on Kan 11: “He did not want to cooperate, he could have come to me at any moment” (Maariv). In the opposition they demanded a state commission of inquiry and resignation, and said the suit undermines Netanyahu’s attempt to place responsibility on others (Haaretz English). But Bar too is criticized: Israel Hayom wrote that the suit exposes his full version in advance, and a column in Ynet determined that his accusations are a “boomerang,” since the responsibility of the political echelon does not remove the responsibility of the security establishment. DailyDrop notes: the filing of the suit and its content as described by the plaintiff were confirmed. The documents themselves do not appear in the reports available to us, and the court has not yet discussed them.

Two further reports are not verified by two sources. Lior Akerman, a former senior Shin Bet official, said on 103FM, according to Maariv, that the Prime Minister’s Office is trying to alter documents. And Ynet published a document that, according to the paper, was written by Netanyahu’s people and with his approval, in which the instruction to the IDF before October 7 was to preserve Hamas as an “effective, restrained and restraining governing address” and to forgo a military decision; according to the paper, the political echelon admitted responsibility for it.

Politics · InvestigationPress ✓ 5 outlets · original source: Channel 12 News

Urich did not rule out meetings with a senior Qatari figure in questioning, but “barely remembers one”; he and Feldstein accuse each other

According to a Channel 12 News report from the evening of October 14, Yonatan Urich, an adviser to Netanyahu, met at least twice in Europe with Ali al-Thawadi, an associate of the Emir of Qatar, about nine months after the war broke out (Israel Hayom, Maariv). He was also present, according to Ynet, at the meeting at which Netanyahu apologized to the Prime Minister of Qatar. According to the same paper, the meetings with Urich took place in summer 2024, two months before Urich asked ChatGPT for a plan to improve Qatar’s image, a detail published in recent days. In his questioning Urich did not rule out the meetings but said he finds it hard to remember their details, and “I barely remember one,” as Israel Hayom quoted. Netanyahu testified, according to the same report, that he did not know about the ties and was not updated about the meetings.

Haaretz adds that the police investigation found additional meetings: Urich and Einhorn met with Qatari representatives in Europe while working on the campaign to promote the 2022 World Cup, and ideas for cooperation arose in them, including promoting a virtual currency for Qatar, but these did not materialize. Haaretz English reports that the Shin Bet began examining the information leaked from Urich’s phone, three weeks after the paper revealed the hack. A public clash has meanwhile erupted between Urich and Eli Feldstein, two Netanyahu associates: Urich accused Feldstein of acting as Qatar’s spokesman in Israel, and Feldstein replied “a giant fraud on me and on others” (Israel Hayom).

DailyDrop notes: the report on the meetings is attributed to Channel 12 News, and the others continue it. An indictment against Urich in this affair does not appear in the reports available to us, and so “met” is a claim in the report and not a legal finding.

Industry · SafetyPress ✓ 3 outlets · cause of the explosion under investigation

Explosion at Intel plant in Kiryat Gat: gas technician dies of his wounds, two workers in serious condition

On the afternoon of October 14 an explosion and fire broke out at the Intel plant in Kiryat Gat. The first report said three people were seriously injured, with burns, and evacuated to Soroka hospital. Police and firefighters said the incident was localized and under control, and the Kiryat Gat municipality said there was no danger to residents and to plants in the area (Globes). In the evening the death of one of the injured, a gas technician, was determined, and the other two are hospitalized in intensive care in serious condition (Ynet).

The first assessment was that the explosion was caused by a gas cylinder, and according to Ynet the suspicion is that it happened while trucks were being unloaded. Israel Hayom quotes people at the plant, according to whom the substance that caused the explosion was nitrogen trifluoride. A poisoning expert told the paper that such a substance can harm the airways and the blood’s ability to carry oxygen, and in severe exposure cause cyanosis, organ damage and even death. The detail about the substance comes from a single source. Intel did not respond in the first report; a later response from the company, the names of the casualties and the body that will investigate the incident do not appear in the reports available to us.

DailyDrop notes what is confirmed and what is not. Three outlets agree on an explosion and fire in Kiryat Gat, on three people seriously injured and on the death of one of them (Globes, Ynet, Israel Hayom). Not determined: the cause of the explosion and the identity of the substance, which was given by a single source only, nor whether other workers or residents were exposed to it.

Singaporean container ship sank in the Strait of Hormuz, and according to some reports a second ship as well; Netanyahu: no intention to strike Iran before the election

On October 14 container ships were hit in the Strait of Hormuz, and one of them sank (Reuters, citing sources). The Maritime and Port Authority of Singapore said that two Singapore-registered ships were hit, that one of them sank and that all crew are safe, and called the attacks “unjustified and in violation of international law” (CNA, Bernama). Some outlets report more: The Guardian wrote of a container ship that sank and a second cargo ship that is sinking, and the Washington Post and Al Jazeera wrote that two ships sank. How many ships ultimately sank is not agreed in the reports available to us.

The Guardian describes the attacks as the sharpest rise in harm to shipping since the start of the war, and as an Iranian attempt to close the route before the US election. Reuters reported earlier, citing officials, that Iran is changing tactics and stepping up attacks on shipping ahead of that vote. Al Jazeera quotes Iran, which says it does not want an endless war but will fight until the US accepts its demands. Who carried out each attack was not determined in the Singapore authority’s statement; the attribution to Iran comes from officials and from the words of the Guardian and Reuters.

In Israel Netanyahu said, according to Reuters, that he is not in favor of a preemptive strike on Iran before the October 27 election, and warned Tehran and its proxies against attacking Israel. The remarks come after a Times of Israel report that Netanyahu urged Trump to resume the fighting before the elections in both countries; the Prime Minister’s Office denied it.

The attacks are translating into prices. Bloomberg wrote on Wednesday that oil shipping costs are rising and threatening fuel supply, and that Brent crossed $106, and Reuters reported overnight that oil fell. The US is considering easing sanctions on shadow fleet ships in order to lower freight prices, according to an official (Reuters). The FT wrote that annual US inflation jumped to 3.7% as the war in Iran makes fuel more expensive; more on that in the side column.
