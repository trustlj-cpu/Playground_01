Politics · October 7Press ✓ 5 outlets · claims attributed to Gil, Netanyahu denies

Netanyahu’s former military secretary: “a pattern of criminal conduct” in the Prime Minister’s Office; Eisenkot demands a criminal investigation, Netanyahu denies

Avi Gil, who was the prime minister’s military secretary on October 7, gave his first interview, on Channel 12. The Likud calls his remarks propaganda, and the election is 18 days away.

In his first interview since the massacre, published on Channel 12 on October 8, Avi Gil, Netanyahu’s military secretary from December 2022 to May 2024 according to the Times of Israel, claimed that the head of the Prime Minister’s Office tried to alter an official record of October 7. He described this as part of a “pattern of criminal conduct” in the office and said that Netanyahu’s answers to the State Comptroller were misleading. He said he had kept silent in order to testify before a state commission of inquiry.

Netanyahu denied it. According to reports, he said that Gil knew of the coming attack and did not wake him in time, and he called the remarks an attempt to conceal his failure. The Likud called the interview part of a false, politically driven propaganda campaign. Gil did not confirm that Netanyahu had been warned by Egypt or the Emirates, but said that the visit of Egyptian intelligence had a reason.

Gadi Eisenkot’s Yashar! party appealed to the Attorney General to examine whether Netanyahu lied to the State Comptroller, and called for a criminal investigation. Eisenkot pledged to set up a state commission of inquiry after the election. Avigdor Liberman demanded that an investigation be opened on suspicion of treason and conspiracy. DailyDrop found no decision by the Attorney General, and Gil’s claims so far remain without legal verification.

Iran · USPress ✓ 6 outlets · Channel 12’s report on Israel is a single source

Trump: US will not strike Iran before the midterm elections; the blockade stays, and Tehran declares it is ready for a protracted war

President Trump said on October 8 that the US would not attack Iran before the congressional elections on November 3, but that the blockade of Iranian ports would remain in place, CNBC, US News, CBS and The National reported. He presented the decision as the result of productive discussions with Tehran. The remarks came a day after American media reports of preparations for a renewed attack. The commitment does not cover the period after the vote.

Channel 12 reported that senior American officials told Chief of Staff Eyal Zamir that preparations had been requested for a broad strike within three weeks, and that Zamir warned that such a strike could delay the election in Israel. This is a single-source report. Tehran, according to Iran International, declares that it is ready for a protracted war, and according to Iranian state media will not give up uranium enrichment.

Energy · MarketsPress ✓ 4 outlets · closing figures differ slightly between sources

Oil jumped 3.6% on a record number of attacks on tankers in Hormuz and a hurricane crisis in the Gulf of Mexico

WTI crude contracts closed on October 8 at $91.50 a barrel, up 3.6%, and Brent rose about 4% to about $104, CNBC, CNN and DTN reported. According to Kpler, 10 tankers were hit in the Strait of Hormuz between September 28 and October 4, a weekly record; the previous record was six.

At the same time, about 500,000 barrels a day, about 25% of output in the Gulf of Mexico, were shut down because of a hurricane. The rise eased after Trump’s statement that he would not attack Iran before the election. Fuel prices in Israel are set by a monthly formula, and any effect will appear in the next update.

TA-35 closes at 4,089.94 points, down 0.16%; the exchange is 2.7% below its level at the start of the month

The TA-35 index closed on Thursday at 4,089.94 points, down 0.16%, according to Tel Aviv Stock Exchange data, while ad-hoc-news reported a 0.2% decline to 4,090. Since the start of the month the index has fallen about 2.7%, and since the start of the year it is up about 12.6%, according to the exchange’s data page. IDF soldier killed and three wounded when a vehicle overturned in south Lebanon; in Gaza four weapons depots destroyed, according to the IDF

An IDF soldier was killed and three were wounded when their Humvee overturned in south Lebanon, the Times of Israel and the Jerusalem Post reported on October 8. No further details were published.

The IDF and the Shin Bet said that four Hamas and Islamic Jihad weapons depots were destroyed across the Gaza Strip, and that two Hamas commanders were eliminated. The figures are attributed to the IDF and were not independently verified. DailyDrop found no independent casualty figures in Gaza for that day.
