KOSPI plunges 3.59% to close at 5,100.50… KOSDAQ falls 4.86%

The KOSPI fell 3.59% and the KOSDAQ 4.86%. No official announcement explaining the decline has come out.

On the 16th the KOSPI closed at 5,100.50, down 190.03 points (3.59%) from the previous session. It opened at 5,305.40, up 0.28%, but widened its losses during the session and slid more than 3%. With the previous close at 5,290.53, that is the same as an index of 100 slipping to 96.4. The KOSDAQ closed at 665.65, down 34.01 points (4.86%), a bigger drop than the KOSPI’s; it works out to 100 becoming 95.1. The won/dollar rate fell 1.0 won to 1,335.7 won as of 3:30 p.m. No explanation from the government, the exchange or any institution directly stating the reason for the day’s decline has been confirmed.

Nvidia news continued on the day as well. US Treasury Secretary Bessent said a successor to Jensen Huang, who stepped down earlier as CEO, should be found from outside Nvidia. A report said Huang is discussing the possibility of a return with aides, but Nvidia has not confirmed it, and there is no announcement that the board of directors will accept a return. An online petition demanding a return continues. However, no official explanation says these developments caused the declines in the KOSPI and KOSDAQ, so a link between the two cannot be written as established fact. This week Nvidia’s earnings release and Anthropic’s listing are scheduled, and whether the index extends its losses tomorrow morning is the immediate thing to watch.

New COFIX at 3.62%, highest in 2 years 8 months… variable-rate mortgage interest set to rise further

COFIX, the benchmark for variable-rate mortgages, rose for a second straight month to its highest level in 2 years 8 months. The October COFIX on a new-amount basis, released on the 16th, is 3.62% a year, 0.19 percentage point above September’s 3.43%. Compared with August’s 3.18%, that is a rise of 0.44 percentage point in two months. COFIX is the average cost banks pay to raise funds through deposits and bonds, and banks add a spread to it to set interest on variable-rate loans. Someone who borrowed 300 million won at a COFIX-linked variable rate would see annual interest rise by about 570,000 won if the rate goes up 0.19 percentage point. However, spreads and the timing of rate adjustments differ by bank, so the actual burden differs by loan, and fixed-rate loans are not affected right away. The November figure will be released next month.

Three days before the CSAT, demands to ‘postpone it’ flood in… education minister: “Postponing would cause greater confusion”

With the 2027 College Scholastic Ability Test (CSAT) three days away, on the 19th, demands to ‘postpone the exam’ are pouring in. Requests to postpone it immediately flooded the website of the Korea Institute for Curriculum and Evaluation, and anxiety over ‘whether the exam should be put off’ has erupted among parents too. Reports that tiny devices such as button-type cameras sold out in a rush added to the unease. People Power Party leader Jang Dong-hyeok said postponement should be considered, and the education minister drew a line, saying postponing would cause greater confusion.

The government and ruling party are answering with tighter enforcement instead of postponement. On the 16th the Democratic Party said it would discuss extending the test eligibility restriction on those who cheat using AI to 10 years or more, or revoking eligibility altogether, and police have begun monitoring for signs of people plotting to cheat online or trading related devices. Cheong Wa Dae said on the 15th it would intensively review the management and supervision system so that there is no AI cheating on the CSAT. No announcement has come that a CSAT postponement is being officially considered.

December international fuel surcharge cut by 2 levels… 60,600 won off New York round trip

The fuel surcharge added to international airfares for December, next month, drops two levels. The overall surcharge falls by about 8%, and a round-trip ticket to New York gets 60,600 won cheaper. The fuel surcharge is an extra fee set in levels each month in line with jet fuel prices, so when the level falls, the surcharge on the same route shrinks too. It reflects the recent drop in international oil prices on expectations of an end to the war between the US and Iran, an oversupply outlook and other factors.

The 60,600 won cut on a New York round trip works out to 30,300 won one way, and for a family of four traveling together the cut amounts to 242,400 won. The cut differs by airline and route. If jet fuel prices rise again, the level could go up in later months.

‘Yeoksam-dong patent law firm serial arson’ Lee Myung-su indicted in detention… 26 injured

Lee Myung-su (77), suspected of setting fire in succession to two patent law firm offices in Yeoksam-dong, Gangnam District, Seoul, and injuring 26 people, was indicted in detention on the 16th. The fires broke out in the daytime on October 20. A fire started in an office on the third floor of the Sinmyeong Building at about 11:54 a.m., then in an office on the fourth floor of the Hanyang Building at about 12:01 p.m. Lee has claimed since right after the arrest to have been defrauded by the patent law firms. Police referred the case to the Seoul Central District Prosecution Service on October 29 on charges of arson of an occupied building causing injury and attempted murder, and disclosed Lee’s personal details on November 3.

The attempted murder charge was added after police secured a victim’s statement that Lee had been looking for a specific person before the fire. There is a report that Lee attempted arson against the same patent law firms in 2022 and was given a prison sentence. The number of injured was initially reported as 19 and rose to 26 as the tally grew, and there was a report that 2 of them are in critical condition. The trial schedule and the specific criminal facts set out in the indictment are not yet known.
