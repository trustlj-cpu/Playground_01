Samsung beat kt 12-1 to win the Korean Series for the first time in 12 years… Koo Ja-wook MVP, ninth title overall

The Samsung Lions beat the kt wiz 12-1 in Daegu on the 8th to take the series 4 games to 1, clinching their first title in 12 years and ninth overall.

The Samsung Lions beat the kt wiz 12-1 in Game 5 of the Korean Series at Daegu Samsung Lions Park on the 8th and won the series 4 games to 1. It is their first title in 12 years, since 2014, and their ninth overall. Samsung dropped Game 1 by 7-8 but won the next four games, Games 2 through 5, in a row. Opponent kt had finished the regular season with 90 wins, so Samsung’s title is called an upset, a win over the team that was stronger on paper. The series most valuable player (MVP) went to captain Koo Ja-wook. Playing on a sore leg, the captain got a hit in all five games and recorded a 0.500 average (10 hits in 20 at-bats) with 1 home run and 6 RBIs. It is the first championship ring since joining the club. It is also the first title won at Daegu Samsung Lions Park.

The last pitch was thrown by Chris Fedek and landed in the mitt of catcher Kang Min-ho. After 22 years as a pro, Kang Min-ho gets a championship ring for the first time. Manager Park Jin-man, who won six titles as a player, wins the first title as manager. Losing kt manager Lee Kang-chul said, “Not winning Game 2 was the reason we lost.” The day before, in Game 4, Samsung scored six runs in the first inning alone to win 10-5 and lead 3 games to 1, and it finished the series in Game 5 on this day. The Korean Series is a best-of-seven in which the first team to win four games takes the title, so Games 6 and 7 will not be played. When the title was clinched, downtown Daegu erupted with the fans’ excitement.

Politics · Special counsel✓ 3 outlets · actual candidates and spending unconfirmed

Cost of fabricated-prosecution special counsel estimated at over 38 billion won… cancellation clause dropped but workaround dispute, Jang Dong-hyuk: “prison-break law”

The cost the National Assembly Budget Office estimated for appointing the fabricated-prosecution special counsel and carrying out its duties is 38.056 billion won. The special counsel act passed the plenary session on the 6th with 167 in favor and 1 against among the 168 members present. The People Power Party did not take part in the vote and fought back with a filibuster (unlimited debate), but it was forcibly ended after about 24 hours. The targets of investigation are six allegations of fabricated investigations and indictments by the Yoon Suk Yeol government’s prosecutors, including President Lee Jae-myung’s cases. Candidates are to be recommended by the Democratic Party, the People Power Party and the Rebuilding Korea Party and appointed by the president, with appointment expected as early as around the 22nd. This cost estimate was prepared in May, so its staffing assumptions differ from the final bill. It then assumed 6 deputy special counsels, 150 special investigators and 30 seconded prosecutors, but the final bill provides for 8 deputies, 70 special investigators and 20 seconded prosecutors. The clause on the power of withdrawal of the indictment, which the president had requested, was deleted during revision in the Legislation and Judiciary Committee. However, a review report by National Assembly committee staff pointed out that, if the Criminal Procedure Act is applied by analogy, an interpretation is possible under which the special counsel could cancel prosecutions. On the 7th People Power Party leader Jang Dong-hyuk called the act “the president’s prison-break law” and even raised impeachment, and on the 8th lawmaker Na Kyung-won wrote on Facebook, “The Lee Jae-myung trial should be reopened and the presidential election held again.” The Democratic Party’s position is that establishing the truth comes first.

International · Middle East✓ 2 outlets · whether it was a direct hit and the tally of 63 dead unconfirmed

Yemen’s Houthis attack financial district of Saudi capital Riyadh… Aramco tower damaged, Saudi Arabia says “minor damage, no casualties”

A projectile from Yemen’s Houthi rebels struck the King Abdullah Financial District (KAFD) in Riyadh, the capital of Saudi Arabia, and a building of the state oil company Aramco inside the district was damaged. It is a business district where financial institutions and corporate headquarters are concentrated. The timing of the attack differs by report, the 7th or the 8th. Saudi Arabia’s Energy Ministry said debris caused minor damage to the building and that there were no injuries or deaths. The Saudi-led Arab coalition announced that it intercepted in the air a missile the Houthis had fired at Riyadh. Whether the damage to the building was caused by a direct hit or by debris from an intercepted projectile has not yet been confirmed.

The Houthis have recently stepped up missile and drone attacks on Saudi airports, ports and oil facilities. On the 7th the Houthis claimed that 27 people died near the Yemeni capital Sanaa in a Saudi airstrike, but Saudi Arabia issued no statement. A tally that 63 people died in two days in Yemen’s civil war also appeared, but it has not been independently confirmed.

Society · Information security✓ 3 outlets · number of people and scope of the leak unconfirmed

Personal data of SM Entertainment and affiliate staff leaked… includes resident registration and account numbers, outside hack around the 4th

Personal information of employees of SM Entertainment and its affiliates leaked in an outside hacking. In its apology, SM said an internal security check confirmed that around the 4th an external attacker had entered its work systems in an unauthorized way and taken document files kept there. The leaked information includes names, mobile phone numbers, addresses, account numbers, resident registration numbers, affiliations and information written in contracts, and the items differ by person affected. DearU, which operates the fan platform ‘Bubble’, and Dream Maker Entertainment were also attacked at the same time.

SM blocked the IP addresses and routes used in the attack and changed unauthorized access keys, and it is investigating with the relevant authorities and outside experts. It said no secondary harm from misuse of the leaked information has been confirmed. It is continuing to notify the affected employees individually, and urged caution about contacts impersonating the company or its associates to guard against voice phishing and smishing (a scam that sends a fake web address by text message to steal information). Victims can claim damages under the Personal Information Protection Act or apply for mediation to the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee. The exact number of victims and the scope of the leak have not been disclosed.

International · Ukraine✓ 3 outlets · agenda details and outcome of the talks unconfirmed

U.S.-Russia-Ukraine peace talks to resume in Abu Dhabi on the 10th–11th… French foreign minister: “Europe will take part too”

The peace talks between Russia and Ukraine, brokered by the United States, will be held again in Abu Dhabi in the United Arab Emirates on the 10th and 11th. The talks were announced on the 2nd by U.S. special envoy Steve Witkoff, and Witkoff and Jared Kushner are due to represent the United States. The agenda is security guarantees after the war ends, easing of tensions and humanitarian measures. It is the fourth formal three-way negotiation brokered by the United States since January this year, and the previous round was in February, before the United States and Israel went to war with Iran.

French Foreign Minister Jean-Noël Barrot said on the 8th that Europe will also take part in the Abu Dhabi talks. Meanwhile Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said on Telegram on the 8th that Dnipro, Pavlohrad, Kamianske and other places in Dnipropetrovsk region were hit by airstrikes that day, leaving more than 600,000 subscribers without electricity at the peak of the power outage, and that restoration is under way. Nothing has been decided yet on whether a ceasefire or agreement will come out of these talks.
