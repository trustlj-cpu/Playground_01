KOSPI hits circuit breaker 3 minutes after open… breaks below 5,000 intraday, closes at 5,059.69

On the 6th the KOSPI was halted by a circuit breaker three minutes after the open, and the 5,000 line broke intraday. The index closed at 5,059.69, down more than 5% for a second straight day.

On the 6th a stage 1 circuit breaker was triggered three minutes after the KOSPI opened, and trading stopped. A circuit breaker is a device that halts trading across the whole market for 20 minutes when the index stays down more than 8% from the previous day’s close. As of 9:17 a.m. the index was down 8.55% at 4,923.47, breaking the 5,000 line intraday, and at 9:34:36 a.m., about 10 minutes after trading resumed, a sell-side sidecar was triggered on the KOSPI market as well. A sidecar is a device that suspends the effect of program selling (sell orders that a computer places automatically under preset conditions) for 5 minutes when futures prices swing sharply. A sell-side sidecar was also triggered on the KOSDAQ market. The KOSPI ended the day at 5,059.69. Because that is 6.02% below the previous day, it recovered somewhat from the intraday low, but after the 5.28% fall on the 5th it dropped more than 5% for a second straight day.

It started in the United States. On the 5th in New York, the Nasdaq fell 5.5% and the S&P 500 fell 4.4%. No official cause for the plunge has been announced. Samsung Electronics and SK hynix fell more than 10% from the pre-market session on the alternative exchange, before the regular session opened. SK hynix dropped as much as the 11% range early in the session. Deputy Prime Minister Lee Hyung-il said at the external economic ministers’ meeting that day that the possibility that concerns over tighter regulation of the AI industry could spread to global markets cannot be ruled out. The size of net buying and selling by foreigners and institutions, and the gains and losses by sector, have not yet been confirmed. What remains a variable is how New York stocks close on the night of the 6th, before the next trading day, Monday the 9th, opens.

Government: “Preemptive steps so the stock market does not overreact”… F4: “Additional market stabilization measures if volatility widens”

Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Finance and Economy Lee Hyung-il presided over an external economic ministers’ meeting at the Government Complex Seoul on the 6th and said, “Relevant agencies will preemptively work out the necessary steps and carry them out in good time so that our stock market does not overreact relative to economic fundamentals.” Fundamentals mean the basic strength of a country’s economy, such as growth, prices and exports. Lee said US stocks had fallen sharply the previous day on concerns over tighter regulation of the artificial intelligence (AI) industry and that the possibility of it spreading to global markets could not be ruled out. The Macroeconomic and Financial Meeting (F4) that followed said it would take additional market stabilization measures if stock market volatility widens. The F4 is a meeting of top economic officials, bringing together the finance and economy minister, the Bank of Korea governor, the Financial Services Commission chair and the Financial Supervisory Service governor. Specific means, such as activating a market stabilization fund in which the financial sector pools money to buy shares, or restricting short selling, had not been announced as of this hour.

Cheonghae Unit’s operating area extended to south of Oman, 650 km from Hormuz… patrol aircraft and mine-clearing team added

On the 5th the government approved at the vice ministers’ meeting the ‘revised motion for consent to extend the dispatch of Korean armed forces units to Somali waters and the open sea south of Oman’. It roughly doubles the operating area of the Cheonghae Unit, which has concentrated on fighting Gulf of Aden pirates, to the open sea south of Oman, about 650 km from the Strait of Hormuz, and adds one maritime patrol aircraft, a bomb disposal team and unmanned minesweeping equipment that finds and removes mines. The additional deployment is 75 personnel, bringing the Cheonghae Unit to 395 in all. The patrol aircraft is a P-8A, whose fielding was completed in July this year. After the Cabinet meeting on the 10th the motion goes to the National Assembly for its consent.

The government’s position is that this is the first step in answering the request for a military contribution to stabilizing the Strait of Hormuz after the US-Iran war, while not getting involved in the war. It plans to explain to Iran that the move is not a threat. Rebuilding Korea Party floor leader Kim Joon-hyung criticized it at the Supreme Council meeting on the 5th as an indirect deployment, saying, “A unit sent to stop pirates cannot be sent to a sea of war.” The Democratic Party explained that it is because pirate activity has surged.

President Lee Jae-myung on first YouTube show ‘The President’s Y’: “Not trapped in an algorithm”… Cheong Wa Dae: “YouTube Live also under consideration”

President Lee Jae-myung on the 5th launched ‘The President’s Y’, a YouTube program for communicating directly with the public. On the first show, asked about the claim that the president is ‘trapped in an algorithm’, President Lee answered “not trapped” and said abusive and critical comments are read too. Saying, “I have come under countless attacks over the years, and if I had not communicated directly with the people at those times, I probably would not be in this seat now,” President Lee called direct communication “like a lifeline.” To the criticism ‘Why is the president on social media?’ the answer was, “There are people on social media too, and those people are also citizens of the Republic of Korea.” President Lee said live broadcasts of Cabinet meetings will continue because, although errors or inappropriate moments sometimes show, they are tolerable and raise trust. Cheong Wa Dae chief spokesperson Kang Yu-jung said on the 6th that “the president is one of the messengers with the greatest media power” and that a YouTube live stream might be considered. Kang added a personal view that it is not appropriate to limit the president’s means of expression or how often they are used.

Seoul Transportation Corporation hit with 240 million won penalty surcharge… union votes for industrial action, work-to-rule from the 20th

At the Railway Safety Administrative Disposition Review Committee held on the 5th, the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport decided to impose a penalty surcharge of 240 million won on Seoul Transportation Corporation. The corporation had received a corrective order for failing to report a near-accident, a dangerous situation that could lead to an accident while trains are running, yet again omitted a report. Penalty surcharges were also imposed on four maintenance organizations that did not follow the procedure for changing rolling-stock maintenance personnel, so the penalty surcharges for the five violations of the Railway Safety Act total 252.5 million won.

The same day, the corporation’s labor union, after wage talks broke down, approved industrial action in a vote of members for and against. The union plans to begin work-to-rule action on the 20th and to strike if there is no progress in bargaining. Work-to-rule is a form of labor dispute in which workers follow the rules to the letter and slow down work. A strike by the corporation, which operates Seoul subway Lines 1 to 8, is mentioned as possible at the end of this month, but no date has been set.
