Finance Ministry cuts 2026 growth to about 0.7%, bets on 3.3% in 2027 and says it will meet the fiscal target

The government projects a structural deficit of 2.3% of GDP and gross debt of 42.7% at the end of 2026. The World Bank, meanwhile, sees growth of 0.8%.

The Budget Office (Dirección de Presupuestos) presented the third-quarter Public Finance Report on Tuesday, October 6, before the Special Joint Budget Committee. The Finance Ministry cuts 2026 growth to 0.7%, according to CNN Chile (BioBioChile speaks of a range of 0.25% to 0.75%), and expects 3.3% in 2027. Minister Jorge Quiroz anticipated that the fiscal target will be met for the first time in four years, according to DF.

The 2026 structural deficit would be 2.3% of GDP, below the target of 2.6%, and gross debt would close the year at 42.7%, according to La Tercera and CNN Chile. Experts consulted by BioBioChile doubt the 2027 projection. DailyDrop could not reconcile the two 2026 figures.

Politics · JusticePress ✓ 5 outlets · account according to Emol, BioBioChile, Cooperativa, T13 and El Mostrador

Court unanimously confirms stripping of immunity for Senator Miguel Ángel Calisto over fraud against the treasury

The Puerto Montt Court of Appeals unanimously confirmed, by 3 votes to 0, the stripping of immunity (desafuero) of Senator Miguel Ángel Calisto, requested by the Prosecutor’s Office, in the case of fraud against the treasury, according to Emol, BioBioChile, Cooperativa and T13. It is the second time he has lost his immunity: he had already been stripped of it as a deputy. Calisto said he was “very calm” and announced he will appeal to the Supreme Court.

The dollar reversed its morning fall and closed at CLP 974.4 as the initial effect of the Finance Ministry’s sales ran out

The dollar fell as low as about CLP 963 on the morning of Tuesday, October 6, but reversed and closed at CLP 974.4, CLP 1.1 more than on Monday, according to DF, which cited the average of ask quotes from Bloomberg. DF noted that the initial effect of the Finance Ministry’s auction program had run out.

A Capitaria analyst cited by Emol said the CLP 970 zone is the market reference. The IPSA rose 0.36% to about 11,164 points, according to The Rio Times. September CPI will be released on Thursday.

Politics · Finance MinistryPress ✓ 3 outlets · Kast and Quiroz according to T13, DF and BioBioChile

Kast previews a “data cross-check” using artificial intelligence and Quiroz ties it to a review of free tuition at universities

President Kast said at the Digital Country Summit that artificial intelligence will make it possible to cross-check data so that “some people get clever and obtain benefits” they are not entitled to, according to T13. He set as a goal the interoperability of 80% of State data, according to DF.

Minister Quiroz had said on October 2 that the cross-check will be used to review free tuition at universities: some 80,000 beneficiaries declare that they live alone. BioBioChile notes that the cross-check has not yet been done because the legal authorization of the Reconstruction Law is missing.

Chile · SecurityPress ✓ 3 outlets · account according to CNN Chile, Emol and T13

After Sergeant Urrea’s killing, the Senate promises “urgency” as debate turns to a 2026 with four Carabineros killed during operations

CNN Chile reported that four Carabineros have died during operations in 2026, two of them in the last week, after the killing of Sergeant Jaime Urrea in Quilleco. The Senate reiterated its commitment to work “with a sense of urgency” to strengthen the police forces.

Minister Martín Arrau said Urrea died “fulfilling his duty to protect others,” according to Emol. The bill on the Rules on the Use of Force awaits its final vote in the Senate, and the PDI (Investigations Police) is investigating the crime.

Unverified — what we could not confirm

“Calisto was convicted.” No: the stripping of immunity is a procedural step that allows him to be charged; it does not determine his guilt. He will appeal to the Supreme Court and will be suspended only if it becomes final (Emol, BioBioChile).
“The Finance Ministry projects 0.7% growth.” It depends on the source: CNN Chile speaks of 0.7%, and BioBioChile of a range of 0.25% to 0.75%. DailyDrop could not reconcile the two figures.
“Quiroz will eliminate free tuition at universities.” What he announced is a data cross-check to review who meets the requirements; the Finance Ministry itself clarified that those already studying with free tuition would not lose the benefit (BioBioChile, T13).
