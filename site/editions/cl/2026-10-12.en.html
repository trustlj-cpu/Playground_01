Chile · MiningPress ✓ 4 outlets · Codelco according to Cooperativa, La Tercera, DF and Reuters

Two subcontractor workers die in an avalanche at Codelco’s El Teniente mine

On Sunday, October 11, the avalanche trapped three people in the open pit; one was rescued and two were found dead. The union wants to know why they were in a critical zone with mining operations suspended.

Codelco reported on Sunday an avalanche in the open pit sector of its El Teniente Division, in the municipality of Machalí, O’Higgins Region. Three workers were affected: one was assisted and taken to a medical center, and at night the state company confirmed the deaths of the other two, according to Cooperativa, La Tercera and DF. Reuters picked up the company’s initial notice.

The dead were workers of the subcontractor San Lorenzo, which provided services through Aura, and they were carrying out inspections in sectors of the pit whose operations had been suspended because of bad weather, La Tercera reported. The union asked for an inquiry into why they were working in a critical zone. President Kast said “it has been a hard winter for Chile”, according to Teletrece. DailyDrop has not yet seen the official cause or the names of the victims.

World · War in UkrainePress ✓ 4 outlets · Reuters, Dawn, New Straits Times and Kyiv Independent

Trump announces an “energy truce” between Russia and Ukraine; Russia struck power plants hours later

On Sunday the 11th, Trump said in a social media post that Russia and Ukraine had agreed to stop attacking each other’s energy infrastructure immediately. In Kyiv it caused surprise: Zelensky said he would support the truce if Moscow also kept it, according to Reuters and Mint. Moscow had not confirmed it, the New Straits Times reported.

Shortly afterward, Russian attacks struck several Ukrainian power plants, including one in Kharkiv, according to Zelensky and Kyiv Independent. In markets, Brent fell on Monday in Asia (−US$1.54) on expectations of more Russian crude, according to Business Recorder. US senators are also questioning a diesel deal with Russia.

World · Middle EastPress ✓ 4 outlets · Saudi authorities according to AP, Japan Times, Dawn and CNA

New Houthi missile strikes Riyadh airport: one dead and eleven injured, a day after the attack that left at least 12 dead

The Saudi civil aviation authority reported that a new Houthi attack struck King Khalid International Airport on Sunday the 11th and killed a Pakistani resident, as well as injuring eleven other people, according to Japan Times and Dawn. On Saturday the 10th a missile had caused at least 12 deaths and more than 300 injuries, according to AP and Straits Times; CNA and GMA report 13 dead.

The United Kingdom and the US issued alerts to their citizens. Iran, for its part, said the US accepts some of its conditions to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, but with an “inadequate” response, according to Livemint and Rappler. DailyDrop did not see a confirmed military response from Riyadh or Washington.
