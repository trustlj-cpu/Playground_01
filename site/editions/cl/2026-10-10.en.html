Mudslide in Las Condes: 2,015 homes affected, 55 uninhabitable, and the containment nets had not yet been installed

The municipality is deploying machinery to clear mud. The Prosecutor’s Office is investigating responsibilities and authorities are weighing prevention measures.

The municipal toll from Thursday’s mudslide in Las Condes is 2,015 homes affected, 55 of them uninhabitable, and some 15 kilometers of avenues damaged, according to CNN Chile. The injured remain six. La Tercera reported that the Prosecutor’s Office opened an investigation into responsibilities.

Mayor Catalina San Martín lamented that installation of the containment nets was due to start on Saturday, that is, they were not in place when the mudslide came down the ravine, according to BioBioChile. The figures from Senapred and the municipality differ and are preliminary.

Kast promulgates Reconstruction Law without ceremony; Comptroller’s Office review and publication still pending

President Kast signed the decree promulgating the Reconstruction Law on Friday, October 9, without a ceremony because of the mudslide, according to El Mostrador and BioBioChile. The Comptroller’s Office’s toma de razón (legality review) and publication in the Diario Oficial are still pending, according to DF. The benefits for DFL2 homes and the capital gains exemption would apply from January 1, 2027, and there will be payment facilities with the Tesorería for 180 days, according to CNN Chile.

The Frente Amplio asks the Government to freeze implementation of the mega-reform and correct the 2027 Budget

The Frente Amplio called on the Government to correct the 2027 Budget, strengthen employment policies and suspend implementation of the Reconstruction Law, according to BioBioChile and La Tercera. The demand coincides with the law’s promulgation.

The party cites unemployment of 9.6% and low-growth projections. DailyDrop saw no response from the Government.

World · EnergyPress ✓ 3 outlets · Trump’s announcement and coverage by News9, ABC 33/40 and blue News

Trump announces that Russia will deliver more than 300,000 tonnes of diesel after speaking with Putin; Washington authorizes the purchases

Trump wrote on Truth Social, after speaking with Putin, that Russia will immediately supply more than 300,000 tonnes of diesel, according to News9, ABC 33/40 and blue News. A further 500,000 tonnes would follow in November; the Treasury issued a temporary license through April 7, 2027.

The Kremlin confirmed the agreement. A Bloomberg columnist estimated that the 300,000 tonnes equal about 2.2 million barrels, a small volume. In Chile diesel rose 10.2% in the September CPI.

Economy · Central BankPress ✓ 3 outlets · Quiroz and the Central Bank per BioBioChile, T13 and CNN Chile

Quiroz pressures the Central Bank over inflation and reopens the debate on its autonomy

Finance Minister Jorge Quiroz said inflation is the Central Bank’s responsibility and that monetary measures must be taken, according to T13. CNN Chile added that he acknowledged that meeting the official 3.8% target “is challenging.”

BioBioChile wrote that his remarks reopen the debate over the Central Bank’s autonomy. The meeting is on October 26 and 27. DailyDrop saw no response from the institution.
