Houthi missile hits Riyadh airport: at least 12 dead and more than 300 injured, according to Saudi Arabia

It is the third attack on the airport in a week and the deadliest. The Saudi coalition promises a response and Trump is weighing joining the strikes.

A missile fired by the Houthis, the Iran-backed rebels in Yemen, struck King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh on Saturday, October 10. Saudi authorities reported at least 12 dead and more than 300 injured, according to CNN, Newsweek and Al Hurra. All flights were diverted and operations were suspended.

It is the third attack on the same airport this week; earlier, a Saudia pilot was killed. The war had already affected the Strait of Hormuz. The Saudi-led coalition promised a “decisive” response, and Secretary of State Marco Rubio called it “reprehensible”, according to Arab News and CNN.

Thirteen opposition senators take the Rules on the Use of Force to the Constitutional Court

According to an exclusive report by Radio Bío Bío, 13 opposition senators went to the Constitutional Court to challenge two provisions of the Rules on the Use of Force, on proportionality and the use of potentially lethal force. Senator Juan Luis Castro (PS) is one of the signatories. The rules affect Carabineros and the PDI.

The action must first go through a formal review. La Tercera had reported on the Senate’s clearance of the bill on October 7. DailyDrop did not see any other outlet confirming the filing.

The week in Chile: 4.1% inflation, mudslide in Las Condes, mega-reform promulgated and new security laws

The week began with the dollar within a step of CLP 1,000, fell after the Dipres announced sales and closed near CLP 975. On Wednesday Congress cleared the Rules on the Use of Force; on Thursday CPI came in at 0.4% monthly and 4.1% annual, and a mudslide hit Las Condes.

On Friday Kast promulgated the Reconstruction Law, which could take effect on November 1 if it is published in October, according to CNN Chile. DF summed up the week with the Budget, the CPI and a new investigation at Codelco.
