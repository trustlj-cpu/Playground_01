Dollar plunges CLP 18.79 and closes below CLP 974 after Finance Ministry announces sales of up to US$600 million a week

The move halted the climb of the greenback, which had closed last week at around CLP 990. The IPSA rose 1.91 percent and the peso led the regional recovery.

The Budget Directorate (Dipres) announced on Monday, October 5, that it will sell dollars of up to US$600 million a week through October, with a cap of US$2,400 million. The dollar fell CLP 18.79 and closed at CLP 973.9 at the selling rate, according to Emol; DF and BioBioChile cite a drop of more than CLP 17, and La Tercera the biggest fall in more than a year.

A Scotiabank trader estimated that the biggest effect has already been seen. An analysis cited by Emol says it is not a Central Bank intervention but sales tied to the financing of the national treasury. The IPSA rose 1.91 percent to about 11,125 points, according to The Rio Times, on a day of regional gains after the election in Brazil. DailyDrop did not see the official close of the Santiago Stock Exchange.

2027 Budget cuts the program that funds the Cholito Law by 86 percent, and the opposition vows to reverse it

The 2027 Budget bill cuts the program that funds the Cholito Law by 86 percent, from CLP 3,634 million to CLP 514 million, according to BioBioChile and CNN Chile. Subdere says that “investment is not stopping,” because there are CLP 3,000 million in projects under way.

How Flávio Bolsonaro surprised the polls and why the right also gained ground in Brazil’s Congress

The Chilean press explained Brazil’s result on Tuesday. BioBioChile and Emol spoke of a surprise: Flávio Bolsonaro, with about 47 percent, beat Lula, with about 45 percent, contrary to what the polls said. Emol added that candidates allied with the Bolsonaros won seats in Congress.

El Mostrador expects a tight runoff on October 25. In the markets, Brazil’s stock market rose 7.7 percent to a record, according to The Rio Times. DailyDrop has not yet seen a public position from Kast.

G7 to release up to 100 million barrels of oil and diesel; WTI fell 1.84 percent to US$89.43

Kast steps out to defend his first Budget and the government mobilizes to contain the criticism

Kast came out to defend his first Budget and the cabinet mobilized to contain the criticism over the cuts, according to La Tercera. DF reported that the bill entered Congress with spending of CLP 91.18 trillion; Emol highlights among the keys the 1.5 percent increase and the economic context.

Unverified — what we could not confirm

“The Central Bank intervened to bring the dollar down.” No: the Finance Ministry is selling dollars for its own financing needs, according to an analysis cited by Emol; the Central Bank did not intervene. Also, the amounts may change (Emol).
“The government eliminated the Cholito Law.” No: the Budget bill cuts the program that funds its implementation by 86 percent, from CLP 3,634 to 514 million; the law remains in force and Subdere says that projects under way continue (BioBioChile, CNN Chile).
