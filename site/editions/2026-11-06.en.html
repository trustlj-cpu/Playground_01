KOSPI plunges 5.28% to close at 5,383.80… sell-side sidecar triggered, foreigners net sell over 3 trillion won

On the 5th the KOSPI widened its intraday losses and fell 300 points in a single day. The KOSDAQ also slipped below the 700 level, and Samsung Electronics and SK hynix dropped 7–9%.

On the 5th the KOSPI closed at 5,383.80, down 5.28% (300.00 points). The previous day’s close was 5,683.80. Early in the session the decline stayed at 1–2%, but a flood of selling in chip stocks widened the loss to the 4% range, and Korea Exchange triggered a sell-side sidecar in the KOSPI market shortly after 10:20 a.m. A sidecar is a device that suspends the effect of program selling (sell orders that computers place automatically according to preset conditions) for five minutes when futures prices suddenly swing sharply, cushioning the shock. At 2:27 p.m. a sell-side sidecar was also triggered in the KOSDAQ market, and the KOSDAQ index closed at 678.41, below the 700 level. Samsung Electronics and SK hynix shares each fell 7–9%, and foreign investors’ net selling over the day came to over 3 trillion won.

The decline was concentrated in large chip stocks on the day the US midterm vote count was under way. Concern that regulation of AI data centers in the United States could tighten was also raised, but no institution has officially named a cause of the drop. With the count in close races continuing, the final balance of US seats has not yet been settled either. The foreign exchange market was relatively calm. In the Seoul market the won-dollar rate rose only 0.6 won from the previous day to 1,342.1 won, and Korean Treasury bond yields, regarded as a safe asset, all fell. In the early hours of the 6th Korean time, the US stock market was still trading, and the Nasdaq slid more than 4% intraday. Korean stocks open today after taking in the US market close.

September current account surplus $61.46 billion… first monthly surplus above $60 billion

In the preliminary balance-of-payments statistics released by the Bank of Korea on the 5th, the current account surplus for September came to $61.46 billion (about 82.5 trillion won). It was the first monthly surplus above $60 billion, and the previous record was broken after only three months. The current account is the value of what a country earned abroad minus what it spent, combining money from buying and selling goods and services, income earned overseas and the like. Semiconductors lifted this surplus. Chip exports rose 260%, and the surplus for the three months of the third quarter alone exceeded last year’s full-year surplus. These figures are preliminary and may be revised later.

‘Fabricated-prosecution special counsel bill’ put on plenary agenda… People Power Party filibuster, vote expected afternoon of the 6th

On the afternoon of the 5th the National Assembly put the ‘fabricated-prosecution special counsel bill’ on the plenary agenda. Its formal title is the ‘Act on the Appointment of a Special Counsel, etc. for Uncovering the Truth Behind Allegations of Fabricated Investigations and Fabricated Prosecutions by State Agencies Including the Yoon Suk Yeol Government’s Prosecutors’ Office, National Intelligence Service and Board of Audit and Inspection.’ The Democratic Party processed the bill in the Legislation and Judiciary Committee on the 4th, three days after adopting it as party policy, and brought it to the plenary session a day later. The contested provision on the power of withdrawal of prosecution was deleted in the committee. The bill has 309 people, including the special counsel, investigate 12 cases, such as the Daejang-dong, Wirye and Baekhyeon-dong development allegation investigations, over 200 days including the preparation period, and cases involving President Lee Jae-myung are also within the investigation scope. It stipulates that the principle of separating investigation from prosecution is not applied to the special counsel.

The People Power Party began a filibuster (unlimited debate) immediately after the bill was put on the agenda. Floor leader Jung Jeom-sik claimed it was “a special counsel act meant to get rid of the trial President Lee Jae-myung should face,” and the party decided to hold an outdoor rally on the 7th. Hong Ik-pyo, senior presidential secretary for political affairs at Cheong Wa Dae, said it is not a law to protect the president but a law to investigate the prosecution’s abuse of power. The Democratic Party plans to start the cloture procedure around 4 p.m. on the 6th, after the debate has run for 24 hours, and then vote. The result of the vote and its exact timing have not yet been set.

Virtual asset taxation delayed two more years to 2029… push for 3-year loss carryforward, fourth deferral

The government decided to push back by two years the taxation of virtual asset income that it had planned to introduce in January next year, so that it applies from 2029. Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Finance and Economy Lee Hyung-il said at a briefing at the Government Complex Seoul on the 5th that the plan would be pursued in consultation with the National Assembly. This is the fourth time implementation has been postponed since the taxation policy was written into law in 2020. The government cited three reasons: the tax information exchange with the United States on virtual assets begins only in 2029, fairness must be ensured between users of overseas exchanges and users of domestic exchanges, and time is needed to put systems such as the Digital Asset Basic Act in place.

Some things do change during the deferral. A loss carryforward deduction will be introduced, carrying investment losses forward for three years to subtract from profits, and tax standards will also be set for new types of trading such as stablecoins and perpetual futures. However, because the government says it will pursue the plan in consultation with the National Assembly, a revision of the law remains to be done.

Personal data of 473,000 cases leaked from 37 financial firms… 1 billion won in funds stolen, authorities give notice of full inspection

On the 5th the Financial Services Commission tallied that about 473,000 cases of personal information had leaked from 37 financial firms over the past month. Of this, the damage in which funds actually left accounts amounts to 1 billion won. Financial Services Commission Chair Lee Eok-won held a ‘meeting of the whole financial sector on innovating security systems for the AI era’ that day at the Financial Security Institute’s Yeouido branch in Seoul. Financial authorities gave notice that they will carry out a full inspection of the security of domestic financial firms.

They are also pushing to legislate a ‘no-fault liability’ system that makes banks pay compensation for phishing damage even when the bank is not at fault. After a security vulnerability was found in PHP, a programming language widely used on websites, the government issued a private-sector cyber crisis alert on the 22nd of last month, and as of the 5th it is at the ‘Caution’ level. Whether this leak is related to that vulnerability has not been confirmed.
