KOSPI rises 3.29% to close at 5,735.66… intraday surge into the 4% range triggers first buy-side sidecar in two months

A day after a 3.73% plunge, the index moved more than 3% in the opposite direction

Yonhap reported that on the 21st the KOSPI ended the regular session at 5,735.66, up 182.92 points (3.29%) from the previous trading day. The index opened up 113.60 points (2.05%) at 5,666.34 and widened its gain to the 4% range in the morning. Dong-A Ilbo reported that around 10:52 a.m. a buy-side sidecar was triggered after KOSPI 200 futures stayed up 5% or more from the previous trading day for over a minute, with futures at 904.84, up 5.22%, at the time. Dong-A Ilbo explained that when a buy-side sidecar is triggered, the effect of program-trading buy orders is suspended for five minutes. Yonhap said it was the first buy-side sidecar in two months, and Electronic Times counted this year’s KOSPI buy-side sidecars at 25, which with 27 sell-side ones brings the total to 52. Dong-A Ilbo and Kyunghyang Shinmun reported that the index regained the 5,800 level intraday. Yonhap and SBS reported that the Kosdaq also rose 1.4%. Yonhap reported that as AI investment sentiment revived, Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix, known together as ‘Samjeon-Nix,’ rose 5%, and foreign net buying continued during the session.

The day before had gone the opposite way. SBS reported that on the 20th the KOSPI plunged 3.73% to close at 5,552.74. Yonhap reported that with the KOSPI on a roller coaster of 3–4% daily rises and falls, anxiety persists even after the rebound. Yonhap and SBS reported that investor deposits, the idle money waiting on the sidelines, fell back to 90 trillion won, and margin-loan balances, the so-called ‘debt-fueled investing,’ also fell to 30 trillion won. Chosun Ilbo reported that up to the day before the rebound the KOSPI had sunk to its lowest level in six months. Chosun Ilbo said iM Securities analyzed that foreign net selling of 200 trillion won weighs on the index even with a stronger won. The won stayed strong. Yonhap and SBS reported that on the 21st, on strong chip exports, the won-dollar rate set a new yearly low for the fourth straight session and stayed in the low 1,320s.

Kim Yo-jong: “Loudspeakers aimed at the South to go into operation along entire border within October”… inspects fortification site with Pak Jong-chon

The Korean Central News Agency reported on the 21st that Pak Jong-chon, adviser to North Korea’s Ministry of National Defence, the day before inspected key points of a southern border fortification construction site together with Ri Il-hwan, a Workers’ Party secretary, Kim Yo-jong, head of the Workers’ Party General Affairs Department, and senior commanders of the Korean People’s Army General Staff, Yonhap, Kyunghyang Shinmun and SBS reported. Yonhap reported that North Korea said at the event it would “counterattack immediately if provoked by the enemy.” Dong-A Ilbo reported that Kim Yo-jong said, “The head of state must yield not a single inch in exercising territorial sovereignty,” and “ordered that the border with a hostile state be made the most permanent of lines.” Chosun Ilbo reported that Kim said “the historic undertaking of making the southern border permanent can never be reversed,” and added that the South Korean military has demanded a halt to the fortification work since a landmine explosion in the Demilitarized Zone (DMZ) on the 21st of last month. Kim Yo-jong also issued a statement that evening. According to Dong-A Ilbo and Kyunghyang Shinmun, Kim said, “Our tactical hunting parties are occupying new positions,” and “within October, psychological-warfare loudspeakers against the Republic of Korea will go into operation along the entire border.” Yonhap also carried the same remarks as breaking news. Kyunghyang Shinmun reported that Kim claimed that “today at two places we carried out warning broadcasts and warning shots” toward South Korean troops. When and where the loudspeakers will actually be switched on has not been confirmed.

‘Insurrection involvement’: ex-Justice Minister Park Sung-jae’s sentence slashed on appeal, from 25 years to 9

Dong-A Ilbo and Yonhap reported that on the 21st Criminal Division 1 of the Seoul High Court (presiding judge Yoon Sung-sik) overturned the first-trial ruling of 25 years and sentenced former Justice Minister Park Sung-jae, indicted on charges including performing key duties in an insurrection, to 9 years. According to Dong-A Ilbo, the first trial found guilt on seven charges and handed down the heaviest sentence among Cabinet members at the time of the emergency martial law, apart from former Defense Minister Kim Yong-hyun, but the appeal court found guilt on only two charges, cutting the sentence by 16 years. Dong-A Ilbo reported that the court judged that Park had been well aware that the emergency martial law was unconstitutional and failed to meet the legal requirements. Kyunghyang Shinmun reported that some charges, such as ‘dispatching prosecutors to the Joint Investigation Headquarters,’ were changed to not guilty, and that Park was also indicted for granting a request related to an investigation concerning Kim Keon-hee. Kyunghyang Shinmun’s weekly magazine had reported that the insurrection special counsel asked for 25 years again on appeal at the closing hearing on September 29.

‘Exhibited his own collection’: National Museum of Korea director Yoo Hong-june resigns… President Lee signs off on resignation the same day

Dong-A Ilbo reported that on the 21st the National Museum of Korea said, “Director Yoo Hong-june has expressed his intention to resign, taking responsibility for all the controversy over the exhibition of his privately owned yumuk (handwritten work) ‘Ilban Sigugeun.’” According to Dong-A Ilbo, the museum had displayed the yumuk in the special exhibition ‘Our Dining Tables’ as the writing of the patriot Park Won-geun, but controversy arose when it was identified as the writing of Park Jung-yang, a pro-Japanese collaborator, and it later emerged that the work was Director Yoo’s own collection. In a statement, Yoo said, “All the criticism so far is entirely my responsibility,” and “to restore the museum’s tarnished honor, it is right for me to step down as director” (Dong-A Ilbo, Kyunghyang Shinmun). Chosun Ilbo and Dong-A Ilbo reported that Cheong Wa Dae chief spokesperson Kang Yu-jung said President Lee Jae-myung signed off on the resignation the same day. Chosun Ilbo reported that a motion to dismiss (haechok) a civilian member of the National Artificial Intelligence Strategy Committee who had caused controversy by improper conduct was also signed off on at the same time. Dong-A Ilbo reported that Yoo left about one year and three months after starting his term.

Oct 1–20 exports reach $61.6 billion, up 105.4% to a record… chip exports surge 222.5%

According to the Korea Customs Service’s preliminary figures for October 1–20 imports and exports, released on the 21st, exports in the period came to $61.6 billion, up 105.4% ($31.61 billion) from a year earlier, Chosun Ilbo reported. Dong-A Ilbo reported that the exact amount is $61.612 billion and that average daily exports, adjusted for working days, also rose 65.9% to $4.74 billion. According to Dong-A Ilbo, both total exports and average daily exports are records for October 1–20. Chips lifted exports. Chosun Ilbo and Dong-A Ilbo reported that chip exports in the period grew 222.5%, more than triple the level of a year earlier.

Yonhap reported that mid-month exports doubled from last year and the run of strong numbers continues. The strong exports also showed up in the foreign exchange market. Yonhap reported that expectations of a falling exchange rate persist on strong chip exports. The same day Yonhap also carried a survey by the Korea Federation of Mid-Sized Companies showing that mid-sized companies’ second-quarter exports rose 12% to $35.8 billion, a record.
