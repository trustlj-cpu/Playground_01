Russian strikes kill at least 12 in Zaporizhzhia a day after Trump's diesel deal; Zelenskyy says Moscow "thanked" the US

Zelenskyy said Russia answered the lifting of diesel sanctions with five glide bombs on homes. Trump, according to France 24, replied that Ukraine's president had better stop hitting Russian refineries.

Russian air attacks on the Ukrainian city of Zaporizhzhia on Saturday, October 10, killed at least 12 people, according to ABC News (Australia) and The Globe and Mail, a day after President Donald Trump announced a diesel supply deal with Russian President Vladimir Putin. France 24 put the toll at 15, including three children. Early reports had given at least seven dead. The numbers have moved with the rescue work and DailyDrop uses a range of 12 to 15 until Ukrainian officials publish a final count.

President Volodymyr Zelenskyy wrote that Russia "expressed its thanks" for the lifting of sanctions on diesel exports by dropping five glide bombs on residential buildings, in a post on his official Telegram channel that outlets quoted. He argued that easing pressure lets Moscow keep bombing cities. France 24 reported that Trump answered that Zelenskyy "better damn well stop" attacking Russian refineries and said Ukraine needs a new leader who can make a deal. DailyDrop has not seen that second remark from a second outlet, so it is reported, not confirmed.

The Treasury waiver covers Russian-origin diesel through about April 2027, reports said on Friday. For readers in Nigeria, the link is fuel: the deal is meant to ease diesel prices, which feed into transport and food costs. Whether Russian diesel reaches the market in the promised volumes has not been confirmed by the Kremlin in the reports seen.

NNPC's petrol discount is N66 a litre and only for app users, with the offer extended to October 14

The 30-day petrol discount announced on Thursday works out to N66 a litre at NNPC stations, and motorists must download the NNPC mobile app and pay digitally to get it, according to Legit, Leadership and Megastar Magazine. Reports say the offer has been extended until October 14 and now includes diesel. Leadership described motorists complaining about the app-only rule.

List prices were N1,355 a litre in Lagos and Rivers and N1,370 in Abuja, Legit reported. Leadership found that at some Abuja stations motorists paying at the pump were charged N1,405 a litre, while app users paid N1,339. A commercial driver in Lagos said petrol sold at N1,350 where he bought. Finance Minister Taiwo Oyedele had said the discount was not a subsidy. DailyDrop has not seen NNPC's statement on the app requirement.

Makinde says he would serve only four years as president

Oyo Governor Seyi Makinde, the Alliance for Progressive Movement (APM) presidential candidate, promised to serve only one four-year term if elected in 2027, Naija News's Sunday roundup said, placing the remark at a Saturday town hall in Yola. DailyDrop could not confirm the Yola venue. A similar pledge to "reset Nigeria in four years" was reported from a North-Central town hall in Lafia in late September by allAfrica, Arise and Blueprint.

Makinde has long proposed a single six-year term for presidents and governors, Channels TV reported in 2025, so the four-year pledge is a personal commitment, not a change to that proposal. The roundup linked his stance to the one Peter Obi has taken. The pledge cannot be enforced by law, and it is a campaign promise.

World · PrizesStatement from Trump's own post · prize confirmed by 6 outlets

After Pillay's Nobel win, Trump posts that he has settled eight wars

Navi Pillay, the South African jurist and former UN human rights chief, was named the 2026 Nobel Peace Prize laureate on Friday, October 9, according to Euronews, CNN, RTE, Time, CBS News and NPR. On Saturday, October 10, President Trump posted on Truth Social that he settled eight wars, part of a post about not winning the prize. DailyDrop has not seen the full text and so reports only that claim as his own statement. Independent analysts have long questioned how many of the eight conflicts were live wars.

Trump also posted on Truth Social on Friday about the Nobel committee. The committee chair said the international legal order is under pressure and the prize was a choice for the rule of law, and a PRIO director said it was not a rebuke of Trump. The Nobel ceremony is on December 10.
