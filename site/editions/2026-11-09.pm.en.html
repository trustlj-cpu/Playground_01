Jeonse fraud victims guaranteed at least one-third of deposits, pre-applications from the 10th… takes effect on the 13th

The Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport will put into effect on the 13th a minimum guarantee scheme that covers the shortfall when the deposit a jeonse fraud victim gets back is under one-third, and Korea Land and Housing Corporation (LH) takes pre-applications from the 10th.

The Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport will put the “minimum guarantee scheme” into effect from the 13th, guaranteeing that jeonse fraud victims get back at least one-third of their lease deposit. Ahead of implementation, Korea Land and Housing Corporation (LH) will take pre-applications for the “minimum support payment” and the “advance payment” from the 10th. Applications can be made online on the LH Cheongyak Plus website, and from the 13th they can also be filed in person at LH regional offices. The scheme does not pay every victim one-third of the deposit across the board. It makes up the shortfall when the amount a victim actually gets back after the auction or public sale is over falls short of one-third. The amount recovered is called the “recovered amount.”

The calculation is simple. One-third of the deposit minus the recovered amount is the minimum support payment. If the deposit is 100 million won and only 10 million won is recovered through the auction distribution, the victim receives an additional 23.33 million won or so, which is 33.33 million won less 10 million won. There is also a way to receive support before the auction or public sale ends. Victims who could not sign a normal lease, as in trust fraud, receive the advance payment first, before the auction ends, and it is settled later against the amount actually recovered (post-settlement). The minimum support payment is applied for after the auctions and public sales of the damaged home are all over, and where LH buys the damaged home, after the calculation of the auction gain is finished. Those with grounds for exclusion, such as a direct purchase, cannot receive support. Specific requirements and documents can be checked in the LH notice and at the call center.

President Lee’s Firefighters’ Day message: “Expand firefighting personnel, strengthen chain of command, secure funding stably”

President Lee Jae-myung said on the 9th, the 64th Firefighters’ Day, that the government will work to expand firefighting personnel and strengthen the chain of command. To that end, the president said funding for firefighting will be secured stably and investment in safety increased, and that the treatment of crew members will be looked after carefully. Suspect in Daejeon killing of former partner who had filed ‘stalking report’ identified… Kwon Jong-pil, 43

The personal details of Kwon Jong-pil, 43, who was detained on suspicion of killing a former partner who had reported stalking in Daejeon, were released on the 9th. The Daejeon Metropolitan Police Agency’s Personal Details Disclosure Review Committee decided on the 2nd to release them, but the suspect objected, and they were released after a grace period. The suspect killed the victim at the former partner’s home in Jung District, Daejeon, in the daytime on the 24th of last month and fled, was placed under emergency arrest the next day at a lodging in Gangneung, Gangwon, and was detained on the 27th.

The victim reported this man to 112 for stalking in October last year but later expressed a wish that there be no punishment, and in November the case ended without referral to prosecutors, a non-referral. In December of the same year a 112 report was also received when the two argued, but police judged there was no sign of a crime and closed the case at the scene. The suspect claimed in police questioning that it was a spur-of-the-moment crime, and police are investigating whether it was premeditated and the motive through digital forensics on mobile phones and profiler analysis.

CSAT D-10: smart-glasses cheating to be blocked… North Chungcheong education office assigns 35 dedicated proctors

The North Chungcheong Provincial Office of Education will deploy 35 dedicated proctors to prevent cheating with smart glasses at the 2027 academic year College Scholastic Ability Test (CSAT) on the 19th. As of the 9th, ten days before the CSAT, checks of exam preparations such as blocking cheating and traffic control continue. Smart glasses are devices with camera and communication functions that can be used to photograph questions and send them out or to receive answers.

Their appearance is almost the same as ordinary glasses, so proctors find it hard to pick them out by eye. That is why the North Chungcheong office has set up 35 dedicated staff who check only this kind of device. The existing rule that test takers cannot bring electronic devices into the exam site still applies. The scale of deployment in other provinces and cities and the detailed criteria for banned items have not been confirmed yet.
