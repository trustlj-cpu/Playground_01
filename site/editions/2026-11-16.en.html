Top passer of tax accountant exam in ‘AI cheating’ self-surrender… pass cancellation and police investigation to follow

The top passer of the 63rd tax accountant exam admitted to cheating with a tiny camera and generative AI and surrendered to the police.

On the 15th, A, the top passer of the 63rd tax accountant exam, admitted to cheating and filed a self-surrender statement with the police. Appearing on a YouTube channel run by A’s lawyer, A said that in both the first and second stages of the exam, a tiny camera was used to photograph the questions and send them to generative AI, and that the answers were received on a smartphone and written down. A also said the same method was used on the first-stage certified public accountant exam, but without passing. Suspicion had persisted over a score so high as to be ‘practically impossible,’ and now the person concerned has admitted the facts directly. Fellow examinees who sat the same exam voiced anxiety and a sense of hollowness.

The Human Resources Development Service of Korea apologized for failing to detect the cheating, said it would file a complaint against A for obstruction of business, and said it would seek to cancel the final pass. The Ministry of Finance and Economy is discussing a suspension of validity of the pass. The offense of obstruction of business is punishable by up to 5 years in prison or a fine of up to 15 million won. The police accepted the self-surrender statement and opened an investigation. That afternoon, Cheong Wa Dae held a response meeting chaired by the chief of policy and ordered a full inspection of suspected AI-assisted cheating across all national qualification exams, and said it would also closely review the supervision system for the CSAT, to be held on the 19th. Whether anyone besides A passed through cheating has not yet been confirmed.

Party leader Kim Min-seok refers Senior Supreme Council Member Choi Min-hee’s case to Ethics Tribunal… freshman lawmakers’ group reaches no conclusion

On the 15th, Democratic Party leader Kim Min-seok ordered that the controversy over the words and conduct of Rep. Choi Min-hee, a senior supreme council member of the party, be referred to the Ethics Tribunal. The Ethics Tribunal is the body that reviews discipline of party politicians, so this reads as the de facto start of disciplinary proceedings. The same day, the freshman lawmakers’ group ‘Deomincho’ held an emergency meeting and agreed that ‘the matter is grave’ but reached no conclusion on how strongly to respond. There are reports that the controversy stemmed from remarks Choi made about a trial involving President Lee Jae-myung, but the exact content of the remarks has not been confirmed. President Lee said the same day, “I can understand those who are disadvantaged by the implementation of reform policy pushing back, but it is hard to tolerate a public figure who speaks of reform and the cause going along with them blindly, or borrowing the name of reform to obstruct reform.” It has not been disclosed whether this remark was aimed at Choi’s case. There are also reports that the pro-Jung camp protested the referral and that some of the pro-Lee camp countered with calls for discipline, but these have not been confirmed. Kim’s leadership is now in its third month, and conflict inside the party keeps recurring.

U.S. aviation chaos: Transportation Secretary says it “occurred during communications network replacement”… Trump blames Biden administration

On the 13th (local time), aircraft takeoffs across the United States stopped all at once. The cause was a failure in the air traffic control communications system, and even after takeoffs resumed, flights stayed tangled all weekend. The scale of cancellations differs by tally, ranging from several thousand to more than 10,000 flights. The U.S. Transportation Secretary said it happened while the network vendor L3Harris was replacing the communications network, and that work errors and equipment defects are under investigation. An official cause has not yet been given.

President Trump made a first statement on the 15th, two days into the crisis. Claiming that the Biden administration ruined the Federal Aviation Administration, Trump said a large part of the current control system is 50 years old and that replacement work has already begun, so Congress should quickly approve the budget. Control communications link the exchanges and route information between controllers and pilots, so when they stop, takeoffs are blocked first for safety.

Cheong Wa Dae launches ‘National Safety Plus’ project… discussing a life term without parole

Presidential Chief of Staff Im Kwang-hyun said at a Cheong Wa Dae briefing on the 15th that the government will pursue the ‘National Safety Plus Project: A Government That Protects My Life.’ The aim is to respond forcefully to crimes that threaten public safety by mobilizing the capabilities of the whole government. Through the end of the year, every ministry’s response and results will be reviewed and supplemented.

Cheong Wa Dae said it is discussing introducing a ‘life term without parole.’ It was reported as one of the measures against relationship-based crime and abnormal-motive crime. Under the current Criminal Act, even a person given life imprisonment can be considered for parole after serving 20 years. If a life term without parole were introduced, it would in effect be a sentence for life. Relationship-based crime refers to crime committed in close relationships such as partners or family, and abnormal-motive crime refers to crime targeting unspecified numbers of people with no clear motive. Whether, when and to what extent it will be introduced has not been decided.

SpaceX Starship spacecraft body caught by launch tower for first time… first-stage recovery fails

On the 14th (local time), SpaceX conducted the 15th Starship test flight at its Starbase launch site in Texas. The returning second stage, that is, the spacecraft body, was caught by the launch tower’s ‘chopsticks’ arms. It is the first time a spacecraft body has been received this way. The first-stage rocket, by contrast, failed to be recovered at the tower and fell into the sea. The cause of the failed recovery has not been confirmed. There is a report that Elon Musk called the success a “historic day,” but it has not been confirmed elsewhere.

Starship is a super-heavy rocket SpaceX is developing, and SpaceX has aimed to catch both the first stage and the spacecraft and reuse them. Catching with arms is a way to receive the vehicle without landing legs. Catching the spacecraft too brings full reusability a step closer, but it is not the case that both the first stage and the spacecraft were recovered in a single flight.
