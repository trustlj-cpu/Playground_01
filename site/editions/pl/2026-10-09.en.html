Second school attack in two days: 15-year-old kills a school caretaker and wounds two people in Leszczydół-Nowiny near Wyszków

The principal and a teacher overpowered the attacker even though they were wounded themselves. The prime minister says the boy had a helmet with a camera to film the attack, and the motive is still unknown to investigators.

On Thursday, October 8, at about 9 a.m., a 15-year-old former pupil attacked three staff members of a primary school in Leszczydół-Nowiny in Wyszków County in Mazovia with a sharp implement, report TVP Info, Bezprawnik, AP and Notes from Poland. The first person attacked was a 37-year-old school caretaker, who died. The principal heard screams and tried to stop the attacker, and a teacher helped him; both were wounded but, according to police spokeswoman Katarzyna Kucharska, managed to overpower the perpetrator. According to a spokesman for the Interior Ministry (MSWiA), the lives of the two wounded are not in danger.

The perpetrator currently attends a vocational school and had three knives on him. Prime Minister Donald Tusk said he also had a helmet with a camera, which he took in order to film the attack. The motives are so far unknown, and reports of an online “challenge” come from early, unverified information. It is the second attack on a school within two days, after Wednesday’s attack in Ostrołęka, where a 19-year-old wounded nine people. On Wednesday police also detained a 17-year-old in Sierpc who was planning an attack.

Politics · SecurityPress ✓ 4 outlets · per KPRM

Tusk convenes an urgent meeting and asks for police in every school at least once a day

Prime Minister Donald Tusk convened an urgent meeting at the Chancellery of the Prime Minister (KPRM) at 1 p.m. on Thursday, to which the ministers of the interior, digital affairs, education and health, the coordinator of the special services and the national police commander, Gen. Marek Boroń, were summoned, report xyz.pl, Eska and TVP Info. After the meeting Tusk asked for a decision that police appear in every school at least once a day, which is meant to reduce the risk of copycat attacks.

The prime minister also pointed out that rules must be introduced allowing quick online blocking of incitement to violence and hate speech, which the president vetoed in January. Education Minister Barbara Nowacka recalled the vetoed act on students’ rights and obligations (Notes from Poland).

Politics · LawPress ✓ 3 outlets · vote count per Bankier.pl

Sejm passes act on blocking illegal content online, 242 votes in favor

The Sejm passed on Thursday evening an act enabling courts and authorities to order the blocking of illegal content online; 242 deputies voted in favor, 61 against, and 136 abstained, reports Bankier.pl. The bill is a response to President Karol Nawrocki’s January veto of an earlier version, which implemented the EU Digital Services Act (DSA). It will now go to the Senate.

The government presented the act as part of its response to the school attacks, and Donald Tusk noted that its earlier version had been vetoed (xyz.pl, Notes from Poland). According to one description, the act allows blocking content related to 27 prohibited acts. The president justified the veto by freedom of speech. DailyDrop has not yet seen the position of the Chancellery of the President on the new version.

Sejm rejects opposition motions and sends 2027 budget bill to committee: 237 to 198

On Thursday the Sejm rejected motions by PiS and the Direct Democracy circle to reject the draft 2027 budget in the first reading; 237 deputies voted against rejection and 198 for, report xyz.pl and Eska. The bill went to committee. According to the figures presented, revenue is to be PLN 696.4 billion, expenditure PLN 977.6 billion, and the deficit no more than PLN 281.2 billion. Other sources give slightly different values, e.g. a deficit of PLN 282.6 billion and revenue of about PLN 695 billion, which may stem from different versions of the draft.

According to the schedule, the second reading is to take place on December 2, and the third reading and vote on December 4; the act is to return from the Senate to the Sejm on January 15, 2027 (TVP Info). The government defends the plan, while the opposition speaks of a “leaky budget” (rp.pl).

WIG20 falls 0.63% on Thursday to 4,128.78 points; dollar costs PLN 3.9132 per NBP

The WIG20 index ended Thursday, October 8, at 4,128.78 points, 0.63% lower than on Wednesday, the ISBnews agency reported. The broad WIG fell 0.59% to 155,764.56 points. Two days earlier the WIG20 closed at a record 4,222.36 points.

The NBP average rate for the dollar was PLN 3.9132 (+0.05%), and for the euro PLN 4.3789 (TVP Info, Comparic). In the US the S&P 500 fell 0.47% to 7,765.36 points, and WTI crude rose 3.60% to $91.50; DailyDrop has not seen a justification for this jump in the reports it checked. An ounce of gold rose 0.20% to $4,157.0.
