Sejm passes nationwide ban on nighttime alcohol sales in shops and a total ban on advertising alcoholic beverages

Shop sales will be banned from 10 p.m. to 6 a.m., and the rules are due to take effect on January 1, 2027. The PSL says it will fight to protect winemakers in the Senate.

The Sejm has passed an amendment to the Act on Upbringing in Sobriety and Counteracting Alcoholism that introduces a nationwide ban on selling alcohol in shops between 10 p.m. and 6 a.m., except in catering venues, report TVP Info, money.pl and xyz.pl. The ban may be tightened further by local governments, to cover 9–10 p.m. and 6–9 a.m. as well. The act also provides for a total ban on advertising and promotion of all alcoholic beverages, including beer. The rules are due to take effect on January 1, 2027.

A total of 400 MPs voted for the amendment; according to xyz.pl nine voted against and four abstained, while money.pl reports that 29 MPs voted against. The bill is based on a proposal from the Left, considered together with a bill from Poland 2050. The PSL had earlier pushed for changes concerning the promotion of wine and says it will fight for them in the Senate. Earlier, on October 5, winemakers protested against fines of up to PLN 1 million (Polsat News). The act still has to pass the Senate and be signed by the president.

Committee backs bringing NBP governor Glapiński before the State Tribunal: 11 votes to 8

The Sejm Committee on Constitutional Accountability adopted on Friday, October 9, a report backing bringing NBP governor Adam Glapiński before the State Tribunal; 11 members voted in favor, 8 against and 2 abstained, report Interia, TVN24, rp.pl and money.pl. The committee upheld eight charges, including the charge that the bond purchases of 2020–2021 indirectly financed the deficit with at least PLN 144 billion.

This is only the beginning of the road. According to PAP, the Sejm may vote at the earliest 21 days after MPs have received the report, and money.pl unofficially writes of November; committee chair Zdzisław Gawlik does not know when the Sejm will take up the matter. The resolution requires an absolute majority with at least 230 MPs present, and the rules on this are questioned by a Constitutional Tribunal ruling, the consequences of which experts dispute.

Nobel Peace Prize goes to Navi Pillay, former UN High Commissioner for Human Rights

The Norwegian Nobel Committee announced in Oslo on Friday, October 9, that the 2026 Nobel Peace Prize went to South African lawyer Navanethem “Navi” Pillay, report Al Jazeera, CNN, Euronews, NPR and the UN. The committee honored her work for peace and international law and her efforts to see war crimes, crimes against humanity and genocide prosecuted. Pillay was active in the anti-apartheid movement, served as a judge of the International Criminal Tribunal for Rwanda and headed a UN commission whose findings concerned Gaza.

The committee failed to reach the laureate before the announcement; the director of the institute passed her the news later, and Pillay said she was surprised. The award ceremony is planned for December 10 in Oslo. Donald Trump later criticized the prize on Truth Social (see Inside). Earlier this week, the Nobel Prizes in medicine, physics, chemistry and literature were awarded.

Police checked more than 10,000 schools after the attacks, more teenagers detained

After the attacks in Ostrołęka and Leszczydół-Nowiny, police visited thousands of schools across the country on Friday, October 9, to reassure students, report Notes from Poland and Arab News; according to Euronews, police announced preventive checks at every primary and secondary school. Notes from Poland writes that more than 10,000 institutions were checked and at least ten more people, mostly teenagers, were detained in connection with threats.

Prime Minister Donald Tusk warned of a growing “cult of violence” online, and the education minister spoke of the radicalization of young people and announced tighter control of online content (Euronews, Notes from Poland). One of the ministers said that no link between the attacks had been confirmed. The perpetrator from Leszczydół will appear before a family court. DailyDrop has not seen an official tally of detentions or charges.

Trump: Russia will immediately deliver more than 300,000 tonnes of diesel to the American and world markets

Donald Trump wrote on Truth Social on Friday, October 9, that Russia would immediately deliver more than 300,000 tonnes of diesel to the American and world markets. The post is the president’s own statement; DailyDrop has not seen confirmation from the Kremlin or details of any deal. Polish US expert Tomasz Płudowski told Wirtualna Polska that Trump wants to lower fuel prices before the November 3 election and that an agreement with Moscow could mean sacrificing Ukraine for short-term gains.

Context: on September 13 Trump appealed to Volodymyr Zelensky to stop strikes on Russian diesel infrastructure, blaming them for a global shortage, and on September 21 he wrote that Russia had lost control of its diesel industry (Pravda, Illawarra Mercury). Analysts cited by spotmedia say that the export ban Russia introduced in response to the attacks took about 800,000 barrels of diesel a day off the market. For Poland, which imports diesel, this means a possible effect on wholesale prices, but DailyDrop has not seen an analysis of such an effect.
