Third school attack in a week: 21-year-old wounds three women with a knife at a post-secondary school in Warsaw’s Praga district

A school employee disarmed the attacker. Police do not link the case to the attacks in Ostrołęka and near Wyszków, and the motive is unknown.

On Saturday, October 10, a 21-year-old student attacked three women with a knife outside the secretary’s office of a school for adults on Mińska Street in Warsaw’s Praga district, according to TVN24, Rzeczpospolita, Interia and Polsat News. A member of the school’s staff took the knife from her and overpowered her, after which she was handed over to police. A 17-year-old girl was taken to hospital with cut wounds to the left forearm, and her life is not in danger; a 24-year-old woman who rushed to help was treated at the scene.

Witnesses told Polsat News that students barricaded themselves in a classroom on the second floor while someone tried to force their way in; one of the injured girls tried to take the knife from the attacker. According to the police’s initial findings, the attack was not planned, the attacker and the victims did not know each other, and police do not link the incident to the earlier attacks in Ostrołęka (October 7) and Leszczydół-Nowiny (October 8). The number of victims differs in the first reports (from two to three people); DailyDrop gives three, following the police.

Polish fighters scrambled again overnight, RCB sends alerts to two voivodeships; no violation recorded

In the night from Friday to Saturday, the Government Security Center (RCB) sent alerts to residents of the Podkarpackie and Lubelskie voivodeships because Polish aviation was operating in connection with Russian attacks on western Ukraine, report Polsat News and Interia. The Operational Command announced the scrambling of fighter jets; the operations were preventive, and no violation of Polish airspace was recorded.

According to Interia, in the morning, before 7 a.m., the RCB announced the end of the attack and no threat in Poland. Polsat News also writes about another evening alert; that text was updated, so DailyDrop does not give its times.

Zelensky: Russia “thanked” the US for easing sanctions on diesel with guided-bomb strikes on Zaporizhzhia

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky wrote on Telegram on Saturday, October 10, that Russia “thanked” the United States for easing sanctions on diesel by dropping guided bombs on homes in Zaporizhzhia. It is his own post; DailyDrop has seen no independent confirmation or official information about a change in sanctions.

A day earlier, Donald Trump wrote on Truth Social that Russia would immediately deliver more than 300,000 tons of diesel to the American and global market (see the October 10 edition). Observers cited by the media, including Wirtualna Polska, link this to Trump’s wish to lower fuel prices before the November 3 elections. Zaporizhzhia is regularly attacked with guided bombs (UNN; Zelensky said earlier of about 220 bombs a day against frontline towns).

Trump after the Nobel Prize for Pillay: “I settled eight wars”

Donald Trump wrote on Truth Social on Saturday, October 10, that he had resolved eight wars, in a post complaining about the lack of a Nobel Peace Prize. A day earlier he shared a post criticizing the Nobel committee and the laureate, Navi Pillay. These are his own posts; DailyDrop does not assess the truth of the claim about eight wars.

Trump had already repeated the claim on October 8 in the Oval Office, saying that refusing the prize would be a “great dishonor” to the committee (PTI/AP, Times of Oman, Deccan Chronicle). The Nobel Committee indicated that Pillay was honored for her work for peace and international law (Al Jazeera, CNN).
