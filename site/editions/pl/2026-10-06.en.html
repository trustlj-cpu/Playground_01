Tusk on Kosiniak-Kamysz’s conversation with Trump: it concerned Poland’s domestic situation, not military bases

The deputy prime minister confirmed that the meeting in Washington took place but does not reveal its content. Media report that Trump urged the PSL leader to leave the coalition; no official statement has confirmed this.

Deputy Prime Minister and head of the Ministry of National Defense (MON) Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz confirmed that he spoke with US President Donald Trump in Washington on March 2 but does not comment on its content, Notes from Poland reports. The meeting came to light after about seven months. According to Onet, citing a government official, Trump urged the PSL leader to leave the coalition and form a government with the right-wing opposition; Gazeta Wyborcza wrote that he proposed that the PSL switch coalitions (the reports are quoted by, among others, rp.pl and Wprost). The government has not confirmed this information, and one government source reportedly told Onet that the meeting changed nothing.

Prime Minister Donald Tusk said he had known about the conversation since March and that it concerned the domestic situation in Poland, not military bases; he gave no details. Kosiniak-Kamysz described the conversation as short and courteous, its main thread reportedly being thanks for the US troop presence in Poland, and added that he informed the prime minister about it right away. PiS MEP Adam Bielan says he helped organize the meeting and that it was about the bases; he and Nikodem Rachoń deny that a proposal to change the government was made. Brussels Signal writes of speculation that the US would like to see Tusk removed; DailyDrop has seen no evidence of this.

WIG20 rose 1.74% on Monday to 4,155.28 points; the dollar gained to PLN 3.9116 per NBP

The WIG20 index closed Monday, October 5, at 4,155.28 points, 1.74% higher than on Friday, the ISBnews agency reported. The broad WIG rose 1.44% to 156,543.51 points; shares of 185 companies rose, 156 fell, and 61 were unchanged. According to a commentary on xyz.pl, the session was a recovery from earlier losses, and the gains were driven by commodity companies, mainly Orlen and KGHM.

The NBP average rate for the dollar was PLN 3.9116 versus PLN 3.8881 on Friday, or 0.60% more, and for the euro PLN 4.3855 (+0.25%), per money.pl. Analysts quoted by xyz.pl point to rising bond yields and a stronger dollar as short-term risks for the stock market. In the US, the S&P 500 closed on Monday at 7,773.95 points (+0.66%).

Nobel Prize in Medicine for Deisseroth, Hegemann and Nagel for optogenetics

The Nobel Committee at the Karolinska Institute announced on Monday, October 5, that the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine goes to Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann and Georg Nagel “for discoveries concerning light-controlled ion channels and optogenetics,” according to Al Jazeera, Khaleej Times and SCMP. Hegemann of Humboldt University in Berlin and Nagel of the University of Würzburg identified the protein channelrhodopsin from a single-celled alga, which acts as an ion channel opened by blue light.

Deisseroth, who works at Stanford University and the Howard Hughes Medical Institute, turned this protein into a tool that makes it possible to switch the activity of individual neurons in a living brain on and off. The laureates are 54, 71 and 73 years old (Al Jazeera). DailyDrop saw no information in these reports about optogenetics-based therapies already used in humans, so it does not describe any.

Poland and Lithuania prepare a plan to evacuate a million residents of the Baltic states in case of war

Lithuania and Poland are jointly drawing up a plan for the international evacuation of the population of the Baltic states in case of war or a serious crisis, Charter 97, UNN and Novaya Gazeta Europe report. According to the Lithuanian interior minister, the scenario assumes about a million people, possibly more, who could be evacuated from the Baltic states to Poland. The first reports of a joint plan appeared at the end of September; the new element is the stated figure. The plan follows a Lithuanian mobilization exercise that for the first time included elements of cross-border evacuation of civilians.

Poland’s interior minister Marcin Kierwiński said that the Podlaskie Voivodeship would bear the greatest burden of receiving refugees and that the scenarios are still being refined (Charter 97). For comparison, according to a 2025 Reuters report cited by The Star, Latvia planned to evacuate about 630,000 of its 1.9 million residents, and Estonia about 140,000 of 1.4 million. The sources give no single breakdown of how many people would end up in Poland and how many would stay in their own countries.

Economy · Fuelxyz.pl, Comparic · citing announcements of the Minister of Energy

Maximum fuel prices for Tuesday: 95 petrol up to PLN 6.79, diesel up to PLN 7.82

A new announcement by the Minister of Energy, published on Monday, October 5, sets maximum prices for Tuesday, October 6: PLN 6.79 per liter of 95 petrol, about PLN 7.66 for 98 petrol and PLN 7.82 for diesel, according to xyz.pl. What is new compared with yesterday’s edition: the cap on 95 petrol rose by 6 groszy compared with the weekend (PLN 6.73), and on diesel it fell by 6 groszy (from PLN 7.88).

The “Lower Fuel Prices” program, under which a lower VAT and the caps apply, runs to the end of the year (Comparic). WTI crude fell 1.84% on Monday to $89.43, and Brent remains above $100; it is worth watching whether the change affects the caps in the coming days. DailyDrop saw no data on actual prices at filling stations.
