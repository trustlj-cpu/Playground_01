Knife attack at a vocational school in Ostrołęka: 19-year-old wounds nine people, had a homemade explosive device in his backpack

The attacker was detained after a few dozen minutes and charged with attempted murder. According to prosecutors, he left a backpack with an explosive in a computer lab, which sappers secured.

On Wednesday, October 7, a 19-year-old student attacked people with a knife at Vocational School Complex No. 4 in Ostrołęka, about 120 km north of Warsaw. According to PAP, nine people were injured: five students, two teachers and two bystanders; other media, including AP, give eight or ten, so the figure has not been definitively established. As TVN24 and Polsat News report, the victims are not in life-threatening danger, although AP wrote of one person in serious condition. The attacker was detained after about twenty to thirty minutes near the school.

The Ostrołęka Regional Prosecutor’s Office said the 19-year-old was charged, including with attempted murder, and that it would seek pretrial detention. According to prosecutors, he had a homemade explosive in his backpack, which he left in a computer lab; the backpack was secured by sappers (xyz.pl, Super Express). There were reports that in the morning he wrote on an internal forum about an attack on his 19th birthday, but neither police nor prosecutors have officially confirmed this. DailyDrop does not describe motives until investigators do.

WIG20 fell 1.59% on Wednesday to 4,155.11 points after a record Tuesday; the dollar gained to PLN 3.9111

The WIG20 index closed Wednesday, October 7, at 4,155.11 points, 1.59% lower than on Tuesday, when it set a record close of 4,222.36 points, PAP Biznes and ISBnews reported. The broad WIG lost 1.46%. According to analysts quoted by xyz.pl, the WIG20 remains in consolidation between 4,063 and 4,242 points, and the direction of the next move will be set only by a break out of that range.

The NBP average rate for the dollar was PLN 3.9111, up 0.63% from Tuesday (PLN 3.8868), and for the euro PLN 4.3797 (TVP Info). In the US, the S&P 500 closed at 7,801.77 points (−0.22%), WTI crude fell 1.30% to $88.28, and an ounce of gold fell 1.11% to $4,140.7.

Ministry of State Assets to review Paramount Skydance’s takeover of TVN as part of its acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery

The Ministry of State Assets will review the takeover of control of TVN after Paramount Skydance completed its acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery, money.pl, xyz.pl, TVP Info and Notes from Poland report. The review is carried out under rules on protecting companies deemed strategic; TVN was added to that list in 2024. The new owner should report the change after the fact, and the ministry has 90 days for an administrative procedure on the transaction’s impact on security or public order.

Minister Wojciech Balczun called it a “pure procedure” and reassured that the process is technical, not political (money.pl). The ministry may accept the ownership structure or bar the new owner from exercising control, and in an extreme case order a sale of stakes in TVN. The sources do not agree on whether Paramount has already filed a notification. DailyDrop has not seen the outcome of the procedure.

Poland · SecurityPress ✓ 4 outlets · per the Ministry of Interior and Administration (MSWiA)

In Sierpc police detained a 17-year-old with two knives who was planning a school attack

On Wednesday, October 7, police in Sierpc, Masovia, detained a 17-year-old after a teacher reported that the student had talked about hurting someone at school, TVP Info, Eska and Notes from Poland report. The detention was announced by Interior Minister Marcin Kierwiński. During a search of the home, officers found two knives packed in a case and a phone with information about a planned attack.

According to the minister, the boy searched online for information on how to organize this kind of bloody event. Eska writes that the detention also followed information from students, while the minister spoke of statements made on school grounds; the accounts are therefore not fully consistent. The case emerged on the day of the Ostrołęka attack, but there are no reports of a link between them. Police have not yet announced charges or a court decision.

Economy · FuelPress ✓ 3 outlets · per the Minister of Energy’s announcement

Maximum fuel prices for Thursday: 95 petrol up to PLN 6.83, diesel up to PLN 7.74

Energy Minister Miłosz Motyka announced on Wednesday, October 7, the maximum prices for Thursday, October 8: PLN 6.83 per liter of 95 petrol, PLN 7.71 for 98 petrol and PLN 7.74 for diesel, according to money.pl, Autocentrum and Otomoto. Compared with Wednesday (PLN 6.86, PLN 7.74 and PLN 7.80), 95 petrol is 3 groszy cheaper, 98 petrol 3 groszy cheaper and diesel 6 groszy cheaper; some sites describe the change differently because they count from Tuesday.

What is new compared with yesterday’s edition: the limits fall after two days of rises in petrol. Maximum prices are a ceiling, not a fixed price: stations may sell for less. The “Lower Fuel Prices” program runs to the end of the year. WTI fell 1.30% on Wednesday to $88.28.
