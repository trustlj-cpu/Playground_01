KOSPI surges 4.34% to close at 4,915.68, KOSDAQ up 8.97% to 708.99… buy-side sidecar triggered intraday

The market rebounded sharply two days after the plunge. The KOSDAQ kept widening its gain even after a buy-side sidecar was triggered at 10:22 a.m.

On the 27th the KOSPI closed at 4,915.68, up 4.34% from the previous session. The day’s gain was 204.47 points, bringing the index, which had plunged 6.16% on the 25th and slipped as far as 4,697.51, back close to its pre-plunge level. The KOSDAQ jumped even more, closing at 708.99, up 8.97%. The KOSDAQ was up nearly 4% early in the session, and when KOSDAQ 150 futures rose more than 6% from the previous session’s close at 10:22 a.m., a buy-side sidecar was triggered. A sidecar is a device that halts the effect of program buy orders for five minutes when futures prices suddenly move sharply, cushioning the shock to the market. This was the 41st sidecar on the KOSDAQ this year, 23 on the buy side and 18 on the sell side. Right after the trigger, at 11:02, the KOSDAQ traded at 694.37, up 5.90%, and the gain widened further afterward.

The KOSPI opened flat, was up 2% to 3% during the session, then rose into the 4% range at the close. On the 26th, with the New York market closed for Thanksgiving, major European markets rebounded. By sector, robot and bio stocks rose sharply while semiconductors paused. At 9:39 a.m. Samsung Electronics was down 0.23% at 170,900 won and SK hynix was down 1.80% at 1,034,000 won. A day earlier, on the 26th, the KOSPI closed up 0.29% at 4,711.21 as the Bank of Korea raised its base rate to 3.25%. The scale of net buying by foreigners and institutions that day and the final sector gains and losses were not confirmed.

Police raid Justice Ministry, Prosecution Service in ‘Han Dong-hoon investigation-file leak’ probe… Han: “the Democratic Party’s cane”

On the 27th police carried out a search and seizure at the Justice Ministry, the Prosecution Service and other bodies in connection with the alleged leak of prosecution investigation files involving independent lawmaker Han Dong-hoon. It is a compulsory investigation to establish how the indictment plan for former Justice Minister nominee Kim Seung-won came to be in Han’s hands. An indictment plan is an internal document prosecutors draw up before sending a case to trial. The allegation began when Democratic Party lawmaker Kim Dong-a filed a complaint with police, saying Han had made the document public while raising allegations that nominee Kim had been solicited for approval of a new-drug clinical trial. Han has said that no cooperation or materials were ever received from prosecutors. When the search and seizure became known, Han pushed back, saying police had become “the Democratic Party’s cane” rather than “the people’s cane,” telling them to go ahead and do as they pleased, and vowing to proceed with eyes only on the public even if the road is roundabout. The Justice Ministry had earlier stated that providing investigation materials to outsiders could constitute crimes such as disclosure of official secrets, and the Prosecution Service had said its own inquiry has limits and an investigation is needed. The full range of institutions targeted by the search and the date of Han’s summons are not confirmed.

Judiciary · Residence relocation trial✓ 4 outlets · whether there will be an appeal and details of the ruling’s reasoning unconfirmed

Former Presidential Chief of Staff Kim Dae-ki gets 2 years, suspended for 3, at first trial over ‘diversion of residence relocation budget’… former Minister Lee Sang-min acquitted

Criminal Division 36 of the Seoul Central District Court (presiding judge Lee Jung-yeop) on the 27th sentenced former Presidential Chief of Staff Kim Dae-ki to two years in prison, suspended for three years, for ordering the illegal diversion of budget during the relocation of the presidential residence under the Yoon Suk Yeol government. The charge is abuse of authority and obstruction of rights. Yoon Jae-sun, former general affairs secretary, and Kim O-jin, former facilities management secretary, who were indicted together with Kim, each received one year in prison, suspended for two years. Former Interior and Safety Minister Lee Sang-min was acquitted. They were accused of diverting about 2.8 billion won of the Ministry of the Interior and Safety budget in the 2022 residence relocation work in order to pay construction money to an unqualified contractor. A suspended sentence sets a punishment but defers its execution for a set period, and if the person stays out of trouble during that period, the sentence loses effect. The defense denied the charges at trial, and whether there will be an appeal is not yet known.

Bank of Korea raises base rate 0.25 percentage point to 3.25%… third hike this year, 5 of 7 Board members in favor

The Bank of Korea’s Monetary Policy Board raised the base rate by 0.25 percentage point to 3.25% on the 26th. It is the third increase this year, following July and August, and comes three months after August. Five of the seven members voted for the increase and two gave an opinion in favor of holding. The Bank raised its growth forecast for next year from 1.8% to 4.1% within nine months. Governor Shin Hyun-song said nominal GDP this year would exceed 3,000 trillion won and semiconductor-sector operating profit would reach 700 trillion won. The governor projected private consumption to grow 2.4% next year and said the Bank would respond preemptively so that inflation converges to the 2028 target level.

When the rate rises, bank lending rates follow. Bank household loan rates already exceed 5%, and forecasts say that loan rates around 5% will climb further with this increase. The October Board held rates by a unanimous vote of all members. This time too the stance keeps open the possibility of further increases while slowing the pace. The timing of the next increase is not confirmed.

Cheating at this year’s CSAT up 70% to 474 cases… smart-device detections from 31 to 112, ruling and opposition parties alike rebuke at Education Committee

In a work report submitted to the National Assembly Education Committee on the 27th, the Ministry of Education said that detected cheating in the 2027 College Scholastic Ability Test (CSAT), held on the 19th, came to 474 cases, about 70% more than the 279 a year earlier. Smart-device-related cases rose 3.6 times, from 31 to 112. On the 23rd, criminal complaints were filed against six serious offenders who used smart devices. Although exam management had been tightened sharply over fears of artificial intelligence (AI) cheating, detections still rose, so lawmakers from both ruling and opposition parties rebuked the measures in unison at the Education Committee plenary meeting that day.

Acting Education Minister Choi Eun-ok said that no cheating using smart devices was detected at the university-administered exams that 14 universities held after the CSAT. The Ministry received 306 reports and tips and pays a reward of at least 100 million won when cheating is confirmed. The Jeju Office of Education runs full inspections with 151 metal detectors at all high schools, and at Jeonbuk high schools, if smart-device cheating is found on an internal-grade exam, all subjects that day are scored zero.
