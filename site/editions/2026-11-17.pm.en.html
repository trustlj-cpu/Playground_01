Tax accountant exam top scorer appears at police as a suspect… “I will take responsibility for what I did wrong”

The top passer of the 63rd Tax Accountant Exam, who surrendered over AI cheating on the 15th, came to the Seoul Metropolitan Police Agency on the morning of the 17th. Whether there were accomplices and how the cheating was done have not yet been disclosed.

The top passer of the 63rd Tax Accountant Exam appeared as a suspect at the wide-area investigation unit building of the Seoul Metropolitan Police Agency in Mapo District, Seoul, at about 9:17 a.m. on the 17th. The candidate told reporters, “I will cooperate sincerely with the investigation and take responsibility for what I did wrong,” but did not answer questions about whether the plan was made alone or how the equipment was obtained. On the 15th, on the lawyer’s YouTube channel, the top passer said AI had been used to cheat on the first- and second-stage tax accountant exams and the first-stage Certified Public Accountant exam, and filed a letter of surrender the same day. The candidate claimed to have photographed the exam questions with a tiny camera, sent them to an AI and received the answers on a smartphone. The camera, laptop, mobile phone and other items were handed over to police.

Police are considering applying charges of obstruction of official duties by deception and obstruction of business. Put simply, the question is whether the exam administration was obstructed by false means. The key issue in the investigation is whether there were accomplices, and the candidate’s side was reported to have said there were none. Police have not disclosed the specific method. Earlier, on the 16th, the government began procedures to invalidate this passer’s exam result, and an exam eligibility suspension of five years was reported. A day after the government formed a response task force, the case moved from the surrender stage to a formal investigation. Cheong Wa Dae ordered on the 15th a full inspection of suspected AI cheating across all national qualification exams, and the Korean Association of Tax Accountants said it would strengthen verification of probationary tax accountants. Whether there are other fraudulent passers besides this candidate has not been confirmed. With the 2027 CSAT two days away, on the 19th, how exam supervision deals with AI is also drawing attention.

KOSPI falls below the 5,000 mark at the close for the first time in 9 months… foreign investors net sell in the 1 trillion won range

On the 17th the KOSPI closed at 4,968.48, down 132.02 points (2.59%) from the previous session. The closing level slipping below the 5,000 mark is the first time in about nine months, since February 2 this year. It opened at 5,008.09 and at one point during the session climbed to 5,084.06, but foreign selling grew in the afternoon and the losses widened. Foreign net selling came to around 1.4 trillion won (the figure differs slightly depending on when it is tallied), while institutions and individuals net bought in the 500 billion won range and the 400 billion won range respectively but could not hold the index up. The KOSDAQ closed at 650.06, down 15.59 points (2.34%). Samsung Electronics ended down 3.30% at 190,300 won and SK hynix down 1.96% at 1,098,000 won. Brokerages point to the sharp fall in AI-related US stocks the previous day and a chip research firm’s cut to its 2027 and 2028 AI accelerator demand outlook as burdens. Net selling means investors sold more than they bought. Nvidia’s earnings release scheduled this week is cited as the next variable.

Several Russian military aircraft enter and leave air defense identification zone in East Sea… Joint Chiefs: “fighter jets deployed”

The Joint Chiefs of Staff said that early on the 17th several Russian military aircraft entered and then left the Korea Air Defense Identification Zone (KADIZ) on the East Sea side. The Joint Chiefs explained that they had identified the flight paths before entry and deployed Air Force fighter jets to take tactical measures against contingencies. There was no violation of airspace. Reports say fewer than 10 aircraft entered, but the exact number and types are unconfirmed.

An Air Defense Identification Zone is an area each country sets above the sea beyond its airspace for security reasons, and unlike airspace it is not a place where sovereignty extends under international law. Russia does not recognize this zone, and there were reports that it entered again without prior notice. It is the first time Russian military aircraft alone have entered the zone since August 25, about three months ago. On June 27, more than 10 Chinese and Russian military aircraft moved in and out of the East Sea and South Sea zones.

October new COFIX at 3.62%, highest in 2 years 8 months… variable-rate mortgage interest rises from the 17th

The Korea Federation of Banks announced on the 16th that the October COFIX on a new-amount basis was 3.62% a year, up 0.19 percentage point from the previous month’s 3.43%. It is the highest in 2 years 8 months, since February 2024. It rose in succession in September and October. COFIX is an indicator of the average cost banks pay to raise funds through deposits, bonds and the like, and banks use it as the benchmark to set variable-rate mortgage interest.

The COFIX on an outstanding-balance basis also rose 0.11 percentage point to 3.26%, the highest in 1 year 7 months. Reflecting this, banks raise interest on new mortgages from the 17th. The lower end of KB Kookmin Bank’s 6-month variable-rate range rises from 4.66% to 4.85%, and Woori Bank is also reported to be raising its rate. The rate applied to an individual differs by bank and product and must be checked separately.

President Lee Jae-myung and Prime Minister Takaichi hold fourth Korea-Japan summit in Manila… agreements unconfirmed

President Lee Jae-myung and Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi are to hold a bilateral summit in Manila, the Philippines, on the 17th. It comes six months after the May 19 meeting in Andong, North Gyeongsang, and is their fourth meeting, counting the talks on the sidelines of last October’s Gyeongju APEC and this January’s meeting in Nara Prefecture, Japan. It has continued within the framework of shuttle diplomacy, in which the two leaders take turns visiting each other’s country.

The agenda of this meeting was reported to be economic and energy cooperation and security. Energy supply chain stability and regional security issues were expected to be coordinated together. It was reported that a separate meeting between Prime Minister Takaichi and President Xi Jinping was not on the announced schedule. What was agreed at the talks and whether a joint statement was issued have not been confirmed.
