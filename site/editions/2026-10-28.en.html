KOSPI jumps into the 4% range, triggering a buy-side sidecar… closes up 3.09%

Kyunghyang Shinmun and others reported that news of Anthropic’s push for an initial public offering spilled over into chip stocks

Kyunghyang Shinmun reported that the Korea Exchange announced it had triggered a buy-side sidecar on the KOSPI market at 10:13:53 a.m. on the 27th. According to Dong-A Ilbo, KOSPI 200 futures stayed more than 5% above the previous session’s close for over a minute, and when the sidecar was triggered the futures were up 5.05% at 893.02. Yonhap reported that the KOSPI rebounded in the 4% range on a recovery in chip-sector investor sentiment, and Kyunghyang Shinmun reported that Samsung Electronics and SK hynix rose 5 to 7%. As the backdrop, Kyunghyang Shinmun wrote that news of U.S. AI company Anthropic’s push for an initial public offering is interpreted as having worked in favor of domestic chip stocks, and SBS reported that as of 9:30 a.m., on news of Anthropic’s Nasdaq listing, SK hynix was up in the 6% range and Samsung Electronics in the 5% range.

Yonhap reported that on the 26th (local time) all three major New York indexes rose together, with the Nasdaq up 0.8%. Chosun Ilbo reported that Kim Dong-won, head of research at KB Securities, expected additional shareholder returns of 120 trillion won in total, including 80 trillion won from Samsung Electronics and 40 trillion won from SK hynix, and Yonhap also reported the same KB Securities forecast. Korea Economic Daily reported that the KOSPI opened strongly, up in the 3% range, early in the session. The KOSPI closed the day at 5,638.34, up 3.09% from the previous session. A 3Pro TV closing-market broadcast presented the KOSPI’s 3% rebound as a response to news of Anthropic’s improved cost efficiency.

Supreme Court upholds 3-year sentence for ex-Marine 1st Division chief Lim Seong-geun… 3 years and 3 months after Cpl. Chae’s death on duty

Dong-A Ilbo reported that Supreme Court Division 1 (presiding justice Shin Sook-hee) on the 27th upheld the lower-court ruling that sentenced Lim Seong-geun, former commander of the Marine Corps 1st Division, to 3 years in prison on charges including professional negligence resulting in death or injury. Yonhap and Chosun Ilbo reported that this was the final ruling 3 years and 3 months after the accident in which Marine Cpl. Chae Su-geun died on duty. According to Dong-A Ilbo, Lim was accused of ordering a search for missing persons in Yecheon, North Gyeongsang Province, in July 2023 without issuing safety equipment, leaving Cpl. Chae swept away by a rapid current and dead. Yonhap reported that the Marine special counsel said the finalized guilty verdict ‘leaves trust that lets people send their children to the military and feel at ease’, and Kyunghyang Shinmun reported that the overall brigade commander received 1 year and 6 months of imprisonment without labor and that imprisonment without labor was finalized for the battalion commander who reported directly to him and others.

Korea’s economy grows 1.0% quarter on quarter in Q3… three times the Bank of Korea’s forecast

Kyunghyang Shinmun and Electronic Times reported that the Bank of Korea said on the 27th that the advance estimate of this year’s third-quarter real gross domestic product (GDP) growth was 1.0% from the previous quarter. According to Dong-A Ilbo, that is 0.7 percentage points above the 0.3% forecast the BOK gave in August, and up 3.4% from a year earlier. Yonhap reported that growth was driven by rising chip exports and equipment investment, while Electronic Times reported that government consumption and equipment investment drove growth whereas private consumption went backward. Electronic Times wrote that gross domestic income (GDI), which shows real purchasing power, grew by the widest margin in about 49 years.

Dong-A Ilbo reported that even if real GDP stays flat in the fourth quarter, full-year growth this year is estimated to reach 3.6%, and Yonhap reported the same thing. Kyunghyang Shinmun reported that the BOK’s previous full-year forecast was 3.3%. Electronic Times reported that in a same-day release the Ministry of Finance and Economy projected growth in the mid-3% range this year and per capita gross national income (GNI) entering the $40,000 range. Chosun Ilbo ran the advance estimate under a headline of a third straight quarter of ‘surprise growth’ amid the chip boom.

Supreme Court issues additional detention warrant for Kim Keon-hee… ‘risk of destroying evidence or fleeing’

Kyunghyang Shinmun reported that from 2:30 p.m. on the 27th, in Supreme Court Courtroom 1, Supreme Court Division 2 (presiding justice Oh Kyung-mi) held a hearing on an additional detention warrant for Kim Keon-hee, whose final appeal on charges including influence peddling under the Act on the Aggravated Punishment of Specific Crimes is under way. Yonhap reported that the hearing ended after 45 minutes and that Kim spoke in person. According to Dong-A Ilbo, the court issued the warrant the special counsel had requested, giving as its reason that ‘there is a risk of destroying evidence or fleeing’. Kim, whose detention period was due to expire on the 31st of this month, will now stand trial in detention.

Yonhap reported that the detention period was extended by 2 months, to the end of this year, while Kyunghyang Shinmun wrote in its headline that detention can be extended by up to 8 months. Dong-A Ilbo reported that Kim pleaded ‘I am in poor health’ but the Supreme Court issued the warrant. KBS reported in an exclusive that Kim’s side holds that the additional detention is a ‘separate-case detention’. Hankyoreh reported that the Democratic Party called the issuance of the warrant a ‘natural outcome’ and urged the Supreme Court to rule swiftly and strictly.

Fire at Gangnam’s Guryong Village brought under control after 10 hours 30 minutes… about 100 residents lose their homes

Yonhap reported that a fire broke out on the afternoon of the 27th in Guryong Village, Gaepo-dong, Gangnam District, Seoul, sending up heavy smoke. Yonhap reported that 39 residents of the area, known as ‘Gangnam’s last shantytown’, evacuated and that no one was injured, and also that the stretch beside the Daemo Underpass on Yangjae-daero was fully closed. According to Kyunghyang Shinmun, fire authorities issued a Level 1 response and then raised it to Level 2, and SBS reported that fire authorities, judging that the fire could spread, deployed 6 helicopters.

Yonhap reported in a breaking story that the main flames were brought under control in the early hours of the 28th, 10 hours 30 minutes after the fire began. Kyunghyang Shinmun reported that the homes of about 100 residents burned and that residents vented their anger, saying ‘This is the second big fire this year, and no one is responsible’. Korea Economic Daily reported that 77 households and 107 people were affected and moved to emergency shelters.
