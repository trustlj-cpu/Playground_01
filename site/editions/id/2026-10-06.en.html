Indonesia win the inaugural FIFA ASEAN Cup, beating Thailand 4-2 on penalties at SUGBK

The score was 2-2 after extra time. Elkan Baggott equalized in the 115th minute, then Emil Audero saved one Thai kick. The title ends a 35-year drought for the senior national team.

Indonesia’s national team won the 2026 FIFA ASEAN Cup by beating Thailand 4-2 in a penalty shootout at Gelora Bung Karno Main Stadium (SUGBK) in Jakarta on Monday night, October 5, according to RCTI+, Investortrust and Akurat. The match ended 2-2 after extra time. RCTI+ and Goodstats reported 1-1 in regular time; Indonesia went ahead first through a Dean James free kick that deflected off a Thai player in the 82nd minute.

In extra time, Peeradol Chamrasamee put Thailand ahead in the 111th minute, but Elkan Baggott leveled the score four minutes later. In the shootout Kevin Diks, Dean James, Baggott and Shayne Pattynama scored for Indonesia. Thailand converted only twice, through Manuel Bihr and Waris Choolthong; Sarach Yooyen and Nattapong Sayriya missed, with goalkeeper Emil Audero reported to have saved one kick.

The tournament was a first edition, and the title is the senior national team’s first in 35 years, according to RCTI+ and Setkab. President Prabowo Subianto attended the match and said he was glad and proud (Investortrust, Setkab). The media have not reported an official attendance figure; about 67,000 tickets were earlier reported sold.

Jakarta Composite Index surges 1.36% to 6,118.86 on Monday, rupiah around Rp17,916

The Jakarta Composite Index (IHSG) closed up 81.97 points, or 1.36%, at 6,118.86 on Monday, October 5, from 6,036.89 on Friday, according to CNBC Indonesia, Bisnis and Databoks. The index dipped to 6,019.84 before turning around. Ten of eleven sectors gained; property led with a 2.31% rise and technology was the only decliner. Shares of BRPT rose 4.58% to Rp1,485, while GOTO fell about 3.33%. In all, 526 shares rose, 153 fell and 114 were unchanged, with turnover of about Rp9.6 trillion to Rp9.97 trillion.

For the rupiah, DailyDrop has found only one source so far. Databoks recorded a spot rate of Rp17,916.4 per US dollar at 4:46 p.m. WIB, about 0.36% weaker than the previous close by its own count. That figure differs from the Rp17,882 Bloomberg reported for Friday, so read it as an estimate, not an official close.

Prabowo inaugurates 204 new TNI units on the 81st anniversary, including six Kodam

President Prabowo Subianto led the ceremony for the 81st anniversary of the TNI at Prima Field at the TNI headquarters in Cilangkap, East Jakarta, on Monday, October 5, Investortrust and RCTI+ said. The venue was moved from the original plan at Monas. At the ceremony Prabowo inaugurated 204 new units: six Regional Military Commands (Kodam), 30 Territorial Development Infantry Brigades, 150 Territorial Development Infantry Battalions, three Combat Support Battalions, one brigade and four battalions of the Marine Corps, and ten new Air Force units, according to Investortrust and Bisnis. He also installed six Kodam commanders.

In his address Prabowo stressed the vastness of Indonesia’s territory, which he compared with Europe, so that defense must reach the whole country. He named the people’s hardships as a responsibility of the TNI. Former Presidents Joko Widodo and Susilo Bambang Yudhoyono and Vice President Gibran Rakabuming Raka attended, RCTI+ and Journalarta said. This year’s theme is ‘A Strong TNI Together with the People, a Sovereign, Just and Prosperous Indonesia’. DailyDrop has not seen budget figures for the new units.

OPEC+ holds November production target, WTI crude falls 1.84% to US$89.43

Seven core OPEC+ countries (Saudi Arabia, Russia, Iraq, Kuwait, Algeria, Kazakhstan and Oman) agreed at an online meeting on Sunday, October 4 to keep the November production target at September’s level, according to detik, JawaPos and Enerdata. Total requested output stays at about 31.01 million barrels per day. The move extends a pause after several consecutive increases. The reason cited: Gulf states’ production is still far below target because of export disruptions from the US-Israel war with Iran, with exports running at 60% to 80% of normal. The next meeting is scheduled for November 1.

In the market, WTI closed down 1.84% at US$89.43 a barrel on Monday, according to market closing data, while the S&P 500 rose 0.66% to 7,773.95 and gold fell 0.13% to US$4,156.8. For Indonesia, oil staying around US$90 weighs on energy import costs and keeps pressure on the rupiah. Pertamina said subsidized fuel prices are unchanged until the end of the year, so the burden of the price gap is borne by the state.

Bonus for the champion national team: Prabowo leaves it to PSSI, Erick Thohir says the president will add to it

Asked about player bonuses after the final, President Prabowo said the matter is handled by the PSSI chairman, according to RCTI+ and RMOL. PSSI chairman Erick Thohir, who is also Minister of Youth and Sports, answered ‘Ready’ (Siap). He told reporters the president will add to the bonus but gave neither a figure nor a form, and called the title a foundation for World Cup ambitions while warning that the challenges remain heavy (RCTI+, Akurat).

Prabowo also said he was glad, grateful and proud of the team’s efforts, and praised the players’ refusal to give up (Investortrust, Industry.co.id). DailyDrop has not seen an agreed bonus amount, so figures circulating on social media cannot be taken as correct.
