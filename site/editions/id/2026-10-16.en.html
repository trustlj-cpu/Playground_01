Incident · South TangerangPress ✓ 5 outlets · casualties and ethylene find according to detikcom, Liputan6, Republika; investigation according to Antara

Blast at Cimanggis Market kills two and injures 10 to 12 others; police find 52 kg of ethylene, rule out a bomb, cause awaits Puslabfor

The explosion on the night of Wednesday, October 14 hit a fruit warehouse in the market area and damaged four blocks of shophouses. Police have secured 18 CCTV recordings and are waiting for forensic laboratory results; the National Nutrition Agency denied that the building was a kitchen of the free nutritious meals program.

A loud explosion shook the Cimanggis Market area in Ciputat, South Tangerang, on the night of Wednesday, October 14, according to Republika, detikcom, Liputan6 and Antara. Two people died; detikcom wrote that the bodies were found under the rubble of the building. The number of injured differs between reports: Republika counted 12 casualties including the two who died, while detikcom wrote two dead and 11 injured, and in another report 12 injured. For that reason DailyDrop uses a range of 10 to 12 injured until there is an official figure.

Four blocks of shophouses were damaged, according to Republika and Liputan6. The site of the blast was a fruit storage and packing warehouse, said Republika and detikcom, and Republika wrote that Puslabfor suspects the source was a fruit-ripening room. Police found 52 kilograms of ethylene gas at the site, according to detikcom and detikNews; Liputan6 mentioned the discovery of ethylene canisters. Earlier, detikcom had suggested the blast was suspected to come from a gas cylinder, so the ethylene find is not yet the same as an official conclusion on the cause.

Police confirmed that the blast was not a bomb, according to Liputan6 and detikcom. The Gegana bomb squad and bomb technicians were sent to the site on Wednesday night, said detikNews and Republika, and the Jakarta Regional Police (Polda Metro Jaya) sent a DVI team to handle the victims, according to Antara. On Thursday, October 15, the Police Forensic Laboratory Center (Puslabfor) of the Criminal Investigation Agency processed the crime scene (TKP), and Antara wrote that police secured 18 CCTV recordings. The Jakarta Regional Police are waiting for the Puslabfor results to determine the exact cause, said Antara.

Questions have also arisen about the free nutritious meals program. Republika wrote that police are looking into the possibility that the blast came from a supply warehouse of an SPPG. BGN spokesperson Tysa Novenny denied it: the building that exploded was not an SPPG, said Republika. DailyDrop has not seen an explanation from police about the link between that warehouse and the program’s kitchen suppliers, so the connection is unconfirmed.

Two victims with severe burns are receiving further treatment at Pamulang General Hospital, according to Republika. A resident told detikcom that the blast felt as if it lifted him off the ground. For readers, the lesson is practical: storing industrial gas and flammable materials in a densely built area requires permits, ventilation and routine cylinder inspections. What to wait for from further sources is the Puslabfor result, who owns and manages the warehouse, whether the gas was stored in line with its permit, and whether police name a suspect. As of this edition’s close, nothing has been announced.

Environment · Forest firesPress ✓ 5 outlets · personnel according to Antara; flights according to Republika, Antara

Forest-fire haze thickens: Palangka Raya hazardous, flights disrupted, Malaysian lawmaker speaks of legal action

Smoke from forest and land fires (karhutla) still blanketed Kalimantan and Sumatra on Thursday, October 15. Antara wrote that air quality in Palangka Raya reached the hazardous category, and reported that three domestic flights at Kualanamu were canceled because of the smoke. Republika wrote that Tjilik Riwut Airport briefly halted flights on Wednesday night. House Commission V said 1,813 flights were affected, according to Republika; we have not seen that figure in a second outlet. Detikcom wrote that pupils from kindergarten to junior high in Parepare studied online.

Law Minister Supratman Andi Agtas said the fires are occurring in six provinces and 60,000 personnel have been deployed, according to Antara; two small portals wrote 70,000 combined personnel, so the figure is not certain. The Forestry Minister said a land route will be opened to Tanjung Puting, said Antara; fires in peatland are hard to put out. North Sumatra Governor Bobby Nasution is preparing weather modification, according to Antara and Republika. A man in Samarinda died, surrounded by flames, while trying to stop the fire from spreading to homes, according to detikcom, Liputan6 and Antara.

The smoke crosses borders. Detikcom wrote that smoke blanketed Singapore. Republika wrote that Malaysian lawmaker Lim Guan Eng urged his government to consider legal steps to demand compensation; that is the proposal of one lawmaker, not a decision of the Malaysian government. Coordinating Minister Yusril Ihza Mahendra said he will coordinate with the State Secretary (Mensesneg) on a lawsuit over the handling of fires in West Kalimantan, according to Antara and Republika. Residents of smoky areas should wear masks and monitor air quality.

Law · CorruptionPress ✓ 5 outlets · sting according to Antara, Republika, detikNews; inauguration according to Republika, detikNews, Antara

KPK uncovers alleged corruption of BNPB ready-to-use funds; Prabowo inaugurates Lt. Gen. Kristomei Sianturi as BNPB head amid forest fires

The Corruption Eradication Commission (KPK) ran a sting operation (OTT) at BNPB and detained five people on Wednesday, October 14, according to Antara. DetikNews wrote that the evidence included Rp3.3 billion in cash, crypto and hundreds of grams of gold. The number of suspects was reported differently: Republika wrote that five people were raised to suspect status, detikcom wrote four. KPK did not name BNPB Principal Secretary Rustian a suspect even though he was caught up in the operation, according to Republika, which also carried an explanation from the KPK chair. KPK suspects the misuse of ready-to-use funds took place since 2022, said Republika and Antara. Republika and Liputan6 wrote that the BNPB head’s younger brother is also a suspect; DailyDrop has not seen an explanation from KPK about his role.

On Thursday afternoon Prabowo inaugurated Lt. Gen. TNI Kristomei Sianturi, previously Pangdam XXI/Radin Inten, as BNPB head at the State Palace, according to Republika, detikNews and Antara. Kristomei left the handling of the OTT case to law enforcement, and KPK stressed that the investigation is proceeding according to procedure, according to Antara. Deputy Chair of House Commission VIII Singgih Januratmoko asked him to fix the institution’s integrity, said Antara. House Commission III member Abdullah called the case ironic because it was exposed just as the forest fires spread, according to Republika.

Law · KPKPress ✓ 4 outlets · Antara, Republika, detikNews, Kompas.com

KPK arrests 20 people in sting on Klaten regent, including Central Java BPK head; about Rp1 billion seized

KPK confirmed on Thursday, October 15 that it ran an OTT in Klaten, Central Java, according to Antara. Klaten Regent Hamenang Wajar Ismoyo is among the 20 people secured, and KPK seized about Rp1 billion in cash, said Antara. Antara also wrote that the head of the BPK Central Java office was arrested as well. According to a detikNews headline, the case relates to alleged project bribery and tampering with a BPK audit; Republika wrote that those arrested were being questioned at the Klaten Police (Polres Klaten).

DailyDrop has not seen a formal naming of suspects in the available sources. The case emerged a day after KPK detained five people in the BNPB case. What to watch: whether KPK announces suspects, the value of the alleged bribe, and the link between the Klaten project and the audit result.

Markets · Global economyPress ✓ 6 outlets · IHSG according to Antara, Republika, CNBC Indonesia, detikFinance; global according to Reuters, Bloomberg

IHSG falls more than 2% on Thursday after US inflation beats forecasts and chip stocks sell off; rupiah opens at Rp17,948

The IHSG opened 25.89 points, or 0.44 percent, lower at 5,917 on Thursday, October 15, according to Antara, then fell deeper: Republika wrote that it plunged 2.20 percent, while CNBC Indonesia and detikFinance wrote that it was down as much as about 2 percent. DailyDrop does not yet have Thursday’s closing figure from two sources, so the left ear of this page shows “—”. Antara linked the weakness to Asian bourses falling after US inflation came in higher than forecast; observer Elandry Pratama cited a combination of global and domestic sentiment, said Republika. The rupiah opened 45 points, or 0.25 percent, weaker at Rp17,948 per US dollar, according to Antara, and Bank Indonesia said it continues to be present in the market.

From abroad: Reuters reported that US consumer prices rose faster in September because gasoline was more expensive, with stronger core inflation. Reuters also wrote that Asian bourses sold off as AI-related stocks tumbled, and the dollar retreated from an 18-month high after Fed officials dampened bets on a rate hike; in its exclusive report Reuters quoted Fed official Daly as saying a rate hike will probably be needed but is not urgent. Reuters and Bloomberg wrote that US chip stocks were under pressure; Reuters said US$2 trillion in value was at risk of being lost, Bloomberg said about US$1 trillion, two figures we have not reconciled. Oil fell as worries about Middle East supply eased, according to Bloomberg and Reuters, after the IEA approved the release of the remaining 100 million barrels, bringing total stock releases to more than 400 million barrels (Reuters, Bloomberg).

We are not printing the Wall Street close because the figure is not yet confirmed by two sources, and Thursday’s session ended only after this edition. For readers who hold shares or buy imported goods: a weak rupiah and a volatile exchange mean prices can move quickly when the exchange opens this morning.
