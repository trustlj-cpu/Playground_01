KOSPI jumps 4.39% to close at 5,239.13… buy-side sidecar on both markets right after the open, KOSDAQ also up 4.48%

The KOSPI, which stood at 5,018.85 the day before, rose to 5,239.13 in a single day. The KOSDAQ finished at 667.61.

On the 10th the KOSPI closed at 5,239.13, up 220.28 points (4.39%) from the previous trading day. The KOSDAQ also gained 28.64 points (4.48%) to finish at 667.61. Both markets soared from the open, and the Korea Exchange triggered a buy-side sidecar on the KOSPI market at around 9:06 a.m. and on the KOSDAQ market at around 9:10 a.m. A sidecar is a device that suspends the effect of program trading orders for 5 minutes when futures prices move sharply at once; when it is triggered by a rise, it is called a buy-side sidecar. During the session both indexes at one point climbed into the 5% range before the gains narrowed, and foreign investors and institutions both moved into net buying.

The closes on the 9th were 5,018.85 for the KOSPI and 638.97 for the KOSDAQ. Someone who had put 1 million won into the KOSPI would have 1,043,900 won after a single day. Still, the index remains 144.67 points short of the close on the 5th, when it fell 5.28% in one day to finish at 5,383.80. That day sell-side sidecars were triggered on both the KOSPI and the KOSDAQ, and the figure for foreign net selling differed from tally to tally, from the mid-3 trillion won range to the upper-3 trillion won range. On the 9th the US market closed with the Nasdaq up 2.74% and the S&P 500 up 2.35%. No institution or person has been found to have directly explained this surge, and whether foreign net buying will continue is not yet known. Whether the 5,200 level holds in the session on the 11th is the next thing to check.

Cabinet approves consent motion to expand Cheonghae Unit’s area of operations and send 1 more maritime patrol aircraft… Defense Ministry: “We do not get involved or intervene in war”

On the 10th the government approved, at a Cabinet meeting chaired by President Lee Jae-myung at Cheong Wa Dae, a consent motion on extending the Cheonghae Unit’s deployment. The motion widens the unit’s mission area and sends 1 additional patrol aircraft, and only the procedure of National Assembly consent remains. According to the plan the Defense Ministry made public on the 5th of this month, the mission area expands to waters about 650 km from the Strait of Hormuz, and the current force of about 320 grows by 75 to 395. The deployment period runs from the time of National Assembly consent to December 31 next year, while the current deployment deadline is December 31 this year. Whether the details in the approved motion match the plan of the 5th has not been confirmed. The Defense Ministry spokesperson said the mission will be carried out under “the principle of never getting involved or intervening in war.” The ministry also set out four deployment principles, including that the patrol aircraft will not detect Iranian territory or territorial waters or the inside of the Strait of Hormuz. Earlier, Iran’s Foreign Ministry stated its position that South Korea should refrain from sending troops to Hormuz. The date of handling at the National Assembly plenary session has not been fixed.

NEC special counsel: signs of concealment and forgery of tally sheets in Gyeonggi education superintendent election… search and seizure at Gyeonggi and Gwangju election commissions

The National Election Commission (NEC) special counsel (Special Counsel Lee Tae-han) carried out a search and seizure on the 10th at the Gyeonggi Provincial Election Commission and the Gwangju city election commission in Gyeonggi. The team believes that during the counting for the Gyeonggi education superintendent election in the June 3 local elections, a Gwangju election commission employee hid and forged tally sheets. A tally sheet is a form on which each candidate’s vote count at each polling place is recorded and totaled. As reported in our morning edition, the team had commissioned a detailed forensic examination of the ballots taken out, and this time it widened the investigation with a search and seizure.

The team is looking into whether the Gwangju election commission systematically hid corrections of input errors during the count. A tally sheet is a document that gathers the vote counts of each polling place and carries them up to the final result, so if it was hidden or altered, the basis of the count is shaken. The special counsel team has not said whether the contents of the materials secured in the search and seizure differ from the count result. Whether the winners are affected has also not been confirmed.

Jensen Huang controversy widens… Trump: “FBI is investigating,” U.S. lawmakers of both parties press for the truth, citing “security threat”

President Trump said on the 9th (local time) that the FBI is investigating the relationship between Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang and a woman. The president added that CEO Huang had explained knowing nothing of the suspicions surrounding the woman, and that the president believes that explanation. CEO Huang admitted the affair and apologized in an internal email on the 8th, and Nvidia’s board said “there is no basis to believe that national security or Nvidia’s confidential information was leaked” while saying it had begun an independent investigation. The House Republican chairs of the Foreign Affairs Committee, the special committee on U.S.–China strategic competition and the Intelligence Committee asked CEO Huang by letter on the 5th of this month to attend a closed-door joint briefing on the 18th, and lawmakers of both parties are demanding the truth on the “security threat.”

Anonymous U.S. officials claim the woman is suspected of being a collaborator of Chinese intelligence. But neither the officials’ names nor the grounds have been made public, so it has not been confirmed. China’s Foreign Ministry rebutted the suspicion at its regular briefing on the 9th as “groundless slander,” and Nvidia’s position is that the contact between the two was routine business exchange. President Trump also called CEO Huang a “great person” on the 8th. Nvidia’s share price fell, but the size of the drop differs from tally to tally, so it is not given as a single figure. Who the FBI is investigating and how far has not been confirmed because the FBI has given no explanation, and neither the deadline nor the result of the board investigation has officially come out.

Fair Trade Commission opens review of Naver Financial–Dunamu business combination… Secretariat: “competition-restriction concern,” 10 markets under review

On the 10th the Fair Trade Commission began deliberation on the business combination of Naver Financial and Dunamu. The Commission’s Secretariat judged that there is a concern of substantially restricting competition in several markets, including the domestic online simple-payment market and the digital-asset exchange market. The review covers 10 markets, including simple payment, digital-asset exchanges, search portals and online shopping, and it also includes the not-yet-formed market for issuing won stablecoins.

The combination is a comprehensive share exchange in which Naver Financial acquires all of Dunamu’s shares, and about 2.54 Naver Financial shares are given for each Dunamu share. As the direction of corrective measures, the Commission mentioned equal treatment of competitors and limits on business practices, and judged that the concern could be resolved if the remedy plans the two companies submitted are supplemented. The final judgment is made by the plenary meeting, and the date of the plenary meeting and the timing of a conclusion have not been fixed. The shareholders’ meeting to approve the share exchange was adjusted to February 26 next year.
