FBI names North Korea behind Binance hack… affected assets in the 8 trillion won range by Binance’s estimate

With investigators pointing to a North Korean group as the party behind the hack, the case has entered a new phase. Binance has reopened withdrawals in stages.

The US Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) on the 31st (local time) named a North Korean hacking group as being behind the hack of Binance, the world’s largest crypto asset exchange. The FBI said North Korea stole more than $6 billion in crypto assets around the 29th. Binance estimated the assets affected in the incident at $6.3 billion, about 8.4 trillion won. That figure is Binance’s own estimate, not a confirmed loss. The hacker took about 20 minutes to pull the assets out, and it is counted as the largest hack in crypto history. President Donald Trump said on the 31st that talks with North Korea would take place if North Korean responsibility is confirmed. Binance said it had covered about 95% of the affected assets with company funds. It had earlier said 90% had been covered, then raised the ratio. The explanation is that the company, not users, will bear the damage. From the afternoon of the 1st, Korea time, it reopened withdrawals in stages, starting with major assets such as Bitcoin.

The group the FBI named is a North Korean government-backed hacking unit known as ‘TraderTraitor’. The FBI said it had also detected signs of money laundering, such as the stolen assets being converted to Ethereum. President Trump’s remark also carried the condition ‘if confirmed’, and Secretary of State Marco Rubio said on the 31st that no North Korea-US talks are scheduled. Binance also put forward a plan for proof of reserves showing that it holds enough user assets, but no date for making it public has been confirmed. The final whereabouts of the funds the hacker took out, and when withdrawals will fully return to normal, are not yet known either.

October exports $115.3 billion, second highest ever… chips are half, trade surplus $42.5 billion

The Ministry of Industry and Trade announced on the 1st that October exports came to $115.3 billion ($115.28 billion), up 93.7% from a year earlier. Following September, it is a second straight month in the $110 billion range and the second highest on a monthly basis. Exports excluding semiconductors were $58.84 billion. Semiconductors surged 259.4% to $56.44 billion and made up about 49% of total exports. That means $49 of every $100 in exports is semiconductors. Imports rose 36.0% to $72.78 billion, and the trade balance, exports minus imports, was a $42.5 billion surplus. Cumulative January–October exports were $930 billion, topping $900 billion for the first time ever. With $70 billion left to reach $1 trillion for the year, forecasts say reaching it in November is likely. President Lee Jae-myung pledged to work harder to broaden the economic territory. Whether the export boom has spread to domestic demand is a separate matter. Third-quarter retail sales fell for the first time in five quarters.

North Korea reveals power test of tactical ballistic missile cluster warhead… claims it can ‘devastate 12 to 13 jeongbo’

North Korea announced on the 1st that its Missile Administration on the 31st conducted a test evaluating the combat applicability and power of a tactical ballistic missile dispersal warhead, the so-called cluster warhead. A cluster warhead is a weapon in which a single warhead splits in the air into many small bombs scattered over a wide area. North Korea claimed that the Standard Type 2 dispersal warhead can devastate 12 to 13 jeongbo, about 13 hectares, at very high density. The head of the Missile Administration divided cluster warheads into four types and said that within this year units would be able to choose among them to suit their purposes.

North Korea also revealed a test of a tactical ballistic missile carrying a cluster warhead last April, and South Korea, the United States and Japan then urged a halt, calling it a violation of UN Security Council resolutions. The area and power now put forward are figures announced by North Korea and have not been independently verified. The test came the day after the foreign ministers of South Korea, the US and Japan reaffirmed on the 30th their resolve for North Korea’s complete denuclearization. The announcement gives no launch site, time or flight distance.

Samsung rout KIA 6-1 in Game 5 and reach Korean Series after two years… will face KT

The Samsung Lions beat the KIA Tigers 6-1 in Game 5 of the playoff in Daegu on the 1st and advanced to the Korean Series. Taking the series 3 wins to 2, they return to the Korean Series after two years. Starter Ariel Jurado held KIA to one run (unearned) on five hits over seven innings, and the lineup piled up 11 hits. Choi Hyeong-woo drove in the first run with an RBI single in the first inning, and Kim Ji-chan had three hits and three RBIs. KIA starter Adam Oller was pulled after two innings and four runs.

Samsung, who entered as the regular-season runner-up, won Games 2, 3 and 5 to stop KIA’s challenge. Their Korean Series opponent is the KT Wiz. Game 1 is on the 3rd at kt wiz Park in Suwon, in a best-of-seven, first-to-four-wins format.

Exchange publishes ‘low-PBR companies’ list of 151 for first time… includes Emart, Hyundai Steel, Hanwha Life

The Korea Exchange on the 1st published for the first time a list of 151 listed companies whose price-to-book ratio (PBR) has stayed low for a long time within their industries. There are 96 on the KOSPI market and 55 on the KOSDAQ market. Companies meeting the first-round requirement numbered 185, but 34 that had disclosed a corporate value enhancement plan were dropped. Of the 62 companies that submitted plans, 49 had been caught by the first-round requirement. Emart (0.15x), Lotte Shopping (0.17x), Lotte Chemical (0.18x), Hyundai Steel (0.19x) and Hanwha Life (0.27x) are included, along with companies with a market capitalization of 1 trillion won or more. A PBR below 1 means the value the market places on the company is lower than its book value.

The list is posted on the value-up page of the listing disclosure system, and a ‘low-PBR’ tag is attached to stock names on brokerage trading screens. The exchange plans to provide one-on-one customized PBR improvement consulting to all 151 published companies during 2027.
