Independent MP Helen Haines critically injured after being struck by a vehicle outside her Wangaratta office

Police and the Prime Minister say the Indi MP was deliberately hit; a man is in custody and counter-terrorism investigators have been activated.

Independent MP Helen Haines, who holds the Victorian seat of Indi, was in a critical condition on Monday evening after being struck by a four-wheel drive outside her electorate office on Murphy Street in Wangaratta, in regional Victoria. ABC News reported that emergency services were called shortly before 4pm. Reuters, carried by the Straits Times and the South China Morning Post, said police and Prime Minister Anthony Albanese described the collision as deliberate. The Guardian Australia reported that she was receiving treatment for critical injuries, and the New York Times said Haines, 65, was hit by a car outside her office.

Albanese told reporters that a suspect was in custody and that the national counter-terrorism team had been activated, according to the New York Times and NZ Herald. The Conversation and The Nightly reported that a 43-year-old man had been arrested. Albanese said all of the country's thoughts were with Haines, her family, her friends and her team, Reuters reported, and told reporters it was too early to say what the motivation was, The Nightly said. The Sydney Morning Herald quoted him describing Haines as a beacon of light and a deeply respected politician. Its editorial argued the attack should be a line in the sand if it is confirmed as politically motivated. Early reports differed on the make of the vehicle, and police had not publicly named a motive or confirmed whether the man and Haines knew each other when the outlets published.

NSW to legislate so Mount Pleasant coal mine can lift output by 32% after High Court ruling

Premier Chris Minns said the NSW government will introduce a one-off bill allowing MACH Energy's Mount Pleasant mine in the Hunter Valley to raise annual production from 12.5 million to 16.5 million tonnes until 2032, the Guardian Australia reported. The Sydney Morning Herald and ABC News said the move follows a High Court ruling that dismissed the miner's appeal and left its expansion approval invalid because planning authorities had not properly weighed the downstream emissions. Minns said about 400 jobs would be lost before Christmas without intervention, and The Nightly reported that miners welcomed what Minns called a one-off intervention. The Guardian said the new consent would take effect only after MACH Energy signs a jobs guarantee covering its permanent workforce through 2032. Environment groups accused the government of bypassing the court, the Guardian reported, and ABC News said the bill is still weeks from becoming law. The larger expansion to 2048 remains tied up in the planning and court process, according to the reporting.

Markets · Technology✓ 3 outlets · guidance impact unconfirmed

NextDC halts ASX trading over power delay at its flagship Melbourne data centre

Data-centre operator NextDC requested a trading halt on the ASX on Monday after saying power supply delays would hit its flagship West Footscray facility in Melbourne, the Sydney Morning Herald reported under a headline linking the delay to a power pole protest. Reuters, carried by TradingView, said the halt runs until an ASX announcement or the start of trading on Wednesday 21 October, and that the company is assessing whether the delay affects its financial year 2027 guidance. The site, known as M3, is described as a 225 megawatt facility serving cloud, AI and large enterprise customers. Bloomberg had reported on Sunday that NextDC flagged an earnings risk from the delay. TradingView's report said the board had not yet met to consider the matter, and that NextDC shares were down more than 25 per cent this year. No outlet had published a revised earnings figure.

Jacqui Lambie condemns One Nation cartoon mocking her prolapse as Pauline Hanson tells her to lighten up

Senator Jacqui Lambie said "enough is enough" after One Nation released a cartoon in Pauline Hanson's Please Explain series that uses her vaginal prolapse as a punchline, the Sydney Morning Herald and The Age reported. ABC News said the cartoon is part of a longer critique of the government's Digital Duty of Care online safety legislation, and Lambie called it nasty and divisive. Lambie, who revealed the condition in an emotional Senate speech in August and linked it to her army service, said those around Hanson should ask her to take it down, ABC News reported. The New Daily said Hanson told her to lighten up and get over it. Labor, the Coalition and several crossbenchers condemned the cartoon, and independent MP Monique Ryan said Lambie had shown courage and honesty, according to 1News and ABC News. The Guardian Australia said it follows a September dispute over a One Nation cartoon aimed at Liberal MP Andrew Hastie.

World · Europe✓ 5 outlets · cause of looting disputed

France imposes local curfews after nationwide rioting over cost of living and school conditions

French Prime Minister Sébastien Lecornu told prefects to impose "very limited" local curfews after a weekend of rioting and looting, RTÉ, ABC News (US) and Al Jazeera reported. Curfews covered areas of Paris including Place de la République and the streets around Les Halles, plus Lille and three other northern towns, Al Jazeera said. At least 2,400 people were arrested and about 3,000 businesses were damaged or looted, according to the same reports. President Emmanuel Macron said the country felt "anger and shame", Al Jazeera reported, and asked for new policing tactics. Interior Minister Laurent Nuñez conceded that police lost control in some cities, the Guardian reported, and offered his resignation, which Macron refused. The Sydney Morning Herald's reporter in Paris described the unrest as starting with protests over school conditions before widening into a revolt over the cost of living. Donald Trump said France is burning, and One Nation leader Pauline Hanson warned it could happen in Australia, the Herald Sun and News24 reported.
