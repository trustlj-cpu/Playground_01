Lula and Flávio Bolsonaro trade corruption accusations in first runoff debate, on Band

Sunday’s clash (Oct. 18) revolved around Vorcaro, Lulinha, security and the cost of living; the runoff is on Oct. 25.

The first runoff debate between President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva (PT) and Senator Flávio Bolsonaro (PL) was held on Sunday (Oct. 18) on Band, from 8 p.m. to 10 p.m., moderated by journalists Eduardo Oinegue and Adriana Araújo, according to Poder360 and the broadcaster. Flávio had said on Oct. 13 that he would not attend, but confirmed on Wednesday (Oct. 14), after Lula confirmed his own presence, according to Gazeta SP and O Povo. In the first round, Flávio won 47.03% of the votes (56.1 million) and Lula 45.16% (53.8 million), figures cited in the reports. The EFE agency, reproduced by CNN em Español and Infobae, reported that the clash revolved around suspicions of corruption in the circles of both candidates. Lula questioned Flávio about payments by Daniel Vorcaro, former owner of Banco Master, that reportedly financed a film about Jair Bolsonaro, a case under investigation by the Federal Police (PF). According to EFE, Flávio replied with the line “your nose is growing, Lula, be careful.”

Also according to EFE and El Tiempo, Flávio attacked Lulinha, Lula’s son, who is under investigation in three cases over alleged influence peddling; Lula replied that the senator was also protected by his father, said he hides nothing and did not fire a judge, an allusion to Sérgio Moro’s departure from Jair Bolsonaro’s government, and stated that “lying is the Bolsonaro family’s strong suit.” Flávio said the Lula government has become a marketplace for deals and that his governments have always been marked by corruption. Lula accused Flávio’s brother, Eduardo Bolsonaro, of conspiring from the United States. On the economy, according to a report reproduced by A Notícia do Vale and Congresso em Foco, Lula defended his legacy, the rise in the minimum wage, more credit and investment in industry and innovation, with the idea of making “money circulate in people’s hands.” Flávio opened with questions about food prices, called the public debt of R$ 11 trillion “yet another negative record” and proposed that the government buy people’s and companies’ debts to banks at a reduced value. Portal Flagrante wrote that both stayed on the defensive, and Cenário MT, that undecided voters left without clear answers.

STF opens second inquiry into Flávio Bolsonaro over suspected money laundering with lawyer Willer Tomaz

The Supreme Federal Court opened a second inquiry to investigate suspected money laundering involving Senator Flávio Bolsonaro and lawyer Willer Tomaz, G1, Estadão, CartaCapital and Metrópoles reported on Oct. 18. According to CartaCapital and Metrópoles, the case began in the Federal Court in Rio Grande do Sul and reached the STF on Oct. 8, because of the senator’s special jurisdiction; Justice Alexandre de Moraes was drawn as rapporteur and the Attorney General’s Office (PGR) agreed to the opening. The outlets report that investigators point to e-mails indicating that Flávio was listed as a “master user” of a bank account of Tomaz’s company, Alcazar Holdings, and to trips by the senator paid for by Cathedral, another of the lawyer’s companies. Tomaz is also suspected of controlling Rede Loto, a betting company authorized by the Rio de Janeiro government. One of the reports says the PF is examining about R$ 130 million moved between March and May 2025 in an account linked to Tomaz. Flávio and Tomaz say they are friends and deny having business together. Tomaz had already been a target in August of Operation Sem Desconto, on INSS fraud, in which Flávio was not a target, according to Poder360.

Bolsa Família with 15.04% increase starts being paid this Monday (Oct. 19); floor rises from R$ 600 to R$ 691

October Bolsa Família payments, already with the 15.04% increase announced by the Lula government, start this Monday (Oct. 19), SBT News, O Povo and Diário do Nordeste reported. According to the reports, the minimum benefit per family rises from R$ 600 to R$ 691, and the estimated average amount goes from R$ 675 to R$ 777. Caixa Econômica Federal makes the deposits, and the calendar is set by the Ministry of Development and Social Assistance (MDS). By the last digit of the NIS, beneficiaries whose number ends in 1 are paid today; ending in 2 on Oct. 20, ending in 3 on Oct. 21, ending in 4 on Oct. 22 and ending in 5 on Oct. 23. The sequence continues on Oct. 26 (ending in 6), Oct. 27 (ending in 7), Oct. 28 (ending in 8) and Oct. 29 (ending in 9), and ends on Oct. 30, for ending in 0. The articles note that registrations with inconsistencies may delay the deposit. SBT News and O Povo do not record any divergence between them on the dates.

Flávio Bolsonaro rally on Paulista draws about 72,000 people, and Lula supporters hold rallies in several capitals

On Sunday (Oct. 18) Flávio Bolsonaro held a rally on Avenida Paulista, in São Paulo, which the Political Debate Monitor (USP/Cebrap, with More in Common) estimated at 71.9 thousand people at its peak, at 12:20 p.m., with a 12% margin of error, according to Exame. Poder360 calculated about 76.2 thousand supporters from aerial photos of the blocks occupied and reported that the rally filled six blocks, one fewer than Lula’s. According to Metrópoles, the gathering began around 11:20 a.m., was called “Bloco do 22” by the campaign and featured Tarcísio de Freitas, Ricardo Nunes and Nikolas Ferreira; the candidate saved his voice for the Band debate that night. Metrópoles and Times Brasil noted that Lula did not attend public rallies on Sunday and that supporters, politicians and artists held decentralized demonstrations in cities such as Brasília, Rio de Janeiro, São Paulo, Belo Horizonte, Salvador, Curitiba and Porto Alegre. Paulista had already hosted a pro-Lula rally on Oct. 11, which the PT said drew 1 million people; Exame reports there is no independent count of that rally, because the USP method could not be applied without authorization for drones.

Politics · Banco Master✓ 4 outlets · payments and contract not proven by a public document

Vorcaro reviewed Gabriela Prioli videos about Banco Master, messages cited by the press indicate; she denies a contract

Messages extracted by the Federal Police from Daniel Vorcaro’s phone indicate that the former banker reviewed videos by lawyer and host Gabriela Prioli about Banco Master, according to an Estadão report of Oct. 16 echoed on Oct. 18 by Diário de Pernambuco, Times Brasil and Mais Goiás. According to Estadão, the texts were scripted and submitted to Vorcaro for prior approval, and he suggested topics and directed changes. The conversations date from April 2025; in one of them, then-superintendent Ana Matos reportedly wrote that there was a 12-month contract. Reports say the negotiation would have been brokered by an agency and that the case would be part of an alleged digital team financed by the bank. Prioli says she never spoke with the owner or employees of the institution, never signed a contract or received payment for content about the bank, and that Vorcaro never edited, approved or directed her content. The outlets found no independent confirmation of payments or invoices.
