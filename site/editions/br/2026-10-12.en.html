TSE president orders the “Exército do Lula” channel taken down on WhatsApp, and the inspector general reverses the order hours later

Sunday’s decision, on the 11th, met a request from the campaign of Flávio Bolsonaro. Lula says he is “distrustful” of the Court, the full bench has yet to decide, and Mendonça blocked another PT boost.

Folha, Valor and Agência Brasil report that on Sunday the 11th the president of the Superior Electoral Court (TSE), Justice Kassio Nunes Marques, ordered WhatsApp to suspend the “Exército do Lula” (“Lula’s Army”) channel. The order came in the morning, at the request of Flávio Bolsonaro’s (PL) campaign, according to G1 and Estadão, and gave the app three hours to comply, according to Folha. The channel was created by federal deputy André Janones (Rede-MG) to spread messages in favor of Lula’s reelection and has more than 3 million followers, according to Folha. Janones called the decision censorship, Agência Brasil reports. In the early afternoon, the Electoral Inspector General, Justice Ricardo Villas Bôas Cueva, suspended Nunes Marques’s order and put the channel back online, G1, Folha and Estadão report. O Globo describes the president’s order as a decision that overrode the inspector general, and the BBC summed up the episode as a back-and-forth of Electoral Justice decisions. A WhatsApp channel sends messages to many followers at once, without letting them reply as a group. The inspector general’s decision is preliminary, and the final word will rest with the TSE’s full bench, according to G1. A single-justice ruling, made by one justice alone, can be reviewed by the collegiate body: in August, the full bench already overturned individual decisions by Nunes Marques and André Mendonça in cases involving Lula, according to Jornal do Brasil and Diário de Pernambuco.

Lula reacted the same day. In an interview, when asked, he said “I think it’s absurd,” and the PT said it will ask the court for explanations, Valor reports. In another statement, the president said he was “distrustful of the behavior of the electoral Court,” which, in his view, has been blocking content from the PT campaign, according to G1. Also on Sunday, the TSE vice president, Justice André Mendonça, barred Lula’s campaign from paying to boost videos that say Flávio is against ending the 6x1 schedule, G1 and Agência Brasil report. Folha notes that Nunes Marques’s decisions against Lula and in favor of Flávio bother a wing of the court and draw criticism inside and outside the bench. On the other side, it was Flávio’s campaign that asked for the channel’s suspension, and the case now depends on the full bench. As of publication of this edition, the sources consulted gave no date for the judgment. Metrópoles followed live the contest on WhatsApp between Janones’s channel and that of deputy Nikolas Ferreira (PL-MG). The runoff is on October 25, and the legal fight over advertising, profiles and channels on social networks is likely to continue until then.

Elections 2026 · PresidentPress ✓ 8 outlets · poll as reported by the press

Lula brings supporters to Avenida Paulista, Flávio schedules a “bloquinho” at the same spot, and PoderData gives Flávio 53% to 47%

Lula, Vice President Geraldo Alckmin and allies held a rally on Avenida Paulista, in São Paulo, on Sunday the 11th, O Globo and Poder360 report. Poder360 published images of the crowded avenue and says the rally filled seven blocks of the avenue, and G1 reports that the subway closed the Trianon-Masp station for a time. The Military Police reported vandalism, disorder and graffiti as the crowd dispersed, according to Estadão. There is no independent crowd estimate: the Political Debate Monitor (USP/Cebrap) and More in Common Brasil said they did not release figures because Lula’s campaign did not authorize the use of drones, Valor reports. Flávio gathered São Paulo mayors and asked allies to mobilize (“We cannot lower our guard”), according to G1, and, after the pro-Lula rally, he and deputy Nikolas Ferreira (PL-MG) confirmed a “bloquinho” on Paulista, Metrópoles and UOL report; the sources consulted give no date. In the PoderData/Aya poll, conducted by phone with 3,000 people from October 5 to 7, with a 1.8-point margin, Flávio has 53% of valid votes against Lula’s 47%, Correio da Manhã and SBT News report. CNN Brasil highlights that the expectation of a Flávio victory rose nine points and that of a Lula victory fell 11. A poll is a snapshot of the moment, and valid votes exclude blank ballots, null ballots and undecided voters.

Household consumption loses steam and should grow at the slowest pace since the pandemic in 2026 and 2027, economists tell Folha

Household consumption, regarded as the engine of the economy in recent years, is losing steam and should post its weakest growth since the pandemic this year and next, according to economists interviewed by Folha. With the main pillar of demand weaker, they expect investment and exports to take up more room in Gross Domestic Product (GDP), the sum of everything the country produces. The report’s detailed figures had not been confirmed by another outlet by the time this edition went to press.

On Friday the 9th, the Ibovespa, the B3’s main index, rose 1.38% and closed at 209,066.90 points, a new closing record, and the dollar fell 0.79% to R$ 4.985, a drop of about 4.4% on the week, Poder360 and Acessa report. Today, Monday the 12th, is the national holiday of Our Lady of Aparecida, and the B3 is closed; trading resumes on Tuesday the 13th, according to the exchange calendar. In New York, Friday ended higher: S&P 500 +0.59% (7,811.54 points), Nasdaq +0.64% (27,366.17) and Dow Jones +0.83% (51,654.95). WTI closed at US$ 91.85 (+0.39%) and gold at US$ 4,216.30 (+1.43%). Today US stocks open, because Columbus Day does not close the exchange, but the bond market is closed. Reuters notes cautious Asian markets and oil slightly lower overnight.

Elections 2026 · Federal PolicePress ✓ 5 outlets

Flávio asks the TSE to investigate the director-general of the Federal Police, and the inspector general orders a probe of Pablo Marçal

Flávio Bolsonaro’s campaign filed a criminal complaint with the Electoral Justice on Sunday the 11th against the director-general of the Federal Police (PF), Andrei Rodrigues, Valor, Poder360, CNN Brasil and UOL report. According to Valor, the reason is a first-round press conference in which he indicated that the force was investigating a suspected electoral interference. For the campaign, the statement would have benefited the PT, according to Folha and UOL. The sources consulted carry no response from the PF.

The same day, Inspector General Villas Bôas Cueva ordered an investigation into the alleged spread of fake news against Lula by the influencer Pablo Marçal and into the activity of the website Brasil 22 Token, Valor reports. Valor also notes that Flávio said he suffered “prejudgment” for having asked banker Daniel Vorcaro for money for a film about Jair Bolsonaro; the other sources consulted do not detail the episode.

WorldPress ✓ 7 outlets

Trump announces an “energy ceasefire” between Russia and Ukraine, Zelensky says he awaits the details, and Russian attacks continue

US President Donald Trump announced on Sunday the 11th, in a Truth Social post, an “energy ceasefire” in effect between Russia and Ukraine, and wrote that both sides had agreed, Reuters, NDTV and the BBC report. According to the BBC, the announcement appeared to take Ukraine by surprise, and Russia had not yet commented. Zelensky said he was waiting for the details of the US proposals and that Russia was keeping up its attacks, Reuters reports. He offered to stop attacks on refineries if Moscow halts attacks on Ukrainian energy, and told ABC he will hold elections if Russia accepts a 60-day ceasefire, according to Reuters. After the announcement, a Ukrainian thermal power plant was hit at least twice, according to Ukrainian officials cited by ABC News, and Russian attacks killed six people in two cities, Reuters reports. The Financial Times says, according to Reuters, that the US is threatening to block arms to Ukraine over the attacks on refineries.

In the Middle East, the Houthis of Yemen renewed their warning about Saudi airports after the attack on Riyadh airport, Reuters reports. O Globo says the kingdom was bombed again after the earlier attack killed 13 and wounded more than 300; Bloomberg TV spoke of 12 dead, and the toll varies among outlets. Saudi stock markets fell after the attack, according to Reuters. Gold rose slightly, with investors weighing the ceasefire and the risk of the conflict widening, Bloomberg reports. In New York, Friday the 9th ended higher, and the figures are in this edition’s economy story.
