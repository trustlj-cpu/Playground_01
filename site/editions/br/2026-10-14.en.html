MDB formalizes majority support for Flávio, but ten state chapters stay with Lula; Flávio says he will not attend the Band debate

With 12 days to go to the runoff, the party split: G1 counted 16 state chapters with Flávio, and Valor, 17. Lula confirmed he will attend Sunday’s (18) debate, and the polls disagree on who is ahead.

The MDB on Tuesday (13) formalized the support of most of the party for Flávio Bolsonaro (PL) in the runoff, at an event with the candidate, according to G1 and Valor. The support is not unanimous. G1 counted 16 state chapters with Flávio and ten with Lula; Valor recorded, earlier, 17 of the 27 chapters with the PL candidate. The sources consulted do not explain the one-chapter difference between the two numbers, and the count may change until the 25th. The ten that declared a vote for Lula were reported by Valor and Poder360, and Agência Brasil recorded the MDB’s backing in the North and Northeast. In the evening, the party’s leader in the Senate came to the defense of these state sections, according to G1. Also according to G1, it was at that event that Flávio said he will not go to the TV Bandeirantes debate on Sunday (18), which CNN Brasil, Valor and Poder360 also report. Lula confirmed he will attend, according to CNN Brasil and Valor. Poder360 reports that Flávio is considering the Globo debate, and Folha says his campaign did not sign the rules for that debate and does not guarantee his presence.

The race is tight, but the pollsters do not agree on who leads. The latest PoderData/Aya gives Flávio 51% and Lula 49%, according to Poder360, which speaks of a recovery by Lula compared with the previous round (53% to 47%, as we reported on Monday). On the BTG/Nexus poll, Veja and Folha speak of Flávio ahead, and CartaCapital of a technical tie; the exact numbers are not in the sources consulted. On Tuesday night, Quaest released state polls, according to G1: in Rio Grande do Norte, Lula has 59% and Flávio 33%; in Acre, Flávio has 60% and Lula 28%; in Amazonas, Lula has 47% and Flávio 43%. Quaest’s first national runoff poll comes out today, according to G1, and Datafolha releases its own on Thursday (15), Folha reports. State polls do not add up to a national result, and all of them have a margin of error. On the streets, Lula’s allies held rallies in 11 state capitals on Tuesday, according to G1; in São Paulo, Folha records the presence of Haddad, Tebet and Marina Silva, after first-round defeats.

Lula announces debt forgiveness for MEIs and a ‘Pix’ of up to R$ 5,000; the cost figures differ

Lula on Tuesday (13) announced a package for individual microentrepreneurs (MEIs), according to Valor, G1, Folha and CNN Brasil. It includes forgiveness of the category’s debts to the federal government and a ‘Pix MEI’ of up to R$ 5,000, according to Poder360 and Folha; G1 also mentions a higher revenue ceiling. The costs do not match: Estadão spoke to a minister who cites more than R$ 12 billion in forgiven debts, while Valor and G1 report that the Finance Ministry estimates an impact of R$ 3 billion over three years. The sources consulted do not explain the difference. Estadão adds that the Pix can only be paid from 2027. Flávio said the government ‘wants to buy votes’, according to Valor. The same day, Lula promised, if reelected, a university Pé-de-Meia, at a cost of R$ 4.6 billion a year, according to Valor, UOL and G1.

Labor · Senate✓ 8 outlets · vote as reported by the press

Flávio changes position and says he will vote to end the 6x1 schedule; the Senate is expected to vote on the PEC today

Flávio Bolsonaro said on Tuesday (13) that he will vote in favor of ending the 6x1 schedule, a proposal he had criticized before, according to Valor, G1, Poder360, CNN Brasil, Folha and Estadão. According to CNN Brasil, he said the government should ‘pay the bill’, and Estadão sums up the remark as a call for compensation by the government. Folha writes that he assured allies that, if elected, he will push for a revision of the PEC; no other outlet confirmed this. Lula attributed the change to popular pressure, according to Poder360 and Valor, and Valor says the PT is preparing a speech to accuse Flávio of hypocrisy. A Lula campaign video narrated by the actor Wagner Moura criticizes his opponent’s position, according to Poder360 and Bnews.

Senate president Davi Alcolumbre confirmed the vote on the PEC for Wednesday (14), according to Valor and Estadão. The government says it has gathered signatures to speed up the process and finish the vote the same day, according to G1 and Poder360, and Folha records that the opposition freed its caucuses to vote as they wish. Bar and restaurant associations, supermarkets and industry criticize the vote and back the worked-hours PEC, according to Folha. In the Senate, a PEC needs at least 49 of the 81 votes, in two rounds.

Elections 2026 · Electoral Justice✓ 8 outlets · decisions as reported by the press

Nunes Marques clears the ‘Lula’s Army’ channel after registration, with a 48-hour wait; the inspector general presses for a plenary vote

The president of the TSE, Kassio Nunes Marques, again suspended the ‘Lula’s Army’ (Exército do Lula) channel on WhatsApp for lack of registration, and the decision was reported on Tuesday (13) morning by Folha, Valor and Agência Brasil. CNN Brasil describes the order as an overturning of the inspector general’s decision, which had cleared the channel. Later on Tuesday, the PT campaign registered the channel with the TSE, and Nunes Marques authorized it, but set a 48-hour wait before the group could resume, according to G1, Folha and Valor. G1 reports that the channel went offline after the decision. According to O Globo, the minister set rules and ordered the court to monitor the channel, and Agência Brasil records that he suspended new posts and new followers on the channel. The inspector general, Villas Bôas Cueva, insists the case go to the plenary, according to G1 and Valor. Valor says the Flávio campaign told the TSE that Janones deleted 499 messages from the channel; the information has a single source.

On Tuesday, Nunes Marques brought together the Lula and Flávio campaigns, according to Valor, G1, Estadão and Agência Brasil; the PT president told Valor that the minister proposed reducing lawsuits until the runoff, a version that appears in no other source. The TSE also struck down ads that generically associate Lula and Flávio with organized crime and set limits on this type of attack, according to G1, Valor and Agência Brasil. The court’s vice president, André Mendonça, ordered the removal of a Lula ad on cuts to social programs, according to G1, and the TSE ordered a rerun of Flávio’s program that aired without audio, according to G1 and Valor.

Pope Leo XIV has lung cancer confirmed after surgery; the Vatican says there were no complications

Pope Leo XIV underwent surgery on the afternoon of Tuesday (13) to remove a nodule in his lung, according to CNN Brasil, G1 and Agência Brasil, and the Vatican said the surgery was a success, according to Folha and UOL. Later, the Vatican said the tests confirmed lung cancer and that part of the right lung was removed, according to AP, CNN Brasil and G1. G1 says that tests done before the surgery had already pointed to suspected cancer, that the diagnosis is adenocarcinoma, and that the doctors said the operation had no complications. Politico also reported the diagnosis.

G1 also published that, at an early stage, the chance of cure for this type of cancer is above 90%; the sources consulted do not give the stage of the pope’s tumor, so that figure is general data, not a prognosis. The US Secretary of State, Marco Rubio, wished the pope well, according to G1. Folha notes that the Vatican has gone from silence about popes’ illnesses to detailed bulletins. The sources consulted give no discharge date or next medical bulletin.
