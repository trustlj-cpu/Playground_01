World · VaticanPress ✓ 8 outlets · the type of nodule has not been reported

Pope Leo XIV to undergo surgery for a lung nodule before his trip to Uruguay, Argentina and Peru; the Peruvian government says the visit is unchanged

The Vatican expects the surgery in the first days of this week and about seven days in hospital. The trip is set for Nov. 6–17 and the Holy See has not reported any changes.

Leo XIV announced on Sunday, October 11, from the window of the Apostolic Palace and after praying the Angelus, that doctors had detected a nodule in his lung during follow-up tests, at a very early stage and well localized, according to Infobae, RPP and La República. The Vatican spokesman, Matteo Bruni, specified that the operation will take place in the first days of the week that begins today, at the Gemelli hospital in Rome, and that the hospital stay afterward will last about a week. A pulmonary nodule is a small mass of tissue in the lung; many are benign, but they have to be studied to find out what they are. The reports consulted do not say what type this one is, and DailyDrop also found no official medical report beyond what Bruni said. Gestión and El Comercio report that the hospital stay would be about seven days. Correo consulted an oncologist to explain what having a pulmonary nodule means. The pope, who was bishop of Chiclayo before arriving at the Vatican, is a familiar figure to many Peruvians, and for that reason the news was followed closely across the country.

For Peru, what matters is the tour. The apostolic journey is scheduled for Nov. 6–17 and includes Uruguay, Argentina and Peru. The dates of the Peruvian leg vary by outlet: RPP speaks of Nov. 11–16 and Infobae of Lima, Chiclayo, Cusco and Pucallpa between Nov. 11 and 17. Labor Minister Juan Sheput said the visit continues “without absolutely any change” and that they trust the program will be carried out (La República, RPP, Panamericana). Argentine President Javier Milei wished him a speedy recovery (RPP, Correo), and the pope greeted the Lord of Miracles and thanked people for their prayers for his pastoral visit (El Comercio, Correo). A surgeon consulted by RPP explained that if only the nodule and the tissue around it are removed, recovery takes two or three weeks; if a lung lobe or segment has to be removed, the picture changes. The Holy See has not said which will be the case, so the November schedule remains open. What is still missing is the exact date of the operation, the result of the study of the nodule and the medical bulletin in the following days; only with that can it be said whether the program stays as it is or is adjusted. In the meantime, the Church and the Peruvian government are continuing preparations, and President Keiko Fujimori spoke with Cardinal Carlos Castillo about the visit, according to a press report that DailyDrop could not check against a second source.

Peru · Climate and emergenciesPress ✓ 6 outlets · the lists of regions vary

Indeci reports 58 active wildfires; the Armed Forces use planes and helicopters and nine regions remain on red alert until Friday the 16th

The head of Indeci, General Luis Vásquez, reported 58 active wildfires in the country, according to Infobae and Correo. The lists of regions differ: Correo includes Puno and Apurímac among the areas with fire, Infobae mentions Cusco, Ayacucho, Apurímac, Junín, Ancash, Arequipa and Cajamarca, and RPP speaks of Ayacucho, Moquegua and Puno.

The Defense Ministry deployed operations by land and air, and the Air Force sent planes and helicopters to Cusco, according to Infobae and Andina. Correo describes fires “out of control” in several districts of Puno. Gestión reports that two blazes remain active in Cusco and that the region has accumulated 289 cases this year.

Senamhi, in its Notice No. 010, maintains the red alert from Sunday, October 11 to Friday, October 16 in Apurímac, Arequipa, Ayacucho, Cusco, Huancavelica, Ica, Moquegua, Puno and Tacna, with conditions that can reach extreme intensity, especially in the southern highlands (Correo, Exitosa, Andina). DailyDrop found no tally of hectares burned or of victims so far in October.

Andina reports that the government is reinforcing the response in Cusco and will deliver relief kits, and RPP that it ordered actions in the provinces of Calca, Anta and La Convención. The context is drought: Gestión notes that the water deficit threatens 17 regions and that the government declared 707 districts in emergency, which makes pastures and brush more flammable.

Peru · CongressPress ✓ 7 outlets · the amount varies by outlet

Congress spent almost S/ 3 million remodeling its dining rooms, tendered as “maintenance”; there was Louis XV-style furniture

Several outlets revealed that Congress earmarked almost S/ 3 million to remodel two dining rooms in the Legislative Palace, among them the one for legislators, according to El Comercio, Infobae, Gestión and Caretas. La República and Correo specify that the contract was for S/ 2.65 million and that it was put out to tender as a “comprehensive maintenance service,” although the work was a complete remodeling.

According to the documents cited, partition walls were removed, majolica was replaced with sintered stone and Louis XV-style furniture was bought, along with LED lighting. The Board of Directors chaired by Fernando Rospigliosi approved the project in April 2026. Cecilia Ruiz, a lawyer specializing in state procurement, questioned the lack of transparency and the need for the spending. DailyDrop found no official response from Congress.

World · UkrainePress ✓ 8 outlets · Moscow did not confirm it

Trump announced on Truth Social an “energy truce” between Russia and Ukraine; Zelensky said he was awaiting details and Russia kept attacking

Donald Trump wrote on Sunday the 11th on Truth Social, at 9:16 a.m. Lima time, that “with immediate effect” there is an ENERGY TRUCE between Russia and Ukraine, that both sides accepted it and that it must not be broken. Gestión notes that Moscow has not yet confirmed it, and Correo reports that Volodymyr Zelensky said he was unaware of the agreement. Later, the Ukrainian president wrote on Telegram that the announcement is “good news for everyone” and that what matters is that it is honored (RPP, El Peruano).

Russia attacked the Kharkiv power plant shortly after the announcement, according to The Hill and The Kyiv Independent, and Reuters reported six dead in Russian attacks on two Ukrainian cities. Republican senators said that Trump’s deal with Putin to sell Russian diesel could be illegal (Reuters, Gestión). Canada, through its prime minister, Mark Carney, said it supports the truce. Gold rose slightly as traders weighed the announcement (Bloomberg).

Politics · SenatePress ✓ 5 outlets · it is not clear who said each phrase

Clash in the Senate between Interior Minister César Astudillo and Senator Iber Maraví during the debate on delegated powers; the committee moved past it with a chain of held hands

On Saturday the 10th, during the debate on delegated legislative powers in the Senate’s Constitution Committee, Interior Minister César Astudillo and Senator Iber Maraví had a heated altercation, according to La República, Exitosa, El Comercio, APN and Agenda País. It all began when Senator Ruth Luque asked Astudillo about police reform; according to Exitosa, the minister reacted off microphone and Maraví objected to the interruption.

The phrase “shut your mouth” appears in the videos, but the reports do not make clear with certainty who said it. Agenda País says both even stood up, close to coming to blows, and that the committee moved past the incident with a chain of held hands. Defense Minister Rafael Belaunde played it down (La República). Luque said that “there is a military-police sector that does not want to be questioned.”
