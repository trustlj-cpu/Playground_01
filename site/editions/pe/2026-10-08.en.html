Keiko Fujimori announces a Unified Command of the Armed Forces and Police against organized crime, with more than 1,350 personnel

The announcement came on Wednesday at Army General Headquarters in San Borja. It includes an elite group for captures, control of prisons and accelerated expulsion of foreigners who commit crimes.

President Keiko Fujimori presented the Unified Command of the Armed Forces and the National Police (PNP) against organized crime on Wednesday, October 7, during the presentation of the Combined Forces, according to Panamericana, Correo, Gestión, Canal N, Trome and Expreso. A control and operations center will be set up at Army General Headquarters in San Borja to direct operations nationwide.

The strategy provides for a special elite group of police and soldiers for search-and-capture operations. Voz and Expreso speak of more than 1,350 personnel, without detailing their makeup. Fujimori said they would turn up “where they least expect it.” Also announced were control of prisons, incommunicado detention of inmates who direct crimes, accelerated expulsion of foreigners who commit crimes and an Executive commission with the UIF, the DINI and Osiptel. There are no budget figures or start date for the operations.

Senate approves Keiko Fujimori’s trip to El Salvador and Spain with 37 votes in favor, 11 against and one abstention

The Senate authorized the president’s trips to El Salvador and Spain with 37 votes in favor, 11 against and one abstention, among the 50 senators who registered attendance, according to Infobae and RPP. Infobae reported the trip as running from October 31 to November 6; RPP said the first leg will be from October 28 to 31.

The dates seem to correspond to different legs of the same trip: the Executive had asked to leave on October 28 and return on November 6. DailyDrop did not see the official agenda or the schedule of meetings.

Security · PrisonsPress ✓ 4 outlets · measures announced, no rule published

The package includes prison control, accelerated expulsion of foreigners and a commission with the UIF, the DINI and Osiptel

Besides the command, the Government announced control of prisons, incommunicado detention of inmates who direct crimes from jail, and accelerated expulsion of foreigners who commit crimes (Trome, Correo, Expreso). Gestión describes who makes up the command and what actions will be taken.

The Special Executive Commission for the Fight against Crime will include the Financial Intelligence Unit (UIF), the National Intelligence Directorate (DINI) and the telecommunications regulator Osiptel. DailyDrop did not find the legal texts creating them or any cost figures.

Elections · Tally sheetsPress ✓ 3 outlets · figures vary by source

The JEE received 6,930 case files on flagged tally sheets; in Lima more than 1,900 remained to be resolved

Gestión reported that the Special Electoral Juries received 6,930 case files tied to flagged, damaged or lost tally sheets nationwide. El Comercio specified that in Metropolitan Lima more than 1,900 flagged tally sheets were pending and that almost no new tally sheets had been counted in the capital since Sunday.

Until the JNE proclaims the result, nothing is final. Infobae explained that a vote recount applies only in exceptional cases and could begin in the coming days. The Lima count holds: Renovación Popular 27.703% and Avanza País 26.841%, a difference of 44,978 votes (El Comercio).

Economy · Central BankPress ✓ 5 outlets · October 7 decision

BCRP holds the reference rate at 4.25% for the 13th month, with inflation at 4.5%; the dollar closes at S/ 3.445

The Central Reserve Bank (BCRP) held its reference rate at 4.25% on Wednesday, October 7, for the thirteenth consecutive month, according to EFE via Infobae, La República, Gestión and Peru Retail. Annual inflation rose from 4.4% in August to 4.5% in September, mainly because of fuel and electricity; monthly inflation was 0.12%. One outlet cites a Bloomberg survey in which nine of thirteen analysts expected a hike to 4.50%.

The overnight deposit rate stayed at 2.25% and the repo rate at 4.75%. The board’s next meeting is November 12. The dollar closed Wednesday at S/ 3.445, about 0.3% higher than Tuesday (S/ 3.435), according to the BCRP table cited by El Comercio. The BVL closed with no confirmed figure and does not open today because of the holiday.
