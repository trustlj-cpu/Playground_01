Economy · Budget✓ 7 outlets · figures from the MEF, financing according to each outlet

Government asks Congress for an S/ 8,228 million supplementary credit for public works, security and El Niño prevention

Economy Minister Elmer Cuba made the case for the request before the Bicameral Budget Committee. Nearly 54% would be financed with debt, according to Infobae and other reports.

Economy and Finance Minister Elmer Cuba defended before the Bicameral Budget Committee a supplementary credit bill of S/ 8,228 million, about US$ 2.3 billion (Infobae, Andina, El Peruano, Caretas, Proactivo, Energiminas). The MEF says the amount covers the continuation of public works, social services and the response to the El Niño phenomenon, as well as obligations that were not in the previous administration’s budget.

The Interior Ministry would receive S/ 1,097 million for pensions, police equipment and public safety, and S/ 581 million would go to prevention and emergencies tied to El Niño. The money would come from ordinary resources, mainly higher SUNAT tax collection linked to copper, and from credit operations: the issuance of sovereign bonds for more than S/ 4,426 million, according to Infobae. The reports differ on the exact percentages. Cuba said the fiscal position makes it possible to meet the deficit rule.

Politics · Congress✓ 5 outlets · votes and scope agree

Deputies approve delegated powers for the Executive for 90 days, on security and El Niño only, with 66 votes in favor; the text goes to the Senate

The full Chamber of Deputies approved on Tuesday, October 6, the delegation of legislative powers for 90 days with 66 votes in favor, 34 against and 24 abstentions, according to Caretas, Proactivo and Canal N. Earlier, the report of the Constitution Committee was rejected with 49 votes in favor and 73 against, and a preliminary question allowed a new vote.

The approved text has 20 measures in two areas, public safety and El Niño, compared with the 66 measures in eight areas that the Executive requested for 120 days. It includes migration control, the blocking of communications linked to crimes and changes to the prison system. The Senate will now review it.

Politics · Elections✓ 3 outlets · no official proclamation

Lima: with 100% of tally sheets processed, López Aliaga has 27.703% and Allison 26.841%; 1,923 tally sheets remain with the JEE

The ONPE processed 100% of the tally sheets for Metropolitan Lima and the count stopped with Rafael López Aliaga (Renovación Popular) at 27.703% and Francis Allison (Avanza País) at 26.841%, a gap of more than 44,000 votes, according to Infobae, Expreso and Caretas. There are 1,923 tally sheets left to resolve at the Special Electoral Juries.

Expreso reports that the head of the ONPE, Bernardo Pachas, estimated that official results would be known in the first week of November. An analyst cited estimated that the lead could grow by more than 6,000 votes, but warned that this is only an estimate. There is no proclamation from the JNE.

Sport · National team✓ 4 outlets · one headline differs on the score

Colombia beat Peru 2-0 in Miami and closed the FIFA window with goals from Velásquez and Arcila

Colombia won 2-0 over Peru on Tuesday, October 6, at Nu Stadium in Miami, with goals from Samuel Velásquez, after a cross from Carlos Gómez in the second half, and from Daniel Arcila, according to El País (Cali), El Universal and Noticias RCN. El Universal describes Peru as mired in uncertainty.

Peru’s tour of North America ended without a win: 4-1 against the United States, 1-1 with Mexico, 0-2 against Canada and 0-2 against Colombia. An América TV headline says Peru lost 1-0, but the goals mentioned by the other sources add up to two.
