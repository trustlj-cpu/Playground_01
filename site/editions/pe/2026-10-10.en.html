World · EnergyPress ✓ 6 outlets · Trump’s announcement; deliveries unverified

Trump announced that Russia will deliver more than 300,000 tons of diesel “immediately,” and the trade is exempt from sanctions

The US president said on Truth Social that he spoke with Putin. Diesel futures fell 4.5% in New York, but there are no verified deliveries.

On Friday, October 9, Donald Trump posted on Truth Social that, after speaking with Vladimir Putin, Russia would immediately deliver more than 300,000 tons of diesel to the United States and the world market, according to Yahoo Noticias, El Español, Portafolio, Xataka and El Economista. He also spoke of 500,000 tons in November and one million immediately afterward, about 1.8 million in total, and of up to three million more subject to the state of Russian refineries.

Portafolio reports that the US sanctions office (OFAC) issued a temporary general license exempting trade in Russian diesel from sanctions until April 2027. Diesel futures in New York fell 4.5%. Volodymyr Zelensky criticized the deal as a “gift to Putin” (Xataka). The announced quantities have yet to translate into verified deliveries, and DailyDrop found no analysis of the effect on diesel prices in Peru.

Politics · CongressPress ✓ 6 outlets · committee session, no vote

Galarreta told the Senate that the Chamber of Deputies’ text “is not enough” and that the Executive seeks to bring in the DINI and the Armed Forces

Prime Minister Luis Galarreta appeared on Friday, October 9, before the Senate Constitution Committee to defend the delegated legislative powers. “So far, what has been approved is not enough,” he said, according to El Comercio, RPP, TV Perú and Altavoz. The Chamber of Deputies approved only citizen security and the El Niño phenomenon; the Executive seeks to add further measures, such as the participation of the DINI and the Armed Forces.

The daily Correo spoke of a “political war” that has moved to the Senate, and La República reported that Senator Mirtha Vásquez announced she will vote against the request. Some senators fear that changing the Chamber of Deputies’ text would “distort” it. There was no vote, and the debate continues.

The dollar closed at S/ 3.446 on Friday, almost unchanged; on Wall Street the S&P 500 rose 0.59% and gold 1.43%

The exchange rate closed on Friday, October 9, at S/ 3.446, according to the BCRP table cited by El Comercio, Infobae, La República, Gestión and La Cámara, up 0.03% from the previous close. It moved between S/ 3.444 and S/ 3.460. The day’s interbank average of S/ 3.4545 is different from the official close.

DailyDrop could not confirm the Lima Stock Exchange close in two outlets. In the US, the S&P 500 rose 0.59% to 7,811.54, WTI 0.39% to $91.85, supported by tension in Hormuz, and gold 1.43% to $4,216.3. The BCRP had held its policy rate at 4.25% on Wednesday.

Politics · ElectionsPress ✓ 3 outlets · the call comes from a single outlet

López Aliaga called a mobilization in front of the ONPE for Saturday, and the JNE begins recount hearings on Monday

La República reported that Rafael López Aliaga called on his followers to join a mobilization in front of the ONPE headquarters, in Cercado de Lima, this Saturday, October 10, at 5:00 p.m., to demand speed in the count. The same paper reported that the JNE will begin vote recount hearings on Monday the 12th, and that recounts can be held until October 28.

In Lima, Renovación Popular has 27.703% and Avanza País 26.841%, a gap of more than 44,000 votes (El Comercio, Expreso). The ONPE expects to know results within the first days of November. DailyDrop could not confirm the call in a second outlet.

World · PrizesPress ✓ 5 outlets · Norwegian Committee announcement

Navi Pillay wins the Nobel Peace Prize; Trump, who said he deserved it for having resolved “eight wars,” was not chosen

The Norwegian Nobel Committee announced on Friday, October 9, in Oslo that the 2026 Nobel Peace Prize goes to South African jurist Navanethem “Navi” Pillay, 85, for her work for peace and international law and her contribution to the prosecution of war crimes and crimes against humanity (Infobae, La Nación, El Financiero, CNN en Español, A24). She chaired the UN commission that accused Israel of genocide in Gaza, according to La Nación.

Trump had said before the announcement that he should be the winner for having resolved eight conflicts; reacting, Pillay joked about sharing the prize with him (CNN en Español). The prize includes 12 million Swedish kronor, about $1.2 million, and the ceremony is on December 10 in Oslo.
