Washington Post: Trump administration is holding back Patriot missiles for Ukraine, Zelensky waits for Hegseth’s approval

According to the paper, the transfer of interceptors that have already been paid for is stalled because Defense Minister Hegseth has not approved it; the Pentagon sees no change in policy.

AD, de Volkskrant, De Telegraaf and Trouw reported on Tuesday, October 20, citing the Washington Post, that the American government is holding back the delivery of Patriot missiles to Ukraine. According to the paper, some of the interceptors come from European and other friendly countries. Because the missiles were made in the United States, Defense Minister Pete Hegseth has to approve their resale to Kyiv. He is said not to have formally rejected the transfer, but also not to have taken the procedural steps for approval. De Telegraaf headlined that Ukraine has only a few missiles left. De Volkskrant wrote that the Pentagon is delaying the delivery, as Zelensky says.

These are weapons that have already been ordered and paid for, among other things through the NATO program PURL, with which European allies buy American weapons for Ukraine. Zelensky said on Monday in an online meeting with American members of Congress, according to the Kyiv Independent, that the missiles are ready but that there is no permission yet. A senior Pentagon official told the media that there is no change in policy on intelligence sharing or on the arrangement under which European countries supply American weapons to Ukraine. Zelensky had said on his Telegram channel that he had spoken in Kyiv with a team from Lockheed Martin about the Patriot systems and F-16 aircraft. Whether Hegseth has meanwhile approved the transfer has not been reported.

Videoland may take over Viaplay Netherlands: the ACM gives the green light and Formula 1 and darts move along

The Authority for Consumers & Markets (ACM) on Tuesday, October 20, approved the takeover of the Dutch arm of Viaplay by Videoland, reported NU.nl, NOS, AD and De Telegraaf. As a result, Formula 1 and darts may move from Viaplay to Videoland for good, wrote NOS. AD headlined that for subscribers nothing changes for now. Videoland is part of DPG Media, which announced in August that it would buy the sports rights and programming of Viaplay Netherlands. According to TPO, the amount is 142 million euros. Besides Formula 1, the Premier League, PDC darts and hockey will also move to Videoland. The current Formula 1 license runs until 2029, after which other parties can bid again. The ACM ruled that Videoland does not become too large through the takeover, partly because Viaplay is relatively small in the Netherlands and focused mainly on sport. The takeover has not yet been completed: according to TPO, no date is known yet, and nothing has been reported on the consequences for subscription prices.

National churches ask thousands of local churches to offer status holders housing for three months

On Tuesday, October 20, the national churches called on thousands of local churches, parishes and faith communities to temporarily house status holders on a small scale, reported NU.nl, NOS, AD and NRC. The initiative is called Actie Onderdak and comes from the Council of Churches in the Netherlands, together with the Protestant Church and the Roman Catholic Church. It concerns mainly families with a residence permit who are still waiting for a home.

Churches are asked to take in one or more families for three months, for example in an empty rectory, a church annex or a holiday home. The COA makes sure that after those three months people can move on to a new place. The churches discussed the plan with asylum minister Bart van den Brink (CDA) and worked it out with the ministry and the COA. Participating churches receive guidance from INLIA and their costs are reimbursed. Participation is voluntary; it is not yet known how many churches will take part.

NS raises train ticket prices by an average of 4.7 percent on January 1 and promises more discounts in quiet trains

NS announced on Tuesday, October 20, that train tickets will become 4.7 percent more expensive on average on January 1, 2027, reported AD, Het Parool and de Volkskrant. Most season tickets also go up. According to the Volkskrant report, this is offset by a higher discount in quiet trains; for that, NS is introducing PrijsTijd Deals with discounts of up to 80 percent.

According to the reports, the increase consists of expected inflation of 2.7 percent, a correction for this year’s inflation and an extra percentage point with which price rises from the years 2020 through 2023 are partly still being passed on. A single trip Zwolle–Amsterdam Centraal goes from 24.20 to about 25.34 euros. The travelers’ association Rover can live with passing on inflation, but not with that catch-up indexation, which costs travelers about 1 percent extra. NS director Daan Schut points to new trains, higher costs and investments.

Economy · Consumers✓ 5 outlets · when the vouchers arrive is not fixed

Thuisbezorgd and Uber Eats will show mandatory extra costs up front from now on and give millions of customers a discount voucher

Thuisbezorgd and Uber Eats will from now on clearly inform customers about mandatory extra costs, such as service fees and surcharges for small orders or packaging, reported NU.nl, AD, De Telegraaf and Het Parool on Tuesday, October 20. Millions of existing customers receive one or more discount vouchers of 5 to 30 euros as compensation. This is a discount on later orders, not cash.

The Consumers’ Association had reported in July that Thuisbezorgd showed mandatory costs only during or at the end of the order and that orders therefore turned out 2 to 4 euros more expensive on average. Thuisbezorgd is counting service fees paid between October 31, 2022 and August 17, 2026; Uber Eats is looking at orders between October 1, 2021 and September 30, 2026. According to the Consumers’ Association, anyone who accepts a voucher gives up further claims over the unclear costs of the past. Uber Eats customers who have turned off commercial email have to request the voucher from customer service.
