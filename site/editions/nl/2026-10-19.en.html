World · France✓ 8 outlets · final count of arrests and damage unconfirmed

France counts at least 1,800 arrests after protests, Limoges city hall burns down and Macron promises punishment

On Saturday, October 17, demonstrations across France turned into looting and arson. AP and CBS News report at least 1,800 arrests; President Macron addressed the nation on Sunday evening.

On Saturday, October 17, France went through a day of nationwide protests that in many places turned into looting and arson. The news agency AP writes, in reports carried by ABC News and the Washington Times, among others, that police arrested at least 1,800 people after masked groups clashed with officers and looted shops. According to AP, that comes close to the nearly 2,000 arrests in a single day during the yellow vest protests of December 2018. CBS News reports that the demonstrators were responding to calls from a trade union, student groups and activists who want to revive the yellow vest movement, and that the anger centers on the cost of living and the state of education. The nineteenth-century city hall of Limoges largely burned down and its roof collapsed, report AP, Fox News and IBTimes. The AP and CBS News reports cover demonstrations across the whole country, not only in Paris. In Marseille, demonstrators briefly forced their way into the lobby of city hall, according to AP. How many people were injured and how great the damage is, is not known.

On Sunday evening President Macron addressed the nation, after an emergency meeting with senior officials, AP writes. RTÉ reports that he said rioters “must pay”, and NPR writes that he wants to hold to account everyone who wanted to bring down the Republic. According to AP, he said looters must compensate for the damage and spoke of rebellious elements from radical-left groups, joined by numerous criminals. According to AP, he also said that anyone who vandalized or looted, including at city halls, will be prosecuted and punished. He called for calm and instructed prefects, together with mayors, to protect public buildings and shops, banning demonstrations if necessary. NOS and the AD described his speech as a lashing out at the rioters while the unrest in Paris continued. On Sunday, hundreds of demonstrators gathered again on the Place de la République in Paris, where police used tear gas, according to AP. Whether the blockades and riots continue on Monday and what punishments follow is not yet known.

Science · Spaceflight✓ 4 outlets · exact launch time may shift

Japan launches a probe on Monday evening to bring back samples from Phobos, a moon of Mars

Japan launches a space probe on Monday evening Dutch time to bring back samples from a moon of Mars, writes NU.nl, which calls it the first space mission to one of those moons. The space agency JAXA plans the first launch attempt for October 20 at 4:41 a.m. Japanese time, which is 21:41 Dutch time on Monday, October 19, report Space.com and Nippon.com. The probe, called MMX, departs on an H3 rocket from Tanegashima. The time may shift within the reserved launch period. According to Space.com and CTV News, the probe arrives at Mars about a year after launch and stays in orbit there for about three years. It will try to land on Phobos and collect at least 10 grams of surface material. A French-German rover, IDEFIX, makes observations on the surface, and the NASA-developed instrument MEGANE measures gamma and neutron radiation. The capsule with the material is due to land in Australia in 2031. The mission was originally planned for 2024 and slipped because of problems with the H3 rocket, say Space.com and CTV News.

Netherlands · Amsterdam✓ 4 outlets · suspect and cause of death unconfirmed

Hajar (29) found dead in the water at the Amsteldijk, police assume a crime

Hajar, 29, from Amsterdam was found dead on Friday, October 16, in the water at the Amsteldijk, report AT5 and NH Nieuws. She was last seen on Sunday, October 4, at the RAI in Amsterdam-Zuid, where, according to police, she cycled away with a man not yet identified, heading toward Buitenveldert. Because the body was found under suspicious circumstances, police assume a crime and a Large-Scale Investigation Team is at work. No one has been arrested yet.

AT5 and NH Nieuws write that police have asked local residents about a suspicious situation involving a suitcase. Moordplekken reports that residents say they saw the body in a suitcase, which police do not confirm. De Telegraaf reports on Sunday that businesspeople in De Pijp say police have requested camera footage in recent weeks, and that people in the neighborhood wonder whether they can still walk the streets safely. A description of a suspect has not been released.

Politics · VVD✓ 4 outlets · poll figures differ by pollster

Yeşilgöz wants to remain lead candidate as the VVD slips to 15 seats in the polls

The VVD is under pressure, write the AD and Het Parool. Party leader Dilan Yeşilgöz is under fire, but the party leadership says there is no panic. WNL reports on an Ipsos I&O poll of October 16 that D66 and the VVD lose heavily and each come in at 15 seats. EenVandaag wrote, based on a poll of October 13, that only about a quarter of VVD voters still want Yeşilgöz to be lead candidate again.

After a meeting with critical members, Yeşilgöz said she wants to remain lead candidate, according to EenVandaag. The criticism within the party, according to those reports, concerns the scrapping of cuts to social security and the problems around box 3. Whether a challenger will stand or the party will wait for a new poll is not known.

Sport · Weekend✓ 10 outlets · prize money differs by source

Lavreysen takes his eighth consecutive world sprint title and Zonneveld wins the Dutch Darts Championship in Maastricht

Harrie Lavreysen took his eighth consecutive world sprint title on the final day of the track cycling World Championships in Shanghai, report CyclingFlash, Wielerflits and Bicycling.nl. That completed a hat-trick: earlier he also won the team sprint with Roy van den Berg and Jeffrey Hoogland, and the keirin, his fifth world title in that event, report CyclingFlash and Global Keirin. Hoogland won the kilometer on Friday, his fifth world title in that event, write CyclingFlash and Ground News.

Niels Zonneveld won the Dutch Darts Championship in Maastricht on Sunday by beating Danny Noppert 8-5 in an all-Dutch final, report the PDC, NOS and DartsNews. It is his first PDC ranking title, and he qualifies for the Grand Slam of Darts for the first time. Noppert led 2-0 early in the final. The AD reports a top prize of 41,000 euros; the British reports give 35,000 pounds. Noppert had beaten Gian van Veen 7-5 in the semifinal, report DartsNews and Dartfreakz.
