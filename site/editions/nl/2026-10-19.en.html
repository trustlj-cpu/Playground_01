World · France✓ 8 outlets · final count of arrests and damage unconfirmed

France counts at least 1,800 arrests after protests, Limoges city hall burns out and Macron promises punishment

On Saturday, October 17, demonstrations across France ended in looting and arson. AP and CBS News report at least 1,800 arrests; President Macron addressed the nation on Sunday evening.

On Saturday, October 17, France lived through a day of nationwide protests that in many places turned into looting and arson. The news agency AP writes, in reports carried by ABC News and the Washington Times among others, that police arrested at least 1,800 people after masked groups clashed with officers and looted shops. According to AP, that comes close to the nearly 2,000 arrests in a single day during the Yellow Vest protests of December 2018. CBS News reports that the protesters were responding to calls from a trade union, student groups and activists who want to revive the Yellow Vest movement, and that the anger centers on the cost of living and the state of education. The nineteenth-century city hall of Limoges largely burned out and its roof collapsed, report AP, Fox News and IBTimes. The reports by AP and CBS News concern protests across the whole country and not only Paris. In Marseille, protesters briefly forced their way into the hall of city hall, according to AP. How many people were injured and how great the damage is, is not known.

On Sunday evening President Macron addressed the nation after an emergency meeting with senior officials, AP writes. RTÉ reports that he said rioters “must pay”, and NPR writes that he wants to hold to account everyone who sought to bring down the Republic. According to AP, he said looters must compensate for the damage and spoke of rebellious elements from radical-left groups, joined by numerous criminals. According to AP, he also said that anyone who destroyed or looted, including in city halls, will be prosecuted and punished. He called for calm and instructed prefects, together with mayors, to protect public buildings and shops, if necessary by banning demonstrations. NOS and the AD described his speech as a lash out at the rioters, while the riots in Paris continued. On Sunday, hundreds of protesters gathered again on the Place de la République in Paris, where police used tear gas, according to AP. Whether the blockades and riots continue on Monday and which punishments follow is not yet known.

Science · Spaceflight✓ 4 outlets · exact launch time may shift

Japan launches a probe on Monday evening to bring back samples from Phobos, a moon of Mars

Japan launches a spacecraft on Monday evening Dutch time that is to bring back samples from a moon of Mars, writes NU.nl, which calls it the first space mission to one of those moons. The space agency JAXA plans the first launch attempt for October 20 at 4:41 Japan time, which is 21:41 Dutch time on Monday, October 19, report Space.com and Nippon.com. The probe, called MMX, departs on an H3 rocket from Tanegashima. The time may shift within the reserved launch window. According to Space.com and CTV News, the probe arrives at Mars about a year after launch and stays in orbit there for about three years. It tries to land on Phobos and collect at least 10 grams of surface material. A French-German rover, IDEFIX, makes observations on the surface, and the NASA-developed instrument MEGANE measures gamma and neutron radiation. The capsule with the material is to land in Australia in 2031. The mission was originally planned for 2024 and slipped because of problems with the H3 rocket, according to Space.com and CTV News.

Netherlands · Amsterdam✓ 4 outlets · suspect and cause of death unconfirmed

Hajar (29) found dead in the water at the Amsteldijk, police assume a crime

Hajar, 29, from Amsterdam was found dead on Friday, October 16, in the water at the Amsteldijk, report AT5 and NH Nieuws. She was last seen on Sunday, October 4, at the RAI in Amsterdam-Zuid, where, according to police, she rode off on a bicycle with a man who has not yet been identified, in the direction of Buitenveldert. Because the body was found under suspicious circumstances, police assume a crime and a Large-Scale Investigation Team (Team Grootschalige Opsporing) is at work. Nobody has been arrested yet.

AT5 and NH Nieuws write that police asked local residents about a suspicious situation involving a suitcase. Moordplekken reports that residents say they saw the body in a suitcase, which police do not confirm. De Telegraaf reports on Sunday that business owners in De Pijp say police have requested camera footage in recent weeks, and that neighbors wonder whether they can still walk the streets safely. A description of a suspect has not been released.

Politics · VVD✓ 4 outlets · level of the polls differs by agency

Yeşilgöz wants to remain lead candidate as the VVD slips to 15 seats in the polls

The VVD is under pressure, write the AD and Het Parool. Party leader Dilan Yeşilgöz is under fire, but the party leadership says there is no panic. WNL reports on an Ipsos I&O poll of October 16 that D66 and the VVD lose heavily and each land on 15 seats. EenVandaag wrote, on the basis of a poll of October 13, that only about a quarter of VVD voters still want Yeşilgöz to be lead candidate again.

After a conversation with critical members, Yeşilgöz said she wants to remain lead candidate, according to EenVandaag. The criticism within the party concerns, according to these reports, the scrapping of cuts to social security and the problems surrounding box 3. Whether a rival candidate will emerge or the party waits for a new poll is not known.

Sport · Weekend✓ 10 outlets · prize money differs by source

Lavreysen takes his eighth consecutive world sprint title and Zonneveld wins the Dutch Darts Championship in Maastricht

Harrie Lavreysen took his eighth consecutive world title in the sprint on the final day of the track cycling World Championships in Shanghai, report CyclingFlash, Wielerflits and Bicycling.nl. That completed a hat-trick: earlier he also won the team sprint with Roy van den Berg and Jeffrey Hoogland, and the keirin, his fifth world title in that event, report CyclingFlash and Global Keirin. Hoogland won the kilometer on Friday, his fifth world title in that event, write CyclingFlash and Ground News.

Niels Zonneveld won the Dutch Darts Championship in Maastricht on Sunday by beating Danny Noppert 8-5 in an all-Dutch final, report the PDC, NOS and DartsNews. It is his first PDC ranking title, and he qualifies for the Grand Slam of Darts for the first time. Noppert led the final 2-0 at first. The AD reports a top prize of 41,000 euros, the British reports mention 35,000 pounds. In the semi-final, Noppert had beaten Gian van Veen 7-5, report DartsNews and Dartfreakz.
