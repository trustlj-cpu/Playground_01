Iran claims it damaged US F-16 and shot down MQ-1 over Persian Gulf… breaking report says US Central Command said “no strike took place”

Iran’s military claimed its air-defense missile damaged a US F-16 and forced it to make an emergency landing. A breaking report said US Central Command denied it, and neither side’s claim has been independently confirmed.

On the 21st, Iran’s military claimed it had hit a US F-16 fighter jet over the Persian Gulf with its own air-defense missile, forcing an emergency landing, and had shot down a US MQ-1 drone. The claim was relayed through state media. A breaking report followed that US Central Command said Iran had not struck an F-16, and reports the same day said there had been no immediate confirmation from the US side. Whether the F-16 was actually damaged, whether the MQ-1 was really shot down, and exactly when and where the incident took place have not been independently confirmed. Earlier, on the 20th, an Iranian semi-official news agency reported explosions and flashes of light over Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz and said an enemy aircraft may have been shot down. It is also not known whether the two claims refer to the same incident.

At the time of this claim, there are reports that calls for further attacks on Iran are growing inside the United States and Israel. According to reports citing multiple sources, Israeli Prime Minister Netanyahu is worried about how fast Iran is restoring its ability to make ballistic missiles and moving nuclear and military facilities deep underground, and prefers joint operations with the United States if military action is taken. President Trump, by contrast, is said to be taking the position that there is no hurry, and it was reported to be unclear whether further strikes have been decided. The same day, Iranian Foreign Minister Araghchi restated the existing position that Iran still wants a diplomatic solution with the United States to end the war. It is not yet known whether the United States will give an official position on Iran’s claim, or when it will decide on further strikes.

President Lee: “Is recovering 550 billion a breach of trust?” rebuts Daejang-dong charges… Jang Dong-hyuk: “If you feel wronged, stand trial”

President Lee Jae-myung rebutted head-on the indictment related to the Daejang-dong case. Citing the recovery of 550 billion won, the president said in effect that it was not a breach of trust, and reportedly shared on social media a video titled ‘Daejang-dong was the biggest success since Dangun.’ On the 21st, Jang Dong-hyuk retorted, ‘If you feel wronged, stand trial.’ Jang asked, ‘If you steal 10 million won and give back 5 million won, are you innocent?’ and said, ‘The longer the tongue, the longer the sentence.’ Jang also demanded, ‘Why push a “prison-break law” instead of standing trial?’ On the ruling-party side, there was a reaction that Jang is badly mistaken. The 550 billion won is the scale of public-interest recovery cited by the president’s side, and interpretations have differed over how this figure should be counted. Whether a breach of trust is established, and the schedule for handling the legislation dubbed the ‘prison-break law,’ were not confirmed in this exchange.

Six cases caught in first national qualification exam since tax accountant exam AI cheating confession… random phone calls during exam set off ringtones

In the first national qualification exam held since the top passer of the tax accountant exam confessed to cheating with artificial intelligence, six cases of cheating were caught. The method used was to place random phone calls to test-takers during the exam and check whether ringtones sounded, and there were test-takers who were caught when ringtones actually sounded. The caught cases included possession of electronic devices. The tally of six is worded differently depending on the report, as six invalidated cases or six people caught. The Human Resources Development Service of Korea had earlier begun a full inspection of other national qualification exams. Which exam this was, the number of test-takers and the individual penalties have not been confirmed. It is also not yet known whether the checking method will continue with the same intensity at the next exam.

EU and Indonesia sign Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement… 90% or more of tariffs to be eliminated

The European Union and Indonesia signed a Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement and an investment protection agreement on the 21st (local time) at EU headquarters in Brussels, Belgium. European Commission President von der Leyen and Airlangga Hartarto, Indonesia’s Coordinating Minister for Economic Affairs, signed. The deal eliminates 90% or more of tariffs on exports and imports. However, the elimination rate varies with the basis used, and European Commission material describes it as 98% or more by number of items. Before the signing, on the 19th, EU member-state governments approved it, and it is known to go next through the European Parliament’s approval process. Indonesia is known to graduate from the EU’s Generalized System of Preferences on January 1, 2027. When the agreement will actually enter into force has not been confirmed.

SK Chairman Chey Tae-won in Tokyo: “Universities must be redesigned for the AI era”… proposes joint Korea-Japan company-university platform

SK Group Chairman Chey Tae-won stressed in Tokyo on the 21st that in the age of artificial intelligence universities must adopt a completely new educational system. Chey said universities must redesign everything starting with exams, degrees and the way they are run. Chey then proposed building a joint platform bringing together companies and universities from Korea and Japan, and reports variously conveyed the intent as jointly nurturing AI talent.

Chairman Chey had also proposed earlier, in June at a Korea-Japan economic event held in Tokyo, growing Korea-Japan economic solidarity into a standing cooperation framework. The companies and universities to take part in the platform, the launch date and the funding are not known. How Japanese universities and companies responded to the proposal has also not been confirmed.
