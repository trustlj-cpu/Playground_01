Haze reaches its worst level this season as the Grand Prix weekend opens, and N95 masks will go to vulnerable people if the PSI passes 200

The 24-hour PSI hit 181 in central Singapore at noon on Friday, the highest of this haze season. The National Environment Agency says it may enter the very unhealthy range.

Air quality in Singapore worsened on Friday to the highest level of this haze season. CNA reported that the 24-hour PSI, the Pollutant Standards Index, reached 181 in the central region at noon. That is inside the unhealthy band of 101 to 200. The Straits Times reported that the National Environment Agency (NEA) said the reading may enter the very unhealthy range, above 200, and that on the current situation it was possible the 24-hour PSI could pass that mark.

The Haze Task Force set out what happens next. CNA reported that N95 masks will be distributed to more vulnerable individuals once the 24-hour PSI exceeds 200, and the Straits Times said eligible Singaporeans will receive them when the PSI hits 201. CNA also reported that school closures will be considered only when the forecast for the next day exceeds a 24-hour PSI of 300. DailyDrop has not seen the full list of who qualifies for a free mask, so check the agency's notices. NEA guidance says vulnerable people should avoid or limit time outdoors when the forecast is very unhealthy.

The Formula 1 Singapore Grand Prix opened in this air. CNA reported that many people put on masks and cut back on outdoor activities, while others kept to their plans, including going to the race and its events. NEA exempted the Circuit Park from the 10-cent deposit on beverage containers under the BCRS, CNA and the Straits Times reported. On track, Max Verstappen took pole position for the sprint ahead of George Russell, Sky Sports and CNA reported, and kept it without a grid penalty after a stewards' inquiry into an overtake under yellow flags, according to NU.nl.

The smoke is worse across the Causeway. CNA reported that 37 areas in Malaysia recorded unhealthy air pollution readings on Friday morning, and that schools were shut in many parts of the country. Reuters said schools were closed in several states and territories. The Straits Times reported that Selangor postponed its National Sports Day celebrations to protect children and other vulnerable groups. On Thursday, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim said Indonesia had agreed to act on the fires; DailyDrop has not seen what steps have been announced. CNA and the Straits Times also reported on orangutans rescued from the fires in Indonesia, one of which walked through flames.

The fine particles known as PM2.5 are what matter most for health. A 24-hour index smooths out spikes, so hourly readings can look worse or better than the headline number. Today is the first full day of the race weekend, and the sprint race and qualifying are scheduled at Marina Bay. Today, watch the NEA readings through the day, whether the 24-hour PSI crosses 200 and triggers the mask distribution, and whether wind shifts clear the air. The Singapore stock market is closed today; the Straits Times Index ended Friday at 5,401.89, down 0.20 per cent.

Malaysia · EconomyPress ✓ 4 outlets · figures as reported by CNA

Malaysia's Budget 2027 raises minimum wages and tax relief, and targets a fiscal deficit of 3.3% before an election

Malaysian Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim tabled Budget 2027 on Friday, pledging reforms to let strong growth trickle down to ordinary Malaysians, CNA reported. The government plans to raise minimum wages and expand tax relief, CNA said. It expects the fiscal deficit to fall to 3.3 per cent of gross domestic product in 2027, on the way to 3 per cent by 2028, even though the budget is larger. Reuters had reported before the speech that spending would be about US$112.5 billion.

The Straits Times said the tax reliefs are aimed at winning back urban voters and small businesses, but that discontent persists, and called the budget a spending push as Mr Anwar enters the final stretch before a general election, with his coalition having lost ground to the opposition. The Business Times said wage rises and tax relief deliver quick gains, but investors will look for productivity and fiscal discipline.

Malaysia is one of Singapore's largest trading partners and a main source of workers. A steadier Malaysian economy helps, and higher wages across the Causeway may narrow pay gaps over time. DailyDrop has not yet seen the full list of measures, so figures beyond those above are not confirmed.

World · LawPress ✓ 6 outlets · statements as reported

US sanctions the International Criminal Court itself, hours after a former ICC judge wins the Nobel Peace Prize

The United States imposed sanctions on the International Criminal Court on Friday, hours after South Africa's Navi Pillay, a former judge of the court, won the Nobel Peace Prize, Reuters, AP and the BBC reported. Al Jazeera said the measures hit the court as an institution and quoted Secretary of State Marco Rubio calling it a "rogue court". AP described them as part of a drive to dismantle the tribunal. In August Washington had sanctioned the court's president, Tomoko Akane, NHK reported.

Ms Pillay, 85, is a former UN human rights chief. The Norwegian Nobel Committee said she had been instrumental in ensuring that war crimes, crimes against humanity and genocide are prosecuted, CNA reported. The ICC said the prize shows that justice is key to lasting peace (Hindustan Times). Eight governments, including the UK, Germany, France, Japan and Canada, issued a joint statement of support for the court (GOV.UK), and Israel's foreign ministry condemned the award (The Hill). Singapore has not joined the court's founding treaty, the Rome Statute. The open question is how far the sanctions reach banks and firms that deal with the court; DailyDrop has not seen the legal text.

Markets · United StatesPress ✓ 5 outlets · index levels from market data

Wall Street ends the week higher as SpaceX rattles US telecoms, consumer mood sinks and bond yields stay near 5.24%

US stocks closed higher on Friday. The S&P 500 ended at 7,811.54 (+0.59%), the Nasdaq Composite at 27,366.17 (+0.64%) and the Dow Jones Industrial Average at 51,654.95 (+0.83%), according to exchange closing data. SpaceX announced on Thursday that it would buy low-band spectrum for its Starlink Mobile service, CNA and Bloomberg reported. The FT said US telecom groups lost about US$45 billion in value, CNBC said Verizon was heading for its worst day since 2002, and Reuters reported Deutsche Telekom fell 7 per cent.

The University of Michigan's consumer sentiment index fell to 46.3 in October, FXStreet reported, and Reuters said sentiment worsened further. Pimco's Ivascyn told the FT the US 10-year Treasury yield risks hitting 6 per cent for the first time since 2000, Reuters reported; the yield was near 5.244%. CNA reported huge inflows into money market funds as bonds sold off. West Texas Intermediate crude was near US$91.63 (+0.15%) and gold near US$4,220 (+1.51%). Reuters said oil fell after President Trump's comments on Iran talks, though ING noted Brent was still above US$100. The Singapore market is shut today and reopens on Monday.

Singapore · TransportPress ✓ 2 outlets · dealer views as reported

Car dealers say the proposed S$15,000 COE surcharge is too low to deter luxury buyers, as mass-market buyers wait

CNA reported that some dealers say the proposed surcharge of up to S$15,000 would be too low to put off buyers of luxury cars. Some prospective buyers of mass-market cars are holding off while the Land Transport Authority runs a public consultation on merging Certificate of Entitlement categories A and B into one category for cars, with rebates and surcharges based on a model's open market value. The Straits Times reported that motorists and dealers are waiting to see how the changes would affect prices, and that dealers want more information as incentives for electric vehicles are due to expire.

The proposal is not final. Premiums for cars depend on how many permits are released and on bidding, so no price effect can be judged until the rules are settled. DailyDrop has not seen figures on how many buyers have paused. If you plan to buy a car, the sensible step is to watch the consultation outcome and the electric-vehicle incentive deadline before committing.
