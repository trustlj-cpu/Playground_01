Showers ease the haze a little but the air stays unhealthy, and Indonesia welcomes Singapore's offer to help fight the fires

NEA forecast the 24-hour PSI to stay in the unhealthy to high unhealthy range, a slight improvement on Friday's outlook. N95 masks go to vulnerable people if it passes 200.

Air quality in Singapore stayed unhealthy on Saturday but improved slightly. CNA's haze update said showers helped a little, and the Straits Times reported that the National Environment Agency (NEA) expects the 24-hour PSI, the Pollutant Standards Index, to stay in the "unhealthy to high unhealthy" range. That is a better outlook than Friday's forecast of "unhealthy to low very unhealthy". On Friday the PSI reached 181 in the central region at noon, the highest of this haze season, CNA reported. The Straits Times said PM2.5 levels were elevated in all five regions. DailyDrop has not seen Saturday's full hourly readings, so check NEA directly. The bands matter: 101 to 200 is unhealthy, 201 to 300 very unhealthy and above 300 hazardous. As DailyDrop reads it, a forecast reaching the high end of unhealthy sits close to the 200 line that triggers masks.

The response plan has not changed. The Haze Task Force said N95 masks will be distributed to more vulnerable individuals once the 24-hour PSI exceeds 200, CNA reported, and CNA also said mask vending machines have been readied for deployment if needed. Minister Desmond Lee said the health and well-being of students and staff remain the top priority, AsiaOne reported.

The fires are in Indonesia, and Singapore moved on that front. Minister for Sustainability and the Environment Grace Fu said Singapore "stands ready" to assist and is in touch with her Indonesian counterpart, CNA and the Straits Times reported. She said: "We are on top of the situation." The Straits Times reported that Indonesia welcomed offers from Singapore and Malaysia, and that an Indonesian official said ground crews would be more useful than helicopters in low visibility. Across the Causeway, Malaysia's Health Ministry said cases of upper respiratory tract infection and conjunctivitis are rising in parts of the country (Straits Times). The SCMP reported renewed pressure on Malaysia to police its own firms that operate in Indonesia, as recurring haze keeps disrupting schools and daily life there. The point about ground crews suggests thick smoke is hampering aircraft, which is DailyDrop's reading, not a statement from Jakarta.

The Formula 1 Singapore Grand Prix carried on in the murk. CNA reported business as usual at the track, with crowds dining and drinking outdoors, although one restaurant manager said fewer locals were turning up because of the haze. Bloomberg Television quoted the Formula 1 chief executive saying the race will go ahead. Qualifying itself was delayed by a downpour, RacingNews365 reported, and Max Verstappen took pole position for today's race (see below).

A 24-hour index smooths out spikes, so hourly numbers can look worse or better than the headline. Today is Sunday, and the Singapore stock market is closed; the Straits Times Index ended Friday at 5,401.89, down 0.20 per cent. Watch the NEA readings through the day, whether the PSI nears 200 and triggers the mask distribution, and whether the wind shifts.

Singapore · HealthPress ✓ 2 outlets · plan as reported by CNA and the Straits Times

IMH is to be expanded and redeveloped by 2040, and renamed the National Centre for Mental Health Singapore

The Institute of Mental Health (IMH) will be expanded and redeveloped by 2040 as Singapore boosts its mental health capacity, CNA reported. The institute will also be redesignated a national specialty centre and renamed the National Centre for Mental Health Singapore in 2027. The Straits Times carried the same plan and added that the Ministry of Health (MOH) will seek public views on parental consent rules for youth mental health. The announcement came on World Mental Health Day, 10 October. In a related local item, the Straits Times reported that Tengah will get its first family service centre in 2027 and that a preventive health hub has opened in Ang Mo Kio, part of the push to bring support closer to home.

IMH is the country's largest psychiatric hospital, with close to 2,000 beds, according to a speech by Health Minister Ong Ye Kung published by MOH on 5 October. DailyDrop has not seen the number of beds planned, the cost or the construction phases, so those are not confirmed. The consent question matters to families: it decides whether teenagers can get counselling or treatment without a parent's sign-off, and MOH has only said it will consult. Families should also note that a new name does not change today's services; IMH and the clinics around it run as before while the plan takes shape over the next 14 years.

World · EnergyPress ✓ 8 outlets · quantities as attributed

Trump's diesel deal with Putin splits allies, and a Russian strike kills 12 in Zaporizhzhia

President Donald Trump said on Friday that Russia has agreed to release diesel to the US and global markets, with Reuters quoting him on 300,000 tons immediately and AP describing a bid to ease prices before the 3 November midterm elections. The New York Times said Mr Trump spoke of potentially millions of tons over coming months, while noting that Russia has a ban on diesel exports. Bloomberg's Chris Kennedy said the proposed November shipment equals roughly one day of American consumption, so pump prices are unlikely to fall much.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskiy called the move an "investment in war" (Reuters). Germany said it will keep its sanctions on Russia (Reuters, Straits Times). On Saturday a Russian attack on the city of Zaporizhzhia killed at least 12 people, including three children, the Straits Times reported; ANSA said Kyiv later put the toll at 15. A Kremlin aide described the Trump-Putin call as "very friendly" (Reuters). DailyDrop has not seen the legal text lifting US sanctions on Russian fuel.

Middle East · AviationPress ✓ 7 outlets · casualty figures differ

Houthi missile attack hits Riyadh's main airport, with injuries reported and flights cancelled

A loud blast hit King Khalid International Airport in the Saudi capital on Saturday, Reuters and the Straits Times reported, amid escalating missile strikes on the kingdom by Yemen's Iran-aligned Houthi group. The Guardian said dozens were reported injured, while Saudi civil aviation authority said several people were hurt and operations were paused, as relayed by FinancialJuice. The New York Times said it was a second attack on the airport after a week of Houthi strikes.

Kuwait Airways, Air India and Air India Express cancelled Riyadh flights (FinancialJuice, Economic Times), and the US and UK embassies told citizens to avoid the airport. Reuters reported that organisers of major Saudi conferences say they will go ahead. The Guardian also reported a claim by government-allied forces in Yemen that they had retaken the Bab al-Mandab strait; that claim is not confirmed. Singapore condemned Houthi strikes on Friday, after earlier attacks on a Saudi airport killed three (Straits Times, CNA).

Singapore · SportPress ✓ 7 outlets · results from the Formula 1 and race reports

Verstappen takes pole for today's Singapore Grand Prix after winning a wet, chaotic sprint

Max Verstappen won Saturday's sprint after a rain delay and a chaotic start. George Russell snatched the lead from him at the start and then crashed out on lap 2, Sky Sports and Formula 1 reported, and Lewis Hamilton finished second. Lance Stroll and Sergio Perez crashed on lap 1, and on the last lap Lando Norris collided with his McLaren team-mate Oscar Piastri (Formula 1, RacingNews365). The stewards issued a decision on that clash; DailyDrop has not seen the details.

Qualifying was held after a downpour, and Verstappen took pole position, the 50th of his career, ahead of Charles Leclerc and Hamilton, the Straits Times, NU.nl and Formula 1 reported. Verstappen said the sprint start was "horrendous" (Formula 1). Race day is today, Sunday, at Marina Bay, in the same haze; organisers have said the race goes ahead.
