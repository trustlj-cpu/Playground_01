Freight train derails at Sojeong-ri Station on Gyeongbu Line… regular train service suspended, 15 extra trains put in

A freight train derailed at Sojeong-ri Station on the Gyeongbu Line on the 21st, disrupting regular services such as Saemaeul and Mugunghwa trains. The cause and the time of restoration are not yet known.

A derailment, in which a freight train left the tracks, occurred at Sojeong-ri Station on the Gyeongbu Line on the 21st. The accident suspended regular train service on a section near Cheonan and disrupted Saemaeul and Mugunghwa trains. Some reports give the site as Sejong and others as Pyeongtaek, so the administrative district is unconfirmed, and the suspended section is also written differently, as between Cheonan and Sojeong-ri Station or between Daejeon and Cheonan. Korail put in 15 extra trains in the wake of the derailment. According to the Korea Railroad Corporation, no casualties have been confirmed so far, and the KTX, which uses the dedicated high-speed line, is running normally. The Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport announced a rail accident crisis alert at the ‘caution’ level.

Second Vice Minister Jeong Ui-gyeong said that restoring the derailment site would put safety first and that inconvenience to passengers would be minimized. A derailment is an accident in which a train’s wheels leave the rail, and a crisis alert is a system by which the government announces its level of response to an accident in stages. The 21st, the day of the accident, is a Saturday. The cause of the accident, the time the restoration will be finished and the time regular service returns to normal have not been confirmed.

President Lee: “CSAT cheating reporting reward is at least 100 million won per case”… add-on for each offender

President Lee Jae-myung said on the 21st that the reporting reward for CSAT cheating is not up to 100 million won but at least 100 million won per case of cheating. In a post on social media, the president said the reward would carry an add-on for each offender. An add-on means an amount given on top of the base amount, but the specific method of calculation has not been confirmed. The conditions for receiving the reward, its funding and when it takes effect are not known either. The remark came after this year’s top scorer in the tax accountant exam confessed to cheating with AI, and after suspicions continued over a perfect scorer on the law school admission test.

President Lee visits SK AI data center construction site in Ulsan: “Speed is life”… first step of SK’s 15GW nationwide plan

President Lee Jae-myung visited the construction site of SK’s artificial intelligence (AI) data center in Ulsan on the 21st and checked progress on the Yeongnam-region mega project. The president said demand for AI data centers and memory would grow further and that speed is life, and compared AI data centers to the Gyeongbu Expressway. The president also made remarks to the effect that balanced development would be achieved through AI investment centered on the provinces. SK Group is pursuing a plan to build AI data centers totaling 15 gigawatts (GW) nationwide, and Ulsan is the first step. One gigawatt is one billion watts of electricity, roughly the power one large nuclear reactor produces.

A data center is a facility that gathers the servers that run AI computation together with power and cooling equipment. Memory chips are used heavily in AI servers, and the president said that demand for memory would grow along with data centers. During this visit, the investment amount, the completion dates by phase and how the power will be supplied were not confirmed. The schedule for the whole 15-gigawatt plan is not known either.

Samsung Electronics names Hwang Sang-jun and Choi Won-joon new heads of memory and Galaxy… Jun Young-hyun and Roh Tae-moon ‘twin-top’ setup kept

In its reshuffle on the 20th, Samsung Electronics put Hwang Sang-jun and Choi Won-joon forward as the new heads to lead the memory business and the Galaxy mobile business, respectively. The ‘twin-top’ setup of Jun Young-hyun and Roh Tae-moon was kept this time as well. Continuing without big change was reported as the feature of this reshuffle. Memory chips are semiconductors that store data, such as DRAM and NAND flash, and Galaxy is Samsung Electronics’ smartphone brand.

The job titles and previous posts of the two newly put forward, and the date from which the reshuffle applies, have not been confirmed. How many people changed in other business organizations is not known either. An explanation of which businesses the twin-top setup divides between them has not been officially confirmed.

South Korea’s exports top $1 trillion for the first time… fourth in the world after Germany, the US and China

South Korea’s exports topped $1 trillion for the first time. The Ministry of Industry and Trade said on the 19th that cumulative exports this year had provisionally been tallied as exceeding $1 trillion. South Korea became the fourth country in the world to set this record, after Germany, the US and China. $1 trillion is ten times $100 billion, and simply dividing it by a population of about 50 million comes to about $20,000 worth per person. Reports said hit products such as K-cosmetics and instant noodles led exports.

One outlet put forward a forecast that the strong exports would lift growth projections and make a further benchmark interest rate hike by the Bank of Korea a near certainty, but the Bank of Korea’s official position has not been confirmed. An editorial also appeared saying that the risks hidden behind the figures must be faced squarely. The final annual tally has not been confirmed.
