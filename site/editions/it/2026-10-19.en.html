News · Public order✓ 12 outlets · list of summit participants and measures not yet known

After the clashes in Milan, Meloni calls a public-order summit for Monday, the Lega demands preventive detention even for minors

Saturday October 17’s march against the cost of living turned violent in Piazza Duomo; Piantedosi speaks of “professionals of disorder” and Mayor Sala of too few officers.

On Saturday, October 17, the Milan march against the cost of living turned violent in Piazza Duomo. Sky TG24 reports that the first clashes between some participants and the police took place in Via Broletto and that the disorder continued in Piazza Duomo, Piazza Cordusio and the Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II after the demonstration had been dissolved. ANSA, Il Mattino and Today.it speak of stones, paper bombs and fireworks thrown at officers, and Il Giorno adds that a construction site was set on fire. The Questura’s toll, picked up by MilanoToday and Tuttonotizie, gives 72 people taken to the police station, 16 arrests rising to 17 in the later update, 22 people formally reported and 104 law-enforcement officers who needed medical care, a figure described as still partial. In the first hours the numbers were lower: ANSA counted about 50 detained, almost half of them minors, and Il Fatto Quotidiano 25 injured, ten of them officers. Leggo puts the damage at over 500,000 euros.

On Sunday, October 18, Meloni said the government stands with the police and that citizens must be able to live in their city without fear, and called a public-order meeting at Palazzo Chigi for Monday, October 19 (ANSA, Il Fatto Quotidiano, Il Messaggero). On Saturday evening she had said that any reticence about street violence becomes justification (ANSA, Today.it). According to Il Fatto Quotidiano and Il Messaggero, Ministers Piantedosi and Crosetto and Deputy Prime Ministers Salvini and Tajani would sit at the table; neither the list of participants nor the measures have been made public. Piantedosi spoke of “actions by professionals of disorder” and said that not even minors will go unpunished (ANSA). The Lega, write ANSA and Il Messaggero, is calling for an urgent decree with preventive detention even for minors, a ban on assembly for those who commit group violence and an offense of group damage; Il Messaggero lists among the options a measure modeled on the Caivano decree or speeding up some provisions of the security bill, under discussion in the Senate. Mayor Sala said he had the impression that the moment was underestimated and spoke of about 200 police officers, “there were few”; Piantedosi replied on X with a “usual reflex” of a certain radical-chic left (Adnkronos, LaPresse, Il Messaggero).

Politics · Magistrates✓ 7 outlets · amounts differ between outlets

Meloni will not sign the Dpcm on magistrates’ raises, the Anm weighs an appeal

On October 18 Palazzo Chigi let it be known that Meloni is not signing the Dpcm that would implement the pay raises for magistrates, for an amount of about one billion euros, report ANSA and Tgcom24. In an interview with Adnkronos the prime minister said the sum is ten times the figure the government is seeking for road haulage, that if the Council of State sees fit it can appoint a commissioner, and that she remains ready to listen to the Anm. Il Sole 24 Ore and Panorama say the dispute concerns the adjustment for the 2018-2020 three-year period, for which the Council of State found the 2021 Dpcm partly unlawful, and that the total bill could approach one billion with the following three-year periods. The Anm’s deputy secretary, Stefano Celli, replied that the association will use the instruments provided by the legal system to assert its rights, the same as other citizens, and will weigh an appeal; in case of non-application or partial application, it would proceed with compliance proceedings (Il Fatto Quotidiano, Adnkronos, Open). The outlets report different amounts and no date has been given for the decree.

Labor · Cgil✓ 6 outlets · participants: organizers’ estimate

Landini announces a Cgil general strike for November 20 from the stage in Rome, Schlein and Conte: let’s work together

On Saturday, October 17, the Cgil marched in Rome from Piazza della Repubblica to Piazza del Popolo with the demonstration “La Via Maestra,” promoted with over a hundred associations and the motto “Work, peace, environment, welfare, Constitution” (Sky TG24, Adnkronos). Landini estimated more than 100,000 participants, a figure from the organizers (Il Fatto Quotidiano), and announced the general strike for Friday, November 20, tying it to the government’s budget package (Adnkronos, Il Sole 24 Ore, Il Post). From the stage he proposed a solidarity contribution of 1% on net assets above 2 million euros, from which he estimates about 25 billion for health, schools and housing (Il Sole 24 Ore, Il Post), and said no to higher military spending. Present at the demonstration were Pd secretary Schlein, M5s leader Conte and Avs leaders Fratoianni and Bonelli; Schlein and Conte said they want to work together (Sky TG24, Adnkronos). Il Post recalls that November 20 coincides with a European student strike.

Politics · Pd✓ 6 outlets · rules of the primaries still to be set

The Pd assembly extends Schlein’s mandate, and she says she is ready to run for prime minister “with or without the primaries”

On Sunday, October 18, the Pd national assembly, meeting at La Nuvola in Rome, approved the extension of Elly Schlein’s mandate at the head of the party (ANSA, Il Messaggero, Today.it). The statute change postpones the congress when it falls in an election year, as 2027 will be, whereas the deadline had been set for March 2027 (Today.it, Tag24). Schlein said she was “ready to lead this community and to lead it as candidate for prime minister, with or without the primaries” and recalled that “they had bet I would not last six months” (ANSA, Il Messaggero). According to the motion approved, if primaries are held the assembly will name the secretary as candidate; the rules of the primaries have not yet been decided (Il Fatto Quotidiano, Today.it). The number of votes in favor, against and abstaining is reported differently by the outlets.

World · Ukraine✓ 5 outlets · talks in Washington unconfirmed

Trump accuses Zelensky of not wanting the war to end, Zelensky replies that Washington is pushing Ukraine toward capitulation

On Sunday, October 18, the clash between Washington and Kyiv sharpened. On Truth, reports ANSA, Trump accused Zelensky of not wanting the war to end and of hitting diesel refineries in Russia while knowing that the raids damage the markets. Zelensky replied that Trump is pushing Ukraine toward capitulation in the face of Russia and proposed halting the attacks on Moscow’s refineries in exchange for Patriot missiles (ANSA, L’Adige). Il Sole 24 Ore reports that the Ukrainian president said the United States has resumed intelligence sharing and that he hopes for talks in Washington; siding with Putin, he added, would be a historic mistake.

The same night, write Il Gazzettino and Il Sole 24 Ore, Ukraine hit St. Petersburg with a drone attack. The outlets do not report a White House response to the Patriot proposal or a date for the talks.
