Salvini resigns as Lega secretary: “A necessary step to run again.” Treasurer Di Rubba leads the party until the congress

On Monday, at the start of the Federal Council, the leader left his post so that he can stand again at the extraordinary congress in Milan. He asked for the deputy secretaries to be cleared out and announced a political secretariat and a new symbol.

On Monday, October 12, Matteo Salvini announced his resignation as secretary of the Lega at the start of the Federal Council, the meeting of party leaders held in via Bellerio, in Milan (ANSA, Tgcom24, la Repubblica, Il Fatto Quotidiano, Sky TG24, Quotidiano Nazionale). The reason, he explained, is formal: to be able to stand at the Lega’s extraordinary congress, scheduled in Milan on October 24 and 25, the rules require him to leave the post first. “It is a necessary step to run again,” he said, according to ANSA. La Repubblica writes that the move was urged by Roberto Calderoli, according to whom it is a “step back necessary under the rules.”

Salvini also asked for the posts of the deputy secretaries to be wiped out (la Repubblica, Tgcom24). Until the congress the party will be led by the treasurer Alberto Di Rubba, whom Tgcom24 calls caretaker leader and ANSA commissioner. The outgoing secretary then announced that a political secretariat will be created and that the symbol will be updated: “The world is changing,” he said, quoted by Il Fatto Quotidiano, and again, according to ANSA, “proud of our roots, but we live in the present and prepare the future.” To those who will not be there, he added, “I am sorry for him.”

Inside the party not everyone is enthusiastic. According to la Repubblica, the party official Durigon reportedly asked: “So we spend fifteen days without a leader?” Il Fatto Quotidiano speaks of mass resignations on the executive board and of an internal settling of scores in the Carroccio, while Il Post describes them as formal resignations that are “a trick”: a judgment by the newspaper, not an established fact. Il Post and La Notizia had already reported in August on calls for a step back from Lombard party officials, worried about the party’s fall in the polls.

What do we not know? In the texts we have read there are no candidates alternative to Salvini and no details of the new symbol, and no source explains how the political secretariat will be made up. Two weeks remain until the congress: these are the days in which we will see whether the Lega really wants to turn the page or whether, as critics claim, the gesture only serves to lock in the leadership. Pope Leo XIV checks into the Gemelli today: audiences cancelled until October 20

The Pope will enter the Gemelli Polyclinic in Rome late this morning for the course of treatment that will lead to the surgical removal of the lung nodule announced on Sunday (Il Fatto Quotidiano, RaiNews, Tgcom24). The Prefecture of the Papal Household has cancelled all audiences until October 20 (ANSA, Quotidiano Nazionale). The date of the operation has not been announced. On the tenth floor of the hospital there is a mini ward reserved for pontiffs, with an apartment, a study and a chapel (ANSA). Among his last meetings before admission, ANSA cites the one with the president of the bishops of Nicaragua, in exile. The pulmonologist consulted by AGI says that the nodule, if operable, can be resolved “without aftereffects”: operability is good news, because it indicates that there are no other lesions. The Argentine newspapers write that the trip to Latin America from November 6 remains in place for now (La Nación, Clarín).

World · UkrainePress ✓ 8 outlets · the truce is not confirmed by Moscow

Ukraine, Trump’s “energy truce” does not hold: Zelensky accuses Moscow, the EU launches new sanctions

On Monday Ukrainian president Volodymyr Zelensky accused Russia of violating the “energy truce” announced on Sunday by Donald Trump, after an attack on a thermal power plant in Kharkiv (Reuters, AP, PBS, The Hill, Al Jazeera). On Sunday he had said he knew nothing about it (Guardian, CBS News) and on Monday he warned that Ukraine will react if the raids continue (Bloomberg). The Kremlin speaks of “contacts under way”: “we do not want a truce but a lasting peace” (ANSA); according to DW, it would consider only a one-day ceasefire tied to elections in Ukraine. According to ANSA, Kyiv reports deaths in Kramatorsk and Kyiv in the evening. On the same day the European Union adopted a new package of sanctions on the Russian military-industrial complex (ANSA, Reuters). High Representative Kallas said she will welcome the truce “when it holds”; asked about an attack by Trump on Zelensky, foreign minister Tajani answered that “it is up to Ukrainians to choose their president.”

News · InvestigationsPress ✓ 6 outlets · conclusions of the commission, not of a court

David Rossi case, the Ris video: “A person in the alley waited for him to die”

On Monday the parliamentary commission of inquiry into the death of David Rossi, the manager of the bank Mps who died in 2013 in Siena, heard the Carabinieri’s Ris, who analyzed a video from the evening of the fall (ANSA, Tgcom24, La Stampa, Il Fatto Quotidiano, RaiNews). According to ANSA the Ris extracted twelve macro sequences in which one or more people pass at the start of the alley. The commission chairman, Vinci, concluded that at least one person stayed there for about twenty minutes, waiting for Rossi to stop breathing and making sure he was not helped (Tgcom24). La Stampa speaks of shadows four minutes after the fall. Vinci added that there would be four or five people behind it and that the commission is following the lead of the 'ndrangheta of lower Lombardy (ANSA, Corriere Fiorentino). These are the commission’s conclusions, not a verdict.

Economy · MarketsPress ✓ 5 outlets · closes of Monday, October 12

Piazza Affari rises with the banks, all eyes on the tug-of-war between Mps and Intesa

On Monday Piazza Affari closed higher, with the Ftse Mib index at +0.58% (ANSA, Yahoo Finance); in Europe the stock markets were positive ahead of quarterly earnings, the companies’ financial statements (ANSA, Borsa Italiana). Stellantis and the banks are among the stocks in focus. The spread between Btp and Bund, the yield difference between Italian and German government bonds, moved between 109 and 110.5 points (ANSA). The focus is on Mps: the board of directors met after the close to respond to Intesa Sanpaolo’s raised bid, and Mps said that the premium offered remains below average (ANSA); SoldiOnline explains that the premium is below the 30% average cited by Siena. Il Sole 24 ORE writes that Mps is not moving the shareholders’ meeting and is going ahead with the public tender offers for Bpm and Banca Generali, while la Repubblica speaks of chief executive Lovaglio’s “last cards.” On Wall Street, in Monday’s closes, the S&P 500 lost 0.06% to 7,807.24 and the Nasdaq 0.44% to 27,245.90 (Reuters, Yahoo Finance). Oil closed higher in New York (ANSA, The Hill).
