Strait of Messina Bridge, the investigation widens: the Ros acquires documents at the Mit, the Cipess and the companies

The carabinieri are working on a mandate from the Rome public prosecutor’s office, while other offices are named as the next targets of the document seizures.

The investigation into the Strait of Messina Bridge is widening. According to ANSA, on Wednesday, October 21 carabinieri of the Ros acquired documents at the Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport, the Mit, and at the Cipess, the interministerial committee that approves major projects, on a mandate from the prosecutors of the Rome public prosecutor’s office. La Repubblica and La Stampa write on the morning of the same day that the activity does not stop at the two offices: Ros officers are tasked with acquiring material also at the Dipe, the department of the Presidency of the Council of Ministers that deals with economic planning, at the Stretto di Messina company and at the consortium in charge of the project. The three outlets speak of the acquisition of records and documents and not of measures against individuals.

ANSA places the news in the early afternoon of Wednesday; Repubblica and La Stampa headline the investigation that widens, with prosecutors acquiring records at the Mit and at the companies. In the texts the sources name the Rome public prosecutor’s office as the holder of the case file and the Ros as its operational arm; they give no names of suspects, no alleged offences and no date for the next phases. In the accounts of ANSA, Repubblica and La Stampa there are no comments from the Mit, the Cipess or the Stretto di Messina company. It remains to be seen which records were taken from the offices, how long the examination of the material will last and whether there will be further checks. The government of Giorgia Meloni, which has backed the project, is not cited in the available passages as the recipient of any measures. The number of prosecutors working on the case file has not been disclosed.

Pope Leo, tests confirm a localized adenocarcinoma: doctors rule out further treatment

Pope Leo’s doctors announced on Wednesday, October 21 the final outcome of the histological tests and molecular biology analyses on the material removed during the operation. According to TGCom24, the results confirm the presence of a carcinoma. La Repubblica writes that the analyses found a localized lung adenocarcinoma and that the disease was confined. ANSA reports the doctors’ conclusion: no further treatment is necessary. ANSA adds that the histology report arrived in the evening and that the Pontiff has returned to the popemobile, with a return to normal engagements. The three outlets attribute the statements to the Pope’s doctors and give neither future check-ups nor the schedule of upcoming public appearances.

Tir truck blockades, the government chooses a hard line: 1,339 identified and 71 reported

After the meeting at Palazzo Chigi with Giorgia Meloni, the government chose a hard line against the protest by Tir trucks. ANSA reports that the Viminale put the number at 1,339 people identified and 71 reported to prosecutors for the road blockades, and that Minister Piantedosi asked prefects and provincial police chiefs to pay attention to criminal infiltration. The Viminale, ANSA writes, will guarantee supplies of basic necessities, with distribution of food and delivery of medicines, and law-enforcement forces are ready to escort the trucks. Meloni has called the Council of Ministers to approve aid for hauliers and farmers. According to ANSA, the mayors of Bari and Naples, Leccese and Manfredi, spoke of a risk of paralysis and of difficulties in waste and transport services, while industrialists in Campania and Puglia warned that businesses are ready to stop. In Sicily and Calabria the blockades have been suspended, with empty markets. ANSA also reports that the opposition parties are asking for Meloni to appear in the chamber.

FT: Netanyahu was ready to attack Iran on October 10, Trump said no
