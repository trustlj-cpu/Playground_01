KOSPI plunges 3.59% to close at 5,648.79… KOSDAQ also down 4.08%, giving back previous day’s rebound

The KOSDAQ closed down 4.08% at 704.39. No official explanation pointing to a single cause for the fall has yet emerged.

The KOSPI plunged 3.59% on the 3rd to close at 5,648.79, which is 210.29 index points below the previous day’s closing price of 5,859.08. The KOSDAQ fell 4.08% to 704.39, down 29.97 points in a single day. Both markets opened with declines in the 2% range the moment trading began, and the losses widened during the session. Someone holding 1 million won in the KOSPI would have lost nearly 36,000 won in a single day. The KOSPI is an index that turns the movement of all shares listed on the Korea Exchange’s main market into a single number, and the KOSDAQ is the index of a market with many small and venture companies.

The KOSPI had risen 2.24% on the 2nd to regain the 5,800 line, and in a single day it gave back all of that gain. Working backward from the 2nd’s gain, the close on Oct. 30 was around 5,730, so today’s close is about 80 points lower even than that. US stocks were also weak overnight. On the 2nd the S&P 500 fell 0.51% to 7,541.40 and the Nasdaq fell 1.07% to 25,164.36. Today is the day of voting in the US midterm elections. There is no official analysis yet explaining the fall, and how much foreign investors and institutions sold has not been confirmed.

Hanyang Securities also hit by hack, with some customer and employee data leaked… NIS asks 2,100-plus ministries and local governments for emergency security inspections

Hanyang Securities also had some customer and employee information leaked in a hack. The incident, which became known on the 3rd, follows a string of hacking incidents in the financial sector in recent days. The scale of the leak, details of the data items and the route of intrusion have not yet been confirmed. The government response has continued as well. On the 3rd, prompted by the recent string of hacks in the financial sector, the National Intelligence Service (NIS) asked more than 2,100 bodies, including central government agencies, local governments and public institutions, to carry out emergency security inspections, and it also began security inspections of major infrastructure and core public facilities. A security inspection is partly a search for damage already done, but it is also a preventive measure to check ahead of time whether the same kind of attack would work elsewhere and to stop it. It does not mean that every body on the inspection list suffered damage. Inspection results and details of the scale of the Hanyang Securities incident have not yet come out.

Democratic Party adopts fabricated prosecution special counsel bill as party line… Judiciary Committee agenda on the 4th, push for plenary passage on the 5th

The Democratic Party of Korea on the 3rd adopted the ‘fabricated prosecution special counsel bill’ as its party line. A party line is the official position that a party’s lawmakers have agreed to follow. The bill would have a special counsel investigate allegations of fabricated prosecutions by prosecutors under the Yoon Suk-yeol government. On the 4th the Democratic Party will take the step of putting the bill on the agenda of the National Assembly Legislation and Judiciary Committee (the Judiciary Committee), and it aims to pass it at a plenary session as early as the 5th. At the lawmakers’ caucus, however, the party said that passage on the 5th is possible but not certain and that the timing would be discussed further, and its stated policy is to pass it within the month.

The People Power Party is protesting, saying the bill is aimed at the withdrawal of indictments. What amendments will be made in the Judiciary Committee, and whether the bill will actually go on the agenda of the plenary session on the 5th, have not yet been decided.

Society · Personal data✓ 4 outlets · route by which the file was uploaded and whether it was actually downloaded unconfirmed

Foreign Ministry: “Air Force One case is a personal data leak, not a hack”… starts reissuing passports for 168 people including presidential couple

The Foreign Ministry said on the 3rd that the case in which information on 168 Air Force One passengers was exposed on the internet was a personal data leak, not a hack. It is carrying out reissuance of passports for those named on the list, including the presidential couple, President Lee Jae-myung and spouse. Once a new passport is issued, the exposed passport number can no longer be used. The Foreign Ministry said it had not confirmed that the leaked information had spread, had taken measures to prevent further leaks, and had asked related agencies for investigation and inquiry.

On the 2nd, a day earlier, it became known that an Excel file of the ‘Air Force One passenger list’ from the June trip to Europe had been on a paid malware-scanning site for about five months, and Cheong Wa Dae said it had known in advance and responded. At that time it was not settled whether this was an outside intrusion or an upload from inside; the Foreign Ministry has now explained that it was not a hack. Who uploaded the file and by what route, and whether the file was actually downloaded while it was exposed, remain open; the Foreign Ministry is at the stage of saying it will investigate thoroughly to find the cause of the leak.

Economy · Prices✓ 3 outlets · each item’s contribution and the core inflation rate unconfirmed

October consumer prices up 2.8%, 0.1 percentage points slower than previous month… petroleum products up 14.9%

October consumer prices were up 2.8% from a year earlier. The rate was 0.1 percentage points lower than September’s, slowing for a second month in a row but not leaving the high-2% range. Because consumer prices are an index built from the prices of goods and services that households buy, it means a basket that cost 100 won a year ago now costs 102.8 won. The 0.1 percentage points is the September rate and the October rate subtracted from each other in percent units as they are.

The biggest rise was in petroleum products, up 14.9% from a year earlier. Processed food prices also kept rising, and restaurant prices rose 2.9%, the biggest increase in nine months. On the other hand, stable agricultural prices held down the overall rise. How much each item added to the overall rate, and the core inflation rate, have not yet been confirmed.
