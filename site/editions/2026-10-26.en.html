After Oh Se-hoon’s acquittal on appeal, Han Dong-hoon says “we should go together”… Democratic Party raises parliamentary investigation of Chief Justice Cho Hee-de

Yonhap reported that Seoul Mayor Oh Se-hoon raised ‘party unity’ the day after his acquittal, and that independent lawmaker Han Dong-hoon said on the 25th, “He is someone we should go with for the conservative camp’s victory.”

Kyunghyang Shinmun reported that on the 23rd the Seoul High Court acquitted Seoul Mayor Oh Se-hoon, indicted for violating the Political Funds Act, in the appellate trial of the case over the alleged proxy payment of Myung Tae-kyun’s poll fees. Newsis reported that the first trial had imposed a fine of 10 million won and that Oh’s side appealed on the day of sentencing. According to Kyunghyang Shinmun’s ‘Verdict Close-up’, the second trial, unlike the first, did not accept the statement of political broker Myung Tae-kyun that ‘he received a poll commission from Mayor Oh and was paid the cost by supporter Kim Han-jung.’ Korea Economic Daily looked at how Myung’s statement “Oh Se-hoon doesn’t know” aroused the court’s doubt, and Yonhap reported that the issue in the Supreme Court appeal of this case, in which guilt and innocence were reversed between the first and second trials, could be corroborating evidence for Myung’s statement.

Yonhap reported that Mayor Oh raised ‘party unity’ on the 25th, the day after his acquittal. Independent lawmaker Han Dong-hoon described Oh on the 25th as “a very reasonable person,” saying he is “someone we should go with for the conservative camp’s victory” and “I have never let go of his hand,” Yonhap and Chosun Ilbo reported. Kyunghyang Shinmun reported that, with assessments emerging that the acquittal has cemented Oh’s standing as a presidential contender, the contest for leadership of the conservative camp has become a three-way race among Oh, party leader Jang Dong-hyuk, who the same day became the longest-serving leader since the founding of the People Power Party, and Han Dong-hoon. Hankyoreh reported that Cho Kuk criticized the Democratic Party, calling the ruling a “counterattack of judicial politics.” Kyunghyang Shinmun reported that Oh’s acquittal intensified impeachment calls against Chief Justice Cho Hee-de, that the Democratic Party leadership raised a parliamentary investigation on the 25th, and that party leader Kim Min-seok said it would proceed “step by step, including a parliamentary investigation.”

Economy · External accountsPress ✓ 4 outlets · the full-year ranking is an IMF projection, not a final figure

Korea’s Q2 current account surplus of $116.63 billion ranks second among 36 countries behind China… first time ahead of Germany and Japan combined

Dong-A Ilbo and Chosun Ilbo reported on the 25th, citing International Monetary Fund (IMF) statistics held in the Bank of Korea’s Economic Statistics System, that Korea’s current account surplus in the second quarter of this year came to $116.63 billion. That is second among the 36 major countries for which the same statistics are available, behind China ($193.72 billion), and $13.74 billion more than the surpluses of Germany ($52.28 billion) and Japan ($50.62 billion) combined, Dong-A Ilbo reported. Chosun Ilbo reported that it was 4.1 times the figure of the same period a year earlier ($28.39 billion).

Kyunghyang Shinmun reported that the IMF projects Korea’s full-year surplus this year to rank second behind China, with the largest increase in the surplus among 190 countries worldwide. Electronic Times reported that strong semiconductor exports lifted the surplus, and that strengthening industrial competitiveness to prepare for a slowdown in artificial intelligence (AI) investment and an expansion of chip supply has emerged as a challenge.

Justice minister candidates for post vacant two months narrowed to two lawmakers with prosecutorial backgrounds, Park Gyun-taek and Yang Bu-nam… President Lee in last-minute deliberation

Yonhap reported on the 24th that the candidates for justice minister, a post vacant for two months, had been narrowed to two lawmakers with prosecutorial backgrounds, including the Democratic Party’s Park Gyun-taek and Yang Bu-nam, and that President Lee Jae-myung is in last-minute deliberation. Under the headline ‘Former prosecutor, Lee Jae-myung’s lawyer,’ Kyunghyang Shinmun reported that the two belong to the pro-Lee Jae-myung faction, were involved in handling President Lee’s legal risk, and put their names on a lawmakers’ group calling for the withdrawal of the indictment in President Lee’s case.

Korea Economic Daily reported that Kim Seung-won, who had earlier been nominated for justice minister, voluntarily withdrew his candidacy on the 19th after a National Assembly confirmation hearing on September 15. Yonhap reported that the justice minister post has been vacant since.

An Se-young comes back to beat Sindhu 2-1 in French Open final… third straight title at the event, 50 straight wins, 10th title of the season

Chosun Ilbo and Dong-A Ilbo reported that An Se-young (Samsung Life) beat India’s Pusarla Sindhu (world No. 11) 2-1 (16-21, 21-11, 21-12) in the women’s singles final of the BWF World Tour French Open (Super 750) held at Arena Grand Paris in Paris, France, on the 25th. Kyunghyang Shinmun reported that An lost the first game but led from the second game on. According to Dong-A Ilbo, An won the event for a third straight year after 2024 and last year, claimed her 10th win of the season, and after the title was sealed made a crown shape with her ten fingers in celebration.

Chosun Ilbo reported that with this title An reached 50 straight wins and is 66-1 in 67 matches this year. Chosun Ilbo reported that her head-to-head record against Sindhu went from 10-0 to 11-0, and Yonhap reported that she won her 10th title of the season with a win rate of 98.5%.
