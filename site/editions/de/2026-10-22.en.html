Russia signals agreement to three-way talks with the US and Ukraine

After a meeting with US special envoy Witkoff, Putin is apparently ready for new negotiations; Zelensky wants talks as soon as possible.

Russia has agreed, according to several media outlets, to take part in a three-way meeting with the United States and Ukraine. Tagesschau reported on Wednesday evening that President Vladimir Putin had apparently agreed to new negotiations after a meeting with US special envoy Witkoff. Earlier it had been reported that Witkoff was traveling to Russia; several reports then said Putin had spoken with him about the war in Ukraine. Russia was ready for talks with Ukraine and the United States, was the summary in the reports. The reports named neither a date nor a place for the three-way meeting.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said he wanted talks with the United States and with Russia “as soon as possible.” Spiegel also reported that Zelensky was open to freezing the front line, and raised the question of how Putin would respond to possible peace solutions in his war of aggression. According to Bloomberg, US Secretary of State Marco Rubio defended the Trump administration’s support for Ukraine on Wednesday and said the country would not exist without US help. A reaction from Moscow to Zelensky’s remark on the front line is not contained in the available reports.

Meta to pay 25 billion dollars over Cambridge Analytica

A US court has sentenced Meta to a fine in the billions in the Cambridge Analytica scandal, reports Zeit Online. Meta must therefore pay 25 billion dollars for deception; the case concerns the 2016 US presidential campaign. Spiegel writes that the scandal shook trust in the company because millions of Facebook accounts were affected, and says the 25-billion-dollar fine is coming into play. Handelsblatt headlined that Meta is to pay billions. At the same time, Meta has presented quarterly results. According to Spiegel, together with the new AI Muse they initially calmed nervous investors. Zeit Online and Handelsblatt report that Facebook founder Mark Zuckerberg is having the company invest at least 135 billion dollars in AI infrastructure.

Serious PHP security flaw threatens websites worldwide

A serious security flaw in the widely used web scripting language PHP threatens websites and online services worldwide, according to reports by Zeit Online, Handelsblatt and Tagesschau. According to Zeit Online, blogs, online shops and news portals could be affected. The vulnerability can give attackers on the web complete control over servers.

IT experts call, according to Zeit Online and Handelsblatt, for unprotected websites to be shut down. Tagesschau sums up in a separate piece what is known about the flaw. The reports do not say how many servers have already been attacked.

Le Pen gains in poll after protests in France

Marine Le Pen is gaining slightly in a poll on the presidential election after the protests in France, Spiegel and Zeit Online report. Spiegel writes of chaotic days in France. Tagesschau reports that President Emmanuel Macron is criticizing US President Donald Trump for his remarks on the unrest.

In Germany, police and authorities are, according to Tagesschau, monitoring calls to demonstrate on October 31 that an initiative has published. Bloomberg reports that the yield on the French benchmark bond has risen to 5 percent for the first time since 2002; rising energy prices had heightened concerns.

Business · Elon Musk✓ 4 outlets · Merger not decided

Tesla posts profit slump, Musk backs merger with SpaceX

Tesla has reported a profit slump on stagnating revenue, according to Zeit Online and Handelsblatt. Elon Musk wants to focus the company on robots and robotaxis; for now, however, the money still has to be earned by selling electric cars.

In parallel, the debate over a merger with SpaceX continues. Tagesschau writes that a combination could create a tech group worth several trillion dollars. Bloomberg reports that Musk supports such a merger; in addition, SpaceX has presented strong quarterly results and both companies are talking about a possible combination. Nothing has been decided.
