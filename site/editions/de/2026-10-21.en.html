Trump open to a three-way meeting with Putin and Xi, Zelensky demands a seat at the negotiating table

According to reports, the idea comes from Putin and could take shape in November on the sidelines of the APEC summit in China; nothing has been agreed.

US President Donald Trump has signaled openness to a three-way meeting with Russian President Vladimir Putin and Chinese leader Xi Jinping. Asked by journalists on Monday (local time), he said he would attend such a meeting; the main topics would be nuclear weapons and nuclear disarmament, but also other issues such as Ukraine. Zeit Online and the Tagesspiegel report this. According to information from the Wall Street Journal, relayed by the Tagesspiegel, the idea comes from Putin: the Kremlin leader wants to meet the other two on the sidelines of the APEC summit, which takes place on November 18 and 19 in Shenzhen, China. According to these reports, China has not confirmed the plans, and the Trump administration has so far not officially confirmed them. Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov confirmed that such a meeting had been discussed. According to the reports, nothing has yet been agreed.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky demands a seat at the table in talks on his country’s future; only then would such a meeting be acceptable, the Tagesspiegel writes. Foreign Minister Andrii Sybiha accordingly warned that a three-way meeting would only encourage Putin to continue the war. At the same time, the tone between Washington and Kyiv is sharpening. According to Zeit Online, Trump said the US had given Ukraine a lot and received nothing back. According to Spiegel and Tagesschau, Zelensky accuses the US of blocking paid-for missile deliveries, and, according to Zeit Online, he spoke with Secretary of State Marco Rubio about the throttling of Starlink and about interceptor missiles. In Oslo, Zelensky met the US envoy Steve Witkoff, according to Handelsblatt and Zeit Online.

Major Google Cloud outage in London knocks out Revolut and disrupts ING and other services in Europe

An outage at Google Cloud disrupted banking apps and online services in Europe on Tuesday, October 20. Zeit Online and Handelsblatt report disruptions at the digital banks Revolut and ING; customers could not log in or pay by card, the Tagesspiegel writes. According to Reuters, Google Cloud services in Europe were affected, Zeit names the London region as the origin, and the FAZ speaks of a major outage hitting Revolut and ING in Europe. British banks were also affected, according to Handelsblatt. According to reports, Google named a power problem (in English “power event”) in one zone of the europe-west2 region as the cause; the first disruptions occurred around 7 a.m. British time, and by midday most workloads had recovered. At ING the disruption was resolved, according to the Tagesspiegel; at Revolut, problems initially remained. The reports give no indication of a cyberattack.

AfD confirms hacker attack on email accounts, unknown parties say they have put 421,000 emails online

The AfD has confirmed a hacker attack on email accounts. Tagesschau, Spiegel, Zeit Online and Süddeutsche Zeitung report this. Unknown parties say they have published data from the accounts; the taz cites a data set of 281 gigabytes that appeared on Monday on an anonymously run website, and the number mentioned is 421,000 emails. The operators claim the data comes from ten mailboxes, including one in the name of the party’s former leader Alexander Gauland. According to the reports, the party cites an attack on a hosting provider in September in which older mailboxes were copied; current accounts of the federal party organization are said not to be affected. According to dpa research, the website was registered only in mid-October. The Federal Office for Information Security (BSI) is in contact with the AfD; an independent check of authenticity was initially not available. The party is examining the publication.

World · Middle East✓ 5 outlets · Content of the talks not officially confirmed

US Defense Secretary Hegseth meets Netanyahu on unannounced visit to Israel, one week before the parliamentary election

US Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth met Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu on an unannounced visit to Israel. Handelsblatt and Zeit Online report this, citing media. According to Axios and Al Jazeera, the meeting took place on Monday, October 19, and Netanyahu’s spokesman confirmed it. The location was reportedly the Ovda air force base in the south of the country, in the Negev, where US tanker aircraft and fighter jets are stationed. Haaretz reports that Hegseth also met Defense Minister Israel Katz. Hegseth’s office declined to comment. The visit falls one week before the parliamentary election in Israel; a day earlier, Trump had expressed his support for Netanyahu in the election. According to Axios, talks are also under way about a possible resumption of larger-scale fighting against Iran. Reports of participation by representatives of the Emirates are regarded as unconfirmed.

Amazon announces investments of more than 16 billion euros for 2027 at the investment summit in Berlin

Amazon plans to invest more than 16 billion euros in Germany in 2027. The company announced this at the federal government’s “Invest in Germany Summit” in Berlin, as FAZ, Handelsblatt, ComputerBase and the Amazon press office report. In its headline, Handelsblatt places the sentence “I want your money” next to the pledge and writes that the federal government is courting investors. According to Amazon, the company has invested more than 100 billion euros in Germany since 2010; for 2025 it cites 15 billion euros.

4.8 billion euros alone are to go in 2027 into expanding the AWS region in Frankfurt for cloud and AI services. For the European Sovereign Cloud, a further 7.8 billion euros through 2040 are added, according to ComputerBase, which are not part of the 2027 sum. Amazon Leo is to deliver internet from low Earth orbit; Amazon has secured 24 rocket launches with Arianespace for it. Also planned are a second research lab for robotics and AI in Berlin, new logistics projects with more than 3,000 additional jobs and more than 15 new Prime Video productions.
