Russian attacks on Zaporizhzhia: at least 20 dead, Zelensky links them to the US diesel licenses

New since yesterday: after the US diesel deal with Russia, Ukraine reports heavy attacks with glide bombs on residential buildings. The death toll rose over the course of the day.

In Russian attacks in southeastern Ukraine on Saturday, October 10, at least twelve people were initially killed according to Südtirol News, and at least 20 according to a later dpa roundup on finanzen.net, including, according to Ukraine’s civil protection service, three minors. Rescue work was continuing. President Volodymyr Zelensky spoke of five glide bombs on residential buildings in Zaporizhzhia and of attacks on 13 regions in all.

Zelensky wrote on Telegram that, with this attack, Moscow had apparently said “thank you” for the easing of the diesel sanctions. That is his account and has not been independently verified. The attacks coincided with new consultations among representatives of the US, Ukraine and Europe on ending the war. The US license for Russian diesel withdraws the sanctions temporarily until April 7, 2027 (reports from Friday); reactions from the EU or Austria were not available to DailyDrop.

Trump attacks Norway and Peace Prize laureate Pillay: “indelible stain”

Donald Trump sharply criticized the award of the Nobel Peace Prize to Navi Pillay in an overnight post on Truth Social (Euronews, The Hill, Washington Post, Yahoo). He called the decision an “indelible stain” on Norway, repeated that he had ended “eight wars” and was close to resolving two more, and said, according to Euronews, that “nobody” had ever heard of Pillay. These are his own claims; the reports do not substantiate them independently.

According to the reports, the trigger was a joke by Pillay after the announcement: that she might perhaps share the prize with Trump. The Norwegian prime minister has repeatedly stated that the government has no influence on the decision of the independent Nobel Committee.

Portugal’s federation provisionally suspends Cristiano Ronaldo after he left the national team camp

The Portuguese football federation provisionally suspended Cristiano Ronaldo on Saturday, October 10, and opened disciplinary proceedings (SRF, Sky Sport Austria, Sportschau, ZDFheute, Euronews). The reason is that the captain left the national team’s camp on his own initiative. According to reports, there had been differences with coach Jorge Jesus beforehand: Ronaldo stayed on the bench in the match against Norway although, it is said, he had been promised otherwise, and clashed with Jesus before the match against Denmark.

Ronaldo apologized to the team and fans in a letter and said he would accept any ban. Federation president Pedro Proença said he expected him back as early as the international break in November; the decision, however, lies with the disciplinary committee, which works as an autonomous body. No penalty has been set.

Weekend in Austria: serious road accidents in Upper Austria and Styria

In Pollham in the Grieskirchen district, a car hit a tree head-on in the night to Saturday; two fire brigades were called out to rescue people (portal headtopics). In Bischoffeld in Styria, a seven-year-old was seriously injured on Saturday afternoon in a collision with a car and was flown by rescue helicopter to LKH Graz (Polizeiticker).

In Carinthia, a 34-year-old left the road on the Gurktalstraße (B 93) near Straßburg and hit a concrete pillar; he was injured, and a breath test showed slight intoxication. In Steyr, a 17-year-old hit a tree late in the evening. The details come from police reports on aggregator portals; the number of injured and the causes of the accidents are not conclusively confirmed.

Weekly wrap: ATX loses 2.59 percent, gold rises to $4,216.3 on Friday

New compared with Friday’s report is the weekly wrap: the ATX closed on Friday, October 9, at 6,543.45 points (−0.26 percent), putting it 2.59 percent down on the week but about 22.9 percent up since the start of the year (finanzen.at, finanzen.net, boerse-social). The course of the week: Monday +0.41 percent, Tuesday +0.68, Wednesday −2.65, Thursday −0.78, Friday −0.26. The Vienna Stock Exchange is closed at the weekend and reopens on Monday.

The ECB reference rate was $1.1206 per euro on Friday. On Wall Street, the indices closed higher on Friday: S&P 500 +0.59 percent at 7,811.54 points, WTI +0.39 percent at $91.85 and gold +1.43 percent at $4,216.3. The Nobel Prize in Economics will be announced on Monday.
