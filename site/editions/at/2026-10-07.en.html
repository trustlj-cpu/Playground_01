Babler addresses members in an Instagram video and calls the transition a “huge success”

New since the previous days: Babler’s first direct address to the party since the crisis meeting, a letter from Holzleitner and Kucher, and a new poll with the SPÖ at 14 percent.

SPÖ leader Andreas Babler addressed party members in an Instagram video on Tuesday, October 6, for the first time since Sunday’s meeting (ORF, SN, derStandard). He called the agreed “orderly transition” a “huge success” and said the party’s vision was bigger than any one person. As a minister, he wants to continue “at the request of” the designated vice chancellor Markus Marterbauer.

Eva-Maria Holzleitner and Philip Kucher, the acting dual leadership, wrote in a letter to the party, according to derStandard, that people had “talked about each other too often”; according to the reports, neither intends to run themselves at the extraordinary party congress in spring. Exxpress and oe24 mock the phrase “huge success” in light of new poll numbers, with the SPÖ at 14 percent. According to the reports, Marterbauer has declared his willingness to take the vice chancellorship.

2026 Nobel Prize in Physics goes to Francis Halzen for detecting cosmic neutrinos

The Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences has awarded the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics solely to the Belgian-American astrophysicist Francis Halzen of the University of Wisconsin–Madison, report taz, Spektrum, SRF and ZDFheute. He is honored for his decisive contributions to the IceCube neutrino observatory and to the discovery of high-energy neutrinos of astrophysical origin.

Halzen recognized that the ice at the South Pole could be used as a gigantic detector for these particles. Around 450 people from 14 countries work on IceCube. For the first time, a woman announced the decision: Ellen Moons, the Academy’s new Secretary General. Last year the prize went to John Clarke, Michel Devoret and John Martinis for quantum physics.

E-mopeds have been motor vehicles since October 1: bike paths off limits, driver’s license and license plate mandatory

Since Thursday, October 1, e-mopeds of class L1e-B no longer count as bicycles but as motor vehicles (ÖAMTC, Transport Ministry BMIMI, WKO, NÖN). They may no longer ride on bike paths, bike lanes and other cycling facilities but must use the roadway. Required are a driving permit (Lenkberechtigung), registration with a license plate, liability insurance, a helmet and the vehicle inspection (“Pickerl”) under § 57a.

On Tuesday, ORF noted on “Aktuell nach fünf” that e-bikes are increasingly faster on the road under the new rule. On details the sources contradict each other: WKO and klv.at give different answers on which license class suffices and which alcohol limit applies. DailyDrop therefore states no blood-alcohol limit; the Road Traffic Regulations (Straßenverkehrsordnung) and the Motor Vehicles Act (Kraftfahrgesetz) as well as the ministry’s guidance are binding. Vehicles with a low seat height under the EU regulation are exempt.

Poll: SPÖ falls to 14 percent, Babler at 5 percent in the chancellor question

A poll by the Lazarsfeld institute for oe24 puts the SPÖ at 14 percent, after 17 percent the previous week; oe24 calls it the lowest value in its series (oe24, Exxpress). In the chancellor question, Babler came last of five politicians with 5 percent, after 8 percent. FPÖ leader Herbert Kickl leads with 34 percent, Chancellor Christian Stocker (ÖVP) has 13 percent.

Lazarsfeld also tested Finance Minister Markus Marterbauer as lead candidate: with him the SPÖ would stand at 16 percent, two points above the figure with Babler. The reports give different sample sizes (1,000 and 2,000); a second poll, by the OGM institute for ServusTV, had previously put the SPÖ at 12 percent, behind the Greens (Börse-Express). Polls are snapshots with a margin of error, not election results.

Markets · Vienna Stock ExchangePress ✓ 4 outlets · prices per dpa-AFX and boerse-social

Vienna Stock Exchange: ATX rises 0.68 percent to 6,792.23 points, Wienerberger cuts its forecast

The ATX closed on Tuesday, October 6, at 6,792.23 points, up 0.68 percent; the broader ATX Prime gained 0.63 percent to 3,329.63 (finanzen.at, boerse-social, Ariva, finanznachrichten.de). The market was supported by positive cues from the US and Asia and a calmer bond market. At the top were CA Immobilien (+2.10 percent), Andritz (+2.05), Palfinger (+2.03), voestalpine (+1.55) and Erste Group (+1.22).

Andritz presented ambitious financial targets through 2029, Strabag reported a large order in the Czech Republic. Wienerberger cut its forecast because of weak construction activity and lost 3.77 percent; Lenzing gave up 5.15 percent, Porr 1.29, Mayr-Melnhof Karton 1.03 and Verbund 0.63 percent. The ATX’s year high stands at 7,043.27 points. The ECB reference rate rose to $1.1269 per euro from 1.1204. In the US, the S&P 500 closed up 0.58 percent at 7,818.93 points, WTI was almost unchanged at $89.44, and gold rose 0.73 percent to $4,187.1.
