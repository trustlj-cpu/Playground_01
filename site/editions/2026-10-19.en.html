Funeral today for Bucheon’s Fire Lieutenant Kim Jin-seok; party leader Jang Dong-hyeok answers Gov. Choo Mi-ae’s apology with “Resign”

The funeral for Kim Jin-seok, who died fighting the Bucheon factory fire on the 16th, is held on the 19th, and on the 18th the People Power Party demanded that Gyeonggi Governor Choo Mi-ae resign over her apology the day before.

Fire Lieutenant Kim Jin-seok, who was dispatched to a factory fire in Naedong, Ojeong District, Bucheon, Gyeonggi, around 8:38 a.m. on the 16th and died, was nearly at the end of a 24-hour shift and about 20 minutes from going off duty at 9 a.m., Financial News and Jaekyung Ilbo reported. He died in the early hours of the 17th. In front of his portrait at the memorial altar set up at the funeral hall of Soonchunhyang University Bucheon Hospital on the 18th lay a handwritten letter from his seven-year-old daughter, Kyunghyang Shinmun and Hankook Ilbo reported. The letter carried the child’s wish to meet her father again when she grows up, and Democratic Party lawmaker Song Young-gil, who visited the altar, posted a photo of the letter on social media, Hankook Ilbo and JoongAng Economy News reported. He has been posthumously awarded the Green Stripes Order of Service Merit and a one-rank special promotion. The funeral service will be held on the 19th as a Gyeonggi provincial funeral, and Gyeonggi Governor Choo Mi-ae will also attend, according to the National Fire Agency and Gyeonggi Province, Financial News reported. The family and the union are demanding that authorities explain how the decision to go inside was made when workers had already evacuated, and police have formed a dedicated team to investigate the cause of the fire and how the entry came about.

On the 18th, People Power Party leader Jang Dong-hyeok wrote on Facebook that Gov. Choo’s apology was one “forced out of her after five days” and demanded, “Since you want her to resign, resign,” Kyunghyang Shinmun, Newspim and Kukmin Ilbo reported. It was a direct retort to what Choo said in front of the memorial altar on the 17th: “Since you want an apology, I truly apologize with all my heart.” Newspim reported that Jang, citing that Choo moved into an official residence worth 1.2 billion won and bought a new official car, took aim at the governor herself as a “fiscal straw.” Jeong Jeom-sik, the People Power Party floor leader, also said, “An apology made because someone else told you to cannot be regarded as a true apology,” Gyeongbuk Domin Ilbo and Lawissue reported. At the Gyeonggi Province parliamentary audit held by the National Assembly Public Administration and Security Committee on the 13th, Choo had stirred controversy by saying the fire-service budget was like “the biggest, most troublesome fiscal straw.” As of reports on the 18th, there had been no further response from Choo’s side.

President Lee Jae-myung: “It’s a lie that people can’t speak because the meeting is public,” rebuts demand for closed-door Cabinet meetings again

President Lee Jae-myung posted on social media on the 18th, again rebutting the argument for narrowing the public scope of the Cabinet meeting, Kyunghyang Shinmun, Financial News and Seoul Shinmun reported. Watching the open Cabinet meeting, the president wrote, shows that not only Cabinet members but even heads of local governments say all they have to say, so the claim that they cannot speak because the meeting is public is a lie. He also explained that after the open session a separate closed meeting follows to handle agenda items that need security. Financial News reported that the president stressed that an aide who speaks bitter truths even when it is uncomfortable is the real aide. The post came three days after reports that some Cabinet members had asked to narrow the public scope of Cabinet meetings, and Cheonji Ilbo reported that on the 16th too the president said he could not agree with the argument for making Cabinet meetings fully closed. The government also rebutted the claim that Cabinet members cannot oppose the president’s views because of the live broadcast, saying it is not true.

KIA beat LG 6-3 on a 5-run first inning to go 2-0 up in Semi-Playoff… Oller 6 innings, 2 runs

KIA beat LG 6-3 in Game 2 of the 2026 KBO Semi-Playoff (best-of-five, first to three wins) at Gwangju-Kia Champions Field on the 18th, making it two straight wins after the 5-1 victory in Game 1, Seoul Shinmun, Cheonji Ilbo and MediaPen reported. KIA scored 5 runs in the bottom of the first on 6 hits off LG starter Lim Chan-kyu. Newdaily and Seoul Shinmun reported that the 6 hits in that inning tied the Semi-Playoff record for most hits in an inning, and the 5 runs tied the all-time postseason record for most runs in a bottom of the first. KIA starter Adam Oller was the winning pitcher, allowing 6 hits and 2 runs with 8 strikeouts over 6 innings. Sports Kyunghyang reported that Oller, who won as the starter on his birthday, said he got “the run support in the fall that I don’t usually get,” calling it the best birthday present. KIA need just one more win in the remaining three games to reach the Playoffs, where Samsung are waiting.

An Se-young rallies to beat Yamaguchi 2-1 for second straight Denmark Open title… 61-1 this year, 10th title of the season

An Se-young beat Japan’s Akane Yamaguchi 2-1 (21-23, 21-14, 21-18) in the women’s singles final of the Denmark Open in Odense, Denmark, on the 18th to win the tournament for a second straight year, Money Today, Newspim and Hankook Ilbo reported. It is the first time a Korean player has won the Denmark Open two years in a row. She lost the first game after conceding consecutive points from 21-21, then turned the tide in the second by winning 7 points in a row. Newspim reported that, even with an ankle injury, An came back from a 6-point deficit to win the last game. Hankook Ilbo presented this as her 10th title trophy this year. With this win her record this year stands at 61 wins and 1 loss in 62 matches, a 98.4% win rate. Money Today and Financial News counted this as her 10th title this year including the Asian Games gold medal, and reported that she extended her winning streak against Yamaguchi to 10.

Footballer Kim Young-gwon loses final appeal at Supreme Court in suit to cancel about 830 million won comprehensive income tax

Football player Kim Young-gwon (Ulsan HD), a former national team member, has lost for good in his suit asking to cancel an assessment of about 830 million won in comprehensive income tax, Money Today, Asia Economy and Dailian reported on the 18th. The Third Division of the Supreme Court upheld the lower-court ruling, which had not accepted Kim’s claim. Kim had sought to deduct about 605 million won in foreign taxes paid while playing in China, which exceeded the deduction limit and was carried over to the next year, by combining it with his income from his time in Japan, but the court found that the tax authority was right that the deduction must be made country by country. Ulsan Tax Office imposed about 830 million won in additional tax, including penalty tax, for the 2019–2021 attribution years. Kim claimed he was a non-resident of Korea, but the court judged him a Korean resident, citing that he kept his resident registration in Korea and sent most of his income to Korea, Jaekyung Ilbo reported. The first and second trials had also both dismissed Kim’s claim.
