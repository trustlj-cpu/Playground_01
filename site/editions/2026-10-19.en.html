Funeral service today for Bucheon Fire Lieutenant Kim Jin-seok, killed in the line of duty; People Power Party leader Jang Dong-hyeok tells Gov. Choo Mi-ae, after her apology, to “resign”

The funeral service for Fire Lieutenant Kim Jin-seok, who died on the 16th while fighting a factory fire in Bucheon, is held on the 19th as a Gyeonggi provincial funeral, and on the 18th the People Power Party demanded the resignation of Gyeonggi Governor Choo Mi-ae over her apology of the previous day.

Fire Lieutenant Kim Jin-seok, who died after being dispatched around 8:38 a.m. on the 16th to a factory fire in Naedong, Ojeong District, Bucheon, Gyeonggi, was nearly at the end of a 24-hour shift and about 20 minutes from his 9 a.m. clock-out time, Financial News and Jaekyung Ilbo reported. He died in the early hours of the 17th. In front of the portrait at the memorial altar set up at the funeral hall of Soonchunhyang University Bucheon Hospital on the 18th lay a handwritten letter from his seven-year-old daughter, Kyunghyang Shinmun and Hankook Ilbo reported. The letter carried her wish to meet her father again when she grows up, and Democratic Party lawmaker Song Young-gil, who visited the altar, posted a photo of the letter on social media, Hankook Ilbo and JoongAng Economy News reported. It was decided to confer the Green Stripes Order of Service Merit on the deceased posthumously and to give him a one-rank special promotion. The memorial service is held on the 19th as a Gyeonggi provincial funeral, and Gyeonggi Governor Choo Mi-ae will also attend, according to the National Fire Agency and Gyeonggi Province, Financial News reported. The bereaved family and the union are demanding that the circumstances of the decision to go inside, when workers had already evacuated, be made clear, and police have formed a dedicated team to investigate the cause of the fire and how the entry came about.

People Power Party leader Jang Dong-hyeok wrote on Facebook on the 18th that Choo’s apology was “an apology pushed out of her after five days” and demanded, “Since you are asking for a resignation, resign,” Kyunghyang Shinmun, Newspim and Kukmin Ilbo reported. It was a direct return of Choo’s words in front of the altar on the 17th, “Since you are asking for an apology, I apologize with all my heart.” Newspim reported that Jang took aim at Choo herself as a “fiscal straw,” citing that she moved into a 1.2-billion-won official residence and bought a new official car. People Power Party floor leader Jeong Jeom-sik also said, “An apology made because someone else told you to cannot be called a true apology,” Gyeongbuk Domin Ilbo and Lawissue reported. Choo had caused controversy at the National Assembly Public Administration and Security Committee’s parliamentary audit of Gyeonggi Province on the 13th by saying the fire budget is like “the biggest, headache-causing fiscal straw.” As of reports on the 18th, no further reaction from Choo’s side had emerged.

President Lee Jae-myung: “Saying people can’t speak because the Cabinet meeting is open is a lie,” rebuts renewed demand for closed doors

President Lee Jae-myung posted on social media on the 18th rebutting again the argument that the extent to which Cabinet meetings are open should be narrowed, Kyunghyang Shinmun, Financial News and Seoul Shinmun reported. The president wrote that when you watch an open Cabinet meeting, not only Cabinet members but even heads of local governments say everything they have to say, and that the claim that people cannot speak because the meeting is public is a lie. He also explained that after an open meeting a separate closed meeting follows to handle agenda items that require security. Financial News reported that the president stressed that an aide who speaks bitter truths even when it is uncomfortable is the real aide. The post came three days after reports emerged that some Cabinet members had proposed narrowing the public portion of Cabinet meetings, and Cheonji Ilbo reported that on the 16th as well the president said he could not agree with the claim that Cabinet meetings should be fully closed. The government also rebutted that the claim that Cabinet members cannot oppose the president’s views because of live broadcast is not true.

Sports · Fall BaseballPress ✓ 7 outlets · Game 3 starter not yet set

KIA beat LG 6-3 with 5 runs in the 1st for 2 straight Semi-Playoff wins… Oller 6 innings, 2 runs

KIA beat LG 6-3 in Game 2 of the 2026 KBO Semi-Playoff, a best-of-five series (first to 3 wins), on the 18th at Gwangju-Kia Champions Field, to make it 2 straight wins after the 5-1 victory in Game 1, Seoul Shinmun, Cheonji Ilbo and MediaPen reported. In the bottom of the 1st KIA hit 6 hits off LG starter Lim Chan-kyu and scored 5 runs. Newdaily and Seoul Shinmun reported that the 6 hits in that inning tie the Semi-Playoff record for most hits in an inning, and the 5 runs tie the postseason record for most runs in the bottom of the first. KIA starter Adam Oller was the winning pitcher, allowing 2 runs on 6 hits with 8 strikeouts over 6 innings. Sports Kyunghyang reported that Oller, who earned the win as starter on his birthday, said he had received in the fall the run support he usually does not get, calling it the best birthday present. With just 1 more win in the remaining 3 games, KIA moves on to the Playoffs, where Samsung awaits.

Sports · BadmintonPress ✓ 6 outlets · extent of ankle injury not confirmed

An Se-young beats Yamaguchi 2-1 in comeback to win 2 straight Denmark Open titles… 61-1 this year, 10th title of the season

An Se-young beat Japan’s Akane Yamaguchi 2-1 (21-23 21-14 21-18) in the women’s singles final of the Denmark Open in Odense, Denmark, on the 18th to win the tournament for the 2nd straight year, Money Today, Newspim and Hankook Ilbo reported. It is the first time a Korean player has won the Denmark Open 2 years in a row. She lost the first game by conceding consecutive points from 21-21, then turned the tide in the second game by winning 7 points in a row. Newspim reported that An came back from a 6-point deficit in the last game even while carrying an ankle injury. Hankook Ilbo presented the win as her 10th title trophy of the year. With this win her record this year is 61 wins and 1 loss in 62 matches, a 98.4% win rate. Money Today and Financial News counted it as her 10th title of the year, including the Asian Games gold medal, and reported that she extended her winning streak against Yamaguchi to 10.

Society · TaxPress ✓ 6 outlets · outlets give different dates for when the ruling became final

Footballer Kim Young-gwon loses final appeal at Supreme Court in suit to cancel roughly 830 million won comprehensive income tax

Football player Kim Young-gwon (Ulsan HD), a former national team member, lost his final appeal in a suit asking to cancel the imposition of about 830 million won in comprehensive income tax, Money Today, Asia Economy and Dailian reported on the 18th. The Supreme Court’s Third Division upheld the lower-court ruling that had not accepted Kim’s claim. Kim tried to combine about 605 million won in foreign taxes paid, which had exceeded the deduction limit while he played in China and been carried over to the following year, with his income from his time in Japan and deduct it, but the court found the tax authority’s judgment that the deduction must be split by country to be correct. The Ulsan Tax Office assessed an additional roughly 830 million won, including penalty tax, for 2019–2021. Kim argued he was a non-resident of Korea, but the court judged him a Korean resident, citing that he had kept his Korean resident registration and sent most of his income to Korea, Jaekyung Ilbo reported. The 1st and 2nd trials also both rejected Kim’s claim.
