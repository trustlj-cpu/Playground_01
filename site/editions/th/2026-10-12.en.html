Social Security Board election: turnout up more than 300%; Progressive Social Security Team leads the unofficial count on both sides

The Social Security Office is rushing to certify the results for fear of a board vacuum, while complaints have been raised over voting rights that were cut and the counting at some units

Matichon, Khaosod, Thai Post and Prachachat Turakij report that in the election of Social Security commissioners representing employers and insured persons yesterday, Oct. 11, the ballot boxes opened at 8:00 a.m. and closed at 4:00 p.m. nationwide. More than 670,000 people voted, up more than 300% from the previous election, and up more than 500% on the employer side (Prachachat Turakij). The Social Security Office (SSO) opened a dashboard on its website reporting unofficial results, and officials said that everyone still in line at closing time had to be allowed to vote before they could close the ballot boxes (Matichon).

When counting began, the Progressive Social Security Team took a steady lead, holding ranks 1–7 on both the employer side and the insured-person side (Khaosod, Thai Post). Matichon reports that unofficial scores passed one million and that the team won 14 board seats. Sattharam, the team’s leader, declared victory and said 14 votes were enough to reform social security (PPTV, Matichon). But he also contested the result at the Bang Phli 2 unit, where one candidate received 0 votes (Matichon).

Two complaints have been raised. Some insured persons had their voting rights cut even though they registered in advance and paid contributions every month, because their employers paid contributions late (Khaosod). And some spoiled ballots carried the number of a candidate whose name had been revoked; officials explained that these still count as votes, just not for the person who was revoked (Khaosod, Matichon). The SSO Secretary-General said the office would watch candidates linked to politicians and investigate if anything unlawful is found, and rushed to certify the results out of concern about a board vacuum. Insured persons should wait for the official announcement of results, not figures from the dashboard.

IMF–World Bank annual meetings open in Bangkok today through Oct. 18; Middle East war and high debt top the agenda

Reuters, Bangkok Post, Matichon, Khaosod and Thai Post report that the annual meetings of the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and the World Bank run Oct. 12–18 at the Sirikit Convention Center, with the plenary session on Oct. 16, according to the World Bank website. Reuters and Bangkok Post say the Middle East war and high debt levels will be the main issues. The World Bank president said he is preparing funds to help countries hit by the energy crisis and high prices, and is in talks with dozens of countries (Matichon, Bangkok Post).

Finance Minister Ekniti plans about 30 meetings with heads of organizations and is proposing 4 forums on the global economy, debt, energy and AI (Matichon). Thai media report that US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent is not attending. The Bangkok Metropolitan Administration announced 24 locations where gatherings are banned on Oct. 10–20, while police confirmed there will be no road closures but advised avoiding 5 routes (Matichon, Thai Post).

Disaster · FloodsPress ✓ 6 outlets · per official announcements

Warnings for 15 provinces and 29 districts over runoff floods and landslides Oct. 11–13; Chachoengsao flooded again by tidal surge

Khaosod and Matichon report that the Department of Mineral Resources warned 15 provinces and 29 districts to watch for mudslides and runoff floods on Oct. 11–13, and Suchat ordered the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment to stay on watch. The Thai Meteorological Department issued Announcement No. 10 warning of unsettled weather, heavy rain, strong gusts and possible lightning (Thai Post, Matichon). The Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation (DDPM) sent Cell Broadcast warnings to Kanchanaburi, Phetchaburi and Krabi.

In Chachoengsao, the Bang Pakong River overflowed into the municipal area because of a renewed tidal surge, while in Phetchaburi 8 hours of rain flooded several districts (Khaosod). Bangkok Post reports flooding in 21 provinces. The Defense Minister rushed paper toilets to Chachoengsao (Matichon). The DDPM and the Government Savings Bank also transferred relief payments on Sunday (Siam Rath), and the Ministry of Public Health said hospitals are gradually reopening more services (Matichon). People in risk areas should follow local announcements and prepare essentials.

Politics · ParliamentPress ✓ 5 outlets · debate date not set

Opposition seeks censure of 18 ministers including the PM; Bhumjaithai calls it “entertainocracy”; opposition dares ministers to stay and listen throughout

The Star and The Nation report that the opposition filed a no-confidence motion on Oct. 8 under Section 151 of the Constitution, signed by 145 MPs. The targets are Prime Minister Anutin and ministers, 18 in all, most of them from the Bhumjaithai Party. The allegations include corruption in the selection of senators, the local government exam, and flood management. The debate date is not confirmed; it is expected in late Oct., and the government side will arrange it to fit around international meetings.

The Bhumjaithai Party told its MPs to study the motion and keep their debate within bounds (Thairath). The government side jeered that it was a “script of entertainocracy.” Natthaphong Ruengpanyawut, the opposition leader, replied that if they are confident they should stay and listen throughout, and insisted that all 18 targets are on the mark (Khaosod, Matichon). Akkaradet criticized the request for a 5-day censure as “nothing but water,” saying 2–3 days would do (Matichon, Prachachat Turakij). The report that Anutin is ready to accept votes from the Kla Tham Party and pays no attention to rumors of a cabinet reshuffle came from a single outlet (SondhiX).

World · Russia–UkrainePress ✓ 5 outlets · Moscow has not responded

Trump announces an “energy ceasefire” between Russia and Ukraine, effective immediately; Zelensky calls it “news to me”; Moscow stays silent

Reuters, Thairath, WION and PM News Nigeria report that Trump announced on Truth Social on the evening of Oct. 11, Thai time, that Russia and Ukraine had agreed to an energy ceasefire, effective immediately. Earlier, Zelensky had said Ukraine would stop attacking refineries if Russia stopped attacking energy targets (Reuters), and he told WION that this was “news to me.” PM News Nigeria said Moscow has not replied, so details and confirmation from Russia are still lacking.

On the same day, Trump said Ukraine should have a new president and blamed Zelensky for expensive diesel in the US (Manager Online, Matichon). Zelensky told ABC he would hold elections if Russia agreed to a 60-day ceasefire (Reuters). Germany announced its support for Zelensky (Reuters). Bloomberg said the diesel deal with Russia deepened the rift with Ukraine. Thai readers should watch WTI crude, which closed Friday at $91.85 (+0.39%), to see how the market responds when the week opens.
