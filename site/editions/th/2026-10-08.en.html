Opposition to file censure motion against individual ministers today at 14:00, targeting more than 10 ministers linked to the Senate collusion case

Thai PBS and The Standard reported on Oct. 7 that it would be filed with the House Speaker under Section 151, while Post Today said it could be 12 people and stressed ministers from the Bhumjaithai quota

Thai PBS and The Standard reported on Oct. 7 that opposition parties are preparing to file a motion requesting a no-confidence debate against ministers individually, under Section 151 of the Constitution, with the Speaker of the House of Representatives at 14:00 today (Oct. 8). The targets are more than 10 ministers linked to the fraud case over the selection of senators, or “Senate collusion.”

Post Today said it could be 12 people from the Bhumjaithai Party, and that the debate would also cover the flood problem, viewing the vote as a gauge of the Pheu Thai Party, a coalition partner. By law, a motion of this kind must be co-signed by MPs numbering at least one-fifth of the House’s members.

The actual number of target ministers and the actual allegations will become clear once the motion is filed. The debate date depends on the House Speaker and on scheduling with the government. Reports before filing still use different figures (10 and 12), so DailyDrop has not confirmed the names. Readers should wait for the actual text of the motion and for statements from the ministers named.

Law · TaxConfirmed ✓ 4 outlets · tax figures differ by outlet

Thaksin testifies in Central Tax Court seeking interim protection in Shin Corp share-tax seizure case; court to rule Nov. 16

Siam Rath, Bangkok Biz News, Daily News and Thai PBS reported that on Oct. 7 the Central Tax Court held a hearing on a petition by Thaksin Shinawatra asking the court for interim protection so that the Revenue Department could not pursue seizure and asset freeze to collect tax on the sale of Shin Corp shares. Thaksin testified himself, and the court set Nov. 16, 2026 to hear its order.

The tax figures differ by outlet, from about 15 billion to 17.6 billion baht. Kaohoon reported that the defense lawyers cited an income base of 15,883.90 million baht for 2006 and double seizure. The court has not yet issued an order.

Fed minutes show most participants saw another rate hike by year-end as likely appropriate; US stocks fall, S&P 500 −0.22%

The Fed minutes for the September meeting, released on Oct. 7 at 14:00 U.S. time (equal to 01:00 today, Thai time), say that most participants judged another increase in the policy interest rate by the end of the year likely to be appropriate, without specifying timing, and stressed that they would stay open at the next meeting (CNBC, Reuters). One analyst noted that the market gives only about a 20% chance of a 0.25-point rate hike this month.

The US stock market closed lower: Dow Jones 51,179.87 (−0.66%), S&P 500 7,801.77 (−0.22%), Nasdaq 27,538.69 (−0.22%). WTI crude closed at $88.28 (−1.30%), Brent at $100.20 (−0.4%), and gold closed at $4,140.7 (−1.11%).

Politics · EconomyConfirmed ✓ 6 outlets · no investment deal announced

Anutin flies to Singapore for Forbes Global CEO Conference, pitching Thailand on investment, clean energy and the OECD goal

Prime Minister Anutin arrived in Singapore on Oct. 7 to speak to business leaders at the 24th Forbes Global CEO Conference, which has about 400 CEOs. Before he took the stage, Singapore Prime Minister Lawrence Wong hosted a welcome tea (The Standard, Thai Post), and he joined a one-on-one conversation with Steve Forbes at 20:30 at the Shangri-La Hotel (PPTV).

The content stressed attracting foreign investment, energy security and clean energy, and the goal of joining the OECD (Manager Online, Bangkok Biz News). He is scheduled to return to Thailand at 15:00 today. No concrete investment deals have been reported.

Business · PetrochemicalsConfirmed ✓ 4 outlets · deal is still a non-binding MoU

SCC jumps about 6% after management reaffirms olefins joint venture with PTTGC; shareholding structure to be announced Oct. 29

SCC rose as much as 6.80% to 267 baht in the afternoon on Oct. 7 (Infoquest) and closed at about 265 baht (+6%), according to Thai Enquirer, after management confirmed the olefins and polyolefins joint venture between SCGC and PTTGC, which will announce the shareholding structure and synergy benefits on Oct. 29 (Manager Online, Kaohoon).

The deal has a non-binding preliminary memorandum of understanding signed on Apr. 29, 2026. The joint venture would have 7 olefins plants with capacity of 7 million tonnes a year, and PP and PE plants with capacity of 6 million tonnes a year (Manager Online). It must pass approval by the companies and the competition regulator. Brokers set target prices of 276–280 baht.
