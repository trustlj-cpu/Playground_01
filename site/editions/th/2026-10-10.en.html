Trump says Russia will send more than 300,000 tonnes of diesel to the US and the world at once; US grants temporary waiver of Russian energy sanctions

Trump posted after a call with Putin that another 500,000 tonnes would follow in November and 1 million tonnes after that. Critics and Zelensky see a concession to Moscow, while Thailand raised fuel prices by 0.75 baht a liter from Oct. 9.

Jerusalem Post, CBS, The Hill, OPB and Al Jazeera report that President Donald Trump announced on Truth Social on Oct. 9, after a call with President Vladimir Putin, that Russia will send more than 300,000 tonnes of diesel to the US and world markets at once, another 500,000 tonnes in November and 1,000,000 tonnes after that. The US Treasury Department said it would grant a temporary waiver of sanctions on Russia’s energy sector so that the diesel can reach the market.

A Bloomberg columnist notes that the first 300,000 tonnes equal about 2.2 million barrels, which is small against global demand. Democrats such as Senator Blumenthal criticized the move, and Zelensky called the easing “weakness.” The volumes and timing are Trump’s announcement, not a delivery schedule confirmed by the Russian side.

For Thailand, diesel is the main fuel for transport and farming. PTT and Bangchak raised most gasoline, gasohol and diesel grades by 0.75 baht a liter from 5:00 a.m. on Oct. 9 (Thairath, Thansettakij, AFC10 News). There is no report that Thailand will benefit directly from Russian diesel. Prices will depend on the crude oil market when the new week opens.

World · Nobel PrizePress ✓ 6 outlets · Trump’s remarks are his own

2026 Nobel Peace Prize goes to Navi Pillay; Trump offers no congratulations and posts an attack on the committee

Al Jazeera, CNN, Euronews, The Hill, Forbes and NBC report that the Norwegian Nobel Committee announced on Oct. 9 that the 2026 Nobel Peace Prize goes to Navi Pillay, an 85-year-old South African jurist, a former judge of the International Criminal Tribunal for Rwanda and a former UN High Commissioner for Human Rights, for her work promoting peace and international law. It praised her “judicial impartiality, personal integrity and courage.”

Trump did not congratulate her. He reposted a message saying the committee had “irreparably disgraced itself,” and in a long post that night called it a “stain,” claiming he had ended 8 wars, which is his own claim (Forbes, Euronews). Israel’s prime minister and foreign ministry criticized the decision. Pillay replied jokingly that if the audience applauded more, she might share the prize with Trump.

Phuket opens Vegetarian Festival as more than 40 shrines raise the Go Teng pole, with locals and tourists joining in

Siam Rath, Manager Online, Khao Phuket and Thansettakij report that at about 5:00 p.m. on Oct. 9 the Go Teng pole was raised to open the 2026 Phuket Vegetarian Festival. Romadon Hajiawae, deputy governor of Phuket, presided over the invitation of the 9 deity lamps at the Jui Tui Tao Bo Keng Shrine in Talat Nuea subdistrict. More than 40 shrines held ceremonies, and many participants wore white.

The festival runs Oct. 10–18, nine days in all, with the shrines’ deity processions on Oct. 11–18 and the send-off ceremony on the night of Oct. 18. Some outlets give different venues for the pole-raising ceremony, so anyone planning to go should check the schedule of the shrine they want to see.

Economy · TravelPress ✓ 5 outlets · holiday details await official announcement

Sirikit Convention Center closed to the public Oct. 11–18; Bangkok government offices off Oct. 16 and working from home Oct. 12, 14, 15

Bangkok Post and Nation Thailand report that the Queen Sirikit National Convention Center will close all areas to the public from Oct. 11–18 and reopen as normal on Oct. 19 to host the IMF–World Bank Annual Meetings. More than 15,000 participants from 191 countries are expected. Security has been stepped up at the convention center and 21 official hotels, supported by about 500 tourist police.

Nation Thailand reports that civil servants and government agencies in Bangkok get a special day off on Oct. 16 and work from home on Oct. 12, 14, 15. Benchakitti Park stays open with restrictions on Oct. 5–18; parking and cycling in the park are banned (Bangkok Post, Thai PBS World). Travelers should avoid Ratchadaphisek, Sukhumvit, Rama IV and Asok Montri roads while checkpoints are in place.

Wall Street ends the week at a record: S&P 500 at 7,811.54 (+0.59%), gold jumps 1.43%, while SET gains about 0.38% on the week

US stocks closed higher on Friday, Oct. 9. The S&P 500 rose 46 points to 7,811.54 (+0.59%), the Dow Jones gained 423 points (+0.8%) and the Nasdaq rose 0.6%, ending a record-setting week (AP, DTN). WTI crude closed at $91.85 (+0.39%). Gold closed at $4,216.30, up $59.30 (+1.43%); the reports give no specific cause.

In Thailand, the SET closed at 1,577.65 (+0.45%), up about 0.38% on the week, and the baht closed at 33.53. The market puts the odds of a Fed rate hike in December at about 81% according to FedWatch (Kaohoon). Kasikornbank expects the baht at 33.30–33.80 next week.
