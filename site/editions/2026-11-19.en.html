Choo Kyung-ho, accused of obstructing vote to lift martial law, acquitted in first trial

Ruling on the 18th… the court was reported to have found that former President Yoon’s request was not sufficiently proven

Daegu Mayor Choo Kyung-ho was found not guilty in a first-instance trial on the 18th. When emergency martial law was declared on December 3, 2024, Mayor Choo was the People Power Party’s floor leader, and was indicted on a charge of obstructing the People Power Party lawmakers’ vote to lift martial law. The special counsel was reported to have made a sentencing request of 20 years. The court was reported to have found that former President Yoon Suk Yeol’s request to Mayor Choo to block the vote was not sufficiently proven, but details of the reasoning have yet to be confirmed. In a criminal trial, an acquittal is a finding that the evidence to prove guilt is insufficient, and it may not mean that nothing of the kind happened at all.

Reactions in political circles split sharply right after the ruling. The People Power Party issued a position that “the insurrection hunt is over now” and said it would consider filing a complaint against the investigating special counsel for the crime of distorting the law. Han Dong-hoon, a former People Power Party leader, was reported to have said the result was the natural consequence of the special counsel’s excessive investigation and indictment. Democratic Party leader Jung Chung-rae was reported to have argued that Chief Justice Cho Hee-de should be impeached. Whether the special counsel will appeal is not yet known; the deadline to appeal is seven days from the day after the ruling.

Diplomacy · Korea-China✓ 4 outlets · agreement documents and whether the hallyu ban will be lifted unconfirmed

President Lee holds summit with Xi Jinping in Shenzhen… cooperation to expand after conclusion of FTA services and investment talks

President Lee Jae-myung held a summit with President Xi Jinping on the 18th, on the occasion of attending the APEC summit in Shenzhen, China. It was the two leaders’ third meeting, and the talks were reported to have lasted about 34 minutes. Cheong Wa Dae said President Xi expressed support for various efforts to ease tensions on the Korean Peninsula, including U.S.-North Korea dialogue, and also explained developments in North Korea-China relations to President Lee. President Lee was reported to have asked China to play a constructive role in resolving Korean Peninsula issues and to have said the two sides should work together toward a fundamental solution to the Yellow Sea issue. President Xi was reported to have said China and South Korea should uphold strategic autonomy and contribute to regional peace and development. The economic backdrop is that the follow-up Korea-China FTA services and investment negotiations were substantially concluded on the 18th. The two leaders agreed to broaden cooperation on the back of this conclusion. Reports said the terms include most-favored-nation treatment in China’s content market and more co-production, and attention is on whether the hallyu ban in film and tourism will be lifted. The hallyu ban refers to informal measures by which China has restricted the distribution of Korean cultural content. No official announcement on whether or when it will be lifted has been confirmed, and details of the agreement text and the signing schedule are also not yet known.

Society · Education✓ 6 outlets · whether the reward and exam-eligibility restriction schemes are finalized unconfirmed

One day before the CSAT… president: “If disaster alert texts disrupt the exam, extending the time will be considered”

The 2027 academic year CSAT is held on the 19th. A preliminary gathering for examinees was held on the 18th, and forecasts said rain would fall nationwide from the afternoon amid chilly weather. The government is additionally considering sending a disaster alert text when cheating is caught during the exam, and sending it without warning is also being floated. President Lee Jae-myung was reported to have said that extending exam time by the same amount would be considered if a disaster alert text disrupts the exam.

The president was reported to have ordered a full survey of cases in which CSAT question papers were uploaded to artificial intelligence (AI) and a stern crackdown on cheating. Reports also said a 100 million won reward, a 10-year restriction on taking the exam and tracking of AI usage records were being pursued in connection with cheating, but it is not confirmed whether these are finalized schemes. People Power Party lawmaker Na Kyung-won was reported to have criticized the reward proposal as telling examinees to watch one another. Ahead of the CSAT, a magnitude 3.6 earthquake struck Sangju, North Gyeongsang, and voices called for postponement, but the exam is reported to be going ahead as scheduled.

Society · Elections✓ 4 outlets · the specific election, the procedure and the parties’ positions unconfirmed

Election commission special counsel seeks arrest warrant for 8 staff… charged with falsely entering turnout figures and forging or altering official documents

The National Election Commission special counsel requested an arrest warrant on the 18th for eight National Election Commission staff. The charges were reported as falsely entering turnout figures and forging or altering official documents, and the expression forging or altering voting records was also used. The warrant request came five months after the local elections. An arrest warrant is a procedure in which an investigative agency asks a court after judging it necessary to investigate a suspect in custody, and a request is different from issuance. Whether it is issued is decided by the court after a hearing.

Which election and which procedure this happened in, and what rank and role each of the eight held, has not been confirmed. The parties’ positions and the court’s hearing schedule are also not known. The charges applied by the special counsel are not established fact until the court rules.

Security · North Korea✓ 1 outlet · whether equipment was brought in or is running, and the scale of enrichment unconfirmed

IAEA chief reportedly says external construction of expansion of North Korea’s Yongbyon uranium enrichment facility is complete

The head of the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) was reported to have said on the 18th that additional expansion work on North Korea’s Yongbyon uranium enrichment facility reached ‘externally complete’ status last month. Uranium enrichment is a process that raises the proportion of fissile material in uranium, a technology that can be used both for power-generation fuel and for nuclear weapons material. ‘Externally complete’ is an expression meaning construction on the outside of the building has ended.

Whether equipment has been installed inside, whether operations have already begun, and how much the enrichment scale has grown cannot be confirmed from this remark alone. North Korea’s position is also not known.
