Bucheon paint plant blaze: firefighter in cardiac arrest, another critically hurt… the fire came, but no safety inspection had been done

A firefighter collapsed while fighting a fire at a paint plant in Bucheon, Gyeonggi, on the morning of the 16th. There were 100 containers of flammable materials on site, but a report said there was no record of a fire safety inspection.

A large fire broke out around 8:38 a.m. on the 16th at a metal-parts paint plant in Naedong, Ojeong District, Bucheon, Gyeonggi Province. Fire authorities soon issued a Level 2 response. Yonhap reported at 10:39 a.m. that 3 firefighters were injured and 1 was in cardiac arrest, and at 11:14 a.m. it ran a bulletin saying the injured firefighters had risen to 5. A later roundup put it at 1 firefighter in cardiac arrest and 1 critically injured, with 7 injured in all. In the afternoon Kyunghyang Shinmun reported that 8 people, including firefighters and police, were seriously or slightly hurt. The one-person gap between the two outlets appears to come from the count changing over time or from a different scope of inclusion, and which is the final figure has not yet been confirmed.

The issue is not what happened after the fire but what came before it. Kyunghyang Shinmun reported that although 100 containers of flammable materials were on site, no fire safety inspection had been carried out at this plant. Because a paint plant uses organic solvents, once a fire starts, smoke and heat spread quickly and the smell of burning drifts all the way to the access road. A Kyunghyang Shinmun reporter described a pungent smell of burning from the plant's access road onward. However, whether the plant was not subject to inspection or was subject but skipped has so far appeared in only one outlet's report and has not been confirmed elsewhere. The cause of the fire and whether the flammable materials were stored as required can be known only after an investigation and a joint inspection.

Politicians reacted at once. At the opening ceremony of the 107th National Sports Festival at Jeju World Cup Stadium the same day, President Lee Jae-myung said in his commemorative address that it is a regrettable reality that treatment and compensation for firefighters are not sufficient, and that he hopes the firefighter hovering between life and death will recover soon. Gyeonggi Governor Choo Mi-ae canceled her scheduled events, visited the scene and said she would give maximum support for treatment and recovery. Kyunghyang Shinmun also reported a view that her visit was made with the so-called ‘fire service straw’ controversy in mind, but that is the outlet's own interpretation.

There are three things to watch: the condition of the firefighter taken away in cardiac arrest, the cause of the fire to be established by the joint inspection, and how to close the gap in safety inspections at workplaces that handle flammable materials. Because the treatment of firefighters is raised after every individual accident, the key is whether this time too it leads to a discussion of the compensation system.

September employment up 113,000… manufacturing gains for the first time in 2 years 3 months, youth on a 29-month slide

In the September employment report released on the 16th, the number of employed people rose by about 113,000 from a year earlier. A Yonhap-affiliated bulletin put the gain at 110,000, and a follow-up article at 113,000. What stands out is that manufacturing employment turned up for the first time in 2 years 3 months. One reading is that the recovery in semiconductors and export industries has begun to carry over into jobs, but that reading is not confirmed in the articles; it is the writer's inference. The shadow is also clear. The youth employment rate kept falling for a 29th straight month. It means that even as total jobs grow, the share going to young people is shrinking. Whether jobs for older people are propping up the rise could not be confirmed in this round of reporting. Breakdowns by industry and age need to be checked again once the raw data from Statistics Korea come out.

Incheon knife attack suspect identified… Choi Chung-rak, 59; 8 injured

On the 16th police released the personal details of a suspect who injured 8 people by wielding a knife in Incheon. His name is Choi Chung-rak and he is 59 years old. This was the most widely reported case, covered by 8 outlets in this edition's tally. Yonhap's roundup and KBS News carried the same content.

A decision on identity disclosure is made by a committee that weighs the brutality of the crime, the extent of the harm, whether evidence has been secured and other factors. However, the motive and the victims' condition were not confirmed in this reporting. Why the suspect did it can be known only when the investigation is over.

Markets · StocksPress ✓ Yonhap, SBS, Korea Economic Daily · the market-value wipe-out figure is a single report

Kosdaq surges 4.9%, triggering buy-side sidecar… AI and chip stocks rebound after $1 trillion in market value vanished

Yonhap, SBS and Korea Economic Daily reported in simultaneous bulletins that around 1 p.m. on the 16th the Kosdaq jumped 4.9% intraday and a buy-side sidecar was triggered. A sidecar is a device that halts program trading for 5 minutes when futures prices swing sharply. Earlier there had been a report that about $1 trillion in market capitalization of AI and semiconductor stocks had vanished in four days and that the shock from China could concentrate on Korea, but that is one outlet's tally, so the figure is for reference only.

On the exchange rate, Korea Economic Daily put the won-dollar rate at 1,341.5 won, down 3.1 won, while Chosun Ilbo wrote that it had come down to the low 1,330s. The two numbers are from different times, so we do not take either as the close. On the U.S. side, Korea Economic Daily reported that PPI also came in higher than expected after CPI, yet Treasury yields actually fell.

Politics · LawPress ✓ Yonhap, Korea Economic Daily, Kyunghyang Shinmun · poll figures from Gallup alone

39% approval, pressure for constitutional revision, even a sentencing request for ex-President Yoon at the appeal trial… politics and the courts on the 16th

In a Gallup Korea poll, President Lee Jae-myung's job approval was 39%, with the downward trend halting for now. In the general election, the view that the government should be checked was 47% and the view that it should be supported 34%. These figures are from Gallup alone. The president said the ruling and opposition parties talk about enshrining the Bu-Ma Democratic Protests and 5.18 in the preamble of the Constitution only when the occasion arises and never act on it, urged the National Assembly to discuss constitutional revision, and said he could not agree to holding all Cabinet meetings in private.

On the judicial side, the special counsel again made a sentencing request of 30 years in prison for former President Yoon Suk Yeol at the second trial in the Pyongyang drone operation case. The fact-finding team of the Ministry of Justice's Prosecution Human Rights Respect Future Committee said it could not confirm manipulation in the Wirye, West Sea and statistics cases, and attention is on whether the committee will reach a conclusion at its meeting on the 29th. In the Hyundai Outlet fire that killed 7 people, the first sentence came after 4 years, and the manager of a fire-safety company received 3 years in prison.
