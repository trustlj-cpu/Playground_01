‘Perfect LEET scorer’ Lee Jin-woo, facing AI cheating allegations, found dead… scheduled public verification never took place

Lee Jin-woo (32), who scored perfectly on the Legal Education Eligibility Test, was found dead. The allegations were never confirmed as fact, and the investigation is left with the task of securing evidence.

Lee Jin-woo (32), a perfect scorer on the Legal Education Eligibility Test (LEET), was found dead. This year, after the top passer of the tax accountant exam confessed to cheating with AI, Lee came under the same kind of suspicion and said the allegations would be cleared through a public verification livestreaming the solving of the CSAT Korean section. But Lee did not show up at the appointed time, and the cram school in charge of the verification announced that it had fallen through and asked police to check on Lee’s whereabouts. On the 18th a criminal complaint was filed, and there was also a move to pursue a class action. Lee had denied the allegations. There are reports that the National Forensic Service conducted an autopsy and said there were no signs of foul play, but the circumstances of the death have not yet been officially confirmed.

Whether the allegations were true was never settled. On the 20th the lawyer for the complainant urged the investigation to find whatever traces remain by any means, and in legal circles it is said that securing evidence to prove the use of AI devices or outside help is the key. Meanwhile, the Human Resources Development Service of Korea filed a complaint against the top passer of the tax accountant exam on charges of obstruction of business and began a full inspection to see whether anything irregular occurred in other national qualification exams. How the investigation into Lee Jin-woo will proceed, and whether the autopsy findings will be officially released, are not yet known.

President Lee approves Kim Jong-cheol as National Police Agency chief… acting arrangement ends after 2 years

President Lee signed off on the appointment of Kim Jong-cheol as National Police Agency chief on the 20th. It has been about 2 years since the post began to be run under an acting arrangement. Kim received an appointment recommendation from the Ministry of the Interior and Safety on the 6th of this month, and the National Police Commission agreed to the recommendation the same day. The acting arrangement continued after the previous chief’s duties were suspended in December 2024. With this appointment, the current government’s first permanent police chief takes the post, and organizational stability and police reform are cited as tasks facing the new chief. The date of the inauguration ceremony and the first senior personnel appointments are not yet known.

CJ ENM files for provisional injunction against LG HelloVision to bar halt to channel carriage

On the 20th, CJ ENM asked a court for a provisional injunction to stop LG HelloVision from cutting off carriage of its channels. A provisional injunction is a procedure that asks for rights to be protected temporarily before a ruling on the merits. LG HelloVision has given notice that, with talks broken down, it will end carriage of four channels, tvN SPORTS, tvN SHOW, Tooniverse and Junghwa TV, from the 26th. The two sides have clashed over how to calculate content usage fees, and LG HelloVision is reported to have paid reduced fees for September and October. The main lawsuit over the fees has its next hearing set for December 11. When the court will rule on the injunction is not known.

POSCO labor and management reach tentative wage deal 13 days after first agreement voted down… basic wage up 2.6%

POSCO labor and management tentatively agreed on 2026 wage talks on the 20th. The core is a 2.6% increase in basic wage. The first tentative agreement, produced on October 30, was voted down in the union members’ ballot on the 7th of this month with 51.69% against, and this deal comes 13 days after that. In addition to the 2.6% basic wage increase, the first agreement contained incentive payments totaling 6 million won, among other items.

The union was reported to have originally demanded a 7.1% increase in base pay while the company offered 2.5%. As the talks dragged on, there was reportedly a partial strike in September for the first time since the company’s founding. The incentive payments and other remaining terms of this agreement, and the schedule for the members’ vote, have not been confirmed. A tentative agreement must pass a members’ vote to lead to a final settlement.
