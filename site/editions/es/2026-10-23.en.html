PSOE accepts the Sánchez-Feijóo face-to-face debate on Nov. 16, but the PP rejects the four-way debate on the 26th on RTVE

The PSOE takes the televised duel on a private channel as agreed and demands that the debate with the leaders of Vox and Frente Amplio be broadcast on TVE.

The PSOE announced on Thursday, October 22, that it accepts a face-to-face debate between its candidate, Pedro Sánchez, and PP leader Alberto Núñez Feijóo, on Monday, November 16, on a private channel, Europa Press and El País reported. La Vanguardia places that encounter on Mediaset. The Socialists also accept RTVE’s invitation to a four-way debate on Thursday, November 26, with the leaders of Vox and Frente Amplio, according to Europa Press, and ask that it be broadcast on TVE and not on Atresmedia, as the Popular Party proposes, according to El País. El Mundo reports the PSOE’s warning that “it is essential that both encounters take place.” Sánchez is the Socialist candidate for the Presidency of the Government and Feijóo is the leader of the opposition, according to Europa Press. The general election is on November 29.

The PP rejects holding the four-way debate on RTVE, according to La Vanguardia’s headline, which adds that the Socialists reject the PP’s veto of public television. El Confidencial recalls that in the July 2023 campaign Sánchez attended both of the events proposed to him: the face-to-face organized by Antena 3 and the four-way debate held on RTVE. With the current positions, the November 16 face-to-face has the PSOE’s yes on a private channel and the November 26 four-way debate still has no agreement on which channel will broadcast it: the Socialists want TVE, according to El País, and the PP proposes Atresmedia, which organized the 2023 face-to-face on Antena 3. The calendar stands as follows: face-to-face on Monday, November 16, four-way debate on Thursday, November 26, three days before the November 29 election. The four-way debate would bring together, according to Europa Press, the leaders of the PSOE, PP, Vox and Frente Amplio. The final format of the face-to-face is not confirmed, and the PSOE insists, according to El Mundo, that both encounters be held.

Feijóo picks Cayetana Álvarez de Toledo as number two and Borja Sémper as number three on the PP list for Madrid

PP leader and candidate for the Presidency of the Government Alberto Núñez Feijóo has chosen deputy Cayetana Álvarez de Toledo as his number two on the Madrid list for the Congress of Deputies in the general election of November 29, the party confirmed on Thursday, October 22, Europa Press reported. The PP’s national spokesperson, Borja Sémper, will be number three, the same slot he held in 2023, according to El Confidencial, which adds that Génova confirms “what had been an open secret for days.” Sémper said, according to El Mundo: “There is room in the PP for all sensibilities.” El Confidencial maintains that the appointment brings the party’s two sensibilities onto the same list. 20minutos recalls that the deadline to submit lists ends on Monday, October 26, and that the PP is in the final stretch of preparing them. The candidacies must be submitted before that date.

Politics · Campaign Nov. 29✓ 4 outlets · the electoral effect of the visit has not been measured

Ayuso defends her meeting with Milei and the PP seeks to keep the visit from weighing on the campaign after the Argentine president’s insults to Sánchez

Argentine President Javier Milei gave a speech in Madrid on Wednesday, October 21, in which, according to ABC, he said he came from the future that awaits Spain “if it continues to embrace the ideas of the Socialists.” 20minutos reports that in that speech he insulted Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez, and El País calls the remarks unacceptable in an editorial. La Vanguardia reports that Milei joked by video call with Vox leader Santiago Abascal, and 20minutos quotes his line: “There is nothing more fun than kicking leftist asses.” The president of the Community of Madrid, Isabel Díaz Ayuso, received him for almost two hours at the headquarters of the Canal de Isabel II, where they exchanged invitations for further visits, 20minutos reported.

On Thursday, October 22, Ayuso defended the meeting: “He came out of the ballot boxes, not out of a herriko taberna like Sánchez’s partners,” La Vanguardia reported. El País reports that Feijóo is dodging the Argentine president and that people in the PP trust the impact of the visit will fade by November 29: “If he were to come in the last week it would be different.” According to La Vanguardia, Borja Sémper argued that the Government cannot “lecture” anyone when it has ministers “who call journalists rats.” Sánchez answered with a wink at “Fanático,” the song by Lali Espósito, who has been at odds with Milei since 2023, according to 20minutos and El País.

Markets · Stock market✓ 5 outlets · it is not known whether this is the start of a longer correction

Ibex plunges 2.12% and loses the 19,000 points because of Bankinter, French debt and fear of the AI bubble

The Ibex 35 closed on Thursday, October 22, down 2.12%, at 18,913.50 points, thus losing the 19,000 level, because of the drop in Bankinter, which fell 7.56% after publishing its quarterly results, and because the French risk premium jumped above 170 basis points, ABC reported. Bankinter earned 900 million euros through September, 11% more, according to the same newspaper. La Vanguardia attributes the decline to the rise in oil and to doubts about technology, and notes that Meta plunged more than 7%.

El Mundo speaks of a black day on world stock markets because of oil and fear of a bubble in artificial intelligence, with losses on Wall Street and in Asia. On Wall Street, the S&P 500 fell 1.21%, to 7,663.43 points, according to Alto Nivel. Infobae reports that the Nasdaq lost 2.58% over doubts about the cost of the expansion in artificial intelligence, that Nvidia fell 4.4% and that Brent rose to about 113 dollars a barrel, its highest level in five months.

Politics · Amnesty✓ 4 outlets · date of his definitive return to Catalonia to be confirmed

Toni Comín collects his MEP credentials in Madrid after promising the Constitution “by legal imperative,” nine years after leaving

Toni Comín, a former regional minister of Catalonia, collected on Thursday, October 22, at the Central Electoral Board his credentials as a Junts MEP after promising the Spanish Constitution “by legal imperative and out of loyalty to the people of Catalonia and to the mandate of October 1” 2017, Europa Press reported. El Mundo quotes his line: “For nine years the State has used all its penal, political and espionage resources to prevent what is happening today: that I return in complete freedom and without having spent a second in a Spanish prison.” El País cites his assessment of the procedure: “This right to grumble confirms our victory.”

La Vanguardia reports that Comín crossed the border by car to renew his ID card and collect the credentials, before returning to Northern Catalonia and crossing back in on foot over the weekend. The former minister says he is postponing the end of his exile until Sunday, according to the same newspaper. El Mundo and El País explain that the amnesty lets him return without being arrested after leaving Spain nine years ago.
