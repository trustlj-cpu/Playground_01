Cut-off low Adam keeps the red warning on the Valencia and Alicante coast and leaves a woman dead in a stream in Cabrera de Mar

The Aemet warning runs until Monday morning in southern Valencia and northern Alicante, with classes suspended, while in Catalonia the woman swept away by a stream is found dead.

The State Meteorological Agency (Aemet) activated the red warning for rain on Sunday, October 18, on the southern Valencia coast and the northern Alicante coast because of cut-off low Adam, according to Infobae and El Debate. Both outlets put the start at 10:00 on Sunday and the end on the morning of Monday the 19th: El Debate sets it at 10:00 and Valencia Extra at 9:00. Telecinco reported that the red alert remains in place on Monday. The forecasts the outlets cite range from 200 liters per square meter in 12 hours, cited by Estrella Digital, to at least 300 liters between Sunday and Monday, cited by El Debate. During the day, OKDiario recorded more than 200 liters per square meter in the Gandía area, and El Debate counted 230 liters at the wettest point, with the figure still rising. El Debate and El Periódic report that several municipalities in Valencia and Alicante suspended classes for Monday, October 19. Estrella Digital notes that the red warning is the highest level. Moncloa and Metrópoli Abierta say that Barcelona activated its emergency plan on Saturday because of the rain alert and restricted mobility.

In Catalonia, Saturday’s storm left one fatality. A 35-year-old woman, according to Metrópoli Abierta, disappeared after being swept away by the stream at Cabrera de Mar, in the Maresme, and emergency teams searched for her through Saturday night and Sunday morning, according to Infobae and El País. Her body was found in the port of Premià de Mar, according to eldiario.es, El Nacional, Infobae and Diari de Tarragona. Infobae links the incident to cut-off low Adam. On the railways, Rodalies cut the R2 Sud and Regionals Sud service from 22:00 on Saturday because of the rain in the Baix Llobregat and the Garraf, and restored it on Sunday, according to Última Hora, Teleprensa and Catalunya Press. None of those outlets yet offers a full tally of damage or of the total accumulated rainfall.

World · France✓ 8 outlets · the figures for arrests and injuries are provisional and differ between sources

France counts between 1,800 and 2,000 arrests after Saturday’s protests and Macron promises those responsible will be held to account

Saturday’s protests, on October 17, in France left close to 2,000 people arrested. Interior Minister Laurent Nuñez spoke of more than 2,000 arrests across the country, according to Milenio and eldiario.es, while Infobae and Telemundo cite a government tally of 1,800. Infobae and El Comercio add that more than 500 officers were injured. Milenio puts turnout between the 550,000 people counted by the authorities and the 800,000 counted by the CGT. Público and Milenio report one person dead in Paris, who suffered a cardiorespiratory arrest. Telemundo describes the day as one of the most violent in decades, with fires and looting. Emmanuel Macron promised that those who “tried to overthrow the Republic” will be held to account, according to CNN en Español. El País reports that the French president accuses the “far left” of inflaming the street, although he distances the students from the violence and vandalism. Milenio agrees that Macron and the prime minister attributed the riots to the far left.

Politics · General election✓ 7 outlets · the exact number of constituencies differs between outlets

PSOE and Frente Amplio present a joint Senate ticket for Nov. 29 and Vox is considering not running in some provinces to support the PP

The PSOE and Frente Amplio registered a joint Senate ticket for the general election of November 29, according to Infobae, Deia, ElNacional.cat, CMMedia and Público. The deadline to notify coalitions ended on Friday, October 16. Infobae and ElNacional.cat explain that the aim is to prevent the PP from repeating an absolute majority in the upper house. The outlets consulted speak of a single ballot in 47 constituencies, although one wire report puts the alliance at 40 provinces. Público adds that the ticket also brings together territorial left-wing groups.

El País reports that Vox is considering not fielding candidates for the upper house in some provinces and backing the PP to stop the left-wing coalition, and it headlines that a left-wing front and a right-wing front will compete for control of the Senate. EsDiario writes that the PP and Vox are preparing a response to the left’s agreement. According to EsDiario, there is so far no public confirmation of those contacts from either of the two parties.

Sport · LaLiga✓ 10 outlets · the minutes of several goals differ between match reports

Barcelona thrash Betis 5-2 at La Cartuja, with a golazo from Lamine Yamal, and equal Real Madrid’s nine straight wins of 1968-69

Barcelona beat Betis 5-2 at La Cartuja on Saturday, October 17, in the ninth round of LaLiga, according to La Jornada, Los Primeros TV and Mediotiempo. Lamine Yamal opened the scoring in the 10th minute with a golazo, according to La Jornada and Excelsior. With this win, the Blaugrana have nine wins in nine rounds and equal the record Real Madrid had held since the 1968-69 season, according to Meridiano, El Diario de Chihuahua and OKDiario. OKDiario adds that Barcelona threaten to break it in the Clásico.

The VAR ruled out a goal by Giovani Lo Celso for Betis because of a prior handball by Cucho Hernández in the move, according to Ahora and Emol. Betis coach Manuel Pellegrini was shown a yellow card after berating the referee, according to Emol and La Nación. Several French-language match reports place the game three days before Barcelona’s Champions League tie with PSG.

World · War in Ukraine✓ 5 outlets · the tallies come from Russian authorities and are not independently verified

Ukraine launches one of its largest drone attacks on St. Petersburg and hits an oil terminal and a logistics center, with one dead and five injured

Ukraine launched one of its largest drone attacks on St. Petersburg on October 18, according to Infobae. The city’s governor, Alexander Beglov, confirmed one person dead and five injured, according to Infobae and La Patria. The attack hit the city’s main oil terminal, according to Libertad Digital, and a logistics center, according to Infobae and La Patria. Infobae speaks of two oil terminals. Moncloa and El Mundo report that Russia admitted shooting down 144 drones, a figure that El Mundo attributes to the regional governor, Alexander Drozdenko. La Patria describes the blow as an attack on the city’s oil and logistics facilities, and Libertad Digital speaks of damage to the main oil terminal. The casualty and shoot-down figures come from Russian authorities.
