SK Hynix Q3 operating profit 83.3338 trillion won… announces 60 trillion won in additional shareholder returns

Operating margin of 80%, and DRAM and HBM volumes through 2029 effectively sold out, the company said, Yonhap reported

Yonhap and Kyunghyang Shinmun reported that SK Hynix announced its Q3 results on the 28th, saying operating profit was 83.3338 trillion won, up 632% from a year earlier. Yonhap reported that revenue was 104.6 trillion won, topping 100 trillion won in a quarter for the first time, and Chosun Ilbo wrote that it was the sixth straight quarter of record results. One Chosun Ilbo flash gave operating profit as 83.3334 trillion won, so the last digits differ by article. Korea Economic Daily reported that the operating margin reaches 80%. Kyunghyang Shinmun and Dong-A Ilbo reported that the company announced 60 trillion won in additional shareholder returns the same day. According to Yonhap, it will buy back 20 trillion won of treasury shares, cancel 10 trillion won of them and use 10 trillion won for employee compensation, and will also pay a quarterly dividend of 20,000 won per common share, 14 trillion won in total. Korea Economic Daily reported that, taken together, this makes 100 trillion won in shareholder returns this year.

Yonhap and Korea Economic Daily reported that on a conference call that day the company said DRAM and HBM volumes for the next three years, through 2029, were effectively all sold out. Yonhap reported that this is a judgment combining volumes already secured under long-term supply contracts with demand under discussion with customers, and that half of production capacity for the next two years is already under contract. According to another Yonhap article, the company said that in gauging demand it also looks at customers’ financial soundness and capacity to raise funds and the readiness of key supply chains, and will manage investment risk closely. Korea Economic Daily reported that the company expects demand to stay ahead of supply through 2030. Kyunghyang Shinmun reported that the news led to a buy-side sidecar on the KOSPI that day.

Daishin Securities leak hits 38,490 people’s personal data… hacking spreads across finance to Bithumb and Mirae Asset Management

Yonhap and Kyunghyang Shinmun reported that Lee Chan-jin, head of the Financial Supervisory Service, said on the 28th, ‘I understand that a broad hacking incident occurred at major financial investment firms yesterday,’ and urged attention to IT security. Chosun Ilbo reported that Daishin Securities said the personal and credit information of 38,490 customers had leaked, while Dong-A Ilbo wrote of more than 38,000 cases and Kyunghyang Shinmun gave 30,000 in its headline, so the reported scale of the leak differs by article. Korea Economic Daily reported that hacking damage was also confirmed at Mirae Asset Management, the first in the financial investment sector. Yonhap reported that Bithumb said its internal mobile system was hacked and personal information leaked, and that it is analyzing whether member information is included. Korea Economic Daily reported that Bithumb said there was no sign that customer assets were accessed. Yonhap reported that Cheong Wa Dae held an emergency response meeting chaired by the third deputy chief for national security, and that the government is starting security inspections of more than 170 key facilities, including telecommunications and internet data centers. Korea Economic Daily and Chosun Ilbo reported that 4.4 million accesses from North Korean IPs were detected in hacking attempts aimed at the financial sector. According to Dong-A Ilbo, the Financial Security Institute detected large-scale scanning from a range of addresses presumed to be North Korean IPs on the 21st and issued advisories to financial firms, and financial authorities said it is difficult to pin down a real hacker’s nationality from the country of an IP address alone.

Government accepts final loss in Elliott international investment dispute… award of about 153 billion won confirmed

Kyunghyang Shinmun and Korea Economic Daily reported that on the 28th the government decided not to file an annulment suit or similar action in a British court against the remanded arbitration ruling in the international investment dispute (ISDS) with the US private equity fund Elliott. According to Kyunghyang Shinmun, the Ministry of Justice and other ministries announced this in a joint press release, and the government will have to pay Elliott about 153 billion won. Yonhap reported that the government decided to accept the losing ruling, and Chosun Ilbo headlined it ‘final loss in remanded arbitration’.

Dong-A Ilbo reported that the ruling was handed down on the 30th of last month and that the government decided after several rounds of discussion with ministries, the law firm representing the government and outside experts. Dong-A Ilbo reported that the dispute, which began in 2018 over the 2015 merger of Samsung C&T and Cheil Industries, is now heading toward a close after eight years. Citing a Ministry of Justice announcement just after the ruling, SBS Biz reported that the order was to pay about $48.49 million (about 65.8 billion won) plus late-payment interest.

KOSPI surges 6.63% to close at 6,012.44… sidecar two days running, 6,000 regained after 10 trading days

The KOSPI surged on the 28th on SK Hynix’s earnings and its announcement of additional shareholder returns. Yonhap reported that a buy-side sidecar was triggered on the Korea Exchange’s main market at around 9:57 a.m. when the KOSPI surged into the 4% range, and Dong-A Ilbo and SBS reported it was the second day in a row, following the previous day. Kyunghyang Shinmun described the surge as ‘good news from SK Hynix’. Yonhap flashed that the intraday gain widened to the 6% range and the index regained the 6,000 level at around 11:02 a.m., after 10 trading days. The KOSPI’s closing figure that day was 6,012.44, up 6.63% from the previous trading day. The Kosdaq’s closing figure is 771.28.

Yonhap and SBS reported that with the KOSPI rebound, investor deposits, the standby money of the stock market, recovered to the 90 trillion won range, while debt-fueled investing fell below 30 trillion won. Yonhap’s Market View looked at whether the KOSPI’s rise would continue that morning on expectations for Hynix’s shareholder return plans.

NEC special counsel’s ballot verification starts late over People Power Party protest… agreement to film as recorded broadcast

Yonhap and Kyunghyang Shinmun reported that Lee Tae-han’s special counsel team on the 28th entered the Olympic Park Handball Stadium and began an on-site verification of ballots in connection with the ballot shortage in the June 3 local elections. Korea Economic Daily reported that the special counsel, police and the observer group entered the stadium without clashes with protesters. Yonhap reported that the verification began 3 hours later than scheduled because of the People Power Party’s protest, while Dong-A Ilbo wrote that it was delayed by 2 hours 50 minutes.

Yonhap flashed that the special counsel and the People Power Party’s observer group agreed to film the verification site as a recorded broadcast, and in its main article reported that filming by broadcasters was permitted. Yonhap reported that special counsel Lee Tae-han said, ‘We are well aware of public distrust in the electoral system,’ and would investigate with a sense of responsibility. According to Yonhap, the ruling party said the public verification of ballots should be an occasion to resolve the controversy and bring the occupation to an end.
