Sensex tanks 1,045 points and Nifty ends at 22,232, an 18-month low, as oil tops $104 and the Fed's minutes point to another hike

It is the biggest one-day fall in about three months. Foreign investors kept selling, the VIX jumped 10 per cent, and a day after the RBI's hike the rupee stayed near its record low.

Indian equities had their worst day in about three months on Thursday. The Sensex fell 1,045.46 points, or 1.44 per cent, to 71,593.24, and the Nifty lost 371.25 points, or 1.64 per cent, to 22,231.80, which 5paisa describes as an 18-month closing low. The volatility index rose about 10 per cent. Business Standard said crude oil topped $104 a barrel. Hindustan Times reported that the US administration has asked the Pentagon to prepare options for a possible resumption of strikes on Iran, which raised worries about energy supply. Wall Street had ended lower on Wednesday as Treasury yields climbed, and the minutes of the US Federal Reserve's last meeting indicated that another rate increase might be needed before the end of the year, according to Outlook Money.

Home factors added to the pressure. The foreign institutional investors who have been selling all year continued to sell, and analysts told the outlets the market was still adjusting to the RBI's shift to a tightening stance and its 25-basis-point hike a day earlier. The rupee remained under strain. Bloomberg, quoted by ThePrint, said it was within about 0.2 per cent of its record low of 96.97, with traders pointing to RBI intervention; we could not confirm the official close for the day. Banks may see a short-term boost to margins from the hike, Economic Times BFSI noted, but it also warned that costlier credit in the festive season could dent consumption. TCS began the earnings season with its second-quarter results and a dividend.

Nana Patekar, the 'Krantiveer' of Hindi cinema, dies at 75 of a cardiac arrest

Actor Nana Patekar died at 75 on Thursday after a cardiac arrest at his residence. NDTV reports that doctors tried CPR for 45 minutes; The Hindu says he was brought dead to hospital; The Indian Express carried a doctor's account of the "missed golden hour". His funeral will be held with state honours at his farmhouse near Pune, according to the Indian Express and NDTV.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Maharashtra Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis were among those who paid tribute. Hindustan Times noted that he spoke about mortality at his last public appearance four days earlier, and NDTV reports he texted a friend three hours before his death that he was not feeling well. He once left films to serve in the Army during the Kargil war, NDTV recalled.

PoliticsPress ✓ 6 outlets · FIR allegations are the police's, not findings

Three FIRs against Rahul and Priyanka Gandhi over the Delhi protest. CEC Gyanesh Kumar's security is raised to Z+

Delhi Police registered at least two fresh FIRs, and The Indian Express counts three, against Rahul Gandhi and Priyanka Gandhi for allegedly assaulting police officers and vandalising a police vehicle during the INDIA bloc protest. Priyanka Gandhi said police personnel scratched her. Congress general secretary K.C. Venugopal hit out at the police. The Hindustan Times, Mint and NDTV report that Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar's security was upgraded to Z+, with extra deployment at his home and office.

The opposition kept up the pressure. The Hindu reports that the INDIA bloc is pressing for presidential intervention, and NDTV that leaders headed to Rajghat saying they are not being allowed to protest. The Indian Express called it a "hat-trick" of protests, with a lunch at Connaught Place. The Delhi High Court has agreed to hear the Indian Youth Congress's plea for permission to protest at Jantar Mantar on 9 October, and Delhi Police are monitoring bus bookings ahead of a separate CJP protest.

Anne Carson, the Canadian poet and classicist, wins the Nobel Prize in Literature

The Swedish Academy awarded the 2026 Nobel Prize in Literature to Anne Carson of Canada for creating, in its words, "new forms for contemporary literature", Hindustan Times reported. Livemint describes her as a writer and poet with a "bold and inventive oeuvre". The announcement followed Physics (Francis Halzen), Chemistry (Henri Kagan and Kenso Soai) and Medicine (the optogenetics trio) this week. The Peace Prize comes on Friday and Economics on 12 October.

The Hindu notes that this year's prizes left some people out, a reference to the long debate over who gets credit in team science.

Technology · PolicyPress ✓ 5 outlets · Musk's allegation is his own, unproven

Elon Musk blames 'oligarchs' for blocking Starlink in India. New Delhi says its policy is fair

Elon Musk said "monopolistic chokeholds" and the interference of "oligarchs" were stopping Starlink from launching in India, The Hindu, The Indian Express and Hindustan Times reported. NDTV reported that the government rejected the claim that Starlink is stalled, calling its policy fair. Musk did not name anyone. A Hindustan Times explainer says the security clearance is taking long partly because of strict satellite rules shaped after the 26/11 attack.

A SpaceX executive had renewed the company's pitch earlier this week as approvals remained pending, The Hindu reported. No launch date has been announced. Prime Minister Modi, speaking at the India Mobile Congress, said 5G now reaches every district.
