Foreigners sold 1.75 trillion won in a day.
7,000 was gone in a day

KOSPI 6,941.39 (−0.89%), Kosdaq 919.92 (+2.98%). Foreign net selling of 1.7509 trillion won is the main-board figure

On the first trading day after the holiday, the KOSPI touched 7,000 in early trading, slid straight back and closed down 62.35 points at 6,941.39. Foreign investors sold a net 1.7509 trillion won on the main board (1.7689 trillion won including the alternative exchange NXT). The selling came down to two stocks: Samsung Electronics (272,000 won, −1.45%) and SK hynix (1,773,000 won, −3.69%). Individuals absorbed 740.6 billion won; institutions all but sat it out (−2 billion won).

At the same hour the Kosdaq rose 2.98% to 919.92. Secondary batteries, pharma and biotech, materials, parts and equipment, and a cybersecurity theme riding the bank-hacking news. Here foreigners (+109.4 billion won) and institutions (+100.2 billion won) bought together. Money that had crowded into chips changed seats within a single day.

Diplomacy · EconomyPress ✓ 5 outlets · government position attributed

Trump announced first, again. After Alaska gas, $8.4 billion in oil

The first project under last year's $350 billion pledge of investment in the United States ($150 billion in shipbuilding plus $200 billion in strategic investment) has been set: the Encinal combined-cycle gas plant in Texas (total cost $22.3 billion), and by one report the first $2.4 billion transfer is done. But on Sept. 30 (local time) President Trump claimed the two sides had agreed at the Sept. 22 New York summit to begin the investment, mentioning eight nuclear reactors and Alaska LNG, and on Oct. 2 he went further on social media, naming an $8.4 billion enhanced oil recovery (EOR) project that appears in no joint fact sheet. The Korean government says neither its participation in Alaska LNG nor the size of any stake has been decided.

Kim Sung-won, chair of the National Assembly's industry committee, was reported by Reuters to have said that proving commercial viability comes first. Korea pays, the United States announces: the pattern is now repeating for a third time.
